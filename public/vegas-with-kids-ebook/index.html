Strip Safety Tips

"Stay Hydrated: The Vegas heat is no joke, especially during summer. Keep your family hydrated with water and electrolyte drinks. Carry refillable bottles and take advantage of water stations. Don’t Walk The Strip in the Heat: If you are visiting in summer, avoid walking The Strip during the hottest part of the day. Use public transport or the monorail..."

Vegas Pool Seasons

"If hitting the pool is a priority for your family, keep in mind that pools in Las Vegas don't open for the season until April. Also, it can still be pretty chilly in April.
