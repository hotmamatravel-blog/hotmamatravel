<h2><em><span style="color: #000000;">Location:</span></em></h2>
<span style="color: #000000;">The Best Western Plus in Austin is conveniently located in the historic neighborhood of Travis Heights, just a few minutes from most Austin attractions and neighborhoods. Popular Downtown Austin district such as SOCO South Congress District, Rainey Street, 6th street and Lady Bird Lake are easily accessible from the Best Western Austin, making it well-situated for maximizing your time in Austin.<strong> <a href="http://www.kqzyfj.com/click-8336032-13039226" target="_blank" rel="nofollow noopener noreferrer"><span style="color: #ff0000;">Check rates</span></a></strong>.</span>
<h3><span style="color: #000000;">SOCO South Congress District</span></h3>
<span style="color: #000000;">Whether you call it SOCO or <strong><span style="color: #ff0000;"><a style="color: #ff0000;" href="https://www.austintexas.org/listings/south-congress-avenue-(soco)/4359/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">South Congress District</a></span></strong>, both locals as well as visitors agree that this is a lively cultural district worth spending some time exploring. The colorful street is sprinkled with mural art, eclectic shops, restaurants and food trucks as well as music and art venues. Known as the gateway to the capital city, views of the Capital Building can be seen gracefully rising from the end of the street. The great thing is the Best Western Plus Austin City Hotel is less than a mile from all the fun.</span>

<h3><span style="color: #000000;">6th Street</span></h3>
<span style="color: #000000;">Right in the core of Downtown Austin,<strong> <a href="https://6street.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><span style="color: #ff0000;">6th Street</span></a></strong> is a historic district that is well known for the number of entertainment venues, bars and clubs. For those, like us, who love live music, this is the street to visit. Just walking down the street you can hear the tunes of live music pouring out of from almost every building. Although many places are for a 21 and over crowd, afternoon and mornings offer family-friendly spots to have a meal, a drink and enjoy some live music like the famous and historical Driskill Hotel lounge.</span>
<h3><span style="color: #000000;"><a href="/best-western-plus-austin-city-hotel/" target="_blank" rel="attachment noopener wp-att-6874 noreferrer"><img class="aligncenter wp-image-6874" title="The Driskell Hotel Austin TX" src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/11/Driskell-e1511207309496.jpg" alt="The Driskell Hotel Austin TX" width="640" height="427" /></a>Rainey Street</span></h3>
<span style="color: #000000;">Once a tucked away street,<strong> <a href="https://www.austintexas.org/visit/entertainment-districts/rainey-street/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><span style="color: #ff0000;">Rainey Street</span></a></strong> is gaining in popularity as more historic homes that date back before the 1930’s are being renovated into hip bungalow </span><span style="color: #000000;">bars. It is no longer a secret hideout for locals. However, it has still maintained a very casual vibe that people love day or night.</span>
<h3><span style="color: #000000;">Lady Bird Lake</span></h3>
<span style="color: #000000;">This beautiful lake in the middle of Downtown Austin is actually a reservoir of the Colorado River. Although it is a major recreational center in the city of Austin, TX, it is also known for another major Austin attraction, the South Congress Bridge bats. During our visit to Austin, we spent a warm evening on a sunset cruise along the <strong><a href="https://austintexas.gov/page/lady-bird-lake" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><span style="color: #ff0000;">Lady Bird Lake</span></a></strong> as 1.5 million Mexican free-tail bats swarmed overhead. It was an <a href="/austin-bat-cruise/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><span style="color: #ff0000;">amazing experience that we will never forget</span></a>. It just makes things so much easier when you are traveling to have a place within your hotel to grab a quick bite or drink. However if we had felt like driving somewhere, it is also great to know that there are tons of options for <span style="color: #ff0000;"><strong><a style="color: #ff0000;" href="https://everythingaustinapartments.com/the-complete-guide-to-late-night-food-in-austin-including-delivery/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">late night food in Austin</a></strong></span>.</span>

<span style="color: #000000;">If you are looking for an Austin hotel with great free breakfast, look no further than Best Western Plus Austin City. It a great spot to grab a beer at the bar and some dinner. In addition, was also the site of the Best Western complimentary breakfast. Served buffet style, every morning guests can enjoy a hot deluxe breakfast right here in Opal Divine’s Austin Grill. There was so much variety in options and the fresh food was constantly being provided. No need to drive around hunting for a good breakfast in Austin, all you have to do is wake up. <h3><span style="color: #000000;"><a href="/best-western-plus-austin-city-hotel/" target="_blank" rel="attachment noopener wp-att-6877 noreferrer"><img class="aligncenter wp-image-6877" title="Complimentary Breakfast at Best Western Austin City Hotel" src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/11/Breakfast-e1511208736325.jpg" alt="Complimentary Breakfast at Best Western Austin City Hotel" width="640" height="359" /></a>Pool:</span></h3>
<span style="color: #000000;">When traveling with kids, it always helps to have a pool at the hotel. It makes for a great way to start out a hot day or to relax after a long one. At the Best Western Plus, the outdoor courtyard garden pool is complete with native Texas plants, relaxing sounds from the waterfall fountain, pool services and fresh towels. Perfect for traveling families, there is a traditional pool for those who like to have a good swim, but there is also a wading pool for the littlest of travelers. For this mama, wading pools are a rare and pleasant find. Of course there is also a hot tub to relax all those sore muscles from all that walking around sightseeing or after a good workout at the hotel gym.</span>
<h3><span style="color: #000000;">Exercise Facility:</span></h3>
<span style="color: #000000;">Even when I am traveling with my family, I like to squeeze in exercise whenever possible. It helps me have the energy I need to get through a busy day of sightseeing. It also helps me feel less guilty about all the food and drink I will be consuming throughout the day. Luckily, the Best Western Plus Austin City Hotel has an onsite fully-equipped gym open from 8:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m. Perfect for sneaking in some quick morning cardio or strength training.</span>
<h3><span style="color: #000000;">Laundry Facility:</span></h3>
<span style="color: #000000;">This might sound crazy, but I loved the Best Western Plus Austin City Hotel laundry facility! Hey, we were on a 60 day road trip and maintaining laundry on the road was a necessity. First of all, it is open 24 hours a day, so you don’t have to worry about the time. Secondly, the dryer was so nice and hot. I was able to bust out loads of laundry without taking too much time away from our day in Austin.</span>
<h3><span style="color: #000000;">Rooms:</span></h3>
<span style="color: #000000;">The rooms at the Best Western Plus Austin City Hotel are very nice and comfortable. In addition to the standard comforts of many Austin hotels, like cable television, air-conditioning, hairdryers, ironing boards and irons, and a coffee maker, the rooms also have free high-speed wifi, a microwave and mini refrigerator. When traveling with kids it is so helpful to have a mini-fridge and microwave to store milk or heat-up leftovers. However, what we really loved about our room was that we had a lovely view of downtown Austin cityscape.</span>
