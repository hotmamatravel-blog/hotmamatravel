### [Cosmopolitan Las Vegas](/go/cosmopolitan-hotel-las-vegas/)

* 3708 Las Vegas Blvd S, Las Vegas, NV 89109*

The Cosmopolitan Hotel in Las Vegas is the epitome of swanky sophistication. With [posh rooms](https://www.cosmopolitanlasvegas.com/resort/rooms-suites), [trendy bars](https://www.cosmopolitanlasvegas.com/lounges-bars), [luxury pools](https://www.cosmopolitanlasvegas.com/resort/pools), and [**top-notch dining**](/best-family-restaurants-in-las-vegas/), it's the place to see and be seen. Plus, its prime location on the Strip puts you within arm's reach of all the action, from shopping to shows to casinos. It's the perfect spot for a Vegas getaway that's equal parts luxury and excitement!

**The Terrace One Bedroom Suite, Fountain View** at the [Cosmopolitan](https://www.cosmopolitanlasvegas.com/), features panoramic views of the Las Vegas Skyline and famous fountain display from your own 110 square-foot spacious private terrace. This 610 square-foot suite has a living space and a bedroom separated by cool telescoping doors. Soak up the luxury with a dip in the Japanese soaking tub before drifting off to sleep in a plush King bed.

**Terrace Studio Fountain View** offers the same breathtaking [**Las Vegas Strip**](/las-vegas-strip-hotel-map/) and [**fountain views**](/free-family-activities-las-vegas/) as the One Bedroom Suite, except you get 620 square-feet with a 110 square-foot private terrace and no separation between the living space and the King bed.

**Terrace Suite Fountain View** is 910 square-foot suit with a 160 square-foot private terrace. In addition to the spacious King master bedroom, this suite also offers a living area complete with dining table, and two luxurious bathrooms, including a bubbler tub in the master bathroom.

**The Wraparound Terrace Suite &amp; Terrace Fountain View Suite** are the big daddies of the balcony suites at the Cosmopolitan. At 1,200 square-feet the suite features a sleek wet bar and sub-zero wine chiller, dining area, seating area, large luxurious bathroom, master bedroom with King bed, and a 410 square-foot wraparound terrace.

### [Signature at MGM Grand](/go/signature-mgm-grand-las-vegas/)

* 145 E Harmon Ave, Las Vegas, NV 89109*

Signature MGM is all about that luxury life in Las Vegas. Think spacious suites, private balconies with killer views, and a vibe that's both upscale and laid-back. You've got all the amenities you could dream of, from [**awesome pools**](/best-family-pools-in-vegas/) to [**spas**](/pregnant-las-vegas/) to [swanky bars](https://signaturemgmgrand.mgmresorts.com/en/nightlife/the-lounge.html).

 It's tucked away from the hustle and bustle of the Strip, giving you a serene oasis to retreat to after a day of [**Vegas adventures**](/kid-friendly-day-trips-las-vegas/). But don't worry, you're still just a quick walk or ride away from all the action when you're ready to dive back in. It's the perfect blend of tranquility and excitement in Sin City!

**One Bedroom Balcony Strip View Suite** allows you to enjoy breathtaking views of the [**Las Vegas Strip**](/walking-las-vegas-strip/) from your own private balcony. You also get to indulge in a 900 square-foot suite, complete with a full kitchen, separate living space, a master bedroom with a King bed, and a spacious bathroom with a whirlpool spa tub and separate shower.

**One Bedroom Balcony Suite** is a 900 square-foot suite featuring  private balcony, a master bedroom with deluxe King bed, an oversized bathroom whirlpool spa tub and separate shower, separate living area, and full kitchen.

**Deluxe Balcony Strip View Suite **has a private balcony with gorgeous views of the strip connected to 550 square-foot room, featuring one King bed as well as a Queen sofa bed, sitting area, and whirlpool tub.

**Deluxe Balcony Suite**  at [Signature MGM](https://signaturemgmgrand.mgmresorts.com/en.html), features a private balcony and a 550 square-feet room with a one King bed, full kitchenette and a whirlpool spa tub.

### [MGM Grand Las Vegas](/go/mgm-grand-las-vegas-expedia/)

* 3799 S Las Vegas Blvd, Las Vegas*

The Mansion at MGM, where luxury meets exclusivity in the heart of Las Vegas. This hidden gem offers a retreat like no other, with opulent villas, personalized service, and unparalleled privacy. Picture yourself surrounded by lush gardens, relaxing in your private pool, or indulging in gourmet cuisine prepared by [**world-class chefs**](/celebrity-chef-restaurants-las-vegas/) at restaurants like [L'Atelier de Joël Robuchon](https://mgmgrand.mgmresorts.com/en/restaurants/latelier-joel-robuchon-french-restaurant.html) and [Joël Robuchon](https://mgmgrand.mgmresorts.com/en/restaurants/joel-robuchon-french-restaurant.html).

 You're just steps away from the vibrant energy of the Strip, yet worlds apart in terms of tranquility and sophistication. It's the ultimate haven for those seeking the utmost in luxury and discretion during their Vegas getaway.

**The Mansion at MGM Grand** is like escaping to a little piece of Tuscany in the heart of Las Vegas. Inspired by the 18th-century countryside, Tuscan villas, The Mansion is the ultimate in luxury and service. [The Mansion](https://mgmgrand.mgmresorts.com/en/hotel/the-mansion.html) houses 29 Mediterranean-inspired accommodations ranging from 2,400 square feet to 12,000 square-feet and gives access to Mansion facilities, including an outdoor Italian Garden, pool, the Great Hall, Boardroom, the Mansion Dining Room, and the Mansion's private spa treatment rooms.

In addition, there are 21 Atrium Villas, featuring balcony or veranda access with gorgeous views of the Italian gardens, as well as 8 private Garden villas that offer direct garden access.&nbsp;Villas offer anywhere to one to four bedrooms to easily accommodate your group. However, you do have to qualify to book one of these rooms by calling for inquiries at 877.225.2121.
