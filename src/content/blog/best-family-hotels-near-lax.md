**Relaxing Stay Just Minutes from LAX:**
Located just a hop, skip, and a stroller-push from Los Angeles International Airport, Sonesta LAX puts your family at the center of comfort and convenience. Plus, Sonesta LAX offers a free 24-hour shuttle that runs every 20 minutes! Whether you're arriving late, leaving early, or just need a travel breather, this hotel delivers a peaceful urban oasis with soundproofed rooms that keep runway noise where it belongs on the runway.

**Spacious Rooms Designed for Families:**
At Sonesta LAX, space isn’t a luxury, it’s a standard. Families can stretch out in roomy suites with plush beds, cozy corners, and plenty of space for kid gear, toys, and maybe even a sibling dance party. Modern furnishings and calming tones set the scene for a restful night before (or after) a long travel day. We enjoyed two connecting rooms that also included a pullout sofa, making plenty of sleeping space for our big family.

**Kid-Friendly Perks &amp; Travel-Smart Amenities:**
From free airport shuttles to in-room cribs and rollaway beds, Sonesta makes traveling with kids feel less like herding cats and more like checking into comfort. The 24-hour front desk and friendly staff are pros at handling family requests.

**Dining That Works for Real-Life Families:**
Between the on-site restaurants and the casual eats, your crew won’t go hungry. Early flight? Grab-and-go options are available at the Boulevard Market Cafe. Late-night snack cravings? There’s something to munch no matter the hour. Bonus: the homemade brick oven pizza is a winner for the kiddos while parents grab a much-needed cocktail. Cheers to surviving TSA with toddlers!

For a full-service sit-down dining experience, you have two options for dinner. Century Taproom and Lounge is a darker upscale-pub atmosphere with a 60's dinner-house flair. The food is pricey, but that's to be expected in L.A. near the airport. Along with steaks, you can also get salads, sandwiches, pastas, yes, brick oven PIZZA.

Yokoso Sushi Bar is a must if you want authentic and delicious Japanese creations. Chef Kuni is a legit Sushi chef with all the training! I mean, he spent a year perfecting just the rice! He only picks the freshest ingredients to create masterpieces you and your family will love.

Breakfast time is best enjoyed at The Landing, a cute Art Deco, vintage LA diner. Here, you can indulge in the breakfast buffet with hot selections, like bacon, omelets, pancakes, potatoes, and more, as well as cold items, like fruit, yogurts, cereals, and pastries. There are also a selection of menu options and a kids plate available for order. Parents can sip on Mimosas and Bloody Marys too! Giving Palm Springs vibes, it is a great place to start the day.

**Poolside Chill &amp; Airport Thrills:**
After a long flight (or just surviving L.A. traffic), the outdoor pool offers a refreshing reward. Lounge with your littles or take a dip to shake off the travel cobwebs. And if you’re plane-obsessed like most kids (and let’s be honest—some grown-ups too), the rooftop views of incoming flights are oddly mesmerizing.

**Smart Family Stay Near LAX:**
Sonesta Los Angeles Airport’s unbeatable location makes it the perfect launchpad or landing zone for your family adventures. With comfort, convenience, and kid-friendly touches in all the right places, this hotel proves that airport stays don’t have to be boring or chaotic.
