## Description of Hotels on The Vegas Strip

<div class="ugb-heading__bottom-line"></div></div></div></div>

**[Mandalay Bay:](https://mandalaybay.mgmresorts.com/en.html)** A luxurious escape with tropical-themed ambiance and sprawling pools.

[**Luxor**:](https://luxor.mgmresorts.com/en.html) Iconic pyramid structure offering a unique Egyptian-themed experience.

**[Excalibur:](https://excalibur.mgmresorts.com/en.html)** Transports guests to a medieval realm with its castle-like architecture.

**[MGM Grand:](https://mgmgrand.mgmresorts.com/en.html)** Boasts grandeur and excitement with its extensive amenities and entertainment options.

**[New York New York:](https://newyorknewyork.mgmresorts.com/en.html)** Evokes the charm of the Big Apple with its skyline replica and diverse attractions.

**[Park MGM:](https://parkmgm.mgmresorts.com/en.html)** Offers modern elegance and sophistication with a relaxed atmosphere.

**[Cosmopolitan:](https://www.cosmopolitanlasvegas.com/)** Dazzles guests with contemporary design, trendy bars, and vibrant nightlife.

**[Aria:](https://aria.mgmresorts.com/en.html)** A sleek and stylish destination featuring cutting-edge technology and luxurious accommodations.

**[Bellagio:](https://bellagio.mgmresorts.com/en.html)** Enchants visitors with its iconic fountains, elegant décor, and world-class entertainment.

**[Hardrock Hotel:](https://www.hardrockhotelcasinolasvegas.com/)** The former Mirage, now transitioning into the Hard Rock Hotel Las Vegas, blends iconic tropical themes with the new rock 'n' roll flair.

**[Caesars Palace:](https://www.caesars.com/caesars-palace)** Exudes opulence and history with its Roman-inspired architecture and upscale amenities.

**[Treasure Island:](https://treasureisland.com/)** Promises swashbuckling adventures with its pirate-themed attractions and lively atmosphere.

**[Planet Hollywood:](https://www.caesars.com/planet-hollywood)** Delivers star-studded glamour and entertainment in the heart of the Strip.

**[Paris:](https://www.caesars.com/paris-las-vegas)** Transports guests to the City of Light with its romantic ambiance and iconic landmarks.

**[Harrah's:](https://www.caesars.com/harrahs-las-vegas)** Offers classic Vegas vibes and a bustling casino experience.

**[LINQ:](https://www.caesars.com/linq)** Boasts a vibrant atmosphere with its modern design, high-energy entertainment, and the LINQ Promenade.

**[Horseshoe:](https://www.caesars.com/horseshoe-las-vegas)** Exudes excitement and luxury with its upscale gaming and entertainment offerings.

**[Flamingo:](https://www.caesars.com/flamingo-las-vegas)** Captivates with its pink allure and iconic tropical gardens.

**[Venetian:](https://www.venetianlasvegas.com/)** Evokes Venice's romantic ambiance with its stunning architecture, gondola rides, and upscale shopping.

**[Wynn:](https://www.wynnlasvegas.com/)** Epitomizes luxury and sophistication with its elegant design and world-class amenities.

**[Encore:](https://www.wynnlasvegas.com/)** Offers a refined and upscale experience with luxurious accommodations and impeccable service.
