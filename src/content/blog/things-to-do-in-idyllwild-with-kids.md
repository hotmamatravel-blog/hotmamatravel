<p class="has-drop-cap">Tucked away in the San Jacinto Mountains in Riverside County, CA, is a quaint little town bursting with <a href="/best-restaurants-in-idyllwild/" data-type="post" data-id="20178"><strong>amazing restaurants</strong></a>, friendly people, and a delightful art culture. In the winter, Idyllwild is one of the most convenient places near Los Angeles and Orange Counties to experience snow (which is one of the main attractions for us).</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:quote {"className":"is-style-large"} -->
<blockquote><!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Any time of year, Idyllwild is a wonderful place to escape the city, relax and enjoy the fresh mountain air.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph --></blockquote>
<!-- /wp:quote -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We <a href="/best-idyllwild-cabin-rentals/" data-type="post" data-id="20175" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener"><strong>book a cozy cabin</strong></a> and take a family trip to Idyllwild at least once a year. One thing we've discovered is that there are lots of fun things to do in Idyllwild with kids. <strong>Our kids really love it there! </strong>It is an extremely family-friendly (and budget-friendly) weekend escape.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>So follow me and discover some of our favorite kid-friendly activities in Idyllwild. Here are some of the reasons I think you will fall in love with Idyllwild.</p>

<p><em>Town Center Area, North Circle Drive</em></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Most of the shops in Idyllwild are located on North Circle Drive. This is the quaint town center filled with unique local shops and restaurants. We love wandering the streets and peeking into some of our favorite shops in search of fun treasures and magic crystals. Some of our favorites include <a href="https://www.merkabaonline.com/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Merkaba</a>, Far East International, and Idyllwild Gift Shop. For something truly unique, go check out the <a href="http://funkybazaarofficial.com/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Funky Bazaar</a>. It is filled with everything from the creepy to the unique and vintage, and the just plain weird.</p>

<h3 id="idyllwild-historical-society"><a href="/go/historical-society-museum-idyllwild/" linkid="18133">Idyllwild Area Historical Society Museum</a></h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><em>54470 N Circle Dr, Idyllwild-Pine Cove, CA 92549</em></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Located in the center of Idyllwild, this charming museum offers a delightful glimpse into the town's rich history. With a friendly and knowledgeable local guide on hand, visitors have the opportunity to have their questions answered and gain deeper insights into the exhibits. Best of all, admission to the museum is completely free, allowing everyone to enjoy the experience. The collection prominently features a captivating array of photographs that showcase the early days of the town, offering a captivating visual journey through time.</p>

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>Take The Aerial Tramway To Palm Springs</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Taking the <strong><a linkid="12859" href="/go/palm-springs-aerial-tramway-palm-springs-ca/">Palm Springs Aerial Tramway</a></strong> is one of those experiences that instantly makes kids’ jaws drop, and parents secretly love just as much. In about 10 minutes, you travel from the desert floor up to cooler mountain air, with rotating tram cars that give everyone a 360-degree view (yes, kids notice).</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p> It’s equal parts ride, science lesson, and adventure, and a great way to break up the heat if you’re visiting in warmer months. Once you reach the top, you can explore nature trails, grab a bite to eat, and let kids burn off energy before heading back down. The tram pairs perfectly with other family-friendly activities in the area, which I’ve rounded up in my <strong><a href="/palm-springs-with-kids/" data-type="post" data-id="4302" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Things to Do in Palm Springs with Kids</a></strong> guide.</p>
