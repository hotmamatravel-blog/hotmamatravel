<p class="has-drop-cap">When it comes to all-inclusive resorts in Cancun, the Beach Palace Resort is an excellent choice for families.&nbsp; First of all, everything you need for your vacation is included in the price. From gourmet dining to premium beverages and snacks, you and your family can indulge in unlimited food and drink options throughout your stay. <h4 id="medieval-times"><a href="/go/iberostar-selection-cancun/" linkid="17061">Iberostar Selection Cancun</a></h4>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><em> 368C+H7, km 17, Blvd. Kukulcan, Zona Hotelera, 77500 Cancún, Q.R., Mexico</em></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"fontSize":"small"} -->
<p class="has-small-font-size">No Family Suites  -  Kids' Club  -  Teens' Club  -  Babysitting Services  -  Kids' Disco  -  Kids' Pool -  Water Park  -  Nightly Live Entertainment  -  Water Sports  -   Golf  -  Spa  -  Onsite Restaurants</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"dropCap":true} -->
<p class="has-drop-cap">Iberostar Selection Cancun is a luxurious all-inclusive resort located in the heart of Cancun's Hotel Zone. The resort offers a range of amenities and activities to ensure guests have a memorable and relaxing vacation that includes a variety of dining options, including several à la carte restaurants and a buffet-style restaurant serving international cuisine. There are also several bars and lounges serving premium drinks and cocktails.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Accommodations at Iberostar Selection Cancun include spacious guest rooms and suites, many with ocean views, and some with private swim-up pools. All rooms are well-appointed with modern amenities, such as air conditioning, flat-screen TVs, and complimentary Wi-Fi. However, there are not any family suites. The largest rooms are doubles, so big families will need to book two rooms.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>For families, Iberostar Selection Cancun offers a range of activities and amenities, including a kids' and teens' club, Star Camp, for ages 4 to 17 that is not only fun, but educational as well, with cooking classes and workshops. There is also mini-golf and other daily activities and entertainment options for both children and adults, such as dance lessons, beach volleyball, and live music.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The kids' pool and water park, Aqua Fun, is where you kids will have a blast sliding down the pirate ship slides, swim under a shark, and more. Plus, there are daily pool and beachside activities and floating water parks to keep the fun going all day long. The resort's beach is a highlight, with soft sand and crystal-clear water. Guests can also take advantage of water sports activities, such as kayaking, windsurfing, and snorkeling.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Other amenities at Iberostar Selection Cancun include a fitness center, spa, and tennis courts. Every night you and your family can look forward to live entertainment, shows and even a kids' mini disco party!</p>

<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"dropCap":true} -->
<p class="has-drop-cap">Finest Playa Mujeres is a luxurious all-inclusive resort located on a secluded stretch of white sand beach in Playa Mujeres, just north of Cancun in Mexico. The resort offers a range of amenities and activities, including a variety of dining options, with 12 international restaurants, including Mexican, Italian, French, Asian, and more. There are also several bars and lounges serving premium drinks and cocktails to ensure guests have a memorable and relaxing vacation.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Accommodations at Finest Playa Mujeres include spacious family suites, some with private pools and all with private balconies or terraces. The roooms feature modern amenities, such as air conditioning, flat-screen TVs, and complimentary Wi-Fi.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Finest Playa Mujeres offers a huge range of fun activities and amenities for kids and families. The Imagine Kids' Club has three levels for toddlers all the the way up to teens. The Mini Club is for ages 0 to 6, however parents are required to stay for kids 2 and under. This Mini Club has age appropriate games and activities as well as a mini pool playground with water slides. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The Maxi Club is for kids 7 to 11 and offers a comfortable indoor area that includes bathrooms and a multipurpose hall filled with activities like video games, board games, books and more. There is also an outdoor area with games like ping pong and air hockey. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The Teens' Club is for ages 12 to 15 and includes a full list of activities morning, afternoon and night that teens will love, like water balloon fights, bonfires, movie nights, kayaking, snorkeling, sailing, windsurfing, paddle boarding, water polo, table tennis, volleyball, and tons more. What is also really cool, is that Finest Playa Mujeres offers baby care amenities and babysitting services.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>There are 12 pools including the children's pool, water park, and playground. Additionally, there are also a ton daily activities and entertainment options for both children and adults, such as dance lessons, beach volleyball, and live music, nightly entertainment, land sports, and so much more. </p>
