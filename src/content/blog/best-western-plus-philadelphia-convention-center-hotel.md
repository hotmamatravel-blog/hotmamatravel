<h2><em><span style="color: #000000;">Where to Eat in Philadelphia:</span></em></h2>
<span style="color: #000000;">There are many Philadelphia hotels to choose from. However, if you happen to be attending an event at the Philadelphia Convention Center, then The Best Western Plus Philadelphia Convention Center Hotel would be the perfect location. It is literally right across the street. We weren’t planning on visiting the Philadelphia Convention Center, but we were planning on eating a lot. I mean you kind of have to when you visit Philly. Happily, the hotel is located in Center City Philadelphia surrounded by good eats. If you love Asian cuisine, then you will be a happy traveler as The Best Western Plus Philadelphia Convention Center Hotel is in the heart of China town. In fact the <a href="http://www.vietnampalace.net/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><span style="color: #ff0000;">Vietnam Palace</span></a>, known as the jewel of China Town is less than a mile away.</span>

<span style="color: #000000;">In addition to the city’s best Asian dishes, the hotel is also across the street from the famous <a href="http://www.readingterminalmarket.org/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><span style="color: #ff0000;">Reading Terminal Market</span></a>. <h3><span style="color: #000000;">Parking:</span></h3>
<span style="color: #000000;">For a big city hotel, parking wasn’t too bad. Like many hotels in Philadelphia, it cost to park your car overnight at the hotel. To self-park it is $20 per day and you can come and go as you please. However, the parking lot is two blocks away from the hotel. If you are not familiar with the city, I recommend taking advantage of the valet for $35 a day. For unloading there is a convenient spot right in front of the lobby entrance. The front desk will give you a 20 minute pass for your car upon check in, so you can get all your stuff and people out before parking it the off-site lot. Not the best parking situation, but not the worst in our experience given the location in the center of a big city.</span>
<h3><span style="color: #000000;">Amenities:</span></h3>
<span style="color: #000000;">The Best Western Plus Philadelphia Convention Center Hotel is 100% smoke-free and is stocked with many of the modern amenities to suit most travelers’ comfort needs. For instance, there isn’t a pool, but there is a 24 hour fully equipped exercise facility. For those on business, there is a 24 hour business center with printers, faxes, computers and a photocopy machine.</span>

<span style="color: #000000;">Like many big city hotels, there is a need to maximize space. Although the rooms are smaller, they have many in-room comforts. Happily, every Best Western Plus we stayed at had HBO, allowing us to never miss an episode of Game of Thrones the entire summer! The Philadelphia Convention Center Hotel was no different. Every room has a refrigerator, which is great for storing baby’s milk or mama’s beer. If you are traveling with babies, there are cribs available for your room. Let me just say, the view of the Philadelphia from the rooms is breathtaking. After a long day of sightseeing, it was so nice to come back to the room and look at the city aglow.</span>
