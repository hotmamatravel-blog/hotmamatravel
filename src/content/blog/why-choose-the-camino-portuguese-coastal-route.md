When you set out to explore Portugal, the Coastal Route offers an enchanting alternative to the more frequented paths. Known for its&nbsp;**serene beaches, historical towns, and lush landscapes,**&nbsp;this route provides a sensory feast for adventurers and culture enthusiasts alike.

Moreover, it is a well trodden path by Camino de Santiago pilgrims. With this in mind, let’s take a deep dive into why the [Camino de Santigo by way of Portugal](https://santiagoways.com/en/camino-de-santiago-routes/portuguese-camino-coastal-route/from-porto/) is so popular.

## From Santarem to Coimbra: A Journey Through History and Beauty

<div class="ugb-heading__bottom-line"></div></div></div></div>

<div class="wp-block-uagb-advanced-heading uagb-block-42a30e40">

### 1. Santarem – The Gothic Capital of Portugal

</div>

Your Santarem to Coimbra  journey begins near the **Tagus River **in Santarem itself. Known as the Gothic capital of Portugal, Santarem is dotted with **fascinating historical sites** that beckon history buffs. The Igreja de Santa Clara and the São Francisco Convent stand as testaments to the city’s rich past, with their intricate architecture and storied walls. Stroll through the Jardim das Portas do Sol, a park offering panoramic views of the river and the surrounding plains.

<div class="wp-block-uagb-advanced-heading uagb-block-eee3b864">

### 2. The Coastal Charm

</div>

Moving north from Santarem, the route hugs the Atlantic coast, **offering stunning ocean views** that are a constant companion as you travel. The cool sea breeze and the sound of waves create a tranquil backdrop for reflection and relaxation. Stop by the picturesque beach towns like Nazaré, where the traditions of fishing communities come alive and the waves are legendary among surfers worldwide.

<div class="wp-block-uagb-advanced-heading uagb-block-c4cddf71">

### 3. Figueira da Foz – The Beach Gateway

</div>

Midway, you reach Figueira da Foz, celebrated for its expansive sandy beaches and vibrant nightlife. This town is a perfect spot to enjoy sunbathing, windsurfing, or simply a leisurely evening walk along the promenade. The Serra da Boa Viagem, a nearby hill range, offers hiking opportunities and breathtaking views of the Atlantic coastline.

<div class="wp-block-uagb-advanced-heading uagb-block-3670c4e4">

### 4. Coimbra – A Blend of History and Modernity

</div>

As you approach Coimbra, the journey takes a scholarly turn. Home to one of the oldest universities in Europe, Coimbra is a city where **history and knowledge have intertwined** for centuries. The University of Coimbra, a UNESCO World Heritage site, is famous for its stunning Baroque library, the Biblioteca Joanina. Wander through the city’s narrow streets, and you’ll find a vibrant blend of old and new, where traditional fado music fills the air and contemporary cafes serve up local and international flavors.

## Why This Route?

<div class="ugb-heading__bottom-line"></div></div></div></div>

Choosing the Portuguese Coastal Route means stepping into a world where every corner holds a story, every landscape inspires awe, and every town welcomes you with open arms. It’s not just a journey; it’s an experience that enriches the soul.

So, why wait? Pack your bags, set your sights on the Portuguese Coastal Route, and prepare for an adventure that combines the best of nature, culture, and history. Whether you’re a Christian pilgrim, solo explorer, a family, or a group of friends, this route promises memories that will last a lifetime.
