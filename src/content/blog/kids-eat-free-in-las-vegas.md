### Denny's

Multiple locations along the Strip

Denny’s is a classic family favorite, and in Las Vegas, it’s no different. Kids can eat free at participating Denny’s locations with the purchase of an adult entrée on Tuesdays from 4 p.m. to 10 p.m. Enjoy a hearty breakfast any time of the day or a comforting dinner with the family.

#### 3. **The Buffet at Excalibur**

**Location:** Excalibur Hotel and Casino

Who doesn’t love a good buffet? At the Excalibur Buffet, kids 4 and under eat free with a paying adult. This medieval-themed hotel offers a wide variety of dishes, ensuring there’s something for everyone in the family.

- **Kid-Friendly Menu:** Mac and cheese, pizza, chicken fingers, and a dessert station that’ll have the kids grinning ear to ear.

- **Deal Details:** Kids 4 and under eat free with a paying adult.

#### 4. **Café Rio**

**Location:** Various locations including Flamingo Rd and Eastern Ave

For a taste of fresh Mexican food, head over to Café Rio. On certain days, kids eat free with the purchase of an adult entrée. Enjoy flavorful dishes made from scratch while keeping the kids satisfied.

- **Kid-Friendly Menu:** Quesadillas, tacos, nachos, and more.

- **Deal Details:** Kids eat free on select days; check your local Café Rio for specific details.

#### 5. **TGI Fridays**

**Location:** Various locations

TGI Fridays offers a welcoming atmosphere and a great deal for families. Kids can eat free with the purchase of an adult entrée on select days. It’s a perfect spot for a casual, fun meal after a busy day of sightseeing.

- **Kid-Friendly Menu:** Mini cheeseburgers, pasta, chicken fingers, and more.

- **Deal Details:** Check with your location for specific days and times.

#### 6. **Tony Roma’s**

**Location:** Fremont Street

Located in the heart of downtown Las Vegas on Fremont Street, Tony Roma’s is known for its ribs and casual dining experience. On certain days, kids eat free with the purchase of an adult entrée. After dinner, take a stroll and enjoy the vibrant Fremont Street Experience.

- **Kid-Friendly Menu:** Ribs, burgers, chicken tenders, and more.

- **Deal Details:** Check with the restaurant for specific days and times.

### Final Tips for Dining Out with Kids

- **Check Ahead:** Always call ahead or check the restaurant’s website to confirm the kids eat free deal and its conditions.

- **Timing is Everything:** Deals often apply on specific days and times, so plan your meals accordingly.

- **Explore New Flavors:** Encourage your kids to try something new. Las Vegas is a melting pot of cuisines!

Las Vegas isn’t just a playground for adults—it’s a fantastic destination for families too. With these spots where kids eat free in Las Vegas, you can enjoy delicious meals without worrying about your budget. Bon appétit and happy travels! 🍽️👨‍👩‍👧‍👦
