### [Four Queens Hotel and Casino](/go/four-queens-hotel-las-vegas/)

* &nbsp;202 E Fremont St, Las Vegas, NV 89101*

If you're looking for a hotel in Las Vegas that does not charge resort fees, then you might want to check out the Four Queens Hotel and Casino. This classic Downtown Vegas hotel has been around since 1966 and has managed to keep its charm and vintage Vegas vibe intact.

The Four Queens boasts over 600 rooms and suites, offering fantastic views of the city. The rooms are spacious and clean, with comfortable beds that will ensure a good night's sleep after a long day of [**exploring Las Vegas**](/affordable-family-activities-las-vegas/). And best of all, you won't have to pay any pesky resort fees!

There are plenty of entertainment options for guests to enjoy. Whether you're into gambling, [**live music**](/kid-friendly-live-music-las-vegas/), or [**delicious food**](/best-family-restaurants-in-las-vegas/), this hotel has got you covered. The casino floor has all the classic table games, slots, and video poker machines you could want, while the restaurants offer everything from steaks to pizza.

And if you're looking for a bit of excitement, be sure to check out the [Fremont Street Experience](https://vegasexperience.com/), which is just steps away from the hotel. Also the [Mob Museum](https://themobmuseum.org/) is an easy walk and a fascinating place to learn about Vegas history. This pedestrian mall is lined with casinos, restaurants, and shops, and is known for its light shows and live music performances.

So, there you have it, the [Four Queens Hotel and Casino](https://www.fourqueens.com/) is a great option for anyone looking for a hotel in Las Vegas that does not charge resort fees. With its comfortable rooms, excellent amenities, and unbeatable location, you're sure to have a blast in Sin City.
