<span style="color: #000000;">I love to stretch events out as long as possible. Like most birthdays in my family, they are spread out throughout the month and the U.S.A’s BDay is no exception. The last few years we’ve kept the 4th of July pretty mellow with a simple BBQ/pool day and sparklers at home, but this year we went big. We loaded up the family RV and headed up the mountain for an unforgettable weekend long celebration. This year we celebrated 4th of July at Big Bear Lake.
</span>

<a href="/4th-of-july-at-big-bear-lake/"><img class="aligncenter wp-image-743" src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/07/DSCN6473-1024x687.jpg" alt="4th of July at Big Bear Lake" width="640" height="429" /></a>
<h2><span style="color: #000000;"><em><strong>Keep the Cost in Check:</strong></em></span></h2>
<span style="color: #000000;">Big Bear is not huge, but their 4th of July celebration is, so most accommodations book up fast. Week booked our stay a whole year in advance, so the best tip to save money is to prepare early. The restaurants don’t hike up prices or anything and the firework display is free to the public so it is possible to keep the cost in check.</span>
<h2><span style="color: #000000;"><em><strong>Where to stay in Big Bear CA:</strong></em></span></h2>
<h3><span style="color: #ff0000;"><a style="color: #ff0000;" href="http://www.bigbearshores.com/index.html"><strong>Big Bear Shores RV</strong> Resort</a></span>:</h3>
<span style="color: #000000;">Now that we have the RV we try to use it as much as possible and honestly the RV resort is the most beautiful spot on the lake. The views are unreal. The sites are privately owned and some owners rent out their lots. The lots are impeccably maintained, many with built in fireplaces and BBQs. It feels like a wilderness oasis. For information check out my<strong><span style="color: #ff0000;"> <a style="color: #ff0000;" href="/big-bear-shores-rv-resort-with-kids/">Hot Rving in Big Bear</a></span></strong> post.</span>
<span style="color: #000000;">During the 4th of July, there are a number of festivities at the resort such as a chili cook-off, a decorating competition, parade of decked out golf carts and free hot dogs at the club house. It really brings about a certain comradely among the campers as everyone gets in the patriotic spirit mingles throughout the weekend.</span>

<a href="/4th-of-july-at-big-bear-lake/" rel="attachment wp-att-6049"><img class="aligncenter wp-image-6049" title="4th of July decorations of camp site Big Bear" src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/07/4BBL.jpg" alt="4th of July decorations of camp site Big Bear" width="640" height="640" /></a>
<h3><span style="color: #ff0000;"><a style="color: #ff0000;" href="http://www.bigbearshores.com/amenities.html" target="_blank" rel="nofollow noopener">Amenities:</a></span></h3>
<span style="color: #000000;">The owners of the lots have more access to all the amenities such as private slips at the marina and the gym, but visitors have access to the pool, the tennis and basketball courts, ping pong, foosball and of course the gorgeous lake!</span>

<span style="color: #ff0000;"><a style="color: #ff0000;" href="http://www.bigbear.com/places-to-stay/" target="_blank" rel="nofollow noopener"><strong>Other accommodations in Big Bear</strong></a></span>
<h2><span style="color: #000000;"><em><strong>Places to Eat in Big Bear with Kids:</strong></em></span></h2>
<span style="color: #000000;">During the 4th of July BBQ is a must and the Big Bear’s Rotary Club’s 4th of July BBQ, located at the Marina Resort, is a crowd pleaser. With a choice of chicken, steak, hot dog or hamburger, each meal comes with potato salad and a choice of soda or water. There is a beer and wine garden for those who need something more refreshing. The BBQ offers some of the best views of the firework show as well as games, crafts and entertainment for those who attend.</span>

<span style="color: #000000;">In the village there are plenty of delicious options for the hungry. On the 4th we spent the day in town and tried to have lunch at one favorite Mexican restaurant in the area,</span> <span style="color: #ff0000;"><a style="color: #ff0000;" href="http://www.eljacalitogrill.com/" target="_blank" rel="nofollow noopener">El Jacalito</a></span>. <span style="color: #000000;">I say tried, because we were having a wonderful time until a sudden thunderstorm unleashed it’s fury on us as soon as our food hit the table, drowning my delicious Shrimp a la Plancha.</span>

<a href="/4th-of-july-at-big-bear-lake/"><img class="aligncenter wp-image-759" title="Big Bear Restaurant Patio with Kids" src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/07/DSCN6508-1024x684.jpg" alt="Big Bear Restaurant Patio with Kids" width="640" height="428" /></a>

<span style="color: #000000;">Since it was pretty busy this holiday weekend and the restaurants are small, the wait lists were long so we didn’t get to dine at our favorite</span> <span style="color: #ff0000;"><a style="color: #ff0000;" href="http://www.peppercorngrille.com/" target="_blank" rel="nofollow noopener">Peppercorn’s</a></span>, <span style="color: #000000;">but we did get to discover how delicious</span> <span style="color: #ff0000;"><a style="color: #ff0000;" href="http://www.bigbeardining.com/paolis.html" target="_blank" rel="nofollow noopener">Paoli’s</a></span> <span style="color: #000000;">was as they saved the day serving us drowned rats delicious Italian dishes.</span>

<h2><span style="color: #000000;"><em><strong>Where to Grab a Drink:</strong></em></span></h2>
<span style="color: #000000;">At our RV site we had a fully stocked bar. It is a must when camping to have excess libations. We started every morning with a loaded Bloody Mary. HotDaddy’s secret Bloody Mary recipe, garnished with pickles, okra and olives. Starting my morning with one of these bad boys made me more alert, happier and nicer. Sorry coffee. Good Morning and Happy 4th!</span>
<span style="color: #000000;">It is fun to grab a drink in town and there are some great places to quench your thirst. El Jacalito has a great happy hour, a good selection of beer by the pitcher and some amazing margaritas. Luckily, most places in Big Bear serve beer by the pitcher which is great of us lushes. There is also the new Big Bear Brewing Company that just opened this year!</span>

<h2><span style="color: #000000;"><em><strong>Fun Things to Do in Big Bear with Kids:</strong></em></span></h2>
<span style="color: #000000;">Independence Day is a big deal in Big Bear. The Firework Spectacular is a 30 minute fantastic display above the7 mile long lake is rated top five in California. It is free and easily viewed throughout Big Bear Valley. It was beautiful to see the boats floating out on the lake as the fireworks lit up the night sky. For the accompanying music to the show, tune into 93.3 KBear for a truly amazing experience.</span>

<span style="color: #000000;"><strong>Other Festivities included:</strong></span>

<span style="color: #000000;">Captain John’s Independence Day Extravaganza: In North Shore Dr. Fawnskin has live Reggae music, Hawaiian style BBQ.</span>

<span style="color: #000000;">4th of July Cruise on Miss Liberty Paddlewheel Boat: Watch the fireworks from a boat, non-alcoholic drinks and hors d’oeuvres are included with your ticket, but beer, wine, soft drinks and snacks are available for purchase. Adults are $80 kids are $40.</span>

<span style="color: #000000;">Annual Big Bear Lake Independence Day Festival &amp; Craft Show at the Convention Center has one-of-a-kind items, activities and entertainment, Wyatt’s full-service menu &amp; full service bar.</span>

<span style="color: #000000;">We enjoyed walking around the village, stopping for treats and checking out the various shops. The Summer Art Walk which showcases work by local artists had great music and beer/wine garden.</span>
