<p class="has-drop-cap">When was the last time you went on a nice vacation? If you barely remember your last one then it’s probably because holidays are starting to become really boring! We suspect it could be because of all the all-in-one packages that you can get and the dull locations that you’ve been to over and over again.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Sure, some people might find it interesting to go to the same places and do the same things, but you just don’t get to experience the rest of the world. And you know what one of the best ways to do that is? Change your accommodation!</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Most of us like to go with <a href="https://travellemming.com/hostel-vs-hotel">hotels and hostels</a> because they’re reliable, fairly cheap, and we don’t have to take care of our surroundings as much. However, if you’re willing to change things up, such as renting out a holiday home, then you’ll realize that there’s so much more to explore when it comes to accommodation options.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>So here are a few good reasons why you should consider renting out a holiday home the next time you go on a vacation.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><a href="https://docs.google.com/document/u/0/?authuser=0&amp;usp=docs_web"></a></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<div class="wp-block-uagb-advanced-heading uagb-block-42a30e40"><h3 class="uagb-heading-text">You get so much more space</h3></div>
<!-- /wp:uagb/advanced-heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"dropCap":true} -->
<p class="has-drop-cap"><a href="https://furnishedhomeskc.com/">Furnished short-term rentals</a> are usually a lot bigger than other options like a hotel or hoste. Holiday homes let you use the whole home too and not just a small section of it or a single room! This makes it great for people that need more space for things like their luggage, or if they just don’t enjoy cramped rooms and would prefer something with a bit more space so they can enjoy their time.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph --></div></div>
<!-- /wp:uagb/container -->

<!-- wp:uagb/container {"block_id":"9c4c67bd","boxShadowVOffset":18,"boxShadowBlur":40,"boxShadowSpread":-10,"variationSelected":true,"rowGapDesktop":0,"isBlockRootParent":true} -->
<div class="wp-block-uagb-container uagb-block-9c4c67bd alignfull uagb-is-root-container"><div class="uagb-container-inner-blocks-wrap"><!-- wp:uagb/advanced-heading {"block_id":"ab774333","classMigrate":true,"headingDescToggle":false,"headingAlign":"left","subHeadingColor":"","headingTag":"h3","subHeadFontStyle":"italic"} -->
<div class="wp-block-uagb-advanced-heading uagb-block-ab774333"><h3 class="uagb-heading-text">You have a lot more freedom to do what you want</h3></div>
<!-- /wp:uagb/advanced-heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"dropCap":true} -->
<p class="has-drop-cap">Hostels and hotels have rules. While there are generally rules in a rented accommodation too, you usually have a lot more freedom to do what you want. This can include cooking your own meals, bringing people over for parties (assuming you clean up and don’t annoy the neighbors!) and also being able to store your stuff however you want.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph --></div></div>
<!-- /wp:uagb/container -->

<!-- wp:uagb/container {"block_id":"7938e8a7","boxShadowVOffset":18,"boxShadowBlur":40,"boxShadowSpread":-10,"variationSelected":true,"rowGapDesktop":0,"isBlockRootParent":true} -->
<div class="wp-block-uagb-container uagb-block-7938e8a7 alignfull uagb-is-root-container"><div class="uagb-container-inner-blocks-wrap"><!-- wp:uagb/advanced-heading {"block_id":"746d2b4e","classMigrate":true,"headingDescToggle":false,"headingAlign":"left","subHeadingColor":"","headingTag":"h3","subHeadFontStyle":"italic"} -->
<div class="wp-block-uagb-advanced-heading uagb-block-746d2b4e"><h3 class="uagb-heading-text">You get a more local and authentic experience</h3></div>
<!-- /wp:uagb/advanced-heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"dropCap":true} -->
<p class="has-drop-cap">Holiday homes can be found in some of the most picturesque and outstanding places in the entire world. From relaxing countryside cottages to large mansions in the city, there are so many different choices to pick from that you can often be overwhelmed! Of course, you’ll probably be limited by your budget in that case.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Nobody wants to pay crazy amounts for accommodation they don’t enjoy either. If you want to <a href="/travel-tips-to-make-your-next-adventure-memorable/">make your next trip more memorable</a>, then hanging out with the locals and enjoying your time is usually a good option. Take it easy, experience life like a local, and try to avoid the touristy destinations like hotels.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph --></div></div>
<!-- /wp:uagb/container -->

<!-- wp:uagb/container {"block_id":"215e149a","boxShadowVOffset":18,"boxShadowBlur":40,"boxShadowSpread":-10,"variationSelected":true,"rowGapDesktop":0,"isBlockRootParent":true} -->

<div class="wp-block-uagb-advanced-heading uagb-block-e9f6dc6d"><h3 class="uagb-heading-text">You get better value for money in a group</h3></div>
<!-- /wp:uagb/advanced-heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"dropCap":true} -->
<p class="has-drop-cap">Going on a group holiday? If so, then you’ll probably save a lot of money by splitting the costs of a holiday rental, especially if they’re fine with you bringing a much larger group! It’s also often a lot more convenient for the whole group to stay in the same place as opposed to renting out different rooms in a hotel.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph --></div></div>
<!-- /wp:uagb/container -->

<!-- wp:block {"ref":16445} /-->

<!-- wp:block {"ref":17013} /-->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"dropCap":true} -->
<p class="has-drop-cap">Renting a holiday home offers a wealth of benefits that can enhance your vacation experience. From the spacious and comfortable accommodations to the added privacy and flexibility, holiday homes provide a home-away-from-home feel that's hard to replicate in traditional hotels. Additionally, the opportunity to immerse yourself in local communities and enjoy unique, personalized experiences is a priceless aspect of this lodging option. So, whether you're planning a family getaway, a romantic escape, or a group adventure, consider renting a holiday home for a memorable and rewarding travel experience that goes beyond the ordinary.</p>
