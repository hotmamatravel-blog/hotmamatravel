<span style="color: #000000;">You’re hands clasp the salty railing as you peer out towards the horizon only to see darkness. The moon casts an eerie glow upon the sea as the sounds of waves lap against the hull as the ship cuts through the black of night. </span>

<span style="color: #000000;">Suddenly, a creepy sea captain beckons you to listen to the ghostly maritime tales of the restless spirits that haunt the seas. Goosebumps plague your whole body and just when you think you can’t take anymore, Mickey jumps out dressed like a vampire and the real party begins.</span>

<a href="/halloween-on-the-high-seas/" target="_blank" rel="attachment wp-att-4585 noopener noreferrer"><img class="aligncenter wp-image-4585" title=" Mickey and Minnie Halloween on the High Seas" src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/10/DSC07676-1024x683.jpg" alt="Mickey and Minnie Halloween High Seas" width="640" height="427" /></a>

<span style="color: #000000;">No, you are not lost at sea, you are celebrating at sea! Here you get to embark on a spooky adventure filled with costume parties and festive activities. </span><span style="color: #000000;">This is no ordinary Halloween. Oh no. This, my friends, is a Disney Halloween Cruise, also known as Halloween on the High Seas. </span>

<span style="color: #000000;">So climb aboard as we explain everything you need to know about experiencing Halloween on a Disney cruise and find out how Disney does Halloween on the High Seas!</span>

<h2><span style="color: #000000;">Yo Ho and Away We Go!</span></h2>
<span style="color: #000000;">First, you probably want to know when Disney Cruise Line starts their Halloween festivities. I mean, you can’t celebrate if you are not on board, right? </span>

<span style="color: #000000;">Luckily, Disney Cruise Line does Halloween on the High Seas on all four ships: The <a class="thirstylink" title="disney-magic-cruise" href="/go/disney-magic-cruise/" target="_blank" rel="nofollow noopener noreferrer">Disney Magic</a>, <a class="thirstylink" title="disney-wonder-cruise" href="/go/disney-wonder-cruise/" target="_blank" rel="nofollow noopener noreferrer">Disney Wonder</a>, <a class="thirstylink" title="disney-dream-cruise" href="/go/disney-dream-cruise/" target="_blank" rel="nofollow noopener noreferrer">Disney Dream</a>, and <a class="thirstylink" title="disney-fantasy-cruise" href="/go/disney-fantasy-cruise/" target="_blank" rel="nofollow noopener noreferrer">Disney Fantasy</a>. Sail dates usually start in early September and run until the end of October. Dates vary depending on ship, sailing destination and duration.</span>

<em><span style="color: #000000;"></span></em>

Of course you want to do it all, burning the candle both ends!</span>

<span style="color: #000000;">Well, Disney does everything top-notch and how Disney Cruise Line does Halloween on High Seas is no exception! It is frightfully fun and the perfect Halloween cruise for kids. Here are some of the reasons why...</span>
<h3><span style="color: #000000;">The Décor</span></h3>
<span style="color: #000000;">The Halloween Tree, standing tall in main atrium with its bare, winding branches creeping out as if they could reach out and grab you. <h3><span style="color: #000000;">Mickey’s Mouse-querade Party</span></h3>
<span style="color: #000000;">It is time to get dressed-up, get in the spirit and get your monster mash on. Honestly, after a long day at port it was hard to motivate ourselves to put on costumes, but I’m glad we did it, because it was so much fun.</span>

<span style="color: #000000;">It started in the main atrium where music was pumping and the cruise entertainment crew was dancing and helping to keep up the energy. The kids had fun int he atrium trick-or-treating, playing games, interacting with the crew, and taking really cute photo ops with mama and daddy!</span>

<h4><span style="color: #000000;">Haunted Stories of the Sea:</span></h4>
<span style="color: #000000;">This is where my intro will start to make more sense. Late at night on the lido deck, you will gather around the virtual campfire displayed on the funnel vision (the huge outdoor movie screen, located near the Mickey pool) to hear scary stories told by an old sea captain. I love all things scary, but I was prepared for the stories to be rather mild, being that this was a Disney Halloween. </span>

<span style="color: #000000;">However, I was pleasantly surprised see that the stories were quite spooky. Gazing out at a dark sea, made things even spookier. I felt it was a very balanced activity, scary enough that even my tween nephew enjoyed it, but fun enough that it wasn’t too scary for my little kids either - but then again they have me for a mama (aka lover off all things Halloween) and we're not afraid to take the kids to <span style="color: #ff0000;"><strong><a style="color: #ff0000;" title="5 Haunted Historical Places you need to Take your Kids right now" href="/best-haunted-places-with-kids/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">haunted historical places</a></strong></span> across the country. </span>

<span style="color: #000000;">Let’s just say my kids have seen worse.</span>
<h2><em><span style="color: #000000;">Money saving Tip: Best Time to Book</span></em></h2>
<span style="color: #000000;">Depending on cruise’s sailing location, prices can be very reasonable (for Disney) during the Halloween on High Seas celebration. Just coming off the summer rush, this time of year tends to be a bit slower and weather can be a bit less predictable. </span>

<span style="color: #000000;">For instance, we sailed during Hurricane Matthew, but Disney Cruise Line handled it amazing and we weren't affected at all! Actually we had amazing weather.</span>

<!--End mc_embed_signup-->

<span style="color: #000000;">That being said, prices do fluctuate and there are better times to buy than others.  There are two peak times when booking a Disney Cruise; at the release of the new schedules and approximately one year before the sailing date. That’s just when people tend to book more, so prices go up. </span>

<span style="color: #000000;">So try to book just short of a year or even better, last minute as your schedule affords for some really great deals.I like to check with Last Minute Travel.  Cruise Direct, also offers great last minute deals with some that include Disney on board credit.
</span>
<h2><em><span style="color: #000000;">Get Packing:</span></em></h2>
<span style="color: #000000;">As a frequent traveler, I’m always advising others to pack light. However, if you are going on a themed Disney Cruise, you can take that advice and throw it out to sea. There are so many events and parties during Disney Cruise Halloween on the High Seas that you require frequent wardrobe changes. </span>

<span style="color: #000000;">Okay, they don’t <em>REQUIRE</em> it, but it is fun to participate. So, here is what you’ll need if you’re all in:</span>

<span style="color: #000000;">One night of the cruise is dedicated to Halloween and there is rocking Disney Halloween party as well as several photo ops all over the ship.</span>
