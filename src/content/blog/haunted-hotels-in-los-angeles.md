<h2><strong><a href="/go/the-queen-mary-ship-hotel/" linkid="22641">The Queen Mary</a></strong></h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><em>1126 Queens Hwy, Long Beach, CA 90802</em></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The <a href="https://www.queenmary.com/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener"><strong>Queen Mary</strong> </a>is easily one of Los Angeles’s most haunted hotels, and honestly, that’s half the fun. This legendary ocean liner–turned–hotel is packed with ghost stories, shadowy hallways, and decades of spooky history that make it a dream stop for families who love a little thrill. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>For the truly brave, you can even book one of the ship’s most haunted staterooms, B340, complete with paranormal lore and a Ouija board stocked in the room for guests who want the full supernatural experience.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>If you want the full breakdown of the ship’s most haunted spots, tours, and where spirits are said to linger, check out ou<em>r <strong><a href="/the-queen-mary-with-kids/" data-type="post" data-id="530" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Queen Mary haunted hotel guide</a></strong>.)</em></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>What really makes the Queen Mary <strong>HotMamaTravel approved</strong> is how family-friendly it is despite the spooky reputation. By day, families can explore the decks, learn about the ship’s history, and join ghost tours that spark curiosity without crossing into nightmare territory. <strong><a href="/the-queen-mary-scots-festival-with-kids/" target="_blank" data-type="post" data-id="1988" rel="noreferrer noopener">Seasonal events</a></strong>, dining on board, and the sheer wow-factor of sleeping on a historic ship make it an experience kids actually brag about later.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Spooky stories, rich history, and an experience that feels adventurous, not overwhelming, make the Queen Mary a perfect blend of haunted, historic, and totally family-approved.</p>

<h2 id="medieval-times"><strong><a href="/go/hollywood-roosevelt-los-angeles-ca/" linkid="16537">The Hollywood Roosevelt Hotel</a></strong></h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><em>7000 Hollywood Blvd, Los Angeles, CA 90028 </em></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>This iconic hotel is not only a piece of Hollywood history, but it is also rumored to be haunted by some pretty famous ghosts.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>One of the most famous ghosts said to haunt the <a href="https://www.thehollywoodroosevelt.com/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Hollywood Roosevelt Hotel</a> is none other than Marilyn Monroe. The legendary actress lived at the hotel for several years and it is said that her ghost still roams the halls. Guests have reported seeing her ghostly figure in the mirror of her old suite and hearing her laughter in the hallways.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>But Marilyn Monroe is not the only ghost said to haunt the Hollywood Roosevelt Hotel. The ghost of Montgomery Clift, the famous actor who starred in "From Here to Eternity," is also said to haunt the hotel. Guests have reported hearing strange noises coming from his old room and seeing his ghostly figure in the hallways.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>In addition to being haunted by some of Hollywood's most famous ghosts, the Hollywood Roosevelt Hotel also offers plenty of family-friendly activities. The hotel has two pools, a fitness center, and a rooftop bar with stunning views of the city. There are also several restaurants and bars on-site, offering a range of dining options for families.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>But the best part about staying at the Haunted Hollywood Roosevelt Hotel is the chance to experience the ghosts for yourselves. There are several companies, such as <a linkid="16538" href="/go/starline-tours-hollywood-ca/"><strong>Starline Tours</strong></a>, that offer ghost tours where guests can learn more about the hotel's haunted history and try to spot some of the ghosts for themselves. It's a spooky and exciting activity that the whole family will enjoy.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>So why should your family visit the Haunted Hollywood Roosevelt Hotel in Los Angeles? It's a unique and exciting vacation destination that offers something for everyone. Your family will have the chance to experience Hollywood history, enjoy a range of family-friendly activities, and maybe even spot a ghost or two. It's a trip you won't soon forget!</p>

<h2 id="medieval-times"><strong><a href="/go/millennium-biltmore-hotel-los-angeles-ca/" linkid="16541">The Millennium Biltmore Hotel</a></strong></h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><em> 506 S Grand Ave, Los Angeles, CA 90071 </em></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The Millennium Biltmore Hotel is not only stunning, but haunted and it is no wonder why. Who would want to leave?</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>One of the most famous ghosts said to haunt the <a href="https://www.thebiltmorehotels.com/en/biltmore-los-angeles/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Millennium Biltmore Hotel</a> is the ghost of Elizabeth Short, also known as the Black Dahlia. In 1947, Short was found murdered near the hotel, and it is said that her ghost still roams the halls. Guests have reported seeing her ghostly figure in the mirror of her old room and hearing strange noises coming from the room.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>But Elizabeth Short is not the only ghost said to haunt the Millennium Biltmore Hotel. The ghost of George Montgomery, a famous actor who starred in several movies and TV shows, is also said to haunt the hotel. Guests have reported seeing his ghostly figure in the hallways and hearing strange noises coming from his old room.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>If you would like to bring the family along for this spooky stay, Millennium Biltmore Hotel also offers plenty of family-friendly activities. The hotel has a beautiful outdoor pool, a fitness center, and a rooftop bar with stunning views of the city. There are also several restaurants and bars on-site, offering a range of dining options for families.</p>

<h2 id="medieval-times"><strong><a href="/go/hotel-figueroa-hyatt-los-angeles/" linkid="16542">The Hotel Figueroa</a></strong></h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><em> 939 S Figueroa St, Los Angeles, CA 90015</em></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The Hotel Figueroa opened in 1926 and is still going strong as popular stay for locals, travelers to Los Angeles, and even some infamous spirits. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>One of the most famous ghosts said to haunt the Hotel Figueroa is the ghost of a former maid who was murdered in the hotel. Guests have reported seeing her ghostly figure in the hallways and hearing strange noises coming from her old room.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>But the murdered maid is not the only ghost said to haunt the <a href="https://www.hotelfigueroa.com/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Hotel Figueroa</a>. The ghost of a former guest who committed suicide in the hotel is also said to roam the halls. Guests have reported hearing strange noises coming from the room where the suicide took place and seeing the ghostly figure of the former guest in the hallway.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Families can enjoy the beautiful outdoor pool, a fitness center, and several restaurants and bars on-site. There are also several museums and attractions nearby, making it a great location for families to explore Los Angeles.</p>

<h2 id="medieval-times"><a href="/go/chateau-marmont-los-angeles/" linkid="16552">Chateau Marmont</a></h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><em> 8221 Sunset Blvd, Los Angeles, CA 90046</em></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"dropCap":true} -->
<p class="has-drop-cap">The Chateau Marmont exudes exclusivity and is a favorite for the inner Hollywood crowd. Shrouded in an aura of secrecy, this legendary Los Angeles hotel holds a long history, sizzling stories, and spirits, of course!  </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Opening in the late 1920's this hotel has always had a reputation for discretion. In fact, the building was originally meant to be apartments, but was refashioned to be a hotel in the early 1930's. Although there is now a restaurant, gym, pool, and other hotel amenities, it is still a hot spot for celebrities looking for privacy. There are hush-hush scandalous stories about the goings-on of various celebrities that stay at the Chateau Marmont as well as some spooky ghost stories.  </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>In 1982, a famous guest, John Belushi of <em>Saturday Night Live</em>, died by drug overdose at the hotel, but many say he never left the <a href="https://www.chateaumarmont.com/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Chateau Marmont</a>. According to comedian and politician Al Franken, he saw the ghostly figure of his friend when he stayed in the same bungalow, Bungalow 3.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Another haunting is said to by that of German-American photographer, Helmut Newton, who sadly crashed his car into the wall of the driveway at the Chateau and died on scene. Many guests have claimed to see him wandering around looking confused and even bloody. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>There are many other celebrity spirits tied to the Chateau and many guests have reported hearing disembodied laughter and clinking glasses as if a ghostly cocktail party is still in full swing. A couple of the most active rooms are Room 64, which was Howard Hughes' hideout for months and Room 79, where it is said to be so active that even the staff try to avoid going to this room. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<h2 id="medieval-times"><strong><a href="/go/o-hotel-los-angeles-ca/" linkid="16547">The O Hotel</a></strong></h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><em> 819 Flower St, Los Angeles, CA 90017</em></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>This boutique hotel in Downtown Los Angeles is not built to accommodate large families, as most rooms only sleep two, but if you are a traveling pair or flying solo and looking for a spooky, yet trendy stay in L.A look no further than the O Hotel.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>One of the most famous ghosts said to haunt the <a href="https://www.ohotelgroup.com/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">O Hotel</a> is the ghost of a former guest who was killed in one of the rooms. Guests have reported hearing strange noises coming from the room where the murder took place and seeing the ghostly figure of the former guest in the hallway.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>But the murdered guest is not the only ghost said to haunt the O Hotel. The ghost of a former owner of the hotel is also said to roam the halls. Guests have reported seeing his ghostly figure in the hallways and hearing strange noises coming from his old office.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The hotel has a rooftop pool and bar with stunning views of the city, as well as a fitness center and several restaurants and bars on-site. The hotel is also located near several popular attractions, making it a great location to explore Los Angeles.</p>

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 id="medieval-times"><strong><a href="/go/hollywood-hills-hotel-los-angeles/" linkid="16550">The Hollywood Hills Hotel</a></strong></h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><em> 1999 N Sycamore Ave, Los Angeles, CA 90068</em></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Located in the heart of Los Angeles, this historic boutique hotel is said to be haunted by the ghosts of former guests and staff members. Rooms only sleep two in a single King bed, so this isn't great option for families, but it is a great option for a couples trip. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>For those who love all things paranormal, a visit to the <a href="https://hollywoodhillshotel.com/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Hollywood Hills Hotel</a> is a thrilling experience. The hotel's eerie atmosphere and haunted history is sure to give you and your family goosebumps. From strange noises and eerie apparitions to unexplained sightings and ghostly encounters, the Hollywood Hills Hotel is a hot spot for paranormal activity.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>But the haunted Hollywood Hills Hotel isn't just a place for ghost hunters. It's also a beautiful and historic hotel that offers luxurious accommodations and a range of amenities. The hotel's old-world charm and Hollywood glamour make it a unique and memorable place to stay.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>You can enjoy a range of activities during your stay at the Hollywood Hills Hotel. The hotel's beautiful outdoor pool is the perfect spot for a refreshing swim, and the on-site restaurant offers delicious cuisine for breakfast, lunch, and dinner. The hotel's central location makes it easy to explore the sights and sounds of Los Angeles, including the famous Hollywood Walk of Fame and the <a href="https://youtu.be/0isoHWy8S9I?si=YL6KoH6cNLkSMPHg" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener"><strong>Griffith Observatory</strong></a>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>In addition to its haunted history and luxurious amenities, the Hollywood Hills Hotel also offers special ghost-themed events and tours. These events provide guests with the opportunity to explore the hotel's haunted past and learn more about the ghosts that are said to haunt its halls.</p>
