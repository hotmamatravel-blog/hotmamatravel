<p class="has-drop-cap">Turkey is shining up as an attractive place for citizens worldwide as an option for a second citizenship or residence purpose. The situation at the heart of the land between Europe and Asia, Turkey, is a place of divine beauty. It tells the story of its rich history and reflects a perfect blend of culture and modernity. The Global Residence Index also ranks Turkey at higher numbers for its welcoming policies, ease of obtaining citizenship, and the multifold benefits it offers to global citizens.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Turkey is one of the very few countries worldwide that offer the quickest and easiest citizenship to applicants through its<strong>&nbsp;</strong><a href="https://globalresidenceindex.com/turkey-citizenship-investment/"><strong>Türkiye citizenship by investment</strong></a><strong>&nbsp;</strong>program. Moreover, by offering global citizens access to both Asian and European markets, Turkey’s economic citizenship program has become a valuable citizenship program of its kind.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Let's learn some more about why Turkey is attractive for global citizens as an option for second citizenship and its insights given by the Global Residence Index.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"dropCap":true} -->
<p class="has-drop-cap">According to the rules of citizenship of Turkey, anyone who has never been registered for a major crime or violation of the laws of the land of Turkey is eligible for citizenship application. The application is for the individual investor, spouse, and children under 18. Parents or adult children without independent sources of income are eligible for a $5,000 residency permit.</p>

<div class="wp-block-uagb-advanced-heading uagb-block-42a30e40"><h3 class="uagb-heading-text">Citizenship by Investment</h3></div>
<!-- /wp:uagb/advanced-heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"dropCap":true} -->
<p class="has-drop-cap">Under this program, the applicant has to make a minimum investment of $400,000 in any government-certified real estate. The property can be of any kind, such as apartments, hotels, industrial properties, etc.</p>

<!-- /wp:uagb/container -->

<div class="wp-block-uagb-advanced-heading uagb-block-e363e800"><h3 class="uagb-heading-text">Residency by Investment</h3></div>
<!-- /wp:uagb/advanced-heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"dropCap":true} -->
<p class="has-drop-cap">For the people who are not ready to shift permanently to Turkey but want to be residents temporarily here, Turkey also offers a Residency by Investment program. Here also, the applicant, be it an individual or a family, can secure a long-term residency by investing a minimum amount of  <strong>$250,000</strong>. This way, they get to avail themselves of the opportunities of countries without being permanent.</p>

<div class="wp-block-uagb-advanced-heading uagb-block-ff932754"><h3 class="uagb-heading-text">Historical and Cultural Riches</h3></div>
<!-- /wp:uagb/advanced-heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"dropCap":true} -->
<p class="has-drop-cap">Turkey has a charismatic and rich history and culture of its own. Making a bridge between Europe and Asia, it has a unique blend of Eastern and Western influences. The country is home to several UNESCO World Heritage Sites, including the magnificent Hagia Sophia, the ancient city of Ephesus, and the surreal landscapes of Cappadocia. Staying in the land of Turkey, you get to explore its history and culture, which can add beautiful chapters of stories to your life.</p>

<div class="wp-block-uagb-advanced-heading uagb-block-f4159a59"><h3 class="uagb-heading-text">Geographically and Economically Strategic Location</h3></div>
<!-- /wp:uagb/advanced-heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"dropCap":true} -->
<p class="has-drop-cap">Falling between the two big markets of the world, Asia and Europe, Turkey has geographically and economically strategic locations for global business and trade. In Turkey, Istanbul is the hotspot as a vital connecting point for world trade. Thus, for the investors who are entrepreneurs or ambitious business holders, the citizenship of Turkey serves them to expand their business throughout the world.</p>

<div class="wp-block-uagb-advanced-heading uagb-block-c3744b31"><h3 class="uagb-heading-text">Thriving Economy</h3></div>

<p class="has-drop-cap">Turkey isa country with a robust and dynamic economy. Thus, it serves as a great investment opportunity for the applicants. Including diversified industries like manufacturing, tourism, finance, and technology offers the country an attractive and stable environment for business ventures. Turkey has also been drawing foreign direct investment (FDI) and enacting reforms to promote economic growth, which has increased its appeal to global citizens.</p>

<div class="wp-block-uagb-advanced-heading uagb-block-b3d3b2bb"><h3 class="uagb-heading-text">Visa-Free Travel</h3></div>
<!-- /wp:uagb/advanced-heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"dropCap":true} -->
<p class="has-drop-cap">The holders of citizenship or residency in Turkey get visa-free or visa-on-arrival access to various countries. These countries also include European nations. This opportunity is best for the global citizens who want to explore the world. They can do it without worrying about visas when they have Turkish citizenship or residency.</p>

<div class="wp-block-uagb-advanced-heading uagb-block-1a43bade"><h3 class="uagb-heading-text">USA E2 Visa</h3></div>
<!-- /wp:uagb/advanced-heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"dropCap":true} -->
<p class="has-drop-cap">One of the very few nations, including Turkey, has the E2 visa of the USA. This visa, in general, is very complex to gain. Yet, having Turkish citizenship or residency makes it much more attainable. The E-2 visa, often known as the investment visa, allows the treaty country, i.e., Turkey citizens, to enter the United States. This visa also expands to families such as spouses, dependent parents, and offspring. They can also come to the US on this visa for study, job, or health purposes. </p>

Turkey, to its citizens and residents, offers excellent health facilities, standard education, a pleasant climate, and a low cost of living compared to Western countries.
