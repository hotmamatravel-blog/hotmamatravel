## Requirements for Turkish Citizenship and Residency



According to the rules of citizenship of Turkey, anyone who has never been registered for a major crime or violation of the laws of the land of Turkey is eligible for citizenship application. The application is for the individual investor, spouse, and children under 18. Parents or adult children without independent sources of income are eligible for a $5,000 residency permit.


### Citizenship by Investment

Under this program, the applicant has to make a minimum investment of $400,000 in any government-certified real estate. The property can be of any kind, such as apartments, hotels, industrial properties, etc.




### Residency by Investment

For the people who are not ready to shift permanently to Turkey but want to be residents temporarily here, Turkey also offers a Residency by Investment program. Here also, the applicant, be it an individual or a family, can secure a long-term residency by investing a minimum amount of  **$250,000**. This way, they get to avail themselves of the opportunities of countries without being permanent.





## Factors make Turkey attractive to the global citizen



The picturesque beauty of Turkey itself is ample enough to attract global citizens to stay here. However, many other factors play a crucial role in making Turkey renounce as an option for a second citizenship.


### Historical and Cultural Riches

Turkey has a charismatic and rich history and culture of its own. Making a bridge between Europe and Asia, it has a unique blend of Eastern and Western influences. The country is home to several UNESCO World Heritage Sites, including the magnificent Hagia Sophia, the ancient city of Ephesus, and the surreal landscapes of Cappadocia. Staying in the land of Turkey, you get to explore its history and culture, which can add beautiful chapters of stories to your life.




### Geographically and Economically Strategic Location

Falling between the two big markets of the world, Asia and Europe, Turkey has geographically and economically strategic locations for global business and trade. In Turkey, Istanbul is the hotspot as a vital connecting point for world trade. Thus, for the investors who are entrepreneurs or ambitious business holders, the citizenship of Turkey serves them to expand their business throughout the world.




### Thriving Economy

Turkey isa country with a robust and dynamic economy. Thus, it serves as a great investment opportunity for the applicants. Including diversified industries like manufacturing, tourism, finance, and technology offers the country an attractive and stable environment for business ventures. Turkey has also been drawing foreign direct investment (FDI) and enacting reforms to promote economic growth, which has increased its appeal to global citizens.




### Visa-Free Travel

The holders of citizenship or residency in Turkey get visa-free or visa-on-arrival access to various countries. These countries also include European nations. This opportunity is best for the global citizens who want to explore the world. They can do it without worrying about visas when they have Turkish citizenship or residency.




### USA E2 Visa

One of the very few nations, including Turkey, has the E2 visa of the USA. This visa, in general, is very complex to gain. Yet, having Turkish citizenship or residency makes it much more attainable. The E-2 visa, often known as the investment visa, allows the treaty country, i.e., Turkey citizens, to enter the United States. This visa also expands to families such as spouses, dependent parents, and offspring. They can also come to the US on this visa for study, job, or health purposes.





## Opportunities Open for citizens of Turkey



Being a permanent citizen or a temporary resident of Turkey, you can enjoy the following opportunities this place offers.


### Quality of life

The high quality of life is a factor that attracts global citizens more towards it. Turkey, to its citizens and residents, offers excellent health facilities, standard education, a pleasant climate, and a low cost of living compared to Western countries. Its cities, for instance, Istanbul, have a perfect combination of cosmopolitan living, a rich cultural scene, and a thriving business opportunity.




### Diversity of Lifestyle

Turkey has a diverse lifestyle that caters to the different preferences of the global citizens coming for citizenship or residency here. Having coastal towns alongside Mediterranean and Aegean coasts, Turkey offers a livable lifestyle to all. Whether one seeks a bustling metropolis, a peaceful beachside life, or a house in a historical village, Turkey has something to serve them all.




### Multilingual Multicultural Society

Turkey’s history tells the stories of welcoming people from all corners of the world with different cultures and backgrounds. Thus, the multilingual and multicultural society makes everyone feel at home. English, a widely spoken language, being the national language of Turkey, makes it even easier for people to communicate and do business.




### Family Inclusion

Turkey’s citizenship and residency visa extend to the family members. These members can include spouses of retired or dependent parents. It can also be offspring below the age of 18 and dependent siblings who are not married and have no kids. Thus, this visa serves the needs of an individual looking for a family-oriented solution.
