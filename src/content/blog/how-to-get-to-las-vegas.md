<!-- wp:paragraph {"dropCap":true} -->
<p class="has-drop-cap">Depending on where you are coming from, the fastest and most convenient way of getting to Las Vegas is by flying. <a href="https://www.harryreidairport.com/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Harry Reid International Airport</a> (formerly known as McCarran International Airport) is just a stone's throw from the Strip, making it super easy to dive right into the action.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:uagb/advanced-heading {"block_id":"42a30e40","classMigrate":true,"headingDescToggle":true,"headingAlign":"left","subHeadingColor":"","headingTag":"h3","subHeadFontStyle":"italic"} -->
<div class="wp-block-uagb-advanced-heading uagb-block-42a30e40"><h3 class="uagb-heading-text"><strong>Booking Your Flight:</strong></h3></div>
<!-- /wp:uagb/advanced-heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Airlines:</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Most major airlines, including <a href="https://www.southwest.com/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Southwest</a>, <a href="https://www.delta.com/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Delta</a>, <a href="https://www.aa.com/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">American Airlines</a>, and <a href="https://www.united.com/en/us/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">United</a>, offer frequent flights to Vegas. You can often <a linkid="21161" href="/go/vegas-air-hotel-deals/"><strong>find great deals</strong></a>, especially if you book in advance or catch a last-minute sale.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Timing:</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Aim for midweek flights to score the best prices. Tuesday and Wednesday departures are usually cheaper and less crowded.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:uagb/advanced-heading {"block_id":"6d95ba4f","classMigrate":true,"headingDescToggle":true,"headingAlign":"left","subHeadingColor":"","headingTag":"h3","subHeadFontStyle":"italic"} -->
<div class="wp-block-uagb-advanced-heading uagb-block-6d95ba4f"><h3 class="uagb-heading-text">How To Get From Las Vegas Airport To Strip:</h3></div>
<!-- /wp:uagb/advanced-heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Terminals</strong>:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>There are two main terminals: Terminal 1 (domestic flights) and Terminal 3 (international and some domestic flights). Each terminal has its own parking garage, ticketing and check-in area, baggage claim, and a variety of shopping and dining options. Since the two terminals aren't physically connected, it’s crucial to know which terminal your airline operates from.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- /wp:uagb/image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Ground Transportation from Las Vegas Airport to The Strip:</strong> Once you land, you have several options to get to your hotel.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Taxi:</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Taxis, Uber, and Lyft are readily available. A ride to the Strip typically takes about 15-20 minutes. Taxicabs at Terminal 1 are available on the east side of baggage claim, outside exit doors numbered 1-4. Taxis at Terminal 3 are located outside on Level Zero. There are 15 taxi loading positions on the west end of the building, outside the exit door numbered 52.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>RideShare:</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Rideshare companies authorized to provide services at the airport are <a href="https://www.lyft.com/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Lyft</a> and <a href="https://www.uber.com/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Uber</a>. Use the apps on your mobile device to arrange for a ride. RideShare pickup for <strong>Terminal 1</strong> is located on&nbsp;Level 2&nbsp;of the parking garage. <strong>Terminal 3</strong> Rideshare pickup is located on the&nbsp;Valet Level&nbsp;of the parking garage.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Limousines:</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>For a luxurious Las Vegas experience, walk-up limousine service is a fantastic option. You can select from sedans, stretch limousines, and SUVs, all designed to provide a stylish and comfortable ride to your destination. If you've pre-arranged limousine service through your hotel or resort, your driver will meet you at baggage claim, help with your luggage, and guide you to your vehicle.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Shuttle Services:</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Several shuttle companies offer shared rides to major hotels. This is a budget-friendly option if you don't mind a few extra stops. Terminal 1 has group shuttle pickup on the&nbsp;west side of baggage claim, outside door exits&nbsp;7 - 13. Terminal 3&nbsp;shuttles&nbsp;are located outside on Level&nbsp;Zero&nbsp;on the west&nbsp;end of the building for domestic and on the east side for international.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Public Bus: </strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>For those using the bus at Terminal 1, head down to Level Zero from Baggage Claim via the elevator or escalator. Once outside, cross the pedestrian crosswalk and veer right to find the covered RTC bus stop. At Terminal 3, go up to Level 2 from Baggage Claim. Exit the terminal, cross the pedestrian crosswalk, and you’ll find the bus stop across from exit door 44. Routes 108, 109, and Centennial Express (CX) all serve Terminal 1 on Level Zero, with CX also stopping at Terminal 3. You can find more route information at the <a href="https://www.rtcsnv.com/ways-to-travel/transit-services/airport-transit-routes/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">RTC website</a>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Car Rental:</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>If you plan to explore beyond the Strip, renting a car can be a great idea. Rental car facilities are accessible via a free shuttle from the airport. The Rent-A-Car Center is located at&nbsp;<a href="https://goo.gl/maps/i63JcDbHF822" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">7135 Gilespie Street</a>, just three miles south of the airport. The center is open 24 hours a day, 365 days a year, with continuous shuttle service to airport terminals.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Shuttle pickups for Terminal 1 are located outside the baggage claim on the Ground Transportation Level 1 just outside doors 10 and 11. Shuttle pickup for Terminal 3 is located on Level 0 outside doors 51-54 on west end and doors 55-58 on east end.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"dropCap":true} -->
<p class="has-drop-cap">For those who love the freedom of the open road, driving to Las Vegas is an adventure in itself. Here's what you need to know to hit the road.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:uagb/advanced-heading {"block_id":"b542ffa8","classMigrate":true,"headingDescToggle":true,"headingAlign":"left","subHeadingColor":"","headingTag":"h3","subHeadFontStyle":"italic"} -->
<div class="wp-block-uagb-advanced-heading uagb-block-b542ffa8"><h3 class="uagb-heading-text">Driving Directions From Los Angeles To Las Vegas</h3></div>
<!-- /wp:uagb/advanced-heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Route:</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Begin your journey in Los Angeles by getting on the US-101 S. Merge onto Interstate 10 E (I-10 E) toward San Bernardino. Stay on I-10 E for about 15 miles, then take the exit onto Interstate 15 North (I-15 N) toward Barstow/Las Vegas. Follow I-15 N for approximately 230 miles. It's a straight shot, and typically takes around 4-5 hours.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Road Trip Stops:</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Victorville</strong> is a great place to stop for a quick break, grab a bite, or refuel. Check out the California Route 66 Museum if you have time. We took a <a href="/route-66-family-road-trip-itinerary/" data-type="post" data-id="10353" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener"><strong>Route 66 road trip</strong></a>, and it was awesome. I highly recommend.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Barstow</strong> is another good stopping point with plenty of services, including gas stations, restaurants, and the <a href="https://www.theoutletsatbarstow.com/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Tanger Outlets</a> for a quick shopping spree.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Baker</strong> is known as the "Gateway to Death Valley." Baker is home to the world's tallest thermometer. It's a quirky stop for a photo op and a quick rest.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Primm</strong> is right before crossing into Nevada, and offers casinos, shopping, and a roller coaster at Buffalo Bill's Resort &amp; Casino.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:uagb/advanced-heading {"block_id":"3cdb16bc","classMigrate":true,"headingDescToggle":true,"headingAlign":"left","subHeadingColor":"","headingTag":"h3","subHeadFontStyle":"italic"} -->
<div class="wp-block-uagb-advanced-heading uagb-block-3cdb16bc"><h3 class="uagb-heading-text">Driving Directions From Phoenix To Las Vegas:</h3></div>
<!-- /wp:uagb/advanced-heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Route:</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Begin your journey in Phoenix by getting on Interstate 10 West (I-10 W). After about 14 miles, take exit 143A to merge onto Interstate 17 North (I-17 N) toward Flagstaff. Stay on I-17 N for approximately 30 miles, then take exit 223A to merge onto State Route 74 West (AZ-74 W), also known as Carefree Highway. Follow AZ-74 W for about 30 miles until you reach US-60 West (US-60 W). Turn right onto US-60 W. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After approximately 6 miles on US-60 W, take the exit onto US-93 North (US-93 N) toward Wickenburg/Las Vegas. Continue on US-93 N for about 200 miles. Continue on US-93 N from Kingman for about 50 miles until you reach the Hoover Dam Bypass, where US-93 N transitions into Interstate 11 North (I-11 N). </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Cross the state line into Nevada. Continue on I-11 N for approximately 15 miles. Take the exit to merge onto Interstate 515 North (I-515 N) toward Las Vegas. I-515 N will merge into US-93 N/US-95 N, leading you directly into Las Vegas. As you approach the city, you'll start seeing the famous skyline and the <a href="/las-vegas-strip-hotel-map/" data-type="post" data-id="9253" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener"><strong>bright lights of the Strip</strong></a>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Road Trip Stops:</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Wickenburg</strong> is a historic town is known for its charming downtown area and Western heritage. A great place for a quick stop and some photos.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Kingman</strong> is another excellent spot for a break. <a href="/route-66-kingman-classic-stops/" data-type="post" data-id="10876" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener"><strong>Kingman offers amenities</strong></a> such as restaurants, gas stations, and the fascinating Route 66 Museum.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:uagb/advanced-heading {"block_id":"4349e360","classMigrate":true,"headingDescToggle":true,"headingAlign":"left","subHeadingColor":"","headingTag":"h3","subHeadFontStyle":"italic"} -->
<div class="wp-block-uagb-advanced-heading uagb-block-4349e360"><h3 class="uagb-heading-text">Driving Directions From San Diego To Las Vegas:</h3></div>
<!-- /wp:uagb/advanced-heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Route:</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Begin your journey in San Diego by heading north on Interstate 15 (I-15 N). Continue on I-15 N for approximately 330 miles. This route takes you through the diverse landscapes of Southern California and into Nevada. Cross the state line into Nevada. You'll start to see signs for Las Vegas as you approach.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Road Trip Stops:</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Mojave National Preserve</strong> is a vast desert landscape with unique geological formations, including the iconic Joshua trees. It's an excellent spot for hiking, wildlife viewing, and stargazing.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Just before reaching Las Vegas, stretch your legs at the Seven Magic Mountains art installation. These colorful, towering rock formations by artist Ugo Rondinone make for a fantastic photo opportunity and a brief cultural break.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:uagb/advanced-heading {"block_id":"480d5289","classMigrate":true,"headingDescToggle":true,"headingAlign":"left","subHeadingColor":"","headingTag":"h3","subHeadFontStyle":"italic"} -->
<div class="wp-block-uagb-advanced-heading uagb-block-480d5289"><h3 class="uagb-heading-text">Driving Directions From Orange County To Las Vegas:</h3></div>
<!-- /wp:uagb/advanced-heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Route:</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The drive from Orange County to Las Vegas is approximately 270-280 miles and typically takes about 4-5 hours. If you are starting by Interstate 5, start by heading north on Interstate 5 (I-5 N).Merge onto State Route 55 (SR-55 N) toward Riverside. Continue on SR-55 N and then take Interstate 15 North (I-15 N) toward Barstow/Las Vegas. Follow I-15 N all the way to Las Vegas.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>If you are starting at Interstate 91, then stay on the 91 Freeway East for approximately 24 miles. Merge onto Interstate 15 North (I-15 N) toward Barstow/Las Vegas. Continue on I-15 N for approximately 220 miles. This route will take you through the Mojave Desert and past towns like Barstow and Baker. Follow signs as you approach Las Vegas. You'll see the city lights on the horizon as you get closer.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Road Trip Stops:</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong><a href="/calico-ghost-town-with-kids/" data-type="post" data-id="4207" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Calico Ghost Town</a></strong> is great for a taste of Wild West nostalgia. This former mining town offers guided tours, historic buildings, and a glimpse into California's past. We love visiting Calico, either on the way to or from Las Vegas. There are also a ton more <a href="/ghost-towns-around-las-vegas/" data-type="post" data-id="20807" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener"><strong>ghost towns around Vegas</strong></a> to explore, if that's your thing.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Make your drive more enjoyable with a stop at <strong>Peggy Sue's 50s Diner</strong> in Yermo, CA. This retro diner offers classic American comfort food, milkshakes, and a fun 1950s ambiance.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
