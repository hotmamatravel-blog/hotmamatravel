<p class="has-drop-cap">Christmas is the loveliest time of the year. And in the Lone Star State, you can truly have a Christmas to remember. This holiday season, get your loved ones together and enjoy Christmas Texas style. And if you’re looking for even more festive getaways, don’t miss my guide to <strong><a href="/christmas-getaways-for-families/" target="_blank" data-type="page" data-id="19806" rel="noreferrer noopener">Christmas family travel ideas</a></strong>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>From boots to BBQ and everything in between, the Great State of Texas has all you need for holiday fun. Check out our list of awesome Texas holiday favorites to treat your family to this year.</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>If you’re ready to dive headfirst into holiday cheer, <strong><a href="https://www.grapevinetexasusa.com/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Grapevine, Texas</a></strong>, the official <em>Christmas Capital of Texas</em>, is your playground. This town doesn’t just celebrate Christmas; it throws 1,400 festive events over 40 days, which is basically North Pole energy with Texas attitude. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>You have to stay in one of <a linkid="22521" href="/go/hotels-grapevine-texas/"><strong>Grapevine’s Christmas hotels</strong></a>, where you can roll out of bed and walk straight into the magic. You’ll be steps from showstopping holiday attractions like ICE! at the Gaylord Texan, Snowland at Great Wolf Lodge, and all the twinkling fun along Historic Main Street. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Even Grapevine Mills gets in on the action with seasonal shopping and experiences. It’s the kind of place where kids stay wide-eyed the entire trip, and adults don’t stand a chance either. Grapevine hits you with Christmas spirit from every direction, and honestly, you’ll love every glitter-covered second.</p>

<p>Get out on the range with your kin this season and spend some time having a ranch Christmas. There’s nothing quite like having a folksy traditional Christmas, waking up and making coffee as you take in the fresh country air. With plenty of <a href="https://www.airbnb.com/s/Texas-Ranches--Boerne--TX">holiday rental options</a> and <a href="/glamping-texas/" data-type="post" data-id="18149" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener"><strong>family-friendly glamping sites</strong></a> out there, this dream is easy to turn into a reality.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>A great Christmas gift for the horse lover in your family is to surprise them with horseback riding lessons, then spend the rest of your holiday season learning the art of riding. Texas is a great place to do it, with affordable rates and plenty of range, you’ll be riding like a cowboy with a <a href="https://www.stetson.com/store/hats/western.html" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">western hat</a> in no time.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- /wp:ugb/heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Looking for a holiday idea that’s a little less predictable and a whole lot more delicious? While you’re in Texas, round up the crew and go all-in on a true Texas Christmas tradition: BBQ<strong>.</strong> I know, I know—everyone else is out here carving turkeys and glazing hams. Still, nothing bonds a family faster than standing around a picnic table devouring brisket so tender it basically melts on contact.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>And let’s be honest… if you’re spending the holidays in Texas, you’re sitting on a goldmine of <strong>world-class pitmasters</strong> just waiting to feed you. Forget the fancy china, grab some smoked sausage, ribs, mac ’n’ cheese that should require a warning label, and call it your new favorite Christmas dinner. It’s bold, it’s fun, it’s Texas… and your family will talk about it for years.</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>You can’t go all the way to Texas and <em>not</em> experience a rodeo. That’s like visiting Italy and skipping the pasta. The sheer fun of watching real cowboys tear across the arena, hanging on for dear life while bulls do their absolute best to yeet them into orbit, is the kind of holiday memory your family will never forget. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>If you want the classic Texas experience, start with the <strong><a linkid="22529" href="/go/cowtown-coliseum-fort-worth-tx/">Cowtown Coliseum Rodeo in Fort Worth</a></strong>, where the action is nonstop and the atmosphere is pure Lone Star energy. Fort Worth is basically the rodeo capital of the universe, offering <a href="https://cowtownrodeo.com/">year-round rodeo events</a>, so chances are there’s something bucking, roping, or barrel-racing the exact week you’re in town. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>And don’t be shy, check<strong> local rodeo event listings</strong> wherever you’re traveling in Texas. From small-town weekend shows to big holiday rodeo spectaculars, there’s always something wild and unforgettable happening in the arena. It’s rowdy, it’s joyful, and it’s absolutely the Texas holiday adventure your crew didn’t know they needed.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p>Austin, the capital of Texas and one of the coolest cities on the planet, shines even brighter at Christmas. The streets glow with twinkle lights, the neighborhoods go full Clark Griswold, and the whole city feels like it’s humming with holiday cheer. It’s the perfect place to slip into your cutest <a href="https://www.tipsyelves.com/christmas-dresses" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Christmas dress</a> and wander from one festive spot to the next.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p> If you’re planning to stay awhile (and you should), check out my picks for<a href="/best-family-hotels-austin/" data-type="post" data-id="11953" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener"> <strong>family hotels in Austin</strong></a>, including one of our favorites, the <strong><a href="/best-western-plus-austin-city-hotel/" data-type="post" data-id="6860" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Best Western Plus Austin City Hotel</a></strong>. It's a comfy, convenient base for all your merry adventures. And trust me, there’s plenty to see. Stroll through the iconic <strong><a href="https://austintrailoflights.org/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Austin Trail of Lights at Zilker Park</a></strong> for a dazzling walk-through wonderland, then dive into local art, music, and holiday shopping at the <strong><a href="https://armadillobazaar.com/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Armadillo Christmas Bazaar</a></strong>, a beloved Austin tradition. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>For something different, hop aboard a <strong><a href="/austin-bat-cruise/" target="_blank" data-type="post" data-id="7554" rel="noreferrer noopener">Lone Star Riverboat</a></strong> and see the city sparkle from the water. Austin mixes quirky charm with classic holiday magic, and your family will eat it up like peppermint bark.</p>

<!-- /wp:ugb/heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>If you want a little Old World charm deep in the heart of Texas, the <a href="https://www.germantexans.org/christmas-market" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">German Texan Heritage Society Christmas Market</a> in Austin is the place to be. Tucked right into the lively Red River District, this market feels like you’ve wandered into a tiny pocket of Europe. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Picture rows of booths filled with authentic German Christmas treasures, like hand-carved ornaments, classic nutcrackers, twinkling Schwibbogen arches, wooden smokers, and enough charming décor to make even the Grinch feel sentimental. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>And don’t even get me started on the food. You can warm your frozen soul with a cup of Glühwein (hot spiced wine) and snag a slice of Christmas Stollen from The New World Bakery; trust me, it tastes like holiday cheer in loaf form. It’s the perfect blend of Texas vibes and German tradition, and families absolutely eat it up… sometimes literally.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>If San Antonio is more your speed, you’re about to hit the holiday jackpot. The River Walk goes all-out this time of year, glittering with thousands of lights that reflect off the water and turn an ordinary stroll into straight-up Christmas romance. You can even take a <strong><a href="/go/enchanted-lights-riverwalk-san-antonio/" linkid="22534">River Lights Night Walk</a></strong>. You’ll wander past sizzling restaurant patios, hear the echo of mariachi music drifting down the river, and wonder why Hallmark hasn’t filmed twelve movies here already. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>And the festivities don’t stop there. San Antonio packs its <a href="https://www.visitsanantonio.com/events/holiday-events/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">calendar with holiday magic</a>, including the famous Holiday River Parade, where decorated boats float down the San Antonio River. At the same time, spectators cheer from the banks. It's a must-see.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>If you want a taste of Texas tradition, grab tickets to the <a href="https://www.visitsanantonio.com/event/cowboy-christmas/8397/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Cowboy Christmas Rodeo</a>, where boots, bulls, and holiday spirit collide in true Lone Star fashion. Families should also check out the Light Park, an immersive drive-thru light experience that turns the night into a glowing technicolor. And if you’re staying at the<a linkid="22533" href="/go/jw-marriott-san-antonio_hill-country/"> <strong>JW Marriott</strong></a>, you’ll find even more fun without ever leaving the resort, including the Holiday ICE attraction, Light Up the Night, and a whole lineup of festive activities that kids and adults both love.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->

<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>And if you happen to be visiting during spooky season, or you just enjoy a little festive fright, don’t miss my list of <strong><a href="/haunted-hotels-san-antonio/" target="_blank" data-type="post" data-id="18554" rel="noreferrer noopener">family-friendly haunted hotels in San Antonio</a></strong>. Christmas in San Antonio has sparkle, soul, and just the right amount of Wild West fun, and it’s one holiday destination your family won’t forget.</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Dallas is one of the biggest cities in the U.S., and during the holidays, it goes full sparkle mode with southern confidence. You’ve got museums, shopping, nightlife, and plenty of festive fun all without needing to dress like the Michelin Man. With an average December high of around 58 degrees, you can explore the city comfortably while the rest of the country is chiseling ice off their cars. <em>Win.</em></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Start your holiday adventure with a stroll through Stone Street Gardens, a charming pedestrian-friendly spot filled with cafés and bistro dining. It’s perfect for a cozy bite, a round of hot chocolate, or that strategic snack stop before the kids completely unravel like an old string of Christmas lights. Plus, there are great shops nearby, so you can squeeze in some gift buying without mall-level chaos.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>But the real holiday magic in Dallas is in the events, like the Dallas Midtown Lights. If you believe holiday lights should be BIG (because you are in Texas, after all), this one delivers with a dazzling 90,000 square feet of displays. It’s bright, it’s magical, it’s Instagram gold, and yes, you’ll take 83 family photos and maybe two will look normal. That’s the joy of the season.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Be sure not to miss the Downtown Dallas Holiday Parade. This annual parade brings classic holiday energy to the heart of Downtown. Think giant floats, marching bands, performers, and that picture-perfect holiday moment that makes you want to hum <em>Jingle Bells</em> for the rest of the day. It’s festive, it’s lively, and it’s a great family memory, no snow boots required.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Just about a hundred miles north of the state capitol of Austin is the city of Waco, where the whole family can enjoy visiting the famed <a href="https://magnolia.com/silos/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener" aria-label="Silos at Magnolia Market (opens in a new tab)">Silos at Magnolia Market</a>, owned by Joanna and Chip Gaines of the hit TV Show "Fixer Upper" on HGTV. They have a fun, festive Christmas installation during the Holiday season that includes a light display that covers the enormous Silos. Don't forget to stop by their own restaurant, Magnolia Table, just a few miles down the road from the Silos. Parents and kids will enjoy a relaxing holiday at Magnolia Market in Waco.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Just about a hundred miles north of Austin sits Waco, a charming Central Texas stop that becomes downright magical during the holidays. If your family loves <em>Fixer Upper</em>, farmhouse style, or just wants a festive setting that looks straight out of a Hallmark movie (without manufactured snow),  <a href="https://magnolia.com/silos/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Silos at Magnolia Market</a> delivers. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>During Christmas at the Silos, the entire grounds glow under twinkling lights while holiday music and the smell of something delicious in the air make even the most chaotic travel day feel peaceful.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Families can wander the decorated grounds, snap free photos with Santa under the Historic Barn, no reservations or fighting an online ticketing system required, and even upgrade to a special VIP Santa experience if you want a more personal, keepsake moment.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>There are hands-on opportunities, too, like a Holiday Pressed Flower Ornament Workshop, where you can create something meaningful instead of just buying another ornament you’ll forget about by January. And for the story-loving travelers, the exclusive nighttime Castle tour adds after-hours magic and behind-the-scenes details that make the experience feel extra special.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>While you’re in town, make time for Magnolia Table, just a short drive from the Silos. Whether you’re digging into their famous biscuits or seasonal specials, it’s the perfect spot to fuel up before exploring more. Waco may not be the biggest city in Texas, but during the holidays it shines bright—warm, welcoming, and ready to help your family make real Christmas memories.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:block {"ref":17013} /-->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"dropCap":true} -->
<p class="has-drop-cap">Christmas in Texas might not be the first destination that pops into your head when you’re planning holiday travels, but as you’ve seen, the Lone Star State does not play small when it comes to seasonal fun. From river parades and rodeos to German Christmas markets and glittering city light shows, Texas delivers holiday magic with its own bold personality. Whether you’re sipping cocoa under twinkling lights in Grapevine or eating brisket instead of ham in Austin, the memories you make here are the kind that stick.</p>
