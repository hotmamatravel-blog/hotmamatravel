### 🐚 Hyatt Regency Newport Beach

Perfect for a laid-back coastal escape, **Hyatt Regency Newport Beach** offers lagoon-style pools connected by bridges and winding pathways. Kids love the water slide, splash park, evening smores, and lawn games, while parents appreciate the rooms that overlook the pool, onsite dining and proximity to [**Newport Dunes**](/newport-dunes-resort/) and [**Newport Beach attractions**](/newport-coast-with-kids/). It’s an ideal blend of relaxation and family fun.

#### **Why Families Love It**

-  Pool with water slide and splash park

- Family-friendly amenities

- Close to Newport beaches and ferry to Balboa Island

- On-site bike rentals and outdoor activities

**Best For:** A beachy Orange County staycation.

### 🏴‍☠️ Anaheim Resort Suites

Budget-friendly without sacrificing fun, **Anaheim Resort Suites** features a tropical-themed splash zone that keeps little ones entertained after a day at Disneyland. Spacious suite-style rooms make it a great option for larger families.

#### **Why Families Love It**

- Affordable all-suite accommodations

- Tropical-themed water playground and water slides

- Dive-in Movies

- Short drive to Disneyland

**Best For:** Budget-conscious families visiting Disneyland.

<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity"/>

### 🌴 Howard Johnson by Wyndham Anaheim Hotel &amp; Water Playground

Affectionately known as “HoJo” by Disney fans, this hotel’s **Castaway Cove** water playground is a hit with younger kids. Its convenient location near Disneyland makes it a top choice for families wanting both value and fun.

**Why Families Love It**

- Pirate-themed splash area with slides

- Walking distance to Disneyland

- Family-friendly pricing

**Best For:** Families with younger children visiting Disneyland.

<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity"/>

### 🌊 Courtyard Anaheim Theme Park Entrance

If proximity to Disneyland is a priority, this hotel delivers. The **Surfside Waterpark** includes multiple water slides and a splash zone, making it one of the most impressive water playgrounds near the parks.

**Why Families Love It**

- Spacious rooms with bunk beds

- Incredible water park area

- Easy walk to Disneyland

**Best For:** Families wanting convenience and resort-style amenities.

<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity"/>

### 🏋️ Renaissance ClubSport Aliso Viejo

This hidden gem in South Orange County combines a resort feel with a health club atmosphere. The outdoor pool features a fun water slide, and families can enjoy additional amenities like fitness classes and sports courts.

**Why Families Love It**

- Relaxed, less crowded environment

- On-site dining and fitness facilities

- Great for South OC explorers

**Best For:** Active families seeking a quieter stay.

<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity"/>

### 🏄 Hyatt Regency Huntington Beach Resort &amp; Spa

For a luxurious beachfront experience, this resort’s **Slyders Water Playground** offers water slides and family-friendly pools just steps from the sand. It’s the perfect mix of surf and splash.

**Why Families Love It**

- Beachfront location

- Water slides and splash zones

- Family activities and fire pits

**Best For:** Families wanting a high-end coastal getaway.

### 🏰 Disneyland Hotel

Disney magic continues long after you leave the parks at the **Disneyland Hotel**. The monorail-themed water slides and nostalgic design make this a dream stay for Disney-loving families.

**Why Families Love It**

- Monorail-themed slides

- Early park entry and Disney perks

- Immersive Disney theming

**Best For:** Families seeking the full Disney experience.

### 🎬 Pixar Place Hotel

Pixar fans will adore this reimagined hotel featuring playful theming and a rooftop pool with a water slide. It’s stylish, fun, and perfectly located near the Disneyland Resort.

**Why Families Love It**

- Unique Pixar-themed design

- Rooftop pool with slide

- Access to Disney perks

**Best For:** Families looking for a modern Disney stay.

### 🌲 Disney’s Grand Californian Hotel &amp; Spa

For those seeking luxury, the **Grand Californian** offers a serene, redwood-inspired pool area complete with a water slide. Direct access to Disney California Adventure makes it incredibly convenient.

**Why Families Love It**

- Exclusive park entrance

- Elegant, nature-inspired design

- Premium amenities

**Best For:** Luxury Disney vacations.

<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity"/>

### 🐺 Great Wolf Lodge – Garden Grove

If your kids want a full water park experience, **Great Wolf Lodge** delivers with its massive indoor water park, ensuring year-round fun regardless of the weather.

**Why Families Love It**

- Huge indoor water park

- Themed family suites

- Interactive activities like MagiQuest

**Best For:** Families seeking an all-in-one resort.

**Why Families Love It**

**Best For:** Families exploring multiple Orange County destinations.

## 💡 Tips for Choosing the Right Hotel

**1. Consider Your Itinerary**

- Near Disneyland: Anaheim properties.

- Beach getaway: Newport or Huntington Beach.

- All-in-one fun: Great Wolf Lodge.

**2. Think About Your Budget**

- Budget: Anaheim Resort Suites, Howard Johnson.

- Mid-range: Hyatt Regency Irvine, Renaissance ClubSport.

- Luxury: Disney’s Grand Californian, Hyatt Regency Huntington Beach.

**3. Age of Your Kids**

- Toddlers: Splash zones like Howard Johnson.

- Older kids: Larger slides at Courtyard Anaheim or Great Wolf Lodge.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### Do any Orange County hotels have indoor water slides?

Yes! **Great Wolf Lodge** features a massive indoor water park, perfect for year-round fun.

### Which hotels are within walking distance of Disneyland?

Courtyard Anaheim Theme Park Entrance, Howard Johnson Anaheim, Disneyland Hotel, Pixar Place Hotel, and Disney’s Grand Californian Hotel &amp; Spa are all conveniently located near the parks.

### Are there beachfront hotels with water slides in Orange County?

Yes, both **Hyatt Regency Huntington Beach** and **Hyatt Regency Newport Beach** offer water slides along with easy beach access.
