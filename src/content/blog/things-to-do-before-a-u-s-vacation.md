A vacation in the U.S. can be a great option for multiple reasons. There’ll be plenty of places to see and countless things to do. It’s easy to see why so many people go there every year. That doesn’t mean a U.S. vacation is the easiest thing to plan out and book.

Compared to other countries, there might be a few extra steps you need to take. If you don’t know about these, they could add a lot of last-minute stress to your trip. It’s worth diving into what you have to do before going on the trip.



## What To Do Before A U.S Vacation: 3 Top Picks





#### Be Prepared For Security

At some point or another, you’ll have to actually get to the U.S. for your vacation. That’s a trek in and of itself, and you could find yourself flying for most of the day. Then there’s [the security](https://www.dhs.gov/keywords/airport-security) to worry about. You’ll have to be prepared to go through a lot of this when you’re at the airport.

That’ll be the case before you take off and when you get there. You’ll even have to go through it all again on the way home. The U.S. is stricter than most other countries with this, so you’ll need to be extra prepared for it.





#### Do Your Research

You’ll already know you have to do a bit of research before a U.S. vacation, but you mightn’t know where to start with it. It’s best to look into the [specific city or state](/united-states/) you’re going to and lead on from there. If you’re going to Las Vegas, for example, then [Premier Vegas](https://www.premiervegas.com/) could be a great place to start.

Know what you can do while you’re there, how much things cost, how you can get around, and other factors. It’ll make the vacation more of a breeze once you’re there.





#### Know The Prices

It’s natural to want to buy a few mementos when you’re vacationing in the U.S. Be wary when you’re buying these, though. The price likely wouldn’t be what you’d first assume. There’ll be the price on the tag, and then there’ll be what you’ll actually pay. How much it costs is the more expensive of these.

That’s because of [sales tax](https://www.investopedia.com/terms/s/salestax.asp), which is only added as you’re buying the product. It wouldn’t be included on the tag price. When you’re looking around, add between 6% and 10% to the price, depending on where in the U.S. you are.



A U.S vacation is appealing for countless reasons. It’s an almost legendary destination thanks to all the movies and TV shows set there. Compared to other countries, though, it’s one of the more difficult to go to.

## That's a Wrap!

You wouldn’t just have to deal with a longer flight, but also a few other steps as you’re going there. Be prepared for them so you don’t need to deal with any last-minute stress.
