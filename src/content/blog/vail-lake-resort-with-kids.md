<span style="color: #000000;">Vail Lake, a little oasis tucked behind the wineries of Temecula, California. This was the site of our debut RV trip back in August 2012. It is now a part of our regular rotation. We enjoy the open space, beautiful views, fun village, annual events, proximity to town, and the low rates. It's no wonder why we love taking out the RV for the weekend to relax at Vail Lake Resort with Kids.</span>

<a href="/vail-lake-resort-with-kids/"><img class="aligncenter wp-image-613" title="Vail Lake Resort panoramic" src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/06/DSCN2942-1024x683.jpg" alt="Scenic shot of Vail Lake Resort lake and mountains" width="640" height="427" /></a>
<h2><span style="color: #000000;"><em><strong>Keep cost in check:</strong></em></span></h2>
<span style="color: #000000;">In general Vail Lake is a very reasonable with rates ranging from $45-$100, depending on the site area and time of stay. For instance, the lowest rates are found are in the winter months and weekdays. Summer is higher, it's really hot. Ugh. We prefer fall and spring.</span>

If you don't have your own RV, no problem! A nearby family-owned company called <a href="http://www.lakervrentals.com/">Lake RV Storage and Rentals</a> will deliver and set-up an RV rental for you! It's super easy and they have several different sizes to choose from. If you want to check them out, don't forget to mention "Hot Mama Travel" to get a 10% discount!
<h2><span style="color: #000000;"><em><strong>Drop your bags:</strong></em></span></h2>
<span style="color: #000000;">The camp areas at Vail Lake Resort are Arroyo Seco, Lower Arroyo, Merlot, Lower Merlot, The Oaks, Chardonnay Mesa and Lower Chardonnay. The Oaks is inexpensive, close to village and shady, but the lots are small and it is usually pretty crowded. Chardonnay Mesa is has the highest rates and is longer walk to the village and pool, but also has the best space per lot and the best views. Picking which section to camp really depends on what's important to you and if the higher rate is worth it.</span>

<h2><span style="color: #000000;"><em><strong>Get your Grub On:</strong></em></span></h2>
<span style="color: #000000;">Well this is camping, (or at least as close as my family is willing to get to camping) so we carefully plan our menu out weeks before a trip which includes deciding what restaurants we may want to try. We like to keep our camping cooking fairly simple. Since we have a lot of kids running around, we don’t have a ton of time to prep big meals. We have our designated cooks for the trip which are usually HotDaddy and Sis. We plan assembly-only meals that have been prepped at home so when we are good and hungry it only requires us to slap it together, warm it up, or throw it on a grill . Gram's pasta salad is a sample at every family function and camping is no exception.</span>

<a href="/vail-lake-resort-with-kids/"><img class="aligncenter wp-image-602" title="Cooking Breakfast on RV at Vail Lake Resort" src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/06/DSCN5714-1024x768.jpg" alt="Cooking Breakfast on RV at Vail Lake Resort" width="640" height="480" /></a>

<span style="color: #000000;">On the campground, </span>the village <span style="color: #000000;">has a little café to grab a morning breakfast burrito or afternoon burger. If you want to leave the campgrounds you are near the </span>Wine Country, Old Town Temecula and Pechanga Casino <span style="color: #000000;">which all have great dining options.</span>
<h2><span style="color: #000000;"><em><strong>Chug-a-lug:</strong></em></span></h2>
<span style="color: #000000;">When camping, the best place to get your drink on is your campsite, but sometimes you need to change the view. The village at Vail Lake has a fun little bar called the Bunkhouse which is great for a margarita stop after a game of miniature golf. Depending on the time of year, the Bunkhouse can be a pretty happening night spot. For instance, during the Halloween dance party the Bunkhouse as well as the entire village center is rocking!</span>

<a href="/vail-lake-resort-with-kids/"><img class="aligncenter wp-image-604" title="Vail Lake Resort bar cocktails" src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/06/DSCN5774-1024x768.jpg" alt="Vail Lake Resort Village Cocktails at the bar" width="640" height="480" /></a>

<a href="/vail-lake-resort-with-kids/"><img class="aligncenter wp-image-605" title="Vail Lake Resort at Halloween with children" src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/06/DSCN3056-1024x683.jpg" alt="Vail Lake Resort with kids During Halloween" width="640" height="427" /></a>

Off the campgrounds there is plenty of drinking to be had at the many wineries in the area such as Wilson Creek, South Coast, Ponte, Leoness Cellars and many more. For a full list of the wineries in the area check out Temecula Wine Country.

In Old Town, my favorite stop is The Bank. I never camp at Vail Lake without hitting up The Bank at least once. The patio is fun and the beer is cold. What else can you ask for?

<a href="/vail-lake-resort-with-kids/"><img class="aligncenter wp-image-606" title="Vail Lake Resort with young baby" src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/06/IMG_0439-1024x1024.jpg" alt="Parents and baby at Vail Lake Resort" width="640" height="640" /></a>

<span style="color: #000000;">While in Old Town, there is more opportunity to wine taste with many vineyards having tasting rooms within Old Town.</span>
<h2><span style="color: #000000;"><em><strong>Fun Things to do at Vail Lake RV Resort:</strong></em></span></h2>
<h3><span style="color: #000000;">Pool</span></h3>
<span style="color: #000000;">Tons to do at Vail Lake Resort with kids, you really don’t need to leave the grounds. Although lake access is reserved for members only, during the warm months of the year, kids will love splashing around in the pool and going down the water slide. I love that there is a toddler pool. I can sit and relax while my babies can play safely in the shallow water.</span>

<a href="/vail-lake-resort-with-kids/"><img class="aligncenter wp-image-607" title="Vail Lake Resort family fun in pool" src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/06/DSCN5749-682x1024.jpg" alt="Vail Lake Resort family fun in pool" width="640" height="961" /></a>
<h3><span style="color: #000000;">Miniature Golf</span></h3>
<span style="color: #000000;">The older kids (and adults) will enjoy play a round of miniature golf or horse shoes.</span>

<a href="/vail-lake-resort-with-kids/"><img class="aligncenter wp-image-609" title="Miniature Golf with kids at Vail Lake Resort" src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/06/DSCN5902-1024x768.jpg" alt="Miniature Golf with kids at Vail Lake Resort" width="640" height="480" /></a>
<h3><span style="color: #000000;">Karaoke</span></h3>
<span style="color: #000000;">At night the party continues with music, dancing and karaoke! We love karaoke in this family and tend to hog the microphone.</span>

<span style="color: #000000;">During seasonal events the nighttime really get popping like at our favorite Vampire Vail Weekend Halloween Party! See more details about this event, <a href="/vail-lake-resort-halloween-festivities/"><strong><span style="color: #ff0000;">HERE</span></strong></a>.

<h3><span style="color: #000000;"><strong>Temecula Stampede:</strong> </span></h3>
<span style="color: #000000;">In need to get down? Then a night of dancing at the Stampede is sure make for a good night! This is for adults only, but some nights are 18 and over so the older kids in your group can party too. Dance lessons start at 7:00pm, if you aren’t familiar with the line dances, no worries.</span>
<h3><span style="color: #000000;"><strong>Pechanga:</strong> </span></h3>
<span style="color: #000000;">Yes. It is a casino. Yes. It is for adults. Yes. I bring my kids. You’ll definitely need a babysitter if you want to make a night of it, but I usually travel with a large group and we switch off watching the kids. Kids are allowed in the main areas as well as the many dining spots. We will usually grab dinner as a family and then someone will take the kids for desserts while a few of us gamble and then we switch. Also there is an RV resort here, which I will write about in a later post.</span>
<h2><span style="color: #000000;"><em><strong>That’s a Wrap:</strong></em></span></h2>
<span style="color: #000000;">There is something very comfortable about Vail Lake Resort that keeps us coming back for more. We love the country vibe and the relaxing pace. The best a part about RVing at Vail Lake Resort with kids is the amount of quality-time our family gets to spend together.
