So you're planning a trip to Vegas with kids, right? Don't let the haters tell you that you're crazy! We [**take our kids to Vegas**](/las-vegas-with-kids/) *multiple times a year*, **and they absolutely love it**.

And one of the main reasons why my kids love Las Vegas is they get to experience amazing family-friendly Vegas shows with us.

Heck, **[we even took our Baby to Vegas](/advice-las-vegas-with-baby-infant-itinerary/)** for New Year's and experienced the [**Tournament of Kings**](/go/tournament-kings-las-vegas-bov/) at [**Excalibur**](/go/excalibur-las-vegas-exp/). You can tell the kids had an amazing time, even my 11-month-old!

It is *literally only in Vegas* where you can find so many incredible shows and live entertainment every single night of the week. And lucky for us (and you!), most of these Vegas shows are actually kid-friendly! Even many of the [**family restaurants have live music**](/kid-friendly-live-music-las-vegas/).

However, the large volume of Vegas shows also presents a problem* - **How do you know which one to pick!?***

The point of this article is to help you by giving a succinct overview for 20 of the top kid-friendly shows in Las Vegas organized by category and our own personal recommendations.
