## [The Plaza New York](/go/the-plaza-hotel-new-york/)

768 Fifth Avenue, New York City, NY 10019

The Plaza is known for its lavish holiday décor, including a towering Christmas tree, ornate wreaths, and garlands that adorn the hotel's grand interior. The lobby exudes a warm and festive atmosphere, and it's a popular spot for visitors to take holiday photos.

The Palm Court, known for its traditional afternoon tea, often offers special holiday-themed tea experiences during the Christmas season. Guests can indulge in festive tea blends, delicate sandwiches, and a variety of sweet treats in a refined and elegant setting.

For families, [The Plaza](https://www.theplazany.com/) often hosts Eloise-themed holiday events. Eloise is a beloved children's book character who lives at The Plaza. During the Christmas season, kids can enjoy Eloise-themed teas, crafts, and story time sessions.

The Plaza Food Hall often offers holiday-inspired dining options and gourmet gifts. It's an excellent place to explore culinary delights and find unique holiday gifts. The hotel's restaurants offer special holiday menus and elegant dining experiences for Christmas Eve and Christmas Day. Guests can enjoy gourmet meals while taking in the beautifully decorated surroundings.

The Plaza offers some very exciting holiday packages for families to really ring in the season. Experience the latest offering, "The Plaza's Home Alone 2: Escape to New York" adventure and embark on a "Lost in New York" journey throughout the festive season.

The Plaza often features live music performances, including carolers and musicians who contribute to the festive ambiance. Each Thursday, Sarafina's dance troupe, the precision dance ensemble hailing from New York and established by the former Radio City dancer Purdie Baumann, will deliver captivating live performances.

Two East 55th Street, at Fifth Avenue, New York City, NY 10022

Visiting The St. Regis New York during Christmas offers a sophisticated and timeless experience. The combination of opulent décor, festive dining, and live entertainment creates a warm and inviting holiday ambiance for guests looking to celebrate the season in a luxurious setting.

The [St. Regis](https://st-regis.marriott.com/) is known for its luxurious holiday decor. The hotel's lobby and public spaces are adorned with exquisite Christmas trees, elegant wreaths, and tasteful seasonal decorations, creating a warm and inviting ambiance for guests.

The King Cole Bar, located within The St. Regis, is famous for inventing the classic cocktail, the Bloody Mary. During the holiday season, it is beautifully decorated, offering guests a cozy and timeless setting to enjoy classic and seasonal cocktails.

The Astor Court at The St. Regis typically offers a special holiday-themed afternoon tea experience. Guests can indulge in an array of delicate sandwiches, freshly baked scones, and a variety of holiday-inspired pastries and sweets while sipping on fine teas.

The hotel often provides special Christmas Eve and Christmas Day dining experiences, including festive menus and delectable holiday-themed dishes at its restaurants. Guests can enjoy gourmet meals in a refined and festive setting.

The St. Regis frequently features live holiday entertainment, such as carolers and musicians, adding to the festive atmosphere throughout the holiday season.

Indulge in a luxurious holiday retreat within a Master or Designer Suite, complete with a beautifully adorned Christmas tree, delectable Cookies for Santa, and the option for an exquisite 3-course Christmas dinner or Christmas Brunch served right in your suite, accommodating up to 8 guests.
