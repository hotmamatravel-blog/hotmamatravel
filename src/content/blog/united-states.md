**There are so many great places to visit in the United States**, where should you [start](/)!?

**[We are from](/about/) **Southern California and therefore provide a ton of resources and advice for visiting Orange County, Los Angeles and San Diego with kids.

We have also devoted our time and resources to traveling with our kids as much as possible all across America. In Summer 2017, **we took a 60 day road trip from California all the way up to New York, and back, in our personal minivan**. You can read more about it [**here**](/family-road-trip-across-us/).

As a result, we have detailed destination guides for a variety of cities and states all across America. **Check out some our favorite destinations** by clicking a card below.
