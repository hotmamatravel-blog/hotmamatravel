<p><strong>There are so many great places to visit in the United States</strong>, where should you <a href="/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">start</a>!?</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong><a href="/who-is-hot-mama/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">We are from</a> </strong>Southern California and therefore provide a ton of resources and advice for visiting Orange County, Los Angeles and San Diego with kids.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We have also devoted our time and resources to traveling with our kids as much as possible all across America. In Summer 2017, <strong>we took a 60 day road trip from California all the way up to New York, and back, in our personal minivan</strong>. You can read more about it <a href="/family-road-trip-across-us/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener"><strong>here</strong></a>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>As a result, we have detailed destination guides for a variety of cities and states all across America. <strong>Check out some our favorite destinations</strong> by clicking a card below.</p>
