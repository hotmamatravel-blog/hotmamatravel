<h4 id="medieval-times">Take a Nature Walk</h4>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"dropCap":true} -->
<p class="has-drop-cap">The Florida Keys are home to a diverse array of wildlife and plant life. Take a nature walk at one of the many nature reserves in the area, such as the <a href="/go/crocodile-lake-national-wildlife-refuge-florida-keys/" linkid="17879">Crocodile Lake National Wildlife Refuge</a>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Other great places to explore Florida Keys nature and wildlife with a relaxing nature walk is <a href="/go/john-pennekamp-coral-reef-state-park-florida-keys/" linkid="17865">John Pennekamp Coral Reef State Park</a>, located in Key Largo, this park offers a variety of trails through mangrove swamps and hardwood hammocks. Visitors can see a wide variety of plant and animal life, including butterflies, birds, and reptiles.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><a href="/go/curry-hammock-state-parkk-florida-keys/" linkid="17880">Curry Hammock State Park</a> is located on Little Crawl Key and offers a nature trail that winds through a hardwood hammock and offers stunning views of the ocean. Visitors can also explore the beach and picnic areas.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><a href="/go/long-key-state-park-florida-keys/" linkid="17882">Long Key State Park </a>features a nature trail that leads visitors through a hardwood hammock and offers views of the ocean and the park's lagoon. Visitors can also swim, fish, and kayak in the park.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><a linkid="17863" href="/go/bahia-honda-state-park-florida-keys/">Bahia Honda State Park</a>, is home to a nature trail that offers views of the park's beaches, the historic Bahia Honda Bridge, and the ocean. Visitors can also swim, snorkel, and kayak in the park.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><a href="/go/key-west-tropical-forest-botanical-garden-florida-keys/" linkid="17883">Key West Tropical Forest and Botanical Garden</a>, a15-acre garden is home to a variety of native plants and animals, including butterflies, birds, and reptiles. Visitors can take a guided tour or explore the garden on their own.</p>
