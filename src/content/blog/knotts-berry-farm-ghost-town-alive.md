<span style="color: #000000;">It is no secret that this family loves Knott’s Berry Farm as it is historical and hosts a number of seasonal events. Recently, we were invited to experience their summer event, Knott’s Berry Farm Ghost Town Alive. Wow, what an event it is! </span>

<span style="color: #000000;">I immediately thought of Westworld because if you’ve seen the show, you know what’s up. Knotts Berry Farm Ghost Town Alive is not ruthless like WestWorld, but it does suck you into another place and time. The whole town really comes alive as actors play out an entire storyline throughout the day, beckoning guests to play an active part in the unfolding story. </span>

<span style="color: #000000;">I love the ghost town area of Knotts Berry Farm anyway, but when it is alive the fun is on a whole other level. Today, I am spilling all the details so that you don’t miss a thing when you plan your visit.</span>

<h2><em><span style="color: #000000;">Money Saving Tips:</span></em></h2>
<span style="color: #000000;">Great news! There is no extra charge to experience Ghost Town Alive. Daily tickets can be purchased online for as low as $45, but as always I recommend getting the seasonal pass if you can for the best value.</span>
<h2><em><span style="color: #000000;">What, Where, When:</span></em></h2>
<span style="color: #000000;"><a href="https://www.knotts.com/explore/ghost-town-alive"><span style="color: #000000;">Ghost Town Alive</span></a> is a summer event held from the end of May until early September. The whole event happens in the ghost town section of Knott’s Berry Farm, fashioned to replicate California’s very Calico Ghost Town (which Mr. Knott actually owned once) and Tombstone, Arizona. The event is actually a full on storyline that starts in the morning when the park opens and continues throughout the day until 6:00 pm.</span>

<span style="color: #000000;">This interactive story actually started last summer ending with the Mayfield Gang escaping Sheriff Wheeler. Now the story continues with Annual Founder’s Day Celebration. Some of the Mayfield Gang have been locked up, but are set for release. The day starts with the welcoming to the Annual Founder’s Day Celebration before we are gathered to witness the release of the Mayfield gang. After the release, the town functions as it would in the 1800’s. The store fronts are open with actors playing the roles of towns’ people and merchants, interacting with you in full character. There are various town announcements made for visitors to follow the Mayfield storyline including an election for the new sheriff as well as a few skirmishes; ending with big hoedown. It is amazing.</span>

<h2><em><span style="color: #000000;">Why We Loved Ghost Town Alive</span></em></h2>
<span style="color: #000000;">Knotts Berry Farm Ghost Town Alive is truly an interactive and immersive event. It is really easy to get sucked into the storyline. As a visitor, you are considered an honorary citizen of Calico Ghost Town and treated as such. The actors in town respond to you in character and you really begin to feel a part of Calico. My kids were completely invested in the story and actually had emotional reactions to the characters and what happened to them throughout the day.</span>

<em><a href="/knotts-berry-farm-ghost-town-alive/" rel="attachment wp-att-6005"><img class="aligncenter wp-image-6005" title="Kids wanted in Calico Knotts" src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/06/DSC01890.JPG-851x1024.jpg" alt="Kids wanted in Calico Knotts" width="640" height="770" /></a></em>
<h3><span style="color: #000000;">Meeting The Mayfields</span></h3>
<span style="color: #000000;">As part of our family media day we were actually assigned a team. We were given black bandanas and a cowboy hat, signifying that we were a part of the Mayfield Gang. Upon learning our role, the kids were eager to meet the Mayfield, even if they were locked up at the time.</span>

<span style="color: #000000;"><a href="/knotts-berry-farm-ghost-town-alive/" rel="attachment wp-att-6008"><img class="aligncenter wp-image-6008" title="Knotts Berry Farm Ghost Town Alive Jail" src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/06/IMG_5608-e1497330726484.jpg" alt="Knotts Berry Farm Ghost Town Alive Jail" width="640" height="567" /></a></span>
<h3><span style="color: #000000;">Siding With The Law</span></h3>
<span style="color: #000000;">After the release of the Mayfield boys, my kids decided to converse with Sheriff Wheeler. He told them that since they were good Mayfields they could help him keep the bad boys out of trouble. The Sheriff took into the Sheriff’s office and deputized them, which they loved, by swearing an oath to uphold the law and be good citizens of Calico. In fact, my daughter liked Sheriff Wheeler so much that she was very upset when Mayfield won the town election for new sheriff.</span>

<span style="color: #000000;"><a href="/knotts-berry-farm-ghost-town-alive/" rel="attachment wp-att-6009"><img class="aligncenter wp-image-6009" title="Knotts Ghost Town Alive Sheriff" src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/06/CXWB0444-e1497330969138.jpg" alt="Knotts Ghost Town Alive Sheriff" width="640" height="428" /></a></span>
<h3><span style="color: #000000;">Being A Citizen</span></h3>
<span style="color: #000000;">CuddleBear decided to check into the Pony Express to help the town make deliveries. At the Pony Express CuddleBear was given a package to deliver to Goldie’s Boarding House as well as a cute stick pony to ride through town while he made his deliveries.</span>

<span style="color: #000000;"><a href="/knotts-berry-farm-ghost-town-alive/" rel="attachment wp-att-6011"><img class="aligncenter wp-image-6011" title="Knotts Berry Farm Ghost Town Alive" src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/06/IMG_5623-e1497331350529.jpg" alt="Knotts Berry Farm Ghost Town Alive" width="640" height="854" /></a></span>

<span style="color: #000000;">CuddleBear also took the time to read the Calico Gazette and to gossip with the paper boy. There was lots going in town to talk about and CuddleBear loved being a citizen.</span>

<span style="color: #000000;"><a href="/knotts-berry-farm-ghost-town-alive/" rel="attachment wp-att-6012"><img class="aligncenter wp-image-6012" title="Knotts Ghost Town Alive with kids" src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/06/IMG_5613-e1497331524327.jpg" alt="Knotts Ghost Town Alive with kids" width="640" height="854" /></a></span>

<span style="color: #000000;">The old schoolhouse is always a favorite of my children, but it was made even better during Ghost Time Alive as lovely school teachers from the 1800’s were there to engage with the kids. My kids made themselves really comfortable, talking the poor ladies’ ears off. I had to physically remove them to continue on with our day of events</span>

<a href="/knotts-berry-farm-ghost-town-alive/" rel="attachment wp-att-6013"><img class="aligncenter wp-image-6013" title="Knotts Berry Farm Schoolhouse" src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/06/DSC02045-e1497331826404.jpg" alt="Knotts Berry Farm Schoolhouse" width="640" height="426" /></a>
<h3><span style="color: #000000;">Hoedown</span></h3>
<span style="color: #000000;">This was probably our favorite part of the day. The Ghost Town Alive event concludes with the town hoedown. At first the kids said they were tired and weren’t going to dance, but as soon as we arrived they changed their tune fast. All the characters from the town come out to dance with the guest. My kids were immediately swept up by performers they had seen throughout the day to dance as partners, including the beautiful dancers from the Calico Saloon show that we saw earlier in the day. It was so much fun! For kids who said they weren’t going to dance they ended up dancing the entire hoedown. So be sure not to miss the Ghost Town Hoedown!</span>

<span style="color: #000000;"><a href="/knotts-berry-farm-ghost-town-alive/" rel="attachment wp-att-6015"><img class="aligncenter wp-image-6015" title="Knotts Berry Farm Ghost Town Alive Hoedown" src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/06/HoeDown.jpg" alt="Knotts Berry Farm Ghost Town Alive Hoedown" width="640" height="640" /></a></span>
<h2><span style="color: #000000;">Shows at Knott's Berry Farm</span></h2>
<span style="color: #000000;">Between town weddings, elections and skirmishes, there were several summer shows to enjoy.</span>
<h3><span style="color: #000000;">Snoopy's Magnificent Magical Revue Show</span></h3>
<span style="color: #000000;">This show was actually located in the boardwalk section of Knotts Berry Farm, but it was new and we had to see what it was all about. If your kids like magic and illusions, then they will love this show. Featuring illusionist Greg Wilson, Snoopy and the Peanut Gang help bring magic to the stage. What I loved was the dance numbers. The costumes, music and dancing were fantastic. This show is slightly interactive, so have your kids sit toward the front for volunteer opportunities and on the isle to high-five the characters as they make their way to the stage.</span>
<h3><span style="color: #000000;">Calico Saloon Show</span></h3>
<span style="color: #000000;">Located in Ghost Town at the Calico Saloon, the show happens several times per day behind the bar top. The saloon is two stories and the show is viewable from the second story. I love the Calico Saloon Show for a couple reasons. First, there is beer and wine. YEAH! Second, the music, dancing and comedy is darn entertaining that the kids are fascinated the whole time. Third, the atmosphere in the Calico Saloon feels so authentic to the time era it represents. If they sold whiskey, I would never leave.</span>
<h3><span style="color: #000000;">Wild West Stunt Show: Frontiers Feats of Wonder</span></h3>
<span style="color: #000000;">This show is located at the entrance of ghost town and is completely outdoors. The show occurs several times a day, but be aware of hot afternoons. The sun hovers right over the audience and unless you get there early to grab a covered wagon seat, you be roasting. There is a splash zone that the kids might enjoy. It doesn’t get too wet where you would have to worry about a change of clothes, but enough to cool you down. The show is very funny and the stunts are heart stopping. It is definitely worth the visit.</span>
<h3><span style="color: #000000;">Circus of Wonders</span></h3>
<span style="color: #000000;">Held on the Calico Mine Stage, this show is a throwback to the days of the traveling circus. It is held after dark at about 8:45pm, so unfortunately we had to miss it. We were at the park since opening and the kids were done. Plus, it was a school night. I’m totally bummed though, because it looks amazing with crazy stunts, acrobatics, pyrotechnics and more. Since it happens all summer long, hopefully we can catch before it ends.</span>
<h2><em><span style="color: #000000;">That’s A Wrap</span></em></h2>
<span style="color: #000000;">All in all, Knotts Berry Farm Ghost Town Alive is one of my favorite events. The actors really make the town come alive, making you believe you are actually in 1800’s Calico. With so much going on in ghost town, you don’t even need to explore the rest of the park. Every time we thought we would venture off, something new was going down and we didn’t want to miss a thing. We just loved it and I think you will too! Ghost Town will be alive all summer long, so get your butts to Knotts Berry Farm!</span>
