#### [Robert’s Western World](/go/roberts-western-world-nashville/)

* 416 Broadway # B, Nashville, TN 37203*

Located on Broadway, [Robert’s Western World](https://robertswesternworld.com/) was recently voted favorite local honky tonk bar Nashville. Don’t let the word “bar” scare you away as an option for things to do in Nashville with kids. The venue opens at 10:00am and is open to all ages until 6:00pm.

Live music plays from open to close every day! So grab the kids and get there for lunch. The menu features a ton of affordable items from the grill, making this a must if you are looking for fun places to eat in Nashville.

#### [Bluebird Café](/go/bluebird-cafe-nashville/)

*4104 Hillsboro Pike, Nashville, TN 37215*

This famous Nashville listening room opened in 1982, giving many country music legends their start. Some may also know it from the popular show "Nashville". What makes this place unique is that the performers are not cover bands, but produce original material. In fact, this is where many songwriters share their work with the public in either a “in the round” or “in the row” format. It's history and notoriety makes the Bluebird Cafe one of the best kid-friendly restaurants in Nashville with live music!

In that respect there is a “Shhh Policy” at the [Bluebird Café](/go/bluebird-cafe-nashville/), in which the audience is asked to be quiet while the songwriters are performing. This might be hard to do with kids, but they are allowed for all shows except at the bar seats. Also, there is no kids’ menu, but there is a wide selection of appetizers, salads and sandwiches.

Interestingly, the venue is actually not located near the other popular music row Nashville bars. Rather, the Bluebird Café is actually closer to Belle Meade, where [we visited the Belle Meade Plantation](/belle-meade-plantation/) earlier in the day. We actually [stayed in Belle Meade](/staying-best-western-plus-belle-meade-inn-suites/) at the Best Western Plus, which worked out great for our family.
