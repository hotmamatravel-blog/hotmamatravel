<section class="wp-block-uagb-section uagb-section__wrap uagb-section__background-undefined uagb-block-272de9f1"><div class="uagb-section__overlay"></div><div class="uagb-section__inner-wrap"><!-- wp:heading {"level":4} -->
<h4 id="medieval-times"><a href="/go/bluebird-cafe-nashville/" linkid="11731">Bluebird Café</a></h4>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><em>4104 Hillsboro Pike, Nashville, TN 37215</em></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"dropCap":true} -->
<p class="has-drop-cap"><span style="color: #000000;">This famous Nashville listening room opened in 1982, giving many country music legends their start. Some may also know it from the popular show "Nashville". What makes this place unique is that the performers are not cover bands, but produce original material. In fact, this is where many songwriters share their work with the public in either a “in the round” or “in the row” format.</span> It's history and notoriety makes the Bluebird Cafe one of the best kid-friendly restaurants in Nashville with live music! </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><span style="color: #000000;">In that respect there is a “Shhh Policy” at the <a class="thirstylink" title="bluebird-cafe-nashville" href="/go/bluebird-cafe-nashville/" target="_blank" rel="nofollow noopener noreferrer"><span style="color: #ff0000;">Bluebird Café</span></a>, in which the audience is asked to be quiet while the songwriters are performing. This might be hard to do with kids, but they are allowed for all shows except at the bar seats. Also, there is no kids’ menu, but there is a wide selection of appetizers, salads and sandwiches.</span> </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><span style="color: #000000;">Interestingly, the venue is actually not located near the other popular music row Nashville bars. Rather, the Bluebird Café is actually closer to Belle Meade, where <a href="/belle-meade-plantation/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><span style="color: #ff0000;">we visited the Belle Meade Plantation</span></a> earlier in the day. We actually <a href="/staying-best-western-plus-belle-meade-inn-suites/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><span style="color: #ff0000;">stayed in Belle Meade</span></a> at the Best Western Plus, which worked out great for our family.</span></p>
