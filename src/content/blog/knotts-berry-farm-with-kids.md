<span style="color: #000000;">Before Knott’ Berry Farm became the theme park it is today, it was once a literal berry farm owned by Walter and Cordelia Knott way back in 1920. Once a roadside stop selling berries, preserves, pies and fried chicken, they eventually added attractions such as the Ghost Town to entertain their visitors. Even though there have been some major changes in the last 84 years, Knott’s Berry Farm has still maintained many of the original fixtures, keeping the quaint country feel that people love. Knott’s holds a special place in my heart, because it is where my husband asked me to his girlfriend in high school and the location of our wedding in 2006. Now eight years and two kids later, we still visit to enjoy Knott’s Berry Farm as a family. Let's check out all the fun things to do at Knott's Berry Farm with kids!
</span>

<h2><span style="color: #000000;"><em><strong>Keep the Cost in Check:</strong></em></span></h2>
<span style="color: #000000;">Knott’s is actually very reasonable when compared to other amusement parks in the Southern California. Daily tickets for an adult are about $40 and $35 for kids when purchased online. Disneyland is more than double that cost for a single day. Annual passes are the best value with as little as four $24 payments. In addition to low ticket prices, Knott’s is always having some sort of promotion to offer discounts or even free admission.</span>
<h2><span style="color: #000000;"><em><strong>Where to stay near Knotts Berry Farm:</strong></em></span></h2>

<span style="color: #000000;">Walking distance from the park, this hotel is reasonable as well as convenient. <h3><span style="color: #000000;"><a style="color: #000000;" href="https://www.knotts.com/california-marketplace/mrs-knott-s-chicken-dinner-restaurant" target="_blank" rel="nofollow noopener noreferrer">Mrs. Knott’s Chicken:</a> </span></h3>
<span style="color: #000000;">The restaurant that started it all. A staple of the park since 1934, Mrs. Knott’s Chicken is a must. This delicious meal still features Mrs. Knott’s original fried chicken, biscuits and boysenberry pie. It is a huge meal for the price: salad or soup, cabbage or corn, chicken, mashed potatoes and gravy, biscuits and boysenberry pie for dessert. To wash it all down, I recommend the boysenberry punch. There is always a line out the door, testament to how fantastic this meal is and it is worth the wait. The restaurant recently went through remodel and it is adorable farmhouse chic.
</span>

<h2><span style="color: #000000;"><strong><em>Fun Things to do at Knotts with Kids:</em></strong></span></h2>
<h3><span style="color: #000000;"><strong>Ghost Town</strong> </span></h3>
<span style="color: #000000;">This was the first attraction built by Mr. Knott. This old western town represents the real milling villages of the western United States, like Calico and Tombstone, and houses some of the best entertainment in the park. The Bird Cage Theatre has two fantastic seasonal performances, one during Christmas and one during Halloween. From information on these ACTUAL ghost towns, see my post on Calico (<strong><span style="color: #ff0000;"><a style="color: #ff0000;" href="/calico-ghost-town-with-kids/">HERE</a><span style="color: #000000;">)</span></span></strong> and Tombstone (<strong><span style="color: #ff0000;"><a style="color: #ff0000;" href="/tombstone-with-kids/">HERE</a></span></strong>). Also, it is here that all the seasonal events are held such as the Boysenberry Festival i spring, the Ghost Town Alive (<span style="color: #ff0000;"><strong><a style="color: #ff0000;" title="Real-life Westworld at Knott’s Berry Farm Ghost Town Alive" href="/knotts-berry-farm-ghost-town-alive/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">HERE</a></strong></span>) during summer, and Knott's Merry Farm in Winter (<a title="Real-life Westworld at Knott’s Berry Farm Ghost Town Alive" href="/knotts-merry-farm/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><strong><span style="color: #ff0000;">HERE</span></strong></a>).
</span>

<a href="/knotts-berry-farm-with-kids/" rel="attachment wp-att-5996"><img class="aligncenter wp-image-5996" title="Knotts Berry Farm Ghost Town Area" src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/07/DSC01920-1024x683.jpg" alt="Knotts Berry Farm Ghost Town Area" width="640" height="426" /></a>
<h3><span style="color: #000000;"><strong><a style="color: #000000;" href="https://www.knotts.com/rides/Water-Rides-1-5-12-21/Timber-Mountain-Log-Ride" target="_blank" rel="nofollow noopener noreferrer">Timber Mountain Log Ride:</a></strong></span></h3>
<span style="color: #000000;">Original to the park, it has recently gone through a remodel. It was my favorite ride growing up and now I love loading the family into a log for a wild ride together.</span>

<span style="color: #ff0000;"><strong><a href="/knotts-berry-farm-with-kids/" rel="attachment wp-att-5981"><img class="aligncenter wp-image-5981" title="Knotts Berry Farm with Kids on Log Ride" src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/07/TIMBER-768x1024.jpg" alt="Knotts Berry Farm with Kids on Log Ride" width="640" height="853" /></a></strong></span>
<h3><span style="color: #000000;"><strong><a style="color: #000000;" href="https://www.knotts.com/rides/Family-Rides-1-5-12-50/Calico-Mine-Ride" target="_blank" rel="nofollow noopener noreferrer">The Calico Mine Ride:</a></strong> </span></h3>
<span style="color: #000000;">Kids will love going deep into the mountain on this crazy mine train. It got a little intense for my son, but what an adventure.</span>

<a href="/knotts-berry-farm-with-kids/" rel="attachment wp-att-4351"><img class="aligncenter wp-image-4351" title="Knott's Berry Farm Calico Mine Ride" src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/06/IMG_1783-1024x768.jpg" alt="Knott's Berry Farm Calico Mine Ride" width="640" height="480" /></a>
<h3><span style="color: #000000;"><strong><a style="color: #000000;" href="https://www.knotts.com/rides/Family-Rides-1-5-12-50/Calico-Railroad" target="_blank" rel="nofollow noopener noreferrer">Calico Railroad:</a></strong> </span></h3>
<span style="color: #000000;">I love this ride. Load onto a real steam engine and tour the park, but wait there is more! Western Train robbers hop on the Train car to terrorize (entertain) the innocent passengers.</span>

<a href="/knotts-berry-farm-with-kids/" rel="attachment wp-att-4353"><img class="aligncenter wp-image-4353" title="Knott's Berry Farm with Kids near Calico Train" src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/06/IMG_1781-576x1024.jpg" alt="Knott's Berry Farm with Kids near Calico Train" width="640" height="1138" /></a>
<h3><span style="color: #000000;"><strong>Old School House:</strong> </span></h3>
<span style="color: #000000;">An old school house from the 1800’s, this is my daughter’s favorite attraction. She loves to sit in the original school desks and listen to the teacher. Pictures of the original classes adorn the walls as well as old school books and maps of the U.S.</span>

<a href="/knotts-berry-farm-with-kids/" rel="attachment wp-att-5998"><img class="aligncenter wp-image-5998" title="Kids in Old Schoolhouse at Knotts" src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/07/DSC02042-1024x683.jpg" alt="Kids in Old Schoolhouse at Knotts" width="640" height="426" /></a>
<h3><span style="color: #000000;"><strong>Graveyard</strong>: </span></h3>
<span style="color: #000000;">Fun and creepy. It was my favorite spot when I was kid to play and now my kids love to let their imagination run wild. There is grave that when you stand on it you can still feel the heart beat under your feet.</span>

<a href="/knotts-berry-farm-with-kids/"><img class="aligncenter wp-image-719" title="Knott's Berry Farm Graveyard with Kids" src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/06/DSCN1213-1024x768.jpg" alt="Knott's Berry Farm Graveyard with Kids" width="640" height="480" /></a>
<h3><span style="color: #000000;"><strong>Fiesta Village:</strong> </span></h3>
<span style="color: #000000;">This fun village celebrates California’s Mexican heritage. There great live entertainment, fun rides and delicious food.</span>

<span style="color: #000000;"><strong><a href="/knotts-berry-farm-with-kids/" rel="attachment wp-att-5983"><img class="aligncenter wp-image-5983" title="Knotts Fiesta Village with Kids" src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/07/Fiesta-1024x1024.jpg" alt="Knotts Fiesta Village with Kids" width="640" height="640" /></a></strong></span>
<h3><span style="color: #000000;"><strong>The Boardwalk:</strong></span></h3>
<span style="color: #000000;"> 1950’s broadwalk embraces California beach life.</span>

<span style="color: #000000;"><strong><a href="/knotts-berry-farm-with-kids/" rel="attachment wp-att-5999"><img class="aligncenter wp-image-5999" title="Knotts Berry Farm Boardwalk Area" src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/07/DSC02088-1024x683.jpg" alt="Knotts Berry Farm Boardwalk Area" width="640" height="426" /></a></strong></span>
<h3><span style="color: #000000;"><strong>Indian Trails:</strong> </span></h3>
<span style="color: #000000;">A smooth transition from Ghost Town, Indian Trails honors the Native American Culture, with performance and unique shops.</span>

<a href="/knotts-berry-farm-with-kids/"><img class="aligncenter wp-image-722" title="Knott's India Trails section with Kids" src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/06/DSCN1114-682x1024.jpg" alt="Knott's India Trails section with Kids" width="640" height="961" /></a>
<h3><span style="color: #000000;"><strong>Camp Snoopy:</strong> </span></h3>
<span style="color: #000000;">All about the kids. A land filled with age appropriate rides. It was recently remodeled and better than ever.</span>

<h2><span style="color: #000000;"><em><strong>That’s a Wrap:</strong></em></span></h2>
<span style="color: #000000;">Knott’s Berry Farm has been an Orange County favorite for over 80 years and it continues to thrive every year. A favorite in HotMama’s family, Knott’s is affordable enough to visit over and over again. We love the history, the old west theme and all the exciting entertainment. Knott's Berry Farm with kids is always a good idea!</span>
