### [US Space and Rocket Center](/go/us-space-rocket-center-alabama/)

1 Tranquility Base, Huntsville, AL 35805
</div>

If you are going to Alabama then you need to visit the US Space and Rocket Center. This is quite possibly one of the most fascinating and exciting attractions that there is for you to see in Alabama. You can also visit numerous exhibits, which are home to numerous rockets and artifacts. You will see numerous achievements from the US Space Center, which opened up in 1970, right after the moon landing. A lot of the exhibits focus on this but at the end of the day, you also have rotating exhibits throughout the year. This helps you to take advantage of a lot of different exhibits.

### [Robert Trent Jones Golf Trail](/go/robert-trent-jones-golf-trail-alabama/)

</div>

The Robert Trent Jones Golf Trail is a group of golf courses which are all designed by the famous architect. The golf courses can be found throughout Alabama and they include Dothan, Mobile, Point Clear, Auburn and Greenville. A lot of the courses can be found throughout Alabama and they all offer their own amenities. You have golf club memberships and you also have a range of country clubs that you can visit as well so make sure that you keep that in mind.

**[See Photos, Reviews, &amp; More](/go/robert-trent-jones-golf-trail-alabama/)**

<div class="wp-block-uagb-advanced-heading uagb-block-5252ac40">

### [Battleship Memorial Park](/go/battleship-memorial-park-alabama/)

2703 Battleship Pkwy, Mobile, AL 36602
</div>

Located in Mobile Bay, this memorial park has a very rich history and it also has a huge collection of aircraft and chips. On top of this, some of the highlights include the fact that you have a fast battleship, bombers and even fighter planes from the Vietnam War. This is one of the best things to do near the [Gulf Shore](https://www.themobilerundown.com/things-to-do-in-gulf-shores/) and it’s amazing to see how much history it has to offer. Some of the highlights include planes that were flown by the [Tuskegee Airman](https://www.britannica.com/topic/Tuskegee-Airmen) along with various patrol boats. You also have the iconic M4 Sherman Tank which is great, to say the least.

### [McWane Science Center](/go/mcwane-science-center-alabama/)

200 19th St N, Birmingham, AL 35203
</div>

Another place you may want to visit would be the McWane Science Center. One good thing about this location is that it is a science museum and it is also a research center. It’s great for families and it is full of fascinating artifacts. It has minerals as well as a lot of fossils. You have an interactive science exhibit and you also have an aquarium that is home to a number of freshwater species, as well as sharks and rays.

Of course, if you have young children then you have a young children’s play area there along with a water play area. This is a great way for you to help your kids pick up new skills and you may also find that there are a lot of different structures for kids to climb on too which is great, to say the least.
