<span style="color: #000000;">Although my kids have been raised near the sea, my daughter is a country girl at heart. She claims to want to live on a farm, milk cows, get her eggs from her own chickens and grow her own food. She loves the Food Network with her favorite shows being Pioneer Woman, Farmhouse Rules and Heartland Table. Fortunately, my niece married herself a real cowboy and we were invited to visit his family farm. Firecracker had a blast feeding all the animals and letting the baby calves suck on her fingers. She was in country heaven. Loving to indulge their interests we decided to make a special road trip visit to Bravo Farms with Kids!
</span>

<a href="/bravo-farms-with-kids/"><img class="aligncenter wp-image-828" title="Little Girl feeding cows" src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/07/DSCN0655-1024x682.jpg" alt="Little Girl feeding cows" width="640" height="426" /></a>

<span style="color: #000000;">As promised, we kept the farm fun going at<span style="color: #ff0000;"><a style="color: #ff0000;" href="https://www.bravofarms.com/traver/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"> Bravo Farms</a></span>. It was a great way to break up the long drive with cute shops, an interesting diner, and fun attractions for the kids. Bravo Farms is very reminiscent of Knott’s Berry Farm’s beginnings, which you can find out more about on my Knott's post, <span style="color: #ff0000;"><a style="color: #ff0000;" href="/knotts-berry-farm/">HERE</a></span>. We had such a wonderful time, that we all agreed that this will be a regular pit-stop.</span>

<a href="/bravo-farms-with-kids/"><img class="aligncenter wp-image-831" title="Bravo Farms" src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/07/DSCN0927-e1405049157853-1024x250.jpg" alt="Bravo Farms" width="640" height="156" /></a>
<h2><span style="color: #000000;"><em><strong>Get your Grub On:</strong></em></span></h2>
<span style="color: #000000;"><strong>Café 99:</strong> The perfect way to satisfy that road trip hunger. Delicious burgers, sandwiches and salads washed down with a giant milkshake or a cold jumbo beer. Yes, please. With both outdoor and indoor setting, Café 99 has a wild roadside diner atmosphere with vintage road signs splashed all over the wall, pool tables, and neon bar signs.</span>

<a href="/bravo-farms-with-kids/"><img class="aligncenter wp-image-833" title="Bravo Farms with Kids" src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/07/DSCN0950-1024x768.jpg" alt="Bravo Farms with Kids" width="640" height="480" /></a>

<a href="/bravo-farms-with-kids/"><img class="aligncenter wp-image-835" title="Inside Bravo Farms CA" src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/07/DSCN0958-768x1024.jpg" alt="Inside Bravo Farms CA" width="640" height="853" /></a>

<span style="color: #000000;">For more tasty treats be sure to check out the cheese shoppe, ice cream shoppe, fruit stand and Leo’s Barbeque.</span>
<a href="/bravo-farms-with-kids/"><img class="aligncenter wp-image-838" title="Bravo Farms Ice Cream Shop" src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/07/DSCN1002-768x1024.jpg" alt="Bravo Farms Ice Cream Shop" width="640" height="853" /></a>
<h2><span style="color: #000000;"><em><strong>Chug-A-Lug:</strong></em></span></h2>
<span style="color: #000000;">When I’m the road all I think is: Cold Beer. Cold Beer. Cold Beer. Café 99 has a cute rustic bar and a great selection of large draft beers to help keep parents sane on long road trips. Cheers!</span>
<a href="/bravo-farms-with-kids/"><img class="aligncenter wp-image-840" title="Drinking Beers at Bravo Farms" src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/07/DSCN0952-1024x768.jpg" alt="Drinking Beers at Bravo Farms" width="640" height="480" /></a>
<h2><em><strong>Entertain Me:</strong></em></h2>
<span style="color: #000000;">Bravo Farms with kids was a fantastic idea. It is the perfect place to get them out of the car to stretch and wear out. The patio to Café 99 is like an American Pickers honey hole, loaded with bizarre almost junkyard pieces made into a playground for kids. There is a wonderfully whimsical seven-story tree house for kids (and parents) to explore. Actually, I could have moved in, it was so darn cute. There are tons of animals to feed and pet. Slides, tire swings, pretend horse and carriages, random wooden swings, wishing wells, miniature golf, shooting gallery, fun little antique vignettes throughout the property and much more.</span>

<a href="/bravo-farms-with-kids/"><img class="aligncenter wp-image-849" title="Bravo Farms with Kid on swing" src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/07/DSCN0993-768x1024.jpg" alt="Bravo Farms with Kid on swing" width="640" height="853" /></a>

<a href="/bravo-farms-with-kids/"><img class="aligncenter wp-image-845" title="Bravo Farms with Kids on a slide" src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/07/DSCN0978-768x1024.jpg" alt="Bravo Farms with Kids on a slide" width="640" height="853" /></a>

<span style="color: #000000;">The kids loved exploring the playhouses for sale. I wanted to buy each one, because they were so fun and unique. My favorite was the Saloon. It just seemed fitting for our family.</span>

<h2><span style="color: #000000;"><em><strong>That’s a Wrap:</strong></em></span></h2>
<span style="color: #000000;">One of the great things about road trips is the random treasures stumbled upon. Bravo Farms with kids was a wonderful break from the long road ahead and a place went can’t wait to visit again soon!</span>
