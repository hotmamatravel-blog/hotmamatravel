#### [The Saguaro Palm Springs](/go/the-saguaro-palm-springs/)

*1800 E Palm Canyon Dr, Palm Springs, CA 92264*

The Saguaro boasts a poolside paradise that beckons families to indulge in a vibrant and delightful experience. This captivating oasis boasts an array of hues reminiscent of a desert wildflower bloom, setting the scene for a whimsical and colorful retreat. The Saguaro's pool offers something for everyone, making it an ideal destination for families seeking the ultimate escape from the desert's blazing heat.

As the sun blazes high overhead, parents can unwind on comfortable lounge chairs while keeping a watchful eye on their little ones frolicking in the shallows of the family-friendly pool area. The graduated entry allows for safe and easy access, making it perfect for children of all ages to splash and play. The water's refreshing embrace is a welcome relief from the desert's scorching rays, inviting families to bask in the joy of quality time together.

Beyond the refreshing waters, The Saguaro's pool area transforms into a hub of excitement and entertainment for kids and adults alike. An array of poolside games and activities keeps the whole family engaged, from beach volleyball and water balloon tosses to the occasional poolside DJ spinning tunes, creating an energetic atmosphere that is sure to put a smile on everyone's face.

As the sun begins its descent, families can gather around cozy fire pits and enjoy marshmallow roasting, sharing stories of their day's adventures. The Saguaro's poolside bar and grill offer a delectable selection of kid-friendly treats and refreshing beverages for parents to savor while soaking in the vibrant ambiance.

#### [Omni Rancho Las Palmas Resort &amp; Spa](/go/omni-rancho-las-palmas-palm-springs/)

*41000 Bob Hope Dr, Rancho Mirage, CA 92270*

The pool at Omni Rancho Las Palmas Resort &amp; Spa embodies the essence of family-friendly bliss. As one of the best pools in Palm Springs, this enchanting retreat offers an idyllic haven for families seeking an unforgettable escape. Boasting a sprawling water wonderland, the Omni Rancho's pool caters to the desires of every family member, from the littlest splashers to the adults in search of serenity.

Families will love Splashtopia, the resort's water park featuring thrilling waterslides, sending squeals of joy echoing through the air as kids make a splash and create unforgettable memories. The winding lazy river is a perfect way for the whole family to unwind and float leisurely under the warm desert sun, providing an opportunity to bond and savor the beauty of Palm Springs together.

For parents seeking a moment of tranquility, the exclusive adults-only pool area beckons, offering a serene retreat where they can indulge in some much-needed relaxation while keeping a watchful eye on their little ones splashing nearby. Meanwhile, the zero-entry pool provides a safe space for toddlers and younger children to explore the water's wonders under the watchful eye of attentive lifeguards.

Surrounded by towering palm trees and lush greenery, the poolside cabanas present a shaded sanctuary for families to rejuvenate in between pool adventures. Kids can enjoy a variety of activities and games organized by the resort's friendly staff, allowing parents to unwind and revel in the resort's luxurious amenities.
