<strong><span style="color: #000000;">Happy Thanksgiving everyone!</span></strong>

<span style="color: #000000;">It is approaching Turkey Day here in the U.S. and families are busy preparing big festive meals to feed big hungry mouths. In between bites of green bean casserole (my favorite) and turkey stuffing, families are getting mushy as they remember to give thanks for the wonderful things in their lives. Although Thanksgiving is an U.S holiday, I think gratefulness translate worldwide. Being a traveling mama I am feeling very thankful myself, so I’d thought it would be fun to share my <strong>26 Reasons I'm Thankful for Travel.</strong></span>
<h2><span style="color: #000000;"><strong>1. Family Fun
</strong></span></h2>
<span style="color: #000000;"> First thing I am thankful for is my lovely, exciting and crazy family. Without them my travels and life would not be nearly as exciting. Even when we aren’t on the road, everyday feels like a vacation with this wild bunch. I love our teamwork and support system. We work together like a machine to make our group goals a reality. I love them more than words will ever be able to express.</span>

<a href="/26-reasons-im-thankful-for-travel/"><img class="aligncenter wp-image-2290 size-large" title="Thankful for Travel" src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/06/DSCN9302-1024x684.jpg" alt="Thankful for Travel" width="640" height="428" /></a>
<h2><span style="color: #000000;"><strong>2. Self-Motivation
</strong></span></h2>
<span style="color: #000000;">I am thankful my drive. It sounds silly, but multiple multi-generational trips a year can be exhausting. It is easier and cheaper to stay home, but that would not satisfy me. My passion for travel and the joy I get from making memories with my family is worth all the extra effort.</span>
<h2><span style="color: #000000;"><strong>3.Creating Global Citizens
</strong></span></h2>
<span style="color: #000000;">I’m thankful for the travels experiences I have given my kids. I hear them talk about the world and my heart flutters. They already know so much about different cultures, food and geography. They are curious, courageous and compassionate.
</span>

<a href="/26-reasons-im-thankful-for-travel/" rel="attachment wp-att-4633"><img class="aligncenter size-large wp-image-4633" src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/11/DSC06370-1024x683.jpg" alt="Thankful for Travel" width="640" height="427" /></a>
<h2><span style="color: #000000;"><strong>4. Earning Rewards</strong> </span></h2>
<span style="color: #000000;">I am thankful for my </span>travel rewards credit cards!<span style="color: #000000;"> Without them I wouldn’t have been able to accomplish many of our big adventures this year. I even earn enough rewards to get us through future travels!</span>
<h2><span style="color: #000000;"><strong>5. Travel Hacks</strong> </span></h2>
<span style="color: #000000;">I’m thankful for flight trackers like </span>Google Flights, Yapta and SkyScanner <span style="color: #000000;">for helping me get the best deals on airfare equaling more trips.</span>
<h2><span style="color: #000000;"><strong>6. Flexible Work</strong> </span></h2>
<span style="color: #000000;">I’m thankful for my bartending job, because not only has provided me enough money to fund our travels, it also has an amazingly flexible schedule that allows me freedom to spend time with my kids and take time to actually travel. Plus, it is a really fun work environment, a social outlet and gives the gift of cocktails!</span>

<a href="/26-reasons-im-thankful-for-travel/" rel="attachment wp-att-4635"><img class="aligncenter size-large wp-image-4635" src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/11/Craft-Cocktails-8-1024x856.jpg" alt="Thankful for Travel" width="640" height="535" /></a>
<h2><span style="color: #000000;"><strong>7. Serendipity </strong> </span></h2>
<span style="color: #000000;">This brings me to my next travel thanks. If I didn’t work at the bar, I would have never met this awesome couple from Glasgow. I met them just as I was planning my trip to Scotland and they provided me a ton of useful travel tips. I'm also thankful I took notes and their advice, because it made my trip fantastic! You never know who will meet, that will lead to your next adventure.
</span>
<h2><span style="color: #000000;"><strong>8. Going Abroad
</strong></span></h2>
<span style="color: #000000;"> I’m thankful for our two week trip to Scotland for many reasons. HotDaddy and I were able to take my parents and our kids our this grand adventure. There were lots firsts for our group such as, Papa’s first passport, our first trip together to Europe, and our first time to Scotland.</span>

<a href="/26-reasons-im-thankful-for-travel/"><img class="aligncenter wp-image-2536 size-large" title="Thankful for Travel" src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/07/DSCN9640-1024x748.jpg" alt="Thankful for Travel" width="640" height="468" /></a>
<h2><span style="color: #000000;">9. Scotch</span></h2>
<span style="color: #000000;">Scotch. Yes. Scotch. Thank you Scotland for turning me into a Scotch lover. Although I have always been a whiskey lover, I have never cared for the peaty flavor of Scotch, but after having two weeks of nonstop consumption at an affordable cost, I have discovered many Scotch’s I love and now have to have it in my life!</span>
<h2><span style="color: #000000;">10. Accomplishments
</span></h2>
<span style="color: #000000;">I’m thankful for our last trip to</span> St. Pete Beach<span style="color: #000000;">. We love visiting every year, but with our Scotland trip planned a week later, we almost gave it up! I am so grateful I we didn't, because with  HotDaddy starting a new job and we couldn't visit in 2016. Thankfully, we are must likely going to be back in 2017 and I am hopeful for that possibility. For more on St. Pete, see <strong><span style="color: #ff0000;"><a style="color: #ff0000;" href="/st-pete-beach-with-kids/">HERE</a></span></strong> or where to stay, see <strong><span style="color: #ff0000;"><a style="color: #ff0000;" href="/tradewinds-island-grand/">HERE</a></span></strong>.
</span>

<a href="/26-reasons-im-thankful-for-travel/"><img class="aligncenter wp-image-3579 size-large" title="Thankful for Travel" src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/11/DSCN92981-1024x683.jpg" alt="Thankful for Travel" width="640" height="427" /></a>
<h2><span style="color: #000000;"><strong>11. Financial Freedom</strong> </span></h2>
<span style="color: #000000;">I am grateful for HotDaddy’s new job, because it has given us a little more financial freedom and more reasons to travel as he has to work across the country every month. Plus, he gets to work remotely when in town, giving us much more time together during the week.
</span>
<h2><span style="color: #000000;"><strong>12. Local Travel</strong> </span></h2>
<span style="color: #000000;">I’m thankful FireCracker started kindergarten. It has given her an outlet to shine and grow. Plus, coupled with HotDaddy’s new work schedule it has kept my booty in town and forced us to enjoy more local travel.</span>
<h2><span style="color: #000000;"><strong>13. Home Sweet Home
</strong></span></h2>
<span style="color: #000000;"> I am thankful for living in Southern California, because there is so much to do. So, even though we have been restricted to weekend getaways, due to work and school schedules, we are having a blast! We have enjoyed everything from our beaches to our mountains, our amusement parks to our big cities. What an exciting state to live and visit!</span>

<a href="/wp-content/uploads/2015/10/LB-113.jpg"><img class="aligncenter wp-image-3348 size-large" title="Thankful for Travel" src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/10/LB-113-1024x684.jpg" alt="Thankful for Travel" width="640" height="428" /></a>
<h2><span style="color: #000000;"><strong>14. Access to Disney</strong> </span></h2>
<span style="color: #000000;">Disneyland is a big deal to many families and I am so grateful to have Disney Passports, allowing our family to practically live at Disney parks. We don’t need all day marathons that leave us feeling exhausted and grumpy. We have the freedom to pop over for a couple hours during the evening to squeeze in a few rides and a beer. What more do you need?</span>

<a href="/26-reasons-im-thankful-for-travel/"><img class="aligncenter wp-image-3580 size-large" title="Thankful for Travel" src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/11/IMG_4750-1024x1024.jpg" alt="Thankful for Travel" width="640" height="640" /></a>
<h2><span style="color: #000000;">15. Upgrades
</span></h2>
<span style="color: #000000;"> Although I really like our first RV, I’m thankful for Sis’s new RV, The Voltage! Now we have even more room for our big family RV trips! It seems like every year our trips get bigger as the family grows, so an upgrade was needed.
</span>

<a href="/26-reasons-im-thankful-for-travel/"><img class="aligncenter wp-image-2258 size-large" title="Thankful for Travel" src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/06/IMG_5908-1024x768.jpg" alt="Thankful for Travel" width="640" height="480" /></a>
<h2><span style="color: #000000;">16. Firsts
</span></h2>
<span style="color: #000000;">I am thankful that we finally took our first Disney Cruise. Not only was it amazing experience, but we are now official Castaway Club members. This means there are many more Disney cruising in our future and I am very thankful for that!</span>

<a href="/26-reasons-im-thankful-for-travel/" rel="attachment wp-att-4639"><img class="aligncenter size-large wp-image-4639" src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/11/237-24207386-Embrk-Terminal-21509_GPR-1024x819.jpg" alt="Thankful for Travel" width="640" height="512" /></a>
<h2><span style="color: #000000;">17. RV Trips
</span></h2>
<span style="color: #000000;">I’m thankful for all our RV trips this year! We were really busy with other travels and didn’t RV as much as we normally do, but the ones we did accomplish (<span style="color: #ff0000;"><strong><a style="color: #ff0000;" href="/weekend-in-san-diego/">Mission Bay</a></strong>, <a style="color: #ff0000;" href="/newport-dunes-resort/"><strong>Newport Dunes </strong></a></span></span>and <span style="color: #000000;"><span style="color: #ff0000;"><a style="color: #ff0000;" href="/vail-lake-resort-halloween-festivities/"><strong>Vail Lake</strong></a></span>) were memory makers.</span>
<h2><span style="color: #000000;">18. Mountain Escape
</span></h2>
<span style="color: #000000;"> I’m thankful for our annual weekend getaways in </span>Idyllwild<span style="color: #000000;">. Fireside Inn is our favorite place to stay, giving my kids an awesome experience every time. CuddleBear still talks about our cabin weekend even though he has been all over the place. Most likely, because it a slow-travel where all we do is enjoy nature, eat at amazing restaurants and play card games. For more about Idyllwild, see <strong><span style="color: #ff0000;"><a style="color: #ff0000;" href="/things-to-do-in-idyllwild-with-kids/">HERE</a></span></strong> or about Fireside Inn, see <span style="color: #ff0000;"><a style="color: #ff0000;" href="/fireside-inn-idyllwild/"><strong>HERE</strong></a></span>.
</span>

<a href="/26-reasons-im-thankful-for-travel/"><img class="aligncenter wp-image-1859 size-large" title="Thankful for Travel" src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/02/DSCN8705-1024x515.jpg" alt="Thankful for Travel" width="640" height="322" /></a>
<h2><span style="color: #000000;">19. Travel Eats
</span></h2>
<span style="color: #000000;"> Food! I am thankful for all the amazing, delicious and exciting food we have experienced this year on the road and locally. We seriously have eaten the best food this year. From Idyllwild’s unique foodie restaurants to </span>Scotland’s hearty comforts<span style="color: #000000;"> to the endless crab legs in Florida. I have been eating my way around the world and I don’t plan on stopping any time soon.</span>
<h2><span style="color: #000000;">20. Adventurous Eaters
</span></h2>
<span style="color: #000000;">Speaking of food, I am thankful I don’t have <span style="color: #ff0000;"><a style="color: #ff0000;" href="/developing-kids-global-palate/"><strong>picky eaters</strong></a></span> for kids. Actually, that was something HotDaddy was concerned about before we had children. We love food so much that the thought of having picky eaters terrified him. Well good news, we don’t and they are willing to try anything and everything. However, CuddleBear does need a bit more encouragement than FireCracker sometimes.</span>

<a href="/26-reasons-im-thankful-for-travel/"><img class="aligncenter wp-image-3263 size-large" title="Thankful for Travel" src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/09/IMG_8677-682x1024.jpg" alt="Thankful for Travel" width="640" height="961" /></a>
<h2><span style="color: #000000;">21. Smooth Flying
</span></h2>
<span style="color: #000000;">As a fearful flyer (yes a traveler afraid to fly), I am very thankful that all my flights have been very smooth! I was really nervous about flying over the Atlantic, because I knew cruising over it can be very rough. Good news, flying over it is much smoother and probably the smoothest flight I have ever flown!!</span>
<h2><span style="color: #000000;">22. Safety
</span></h2>
<span style="color: #000000;">I’m thankful for safe and healthy travels. As a mom you always stress before a trip that everything goes okay; that nobody will get hurt or sick while away from home. Although I had some sleepless nights of anxiety before every adventure, I am happy to report all went well!
</span>
<h2><span style="color: #000000;">23. SPF
</span></h2>
<span style="color: #000000;"> I am thankful for SPF 50+ sunscreen, because I have some white babies and we traveled to (and live in) some hot places. I don’t want to damage their beautiful baby skin, so before every day at the beach I frosted them like cupcakes.</span>
<h2><span style="color: #000000;">24. Pretty Girls
</span></h2>
<span style="color: #000000;">Britney Spears and Iggy Azalea made the list too! CuddleBear was obsessed (and when I say obsessed, I mean obsessed) with their </span>Pretty Girls video<span style="color: #000000;">. It saved me so many times this year. It calmed him down from meltdowns, it entertained him during long transportation, and it soothed him to sleep! Thank you Britney and Iggy for being so pretty and talented to capture my son’s heart at 3 years old!</span>

<a href="/26-reasons-im-thankful-for-travel/"><img class="aligncenter wp-image-3581" title="Thankful for Travel" src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/11/IMG_5787-768x1024.jpg" alt="Thankful for Travel" width="598" height="798" /></a>
<h2><span style="color: #000000;">25. Meeting New People
</span></h2>
<span style="color: #000000;">When traveling you get to meet so many wonderful people and I am so grateful for all the</span> friends <span style="color: #000000;">I met on the road this year! We had some great times laughing by the pool and in the pubs!</span>
<h2><span style="color: #000000;">26. Travel Writing
</span></h2>
<span style="color: #000000;"> Lastly, I am thankful for taking my travel writing in my own hands and diving into my travel blog head first. I have made some wonderful friends in the travel community, have a creative outlet and platform to share my stories and advice with the world, and I have expanded my opportunities to continue a life of family travel!</span>

<span style="color: #000000;">Although I am always looking forward and planning for the next big adventure, Thanksgiving reminds me to take a step back, slow down and reflect on blessings in my life. Surprisingly, today we are not on the road, but I am excited to be home with my family, gorging on turkey, stuffing and pie. We are going to wear stretchy pants, eat until we pop, watch </span><strong>Trains Planes and Automobiles</strong>,<span style="color: #000000;"> and lock down some more travel plans. No matter where I am in the world, as long as I am with my family I am home. Happy Thanksgiving everybody!</span>
