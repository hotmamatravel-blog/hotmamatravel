<h2><em><span style="color: #000000;">History &amp; Character:</span></em></h2>
<span style="color: #000000;">What I love about Best Western Plus hotels is that they usually have a ton of character and take great lengths to represent the destination. For example, although the Sunset Suites Riverwalk is a contemporary boutique hotel it still contains elements of the past. Representative of the historic late 1800's building which it occupies, the hotel still has the original wood floors and brick walls. The historic lobby is sprinkled with photos from the past, allowing guests to learn about the history as they sip their morning coffee. Grams and I loved walking around looking at each photo and reading the caption below. <h2><em><span style="color: #000000;">Oh The Amenities!</span></em></h2>
<span style="color: #000000;">Whether you are traveling for business or pleasure, or a little bit of both like I was, Best Western Plus Sunset Suites has everything you need for a successful trip. In addition to the hotel business amenities, like meeting and banquet facilities and free access to wifi, photocopying, multi-line phones and dataports, there is also plenty to make your stay comfortable and healthy. Earning an <em>I Stay Green</em> leaf, the hotel is also 100% smoke free and has an exercise facility, perfect for <strong><span style="color: #ff0000;"><a href="/staying-fit-on-the-road/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><span style="color: #ff0000;">staying fit while traveling</span></a></span></strong>. <span style="color: #000000;">We were lucky enough to score one of their spacious suites with two cozy mission-style beds as well as a sleeper sofa, which was perfect for our family size. The rooms also have flat-screen TVs with cable satellite and in-room movies, microwaves, mini-friges, wet bars, coffee/tea makers, full size ironing boards and hair-dryers. <h3><span style="color: #000000;">Complimentary Breakfast</span></h3>
<span style="color: #000000;">I can’t tell you how much I love hotels that offer complimentary breakfast. I mean, it is so helpful when you are traveling with kids or have a jam packed day of activities. It seriously makes travel life so much easier to be able to grab something fast without having to leave your hotel. Being complimentary makes it even better, because it’s great for watching your food budget for extended trips. Kids can be so picky and I hate ordering the food sometimes, not sure what they really want or how much they’ll actually eat. This made it so easy and fun for the kids to choose their breakfast each morning and you know what? They more likely to eat it once they chose it! One of the highlights was the giant Texas-shaped BW waffle. How could you not love that?
