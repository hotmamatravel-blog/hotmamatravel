<p class="has-drop-cap">St. Patrick’s Day might be known for pub crawls and green beer, but in our family, it’s always been about celebrating together by the water. And over the years, one place has become our favorite spot for the occasion, <a href="/go/newport-dunes-rv-resort-newport-beach-ca/" linkid="13849"><strong>Newport Dunes Resort</strong></a> in beautiful Newport Beach.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>What started as a fun idea turned into a family tradition. Not only were we celebrating St. Patrick’s Day, but we also had two March birthdays in the family, which meant double the reason to gather, eat, laugh, and enjoy a beach weekend together. Before we knew it, celebrating St. Patrick’s Day at Newport Dunes became something we looked forward to every year.</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>One of the things we love most about <a href="https://www.newportdunes.com/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Newport Dunes</a> is that it captures the fun spirit of the holiday without the chaos. Instead of crowded bars and loud nightlife, you get a relaxed waterfront setting where families can celebrate together.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Kids can run around on the sand, ride bikes along the bay, and play on the playground while the adults relax nearby. Everyone gets to wear their green, enjoy the sunshine, and soak up the festive vibe without feeling like they wandered into an adults-only party.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The calm waters of the Back Bay make Newport Dunes especially perfect for families. Because it’s a protected lagoon, there are no crashing waves, which means little ones can splash and play safely while parents keep an eye on them from the sand.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Our St. Patrick’s Day celebrations usually involve:</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Beach games</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Kids playing in the sand</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Bike rides around the bay</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Relaxing by the water with family and friends</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>It’s simple, easy, fun, and the kind that makes you realize you don’t need elaborate plans to make great memories.</p>

<p>For us, St. Patrick’s Day at Newport Dunes has never been about big parties and it’s about time together.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We celebrate birthdays, share meals, watch the kids play, and enjoy those moments where everyone is laughing, barefoot in the sand, and not worried about schedules or distractions. Those are the memories that stick.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>And honestly, celebrating St. Patrick’s Day with ocean breezes, sunshine, and family beats crowded bars any day.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p>Over the years, these St. Patrick’s Day weekends at Newport Dunes have become some of our favorite family memories. Between the birthdays, beach days, and time spent together, it’s a tradition that means a lot to us.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>If you’re looking for a family-friendly way to celebrate St. Patrick’s Day, this little bayfront gem in Newport Beach might just become your new tradition, too.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Pack something green, bring the family, and see for yourself why Newport Dunes is such a special place to celebrate. </p>

Between celebrating two March birthdays, enjoying the calm bay waters, and spending the day together by the beach, it’s the kind of easy, joy-filled celebration that reminds us what family travel is really about; time together and memories that last long after the holiday is over.</p>
