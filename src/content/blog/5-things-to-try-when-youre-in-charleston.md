<p class="has-drop-cap">Filled with amazing restaurants, museums, boating experiences, and countless other attractions, Charleston, South Carolina, can be a great place to visit. Depending on how much time you’re spending there, you mightn’t have the time to take it all in, however.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>You’ll still want to enjoy yourself, so it’s worth focusing on a few notable <a href="/things-to-do-in-charleston-with-kids/">thi</a><a href="/interesting-activities-family-vacation/">ngs to do</a> when you’re in Charleston. Some of these could be more appealing than others. They’ll not only have you enjoying yourself, but you’ll end up creating some amazing memories.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>It’s worth looking at some of the more notable of these.</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph {"dropCap":true} -->
<p class="has-drop-cap">While some people say the <a href="https://www.thecharlestoncitymarket.com/">Charleston City Market</a> can be a bit of a tourist trap, it can still be worth seeing. There’ll be plenty of things to see and buy when you’re there. Having been around since the 1800s, it’s one of the oldest markets in the country. It can be worth visiting just for that alone.</p>

You shouldn’t have a problem enjoying yourself there, so it’s worth visiting.</p>

The <a href="https://www.thepinch.com/palmetto-carriage-works-tour">Classic Carriage Works</a> tour can be one of the better recommendations, especially if you want to experience the city’s classic and romantic past. You’ll be taken around on a horse-drawn carriage, making it much more romantic than you would’ve thought.</p>

While this varies depending on what time of the year you go, you’ll end up having an amazing time, no matter what.</p>

<p class="has-drop-cap">Charleston has quite a wide variety of cuisine, and there’ll be plenty of foods to choose from when you’re there. You’ll get tasters of practically everything, and you’ll end up having some amazing food while you’re at it.</p>
