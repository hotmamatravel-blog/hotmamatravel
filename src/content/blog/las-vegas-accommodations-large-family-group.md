<p class="has-drop-cap">Here is an amazing fact. There are over 150 thousand hotel rooms in Las Vegas, which is <a href="https://www.nytimes.com/2007/04/24/business/24vegas.html" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">almost twice as many</a> as in New York City! And <a href="https://www.lasvegasnevada.gov/News/Blog/Detail/strong-growth-for-tourism" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">tourism is not slowing down</a> anytime soon. However, the frustrating thing about Vegas (well, actually about hotels in general) is that <strong>a large majority of these rooms are designed to accommodate only up to four guests</strong>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>You can expect to pay an arm (and most likely a leg) for one of the few family-sized suites available at the main hotels on the Vegas strip – that is, <em>if they aren’t already booked</em>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>This is a huge problem for families like ours planning a trip to Las Vegas with a group of 5+ people. It is painful enough to pay the obligatory $40/night resort fee for a single room at most <a href="/las-vegas-strip-hotel-map/" data-type="post" data-id="9253" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener"><strong>Vegas Strip hotels</strong></a>, let alone for more than one room.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>My kids have always loved our time staying at <a href="/best-family-hotels-in-las-vegas/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener"><strong>family-friendly resorts on the Vegas strip</strong></a>, <em>but now we are a family of 8</em> and have been researching alternative family accommodations in Vegas with more space and kid-oriented amenities at an affordable price.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>In fact, I discovered that there are some really nice Las Vegas accommodations large family group options, including condo, timeshare rentals, that are just off the Vegas strip but close enough to the action that it would be just a quick ride to see a <a href="/las-vegas-shows-for-kids/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener"><strong>kid-friendly Vegas show</strong></a> or eat at a <strong><a href="/best-family-restaurants-in-las-vegas/" data-type="post" data-id="20281" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">family restaurant</a></strong> on the Vegas strip.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"dropCap":true} -->
<p class="has-drop-cap">There are actually quite a few benefits of staying somewhere off the Vegas strip if you desire a more low-key experience on your <a href="/las-vegas-with-kids/" data-type="post" data-id="38" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener"><strong>Vegas family vacation</strong></a>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>To be honest, we <em>do</em> like hanging out on the <strong><a href="/walking-las-vegas-strip/" data-type="post" data-id="16286" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Vegas strip with kids</a></strong>, with its abundance of <a href="/free-family-activities-las-vegas/" data-type="post" data-id="9291" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener"><strong>free family activities</strong></a>, but it does have a pretty wild and exuberant atmosphere that can be intense and may be judged by some parents to be undesirable or inappropriate for their family.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>But perhaps most important is that you can often find exceptional values by staying at a family vacation rental in Vegas that is just off the Vegas strip.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>For instance, you can score a much larger accommodation at a beautiful resort for the same price or less than a typical Vegas hotel, especially after factoring the money you might save on resort fees, parking fees, extra guest fees, etc.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>With that in mind, here are some <strong>great places to stay near the Vegas Strip for large families</strong> (or any group, really) with more than 5 or 6 people.</p>

<p class="has-drop-cap">There are several reputable websites, like <a linkid="20890" href="/go/vrbo-las-vegas/"><strong>VRBO</strong></a>, where you can search listings of homes, condos, and timeshares that are for rent in the Las Vegas area. In many cases, these accommodations can be found in beautiful family-friendly resorts with <strong><a href="/best-family-pools-in-vegas/" data-type="post" data-id="13741" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">extravagant pools</a></strong>, arcades, grocery stores, restaurants, and other conveniences for families. Some of these resorts don’t have gambling on site, which is nice for the kiddos, but you will still be just minutes away from all the action in numerous nearby casinos (e.g., if you can score a babysitter for a few hours!).</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>When you’re planning a Vegas trip with a big crew, <strong><a linkid="22611" href="/go/whimstay-vacation-rentals/">Whimstay is a total game-change</a>r</strong>. It gives you access to the <em>same vacation rentals you see on Airbnb and Vrbo</em>, but often at <strong>way better prices</strong>, which matters when you’re booking something spacious enough for the whole group. Think multiple bedrooms, room to spread out, and no awkward hotel-room Tetris. It’s especially clutch for last-minute Vegas trips, with instant booking and serious savings, so you can spend less on where you sleep and more on shows, food, and making memories together.</p>

<div class="wp-block-uagb-advanced-heading uagb-block-42a30e40"><h3 class="uagb-heading-text"><strong>Rent a Timeshare</strong></h3></div>
<!-- /wp:uagb/advanced-heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"dropCap":true} -->
<p class="has-drop-cap">If you’ve ever been to Las Vegas before, you have probably been asked to go on a timeshare tour by either your resort or one of those kiosks on the strip. That’s because Las Vegas is home to one of the <a href="https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/vacation-ownership-timeshare-market-size-growing-6amnc/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">largest collections of timeshare resorts in the world</a>, and they are some of the most luxurious as well.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Most individuals are not aware, however, that you don’t need to purchase a timeshare to stay in one. Over the years, <a href="http://www.sellmytimesharenow.com/timeshare-rentals/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">timeshare rentals</a> from private owners have grown in popularity for families wanting more space and amenities at an affordable price.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>For example, located within high-end Las Vegas resorts like <a href="https://www.hilton.com/en/hotels/lasvsgv-hilton-grand-vacations-club-on-the-las-vegas-strip/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Hilton Grand Vacations Club</a> at the Flamingo, <a href="https://www.westgateresorts.com/timeshare/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Westgate Timeshares</a>, and <a href="https://www.marriott.com/en-us/hotels/lasvg-marriotts-grand-chateau/overview/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Marriott’s Grand Chateau</a>, timeshares are perfect for families because of the added space and comforts such as 1-6 bedrooms, multiple bathrooms, separate living and dining areas, and more. Here is a screen grab of a quick search I did for Las Vegas accommodations on SellMyTimeshareNow so you can get a flavor of the sheer number of options there are:</p>

<div class="wp-block-uagb-advanced-heading uagb-block-33d5687b"><h3 class="uagb-heading-text"><strong>Buy a Timeshare</strong></h3></div>
<!-- /wp:uagb/advanced-heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"dropCap":true} -->
<p class="has-drop-cap">As mentioned above, timeshares are awesome accommodations you can rent for a one-time, inexpensive vacation. If you find yourselves returning year after year to the City of Lights, however, I recommend looking into a timeshare there.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Though they can be expensive if purchased directly through the resorts themselves, <a href="http://www.sellmytimesharenow.com/destinations/las-vegas-timeshare/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Las Vegas timeshares</a> can be found for much more affordable prices on third-party sites like SellMyTimeshareNow, a trusted timeshare reseller with hundreds of units for sale at 60-70% off.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Since timeshare resorts are super family-friendly, most do not have on-site gambling while also offering children’s pools, arcades, grocery stores, and planned activities. You can think of it as a wonderful and safe home-away-from home. We do not personally own a timeshare, but we visit Vegas so often with kids that I can really see the appeal.</p>
