<div class="wp-block-uagb-advanced-heading uagb-block-42a30e40"><h3 class="uagb-heading-text"><a href="/go/eiffel-tower-paris-las-vegas/" linkid="15580">Go Up The Eiffel Tower At The Paris Hotel</a></h3><p class="uagb-desc-text">3655 S Las Vegas Blvd, Las Vegas, NV 89109</p></div>
<!-- /wp:uagb/advanced-heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"dropCap":true} -->
<p class="has-drop-cap">You don’t need to fly to France to have a fun<strong> <a linkid="15580" href="/go/eiffel-tower-paris-las-vegas/">Eiffel Tower Experience</a></strong>. Towering 46 stories over the <a href="/walking-las-vegas-strip/" data-type="post" data-id="16286" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener"><strong>Las Vegas strip</strong></a>, the Eiffel Tower is a remarkable half-scale replica of the famous Paris landmark, located at the <a href="https://www.caesars.com/paris-las-vegas" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Paris Hotel</a>. Watching the strip get smaller and smaller as you float the 460 feet to the top in a glass elevator, it is hard to imagine that the real Eiffel Tower is double the height!</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Once you reach the observation deck, you are met with gorgeous 360 degree views of the Vegas strip. My kids are kind of obsessed with the Eiffel Tower and have to go up every time we visit <a href="/las-vegas-with-kids/" data-type="post" data-id="38" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener"><strong>Las Vegas with kids</strong></a>. Surprisingly, this can be a very affordable attraction for the family.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Ticket prices vary by time of day and package. The day tickets (10:00am- 7:00pm) are less expensive that the night tickets (7:00pm-1:00am) and buying a family package or group package also reduces the price of individual tickets. <strong>You know what else is great? Kids under 6 are free</strong>. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Be on the lookout for deals too. We were able to score 2 for 1 tickets and at the time both our kids were 6 and under, so all four of us got to go to the top for $16!</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>If you have more money to spend, you should splurge on a fancy family meal at the <a href="https://www.caesars.com/paris-las-vegas/restaurants/eiffel-tower-restaurant" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Eiffel Tower Restaurant</a> on the 11th floor. However, Paris is home to some amazing <a href="/celebrity-chef-restaurants-las-vegas/" data-type="post" data-id="16836" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener"><strong>celebrity-owned and celebrity chef restaurants</strong></a> you'll want to check out, too.</p>

<div class="wp-block-uagb-advanced-heading uagb-block-3d7d524e"><h3 class="uagb-heading-text"><a href="/go/shark-reef-mandalay-bay-las-vegas/" linkid="15584">See Sharks Swim at Mandalay's Shark Reef</a></h3><p class="uagb-desc-text">3950 S Las Vegas Blvd, Las Vegas, NV 89119</p></div>
<!-- /wp:uagb/advanced-heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"dropCap":true} -->
<p class="has-drop-cap">For more underwater fun, check out <a linkid="8725" href="/go/shark-reef-las-vegas-bov/"><strong>Mandalay’s Shark Reef Aquarium</strong></a> at <a href="https://mandalaybay.mgmresorts.com/en.html" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Mandalay Bay Hotel</a>, which features not only sharks, but over 2,000 other creatures of the deep. It is a proper aquarium with giant stingrays, sea turtles and more. Many exhibits are interactive, allowing the kids to get a hands-on learning experience.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Tickets start at $29, kids are $24, and kids 3 and under are free. There are upgrade packages available, for example, if you would like a more up-close and personal experience you can literally dive with sharks. Yikes!</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>However, the basic ticket price gets you admission to all the exhibits, including the awesome Shipwreck exhibit, allowing guests to walk through an underwater tunnel as sharks, sea turtles and stingrays swim overhead.</p>

<div class="wp-block-uagb-advanced-heading uagb-block-bc4dc479"><h3 class="uagb-heading-text"><a href="/go/big-apple-coaster-nyny-las-vegas/" linkid="13357">Ride A Thrilling Roller Coaster at New York New York</a></h3><p class="uagb-desc-text">3790 S Las Vegas Blvd, Las Vegas, NV 89109</p></div>
<!-- /wp:uagb/advanced-heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"dropCap":true} -->
<p class="has-drop-cap">Now this is a thrill for the older kids and for daring parents looking to rekindle the child inside. Located on the second floor arcade in the <a href="https://newyorknewyork.mgmresorts.com/en.html" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">New York New York Las Vegas hotel</a> is the popular Big Apple Coaster. I rode this as a teenager, and now my teens are obsessed! They rode this over and over again on our last couple of trips to Las Vegas and love it. If you are going to <a href="/things-to-do-in-las-vegas-with-teens/" data-type="post" data-id="13348" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener"><strong>Vegas with teens</strong></a>, it is an attraction you don't want to miss.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Soaring high above the Las Vegas strip, the coaster wraps around the hotel, twisting, turning and flipping riders in one heart pumping journey. You have to be at least 54 in. to ride and a single ticket costs $25. However, you can re-ride for a fraction of the cost. Look out for family specials and group deals too. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Plus, <a href="/best-arcades-in-las-vegas/" data-type="post" data-id="21049"><strong>Vegas arcades</strong></a> are a great way to kill hours without breaking the bank. Best part, almost every hotel has a cool arcade that your kids and teens will love!</p>

<div class="wp-block-uagb-advanced-heading uagb-block-779274af"><h3 class="uagb-heading-text"><a href="/go/paris-las-vegas/" linkid="20299">Eat Delicious Crepes On The Cobblestone Streets of Paris</a></h3><p class="uagb-desc-text">3655 S Las Vegas Blvd, Las Vegas, NV 89109</p></div>
<!-- /wp:uagb/advanced-heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"dropCap":true} -->
<p class="has-drop-cap">I have had crepes many places, but I can honestly say that the delectable crepes at the Paris hotel in Las Vegas are my all time favorite. First of all, if you squint you may feel like you are really in France as you stroll along the cobblestone streets of the shopping promenade under the morning sky façade.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>La Creperie is a walk-up sidewalk café where both sweet and savory crepes are made to order. Pick from a variety of flavors and fillings and watch as your treat gets created before your eyes. These crepes are huge and perfect for sharing. They are eye-rolling, mouthwatering delicious.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"color":{"text":"#ffffff"}},"backgroundColor":"vivid-red","fontSize":"regular"} -->
<p class="has-vivid-red-background-color has-text-color has-background has-regular-font-size" style="color:#ffffff"><strong>Hot Tip:</strong><em> They also serve all-you-can-eat crepes in the Paris Buffet, which is one of the reasons it is my favorite buffet in all of Vegas!</em></p>

<div class="wp-block-uagb-advanced-heading uagb-block-9c28624f"><h3 class="uagb-heading-text"><a href="/go/the-luxor-hotel-las-vegas/" linkid="18437">Titanic And Bodies Exhibitions At Luxor</a></h3><p class="uagb-desc-text">3900 S Las Vegas Blvd, Las Vegas, NV 89119</p></div>
<!-- /wp:uagb/advanced-heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"dropCap":true} -->
<p class="has-drop-cap">As if walking into a giant illuminated pyramid isn’t cool enough, <a href="https://luxor.mgmresorts.com/en.html" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Luxor </a>has some of the coolest exhibits in Las Vegas. The <a linkid="8729" href="/go/titanic-exhibition-las-vegas-bov/"><strong>Titanic exhibition</strong></a> contains over 250 artifacts from the ship as well as full-scale replicas of the staterooms, the Grand Staircase, and even the Promenade Deck that blasts freezing air on visitors. One of the most popular exhibitions, the Titanic display has had over 25 million visitors, and I recommend that if you are a history buff like me, to join them. <strong><a href="/go/titanic-exhibition-las-vegas-bov/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Learn more about the Titanic exhibit at Vegas.com</a></strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>For something really crazy, check out the <a linkid="8730" href="/go/bodies-exhibition-las-vegas-bov/"><strong>Bodies exhibition</strong></a>. With 13 full bodies and 260 organs and partial bodies on display, this is a rare chance for the general public to see in gruesome and beautiful detail the inner workings of the human body. Although this may seem a bit macabre, it can be a great educational experience for the whole family as it shows the real-life effects that unhealthy lifestyles, like smoking, overeating, and no exercise, have on our bodies. Visiting the Bodies exhibit could be a positive life life-changing experience for everyone. <strong><a href="/go/bodies-exhibition-las-vegas-bov/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Learn more about the Bodies exhibit at Vegas.com</a></strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Prices for each exhibit are around $30-$35, while kids 3 and under are free. However, if you want to visit both, I suggest getting the combo ticket (if offered during your visit).</p>

<div class="wp-block-uagb-advanced-heading uagb-block-f54505d2"><h3 class="uagb-heading-text"><a href="/go/the-venetian-resort-las-vegas/" linkid="18438">Take A Scenic Gondola Ride At The Venetian </a></h3><p class="uagb-desc-text">3355 S Las Vegas Blvd, Las Vegas, NV 89109</p></div>
<!-- /wp:uagb/advanced-heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"dropCap":true} -->
<p class="has-drop-cap">Experience the enchantment of Venice in Las Vegas with a gondola ride at the <a href="https://www.venetianlasvegas.com/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Venetian</a>. Choose between the outdoor or indoor ride and relax as you glide along the hotel canals in an authentic Venetian gondola while a gondolier serenades throughout the journey. The indoor ride, which is great any time of day, takes guests on a peaceful journey through the hotel’s Grand Canal, passing by inside Venetian streetscape, cafes and shops.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The outdoor ride is best at night, as it is slightly cooler and provides exciting views of Las Vegas aglow. Both rides are lovely and will make you feel like you are really floating through the ancient Venice canals (minus the reportedly stinky sewer smell). Gondola rides are around $39 a person for a four seat gondola, or $40 for a photo package. However, if you want a more private two seat gondola, you’ll have to pay double the price.</p>
