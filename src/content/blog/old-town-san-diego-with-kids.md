<p class="has-drop-cap">Old Town San Diego is the birthplace of California and 200 hundred years later continues to be the hubbub of excitement. The history, the culture, the proximity to sea and the tequila, make Old Town one of my favorite places to visit in San Diego.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Although there are said to be plenty of ghosts, this is no ghost town. There is so much to do with all the shops, restaurants, bars and historic sites. The constant hustle and bustle of activity keeps it full of life every day. With so much to see, do and enjoy, it is no wonder why I love visiting Old Town San Diego with kids on the regular.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
