<span style="color: #000000;">The joys of flying with kids. I will admit, being trapped in a metal tube thousands of feet in the air with two toddlers does not sound like a barrel of fun, but it is the burden I bare in order to travel with my babies. The truth of the matter is that I hate flying. I can feel the panic tightening in my chest as I write this post. Unfortunately, I have to do it if I want to travel, which I do. So, up up and away I go.</span>

<span style="color: #000000;">Now, as if air travel wasn’t stressful enough, I’ve added a couple of high-energy munchkins to my journey. My usual pre-flight routine involves a couple very <strong>big martinis</strong>, but now that I’m a mommy I have a little more to worry about than my own nerves. Gulp. </span><span style="color: #000000;">To plan flying with kids takes a lot of prep work, but it is all worth it to make the flight as pleasant as possible. Here are some of my tips to help your plane trips fly high!</span>

<a href="/flying-with-kids/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><img class="aligncenter wp-image-3703" title="flying with kids" src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/05/DSCN93691-1024x683.jpg" alt="Flying with kids" width="640" height="427" /></a>
<h2><span style="color: #000000;"><em><strong>1. Pep talk</strong></em></span></h2>
<span style="color: #000000;">If your child is old enough to understand, talk to them about your upcoming sky adventure. That's right, before flying with kids, talk, talk, talk. I found that with my two-year-old daughter, building the excitement and explaining my expectations of her behavior months leading up the trip really resonated with her.  She would talk about how excited she was for ride in the plane and then say, “I won’t cry, I won’t scream, I won’t run away from you on the plane,” long before the trip occurred. The same went for my son. Although, he add "no farting on the plane." I didn't tell him that, but I'll take it...it is a good rule.</span>

<a href="/flying-with-kids/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><img class="wp-image-152" title="daughter on airplane" src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/05/DSCN9375-768x1024.jpg" alt="daughter on airplane" width="640" height="853" /></a> <span style="color: #000000;">Look at this lil sweetie</span>
<h2><span style="color: #000000;"><em><strong>2. Plan your flight during kids’ nap-time or bedtime</strong></em></span></h2>
<span style="color: #000000;">Normally as parents we try to avoid activities when our kids are tired, because they tend to be cranky. But, flying with kids is a little different. Tired kids is a good thing. That way they will hopefully sleep the majority of the flight (or you could just give them Benadyrl). <strong>I’ve never tried it</strong> because my kids are great little sleepers and I don’t want to mess up their schedule if possible. Also, <strong>I don't want to unnecessarily drug my kids</strong>.</span>

<span style="color: #000000;"><strong>Beware! </strong>If you are planning on taking the Benadyrl route, keep in mind there are a small percentage of children that experience a reverse effect. Instead of a sleepy child, you could end up with a hyper one. My nephew is one of them, which we discovered due to an allergic reaction. Good thing she never tired it on a plane. So speak with a pediatrician and give it a test run before your trip.</span>

<a href="/flying-with-kids/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><img class="wp-image-3702" title="kids sleeping on airplane" src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/05/IMG_5360-768x1024.jpg" alt="kids sleeping on airplane" width="640" height="853" /></a> <span style="color: #000000;">This is what you want people!</span>
<h2><span style="color: #000000;">3. <em><strong>Layovers are Awesome!
</strong></em></span></h2>
<span style="color: #000000;">Some people like non-stop flights so they can just get it over with, but I don’t mind a stopover when traveling with kids. <strong>This is a great opportunity to give the kids a break, get mommy a drink, eat a proper meal, change diapers and burn off some energy.</strong> You’ll want your layover to be more than an hour, otherwise you’ll be rushing to get to the next gate and create a lot of chaos for yourself. Be careful, a layover that’s too long can drag the trips out and make for some fussy babies. When flying with kids, I like layovers to be between 3 and 4 hours. I have found that 2 hours is not enough time to relax by the time you get your crazy crew off the plane and more than 4 hours can make the trip way too drawn out. I mean, we do want to get there!</span>

Run!</span>
<h2><span style="color: #000000;"><strong>4. Compress your carry-on items.</strong></span></h2>
<span style="color: #000000;">When maneuvering through a busy airport with children the last thing you want to do is be loaded down with stuff. I opted to take umbrella strollers on our trip, because we could wheel our kids safely through the airport and easily check them at the gate. Fold and go baby!</span>

<a href="/flying-with-kids/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><img class="wp-image-3705" title="Flying with kids with umbrella strollers" src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/05/DSCN9373-1024x684.jpg" alt="Flying with kids with umbrella strollers" width="640" height="428" /></a> <span style="color: #000000;">Umbrellas are great and easy for travel</span>

<span style="color: #000000;">I used to love accessorizing my flight outfits with a cute purse! Now that I'm all about family travel, I'm also all about backpacks!! Slap your goods on your back and you are on way; <strong>HANDS FREE!</strong></span>

<a href="/flying-with-kids/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><img class="wp-image-3706" title="daughter boarding plane" src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/05/IMG_5322.jpg" alt="daughter boarding plane" width="640" height="853" /></a> <span style="color: #000000;">Backpacks ROCK</span>

<a href="/flying-with-kids/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><img class="wp-image-3707" title="son boarding plane" src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/05/IMG_5323-768x1024.jpg" alt="son boarding plane" width="640" height="853" /></a> <span style="color: #000000;">Unless, you son wants to drag his through the airport</span>
<h2><span style="color: #000000;"><em><strong>5. Bring surprises</strong></em>!</span></h2>
<span style="color: #000000;">Before a trip I like to<strong> stock-up on toys and gadgets</strong> that I slowly reveal to my kids during the flight. That way it holds their attention and keeps them excited to play with new toys. I pack a new set of goodies for the returning flight to keep the excitement and interest level high. The Lazy Travelers have a great post about <span style="color: #ff0000;"><a style="color: #ff0000;" href="http://thelazytravelers.com/laws-to-travel-by-35/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><strong>planning for down time</strong></a></span> that is worth checking out. You can also read more about my favorite travel toys, <span style="color: #ff0000;"><a style="color: #ff0000;" href="/best-gadgets-for-traveling-with-kids/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><strong>HERE.</strong></a></span></span>

<a href="/flying-with-kids/"><img class="wp-image-3708" title="kids activities on airplane flying" src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/05/IMG_5348-768x1024.jpg" alt="kids activities on airplane flying" width="640" height="853" /></a> <span style="color: #000000;">Keeping them entertained</span>
<h2><em><span style="color: #000000;"><strong>6. Plenty of snacks is a must</strong></span></em></h2>
<span style="color: #000000;">My kids like to eat and like to snack, so I have to come stocked wherever I go. When flying with kids, keep their tummies full and happy. Also, snacks help with ear popping. I gave my kids lollipops or drinks to suck during take-offs and landings and had no problems.</span>

<a href="/flying-with-kids/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><img class="wp-image-3709" title="Flying with kids snacking on food" src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/05/IMG_6460-1024x768.jpg" alt="Flying with kids snacking on food" width="640" height="480" /></a> <span style="color: #000000;">Snacks &amp; a movie</span>
<h2><em><span style="color: #000000;"><strong>7. Bring comfort items</strong></span></em></h2>
<span style="color: #000000;">Make sure to have a blanket or little neck pillow for your little ones, so that if they want to sleep they’ll stay asleep. Flying with kids is a tricky balance of making them comfortable and keeping them entertained. Blankets and pillows help make the cozy and calm. It may sound annoying to carry more things, but just fold the blankets on top of your stroller when it is time to move and you are good to roll!</span>

<a href="/flying-with-kids/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><img class="wp-image-3710" title="kids behaving resting on airplane with pillows" src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/05/DSCN5750-768x1024.jpg" alt="kids behaving resting on airplane with pillows" width="640" height="853" /></a> <span style="color: #000000;">Comfy Cozy</span>
<h2><span style="color: #000000;"><em><strong>8. Walk it out!</strong></em></span></h2>
<span style="color: #000000;">Don’t be afraid to walk the aisles of the plane. Kids are kids and they have energy to burn. A long flight might be too much for your little one to sit still, so keep them happy by giving them room to move. It might seem annoying and tight, but walking is better than screaming. I don't like to do this either, that's why I'm a fan of layovers. When flying with kids, you got to do what you to got to do!</span>

<h2><span style="color: #000000;"><em><strong>That's a Wrap!</strong></em></span></h2>
<span style="color: #000000;">Obviously, these are just my tips that work with my kids. All kids are different and require different needs, but hopefully most of these suggestions will alleviate some of the stress of flying with kids. Have a safe flight and good luck!</span>
