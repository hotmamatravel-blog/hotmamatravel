<p class="has-drop-cap">Las Vegas is the capital of non-stop entertainment. Everywhere you look, there is something to see and do along <a href="/walking-las-vegas-strip/" data-type="post" data-id="16286" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener"><strong>the Las Vegas Strip</strong></a>. Even mealtime is an event in Las Vegas, with many restaurants offering live music.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Finding restaurants with live entertainment is a great way to enjoy Las Vegas night life with kids, making it one of our favorite things to do when taking a <a href="/las-vegas-with-kids/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener"><strong>family trip to Las Vegas</strong></a>. Why have a boring meal when you can have dinner and a show all in one?</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>So there are plenty of options, but to get you started here are our<strong> </strong>favorite kid-friendly live music Las Vegas restaurants.</p>

<div class="wp-block-uagb-advanced-heading uagb-block-42a30e40"><h3 class="uagb-heading-text"><a href="/go/nine-fine-irishmen-nyny-las-vegas/" linkid="15534">Nine Fine Irishmen</a></h3><p class="uagb-desc-text"><strong>3790 S Las Vegas Blvd, Las Vegas, NV 89109</strong> </p></div>
<!-- /wp:uagb/advanced-heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"dropCap":true} -->
<p class="has-drop-cap">Nine Fine Irishmen is an awesome two-story Irish pub with an outdoor patio located inside the <a href="https://newyorknewyork.mgmresorts.com/en.html" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">New York New York Hotel and Casino</a>. The interior is cozy, dark, and gives a very authentic old pub vibe. In fact, the pub was actually built in Ireland and then shipped to Vegas, so it is as authentic as you can get without physically being in Ireland. Named after the nine nationalists noted for their fight in Irish Independence, you will instantly feel the Irish pride as soon as you step through the doors.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>What makes this place even better is that there is nightly entertainment, featuring their Irish house band, <a href="https://www.sinerira.com/about-us" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Sin e Ri Ra</a>. They perform fantastic traditional Irish tunes and even have a live step-dancer, Tara Reid to accompany the music. Since <a href="https://irishpubcompany.com/project/nine-fine-irishmen/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Nine Fine Irishmen</a> is a restaurant until 11:00pm and the band starts around 9:00pm or 10:00pm, you can squeeze an hour or two of live music in with the kids.</p>

<div class="wp-block-uagb-advanced-heading uagb-block-02f9bd88"><h3 class="uagb-heading-text"><a href="/go/ri-ra-pub-mandalay-las-vegas/" linkid="15527">Ri Ra Irish Pub</a></h3><p class="uagb-desc-text"><strong>3930, S Las Vegas Blvd, Las Vegas, NV 89119</strong> </p></div>
<!-- /wp:uagb/advanced-heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"dropCap":true} -->
<p class="has-drop-cap">This super fun Irish pub is located inside the Shoppes at Mandalay between the <a href="https://luxor.mgmresorts.com/en.html" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Luxor</a> and <a href="https://mandalaybay.mgmresorts.com/en.html" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Mandalay Bay</a>. This large single floor pub has multiple little cozy rooms to explore. The Parlour Room looks like a 1970’s living room/library with low seating arm chairs, bookcases and a fireplace. The interior of the Theatre Room was actually salvaged from the Olympia Theatre in Dublin and is a dramatic space with dark mahogony wood and red velvet walls. The Bordello Room is an intimate pub setting while the Shop Bar and Victorian Bar, which make up the main pub area were created using salvaged materials from all over Ireland.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>This pub has fast become one of our favorites! Not only does <a href="https://rira.com/las-vegas/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Ri Ra</a> have live music every night featuring their house band The <a href="https://dustreeproductions.com/las-vegas/solo-duo-trio/the-black-donnellys/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Black Donnelly’s</a> or John Windsor starting at 8:45 pm, but the food is actually AMAZING! It one of our favorite <a href="/best-family-restaurants-in-las-vegas/" data-type="post" data-id="20281" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener"><strong>family restaurants in Las Vegas</strong></a> to dine as well as enjoy live music. This is a rare thing to say about most pubs. Everything from the complimentary soda bread to the Scottish Eggs was beyond delicious.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>It is an easy pub to navigate with a family, including strollers and wheelchairs with the live music easily viewable from any table in the main pub. Kids don’t have to be out until the kitchen stops serving food, so you are good for a couple of hours of Traditional Irish music fun!</p>

<div class="wp-block-uagb-advanced-heading uagb-block-b5fa0181"><h3 class="uagb-heading-text"><a href="/go/casa-di-amore-family-restaurant-las-vegas/" linkid="15529">Casa Di Amore</a></h3><p class="uagb-desc-text"> <strong>2850 E Tropicana Ave, Las Vegas, NV 89121</strong> </p></div>
<!-- /wp:uagb/advanced-heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"dropCap":true} -->
<p class="has-drop-cap">Casa Di Amore cozy old-school Italian restaurant that instantly transports you back in time to the <a href="/las-vegas-dinner-and-show-packages/" target="_blank" data-type="post" data-id="16861" rel="noreferrer noopener">Rat Pack days of Vegas</a>. The atmosphere is warm, cozy and even romantic, but absolutely family-friendly. The wine flows freely, the portions of delicious Italian cuisine are generous and seating allows for large parties.&nbsp;<br /></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The best part? There live music every night during dinner, which means the kids are welcomed to enjoy the whole evening. <a href="https://casadiamore.com/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Casa Di Amore</a> is actually a local’s favorite and not attached to a hotel or on the main strip. Luckily for visitors, they offer a complimentary shuttle van to pick you up from your hotel and transport you to the restaurant and back again, you just need to tip your driver. Casa Di Amore makes for a very pleasant and convenient Vegas night out with the family.</p>

<div class="wp-block-uagb-advanced-heading uagb-block-d10d57e2"><h3 class="uagb-heading-text"><a href="/go/house-of-blues-mandalay-bay-las-vegas/" linkid="15531">House of Blues </a></h3><p class="uagb-desc-text"><strong>3950 S Las Vegas Blvd, Las Vegas, NV 89119</strong></p></div>
<!-- /wp:uagb/advanced-heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"dropCap":true} -->
<p class="has-drop-cap">House of Blues in Las Vegas is located inside the Mandalay Bay, one of the <a href="/best-family-hotels-in-las-vegas/" data-type="post" data-id="8418" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener"><strong>favorite family Vegas hotels</strong></a>. Not only is the House of Blues a concert venue, but it is also a restaurant.  The goal of the owner was to showcase music like  Blues, Rhythm and Blues, Gospel, Jazz, and Roots-based Rock &amp; Roll to the world, which makes this the place to be if you love music! Coupled with a Southern-inspired menu, you can feel free to bring the kids for a deliciously hearty good time. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>There is often live music during dinner service, starting between 6:00pm and 8:00pm, perfect for families wanting to catch a live show with the kids. Plus on Sundays, <a href="https://www.houseofblues.com/lasvegas" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">House of Blues</a> has a really wild and lively Gospel Brunch that the whole family will enjoy. Tickets for the Gospel Brunch do have to be purchased in advance. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>For even more musical entertainment, be sure to check out Michael Jackson ONE. It is one of our top picks for <a href="/las-vegas-shows-for-kids/" data-type="post" data-id="8596" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener"><strong>shows to see in Las Vegas for families</strong></a>, especially <a href="/best-las-vegas-shows-for-teens/" data-type="post" data-id="20400" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener"><strong>families with teens</strong></a>.</p>

<div class="wp-block-uagb-advanced-heading uagb-block-350ff179"><h3 class="uagb-heading-text"><a href="/go/gillys-saloon-bbq-restaurant-las-vegas/" linkid="15533">Gilley's Saloon</a></h3><p class="uagb-desc-text"><strong>3300 S Las Vegas Blvd, Las Vegas, NV 89109</strong></p></div>
<!-- /wp:uagb/advanced-heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"dropCap":true} -->
<p class="has-drop-cap">Gilley’s Saloon is located in the <a href="https://treasureisland.com/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Treasure Island Hotel</a> and is a great place to if you want to slap your boots on and get a little bit country or a little bit rock n’ roll. Most nights Gilley’s Saloon and Dance Hall features a live Country or Rock band. Plus, the Dance Hall is open everyday from 6:00pm t0 8:00pm with free line dancing lessons starting at 7:00pm, which is good for families with kids that want to get their honky tonk on.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Get the kids in before 9:00, so you can grab a table, indulge in some southern BBQ and enjoy the show. Kids don’t have to be out by 9:00pm, but once the kitchen closes kids will not be allowed into the saloon. </p>
