<div class="wp-block-uagb-advanced-heading uagb-block-42a30e40"><h3 class="uagb-heading-text"><a href="/sonesta-redondo-beach-and-marina/" linkid="19433">Why Families Love Sonesta Redondo Beach</a></h3></div>
<!-- /wp:uagb/advanced-heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"dropCap":true} -->
<p class="has-drop-cap">Families, brace yourselves for an unforgettable stay! Sonesta Redondo Beach a family-friendly beachside escape your family will love. From a convenient location to onsite amenities this hotel offers an incredible experience for your next Los Angeles vacation.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Let's start with the location. Sonesta Redondo Beach &amp; Marina is conveniently located beachfront, along the marina and walking distance from the pier. This gives your family easy access to exciting restaurants, shops and attractions. There is even a fun paved bike trail along the coast that makes for a relaxing family ride.  </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The spacious accommodations provide the perfect home away from home, ensuring everyone has their own slice of paradise. The oceanfront views from the rooms and suites are nothing short of spectacular, creating a serene backdrop for quality family time.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The rooftop pool is a haven for both kids and adults, offering a refreshing break after a day of exploring. Watch the sunset while you splash into the evening. As the party lights come on and sunsets over the horizon, you will be so glad you picked this enchanting stay for family Los Angeles vacation. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Not only are you walking distance to tons of great seaside restaurants, but the Sonesta Redondo Beach and Marina offers two on-site dining options. The Harbor bar, located in the lobby is great for grabbing a yummy meal and drink before heading back to your room. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>At SOL you can indulge in a morning feast with an array of a-la-carte and buffet selections for breakfast, including an omelet station, complemented by a Mimosa and Bloody Mary bar. For dinner, savor a unique take on Asian/American Fusion, adding a fresh twist to our evening menu.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Beach, pool, food, and fun. What more could you ask for in a family L.A. vacation?</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph {"dropCap":true} -->
<p class="has-drop-cap">Convenience meets luxury at Sonesta Redondo Beach, making it an ideal choice for travelers flying into LAX. Located just a short 6 mile drive from the airport, the hotel provides a seamless transition from the hustle and bustle of travel to the tranquility of the Pacific coastline. After a day of flying, unwind in style with impeccable service and breathtaking ocean views. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>For those seeking a hassle-free stay, the hotel offers convenient amenities such as airport transportation, making your journey from LAX to Redondo Beach a breeze. Whether you're a solo traveler or a group, Sonesta Redondo Beach ensures your arrival is met with comfort and relaxation.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>It is the preferred hotel for airline professionals. If you are like me, you will enjoy watching the flight attendants and pilots coming and going throughout the day and night. </p>

<div class="wp-block-uagb-advanced-heading uagb-block-10954271"><h3 class="uagb-heading-text"><a href="/sonesta-redondo-beach-and-marina/" linkid="19433">Sonesta Redondo Beach &amp; Marina is Pet-Friendly </a></h3></div>
<!-- /wp:uagb/advanced-heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"dropCap":true} -->
<p class="has-drop-cap">At Sonesta, they share your love for your four-legged friends. Delight in the joy of having your furry companion with you as they offer special amenities such as plush pet beds, bowls, waterproof mats, and delightful treats upon check-in. Your pet will receive a warm welcome with a personalized arrival letter, guiding them towards a fantastic stay in Redondo Beach.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>To enhance your pet's experience, Sonesta Redondo Beach &amp; Marina offer an array of innovative, pet-safe products from Fable, both within the rooms and available for purchase. At Sonesta Redondo Beach &amp; Marina, being pet-friendly means welcoming well-mannered pets without any breed or weight restrictions—up to two pets are warmly permitted per room or suite.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>A nominal fee of $150 per stay applies to ensure your furry friend enjoys a "pawsome" stay.</p>

<div class="wp-block-uagb-advanced-heading uagb-block-b650e788"><h3 class="uagb-heading-text"><a href="/sonesta-redondo-beach-and-marina/" linkid="19433">Amenities that Seal the Deal</a></h3></div>
<!-- /wp:uagb/advanced-heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"dropCap":true} -->
<p class="has-drop-cap">Sonesta Redondo Beach and Marina offers an array of amenities that make this hotel an ideal choice for travelers. Not only are you walking distance from the excitement of Redondo Beach pier, you have all the comforts you need at your fingertips at the hotel</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Fitness enthusiasts can stay active at the on-site fitness center with separate fully-equipped strength training and cardio rooms. If getting outside is more your jam, the hotel offers both electric and traditional bike rentals. The hotel is located directly in front of the the 22-mile fully paved Marvin Braude bike trail along the Los Angeles coast. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Let's not forget about the rooftop pool. Kids and adults alike will love taking in the coastal views while relaxing poolside. This area makes for a great spot to take in the sunset too. Located next to the pool are tennis courts, perfect for working up a sweat before diving into a refreshing pool. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Although you can walk to many local seaside restaurants, Sonesta Redondo Beach has two onsite restaurants, Harbor Bar and SOL. Both offer exceptional cuisine, cocktails, and Lucky 7 Happy Hour. Enjoy fun tapas and drinks all for only $7 each. That's a steal in L.A. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Enjoy the cool coastal breeze in the evenings fireside under the veranda. Adorned with lush curtains and rugs, you'll feel as those the interior has extend outdoors as you take in the views. The outdoor firepits are the perfect place to enjoy an after dinner drink with your friends and family. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>If you are planning a wedding, a conference, holiday party, or some other really cool event. Sonesta Redondo Beach and Marina has 25,000 square feet of event space available. This includes patios and a rooftop terrace. </p>
