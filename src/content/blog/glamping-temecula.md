#### [Bailey's on Palomar Lodging, Retreat, &amp; Event Center](/go/bailys-on-palomar-glamping-temecula-ca/)

*33691 Bailey Meadow Rd, Palomar Mountain, CA 92060*

Family Glamping&nbsp; -&nbsp; Glamping Yurts&nbsp; -&nbsp; Glamping Cabins&nbsp; -&nbsp; Safari Tents&nbsp; -&nbsp; Rooms&nbsp; -&nbsp; Private Bathrooms&nbsp; -&nbsp; Hiking&nbsp; -&nbsp; Fishing

This mountain retreat was homesteaded in 1888, making for a truly unique and historic glamping experience. Here, you have the option of glamping in luxury yurts, historic cabins, safari tents and hotel rooms. The historic Bailey House hotel was built in 1915 and offers 8 rooms that are decorated in a charming 1920’s style. There is a downstairs master bedroom, six upstairs guest rooms and carriage house. Kids will love the Attic Dorm with five single beds. Note that not all the rooms have private bathrooms. You can rent by the room or rent out the whole house for a large group or family as the house sleeps up to 19 guests.&nbsp;

The luxury yurts, safari tents and historic cabins have private en-suit bathrooms and fully equipped kitchens. There are five yurts, sleeping anywhere from two to six guests. The safari tent sleeps two and features a king bed. The five historic cabins are 100 years old and sleep up to six guests and have TVs. There are also tiny cabins that sleep two to four, offering basic essentials. While staying on the property, you can hike, fish, bike or just relax.

#### [Eco-Camps Temecula](/go/eco-camps-temecula-glamping-temecula-ca/)

*23446 California 79, Warner Springs, CA 92086*

Family Glamping&nbsp; -&nbsp; Kids Free&nbsp; -&nbsp; Private Bathroom&nbsp; -&nbsp; Glamping Tents&nbsp; -&nbsp; Hiking&nbsp; -&nbsp; Wine Tours&nbsp; -&nbsp; Wifi

Experience the peace and tranquility of Eco-Camps Temecula. Located in wine country, you and your family can enjoy family glamping in luxurious tents while taking in the scenic views. Every glamping tent has a private en-suite bathroom, free wifi, queen beds, bedding, outlets for charging electronics, cooking equipment, wine glasses, wine opener and coffee and tea. There are two tent options to choose from. The Queen Tent has one queen bed and optional two single beds and sleeps two adults and two kids. The Family Queen Tent sleeps up to three adults and three kids with one queen bed and three single beds.&nbsp;

At Eco-Camps Temecula you can expect beautiful views of sunsets, starry nights and wildlife as well as ample opportunities to enjoy nature with picnics and hiking trails. Across the road you can partake in wine tasting and olive oil tasting. If you are interested in exploring more of the wine country, you can book an organized wine tour.&nbsp;

#### [Temecula KOA at Vail Lake](/go/temecula-koa-vail-lake-glamping/)

* &nbsp;38000 CA-79, Temecula, CA 92592*

Glamping Cabins&nbsp; -&nbsp; Family Friendly&nbsp; -&nbsp; Private Bathrooms&nbsp; -&nbsp; Wine Country&nbsp;&nbsp;- Family Activities  -  On Vail Lake  -  Seasonal Events  -  Onsite Dining  -  3 Pools  -  Hiking, Equestrian &amp; Biking Trails  -

Temecula KOA offers a wide array of activities that cater to families of all ages. From swimming in the lake and fishing to horseback riding, hiking, and biking, there's no shortage of adventures to embark on. The campground also features a playground, mini-golf course, and even three pools to keep the kids entertained. The campground features dedicated spaces for kids to have a blast. The playground, complete with swings and slides, provides endless hours of fun. The pools feature a lap pool, kids wading pool, and a refreshing leisure to beat the heat.

The campground provides excellent amenities designed with families in mind. You'll find spacious RV and tent sites, as well as cozy cabins that offer a comfortable and convenient home away from home. Restrooms, showers, and laundry facilities are easily accessible, ensuring a hassle-free camping experience. Temecula KOA organizes a range of family-oriented events and entertainment throughout the year. From movie nights under the stars to live music performances, themed weekends, and holiday celebrations, there's always something exciting happening that the whole family can enjoy.

Situated within the campground, Vail Lake is a highlight for families. It offers various water activities such as kayaking, paddleboarding, and boating. With its clear waters and picturesque surroundings, the lake provides a beautiful backdrop for family outings and creates lasting memories. The campground also offers stunning views of Temecula Valley with rolling hills, vineyards, and the lake. Families can soak in the natural beauty, go for leisurely walks, or simply relax in the peaceful surroundings.

Temecula KOA is conveniently located near popular attractions such as the [Temecula Valley Wine Country](/kid-friendly-wineries-temecula-ca/), [Old Town Temecula](/top-things-to-do-in-temecula/), and various family-friendly parks. Families can [explore local wineries](/best-temecula-wine-tours/), enjoy delicious dining experiences, or embark on outdoor adventures nearby.

#### [Rusty Fork Ranch](/go/rusty-fork-ranch-glamping-temecula-ca/)

* 33700 Madera De Playa Dr, Temecula, CA 92592*

Ranch Rooms&nbsp; -&nbsp; Airstreams&nbsp; -&nbsp; Private Bathroom&nbsp; -&nbsp; Adult Only&nbsp; -&nbsp; No Pets&nbsp; -&nbsp; Wifi&nbsp; -&nbsp; Work Space

Rusty Fork Ranch offers a ranch experience while still being in the center of it all. Here, you have the option of staying in one of the mini ranch-themed rooms or in a remodeled airstream. All accommodations sleep two guests. Each accommodation has cable TV, mini fridges, wifi, heating, air conditioning and private bathrooms with showers. This is an adults only ranch stay and pets are not allowed due to the existing animals on the property.&nbsp;

You will be staying on a mini farm, so there is livestock to see as well as the beauty of the surrounding wine country. Although you may feel miles away from it all, you are not far from all of Temecula’s most popular attractions. You are just minutes from wineries, Old Town and Pechanga Casino.&nbsp;

#### [P](/go/pechanga-resort-casino-temecula-ca/)[e](/go/pechanga-resort-casino-temecula-ca/)[changa RV Resort](/go/pechanga-resort-casino-temecula-ca/)

*45000 Pechanga Pkwy, Temecula, CA 92592*

RV Glamping&nbsp; -&nbsp; Family Friendly  -  Pool  -  Pechanga Resort  -  Onsite Restaurants  -  Golf Course

Pechanga RV Resort provides spacious and well-maintained RV sites, accommodating various sizes of RVs. Families can comfortably set up their RVs and enjoy a sense of privacy and relaxation. The sites are equipped with full hookups, including water, electricity, and sewer connections.

The resort offers a wide range of amenities that cater to families. The resort features a sparkling swimming pool where kids can splash around and have fun. There is also a dedicated playground area for children to enjoy, complete with swings and slides. There is a variety of family-friendly events and entertainment throughout the year. From live music performances to outdoor movie nights, there's always something enjoyable for the whole family to experience together.

Pechanga RV Resort is located adjacent to the renowned Pechanga Resort Casino. While families can enjoy the RV resort's family-oriented atmosphere, they also have the option to visit the casino for additional entertainment. The casino offers a range of dining options, shows, and activities that may appeal to adult family members.

The RV resort is conveniently located near popular attractions in Temecula, California. Families can explore the charming Old Town Temecula, visit local wineries for wine tastings, or enjoy outdoor activities in nearby parks. The region also hosts events and festivals throughout the year that families can participate in and create lasting memories.
