<p class="has-drop-cap">A well-planned luxury road trip offers <a href="/top-tips-for-a-stress-free-road-trip/">less stress</a> and several unforgettable experiences. The indulgence and freedom of picturesque landscapes flying past you in a moving vehicle is something many wish for. You have the chance to explore charming towns and see the beautiful countryside. Reports indicate that <a href="https://roamthecarolinas.com/road-trip-facts-stats/#:~:text=97%25%20of%20summer%20travel%20in,journey%20500%20miles%20or%20more.">18%</a> of road trips cover 500 miles and over, so if you plan on hitting that distance, you might as well do it in style and comfort. So with that said, here's how to plan a luxury road trip and make it a totally stress-free experience.</p>

<div class="wp-block-uagb-advanced-heading uagb-block-42a30e40"><h3 class="uagb-heading-text">Select an iconic route</h3></div>
<!-- /wp:uagb/advanced-heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"dropCap":true} -->
<p class="has-drop-cap">There is no shortage of iconic routes across the United States that you can leverage for your luxury road trip. Iconic routes like the Pacific Coast Highway in California are a classic example. Better yet, you can try the scenic routes through the Rocky Mountains, where there is no shortage of nature’s beauty. If you love nature, these iconic routes will bring more meaning to your luxury road trip. You may need a map to help plan your route. Better yet, search the internet for iconic routes in your state. You will be amazed at the information a simple Google search brings. The exciting bit is that these routes have coastal vistas, mountain retreats, and urban escapades to suit every traveler’s preference. That means you have options that align with your idea of a luxury road trip nationwide.</p>

<div class="wp-block-uagb-advanced-heading uagb-block-d9d7944c"><h3 class="uagb-heading-text">Plan your itinerary with exclusive experiences</h3></div>
<!-- /wp:uagb/advanced-heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"dropCap":true} -->
<p class="has-drop-cap">Although you’re embarking on a road trip, you may not necessarily be driving throughout. There will be times to stop, rest, eat, and indulge in other activities. That is when you must infuse bits of luxury in all your road trip activities. You may want to go all out with this step. An exclusive experience means partaking or engaging in activities reserved only for a few. It can be solo or in limited groups, but the idea is to enjoy something ordinarily unavailable to the wider public. Some examples are hot air balloon rides over coastal vistas and other picturesque locations. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Consider adding wine-tasting events to your luxury road trip, but this can be tricky. Wine-tasting events are exclusive and not open to the public. Therefore, you may have to search the internet and register in advance to avoid inconveniences. Some wine-tasting events require payments to try exclusive wines, so if you’re game, express your interest before it’s too late. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>One way to search for wine-tasting events is by researching esteemed vineyards within the jurisdiction of your road trip. This way, you don’t have to travel off your original course to experience it. Exclusive experiences are varied, so it’s up to you to determine which ones align with your passions and interests. Check out our favorite <a href="/best-temecula-wine-tours/">wine tours in Temecula</a>.</p>

<div class="wp-block-uagb-advanced-heading uagb-block-9f0b02df"><h3 class="uagb-heading-text">Invest in personalized concierge services</h3></div>
<!-- /wp:uagb/advanced-heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"dropCap":true} -->
<p class="has-drop-cap">A dedicated concierge can make your life easy, so consider getting one for your road trip. Personalized concierge services will handle your reservations and recommend unique experiences to enhance luxury road trip activities. They can also secure coveted reservations at Michelin-star restaurants. It is the same with VIP access to special places.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Reservations in these establishments are often limited, so handling them yourself reduces your chances of getting a spot. Moreover, booking these reservations on your own can be too much stress. It’s better to leave it to experts with unique negotiation skills. Your only job is to show up and have the best time of your life without exerting too much pressure in the preparatory stages.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph --></div></div>

<div class="wp-block-uagb-advanced-heading uagb-block-137b7b6a"><h3 class="uagb-heading-text">Choose a luxury vehicle</h3></div>
<!-- /wp:uagb/advanced-heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"dropCap":true} -->
<p class="has-drop-cap">If you own a luxury vehicle, renting one may not be necessary. However, certain things should be considered before opting for an already-owned luxury car. First, does your luxury vehicle offer ample comfort equal to living in a home? Remember that this is a road trip, and one of the fundamental elements of this activity is rest and comfort. While your sports car fits the luxury category, it may lack vital elements that would take away from a road trip. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>That is why a <a href="https://litrv.com/rentals">motor coach rental</a> may be your best bet. These transport vehicles are designed to meet your needs for a luxury journey. You cannot underestimate the benefits of a motor coach rental on your trip, especially when you have luxury on your mind. This option ensures you travel in style and comfort with plush seating, entertainment systems, and professional drivers. What’s the point of a luxury road trip if you cannot rest to have the best time of your life? Whether you’re with friends or family, a luxury vehicle made for a purpose is your best choice.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph --></div></div>

<div class="wp-block-uagb-advanced-heading uagb-block-1d421564"><h3 class="uagb-heading-text">Embrace spontaneity by enjoying aerial views</h3></div>
<!-- /wp:uagb/advanced-heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"dropCap":true} -->
<p class="has-drop-cap">This is a major twist to your road trip - but remember that spontaneity can enhance any luxury road trip. No matter how capable your luxury vehicle is for the road trip, it cannot beat the experience an aerial view offers. A helicopter sightseeing makes you explore awe-inspiring landscapes that traveling on land cannot offer. Please park your car near the airstrip or a designated area to secure it. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Some touring companies offer the helicopter sightseeing experience as part of a larger service package, so do well to find out what other offers are available. It adds an air of grandeur and luxury to what might have been an ordinary road trip. When it’s all done and dusted, you can return to your safely parked vehicle to resume your road trip and the remaining activities waiting to be explored.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph --></div></div>

<div class="wp-block-uagb-advanced-heading uagb-block-650583ac"><h3 class="uagb-heading-text">Engage the services of a professional photographer</h3></div>
<!-- /wp:uagb/advanced-heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"dropCap":true} -->
<p class="has-drop-cap">If this is not a solo trip, there is so much more to do with <a href="https://www.artifactuprising.com/photography-tips/road-trip-photos">photography</a>. Smartphones have evolved, and the right settings can capture the most breathtaking photos on your luxury road trip. But this time around, you deserve something different and more professional. Consider including a professional photographer in your road trip plan. Professional ones can obscure themselves on these trips to take the most natural-looking pictures. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>With experience, a professional photographer will capture the essence of your road trip with friends and family. These candid shots will look nothing like intentionally posing for the camera. Instead of printing these images, the professional can upload them onto your preferred website or a digital platform to be retrieved at any time.</p>
