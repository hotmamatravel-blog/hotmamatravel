<h4 id="medieval-times"><a href="/go/hop-on-hop-off-trolley-tour-st-augustine-fl/" linkid="17637">St. Augustine Hop-On Hop-Off Trolley Tour</a></h4>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p> &nbsp;<em>167 San Marco Ave, St. Augustine, FL 32084</em></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"dropCap":true} -->
<p class="has-drop-cap">One of the best ways for families to experience the city of St. Augustine is by taking the Hop-On Hop-Off Trolley Tour. This tour allows you to see the city's top attractions at your own pace, while also providing insightful commentary on the city's rich history and culture. Kids will love riding on the open-air trolley and seeing the city from a unique perspective. The tour also stops at all of the major landmarks, such as the Castillo de San Marcos and the Fountain of Youth, making it easy for families to explore at their leisure. The tour even includes Florida Heritage Museum admission.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>And with unlimited hop-on hop-off access, families can customize their itinerary and spend as much or as little time as they want at each stop. The St. Augustine Hop-On Hop-Off Trolley Tour is an excellent way for families to see the city's top sights while also having the freedom to explore at their own pace. I highly recommend the hop-on hop-off tours in any new city you visit. It is something we personally do whenever we visit somewhere new.</p>

<h4 id="medieval-times"><a href="/go/ghost-tour-st-augustine-fl/" linkid="17638">St. Augustine Ghost Tour: A Ghostly Encounter</a></h4>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p> &nbsp;<em>12 St George St, St. Augustine, FL 32084</em></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"dropCap":true} -->
<p class="has-drop-cap">If your family loves a good spooky adventure (like my family), then a St. Augustine Ghost Tour is a must-do! As the oldest city in America, St. Augustine is rich in history and legend, making it a perfect setting for a ghostly experience. The tour takes you through the city's narrow streets and alleyways, where you'll hear chilling tales of ghost sightings and paranormal activity. Kids will be thrilled to learn about the city's haunted past, and may even catch a glimpse of a ghostly figure or two! The tour is led by knowledgeable and engaging guides, who will keep the entire family entertained with their storytelling skills. And with a range of tour options to choose from, including walking tours and trolley tours, families can select the one that best suits their interests and preferences. A St. Augustine Ghost Tour is an exciting and memorable way for families to explore the city's history and get a little spooked in the process!</p>

<p class="has-drop-cap">If you're looking for a fun and educational activity for the whole family, a walking history tour of the St. Augustine Historic District is a perfect choice! <h4 id="medieval-times"><a href="/go/castillo-de-san-marcos-st-augustine-fl/" linkid="17647">Castillo de San Marcos National Monument</a></h4>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p> &nbsp;<em>1 South Castillo Drive Saint Augustine,&nbsp;FL&nbsp;32084</em></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"dropCap":true} -->
<p class="has-drop-cap">Castillo de San Marcos National Monument, dating back to 1672, is an iconic landmark and a must-see for history buffs and casual tourists alike. Walk through the impressive fortifications that have protected the city for centuries and imagine what life was like for the soldiers who once called it home. You can even participate in live cannon firing demonstrations and musket drills, bringing history to life in a thrilling way. Take a stroll along the ramparts for spectacular views of the Matanzas Bay and the surrounding landscape, and explore the museum exhibits that showcase the fort's fascinating history. The kids will love the Junior Ranger program, which includes fun activities and educational materials that will keep them engaged and entertained throughout the visit.</p>

With so many things to see and do, the St. Augustine Alligator Farm and Zoological Park is a must-visit destination for families.</p>

Kids will love the interactive exhibits and hands-on activities, like trying on prisoner uniforms or sitting in the judge's chair. And for the more adventurous, try the Ghosts and Gravestones tour, a spooky nighttime adventure that takes you through the jail and other haunted sites in the area. <p> &nbsp;<em>101 San Marco Ave, St. Augustine, FL 32084</em></p>

<p class="has-drop-cap">The National Shrine is home to a beautiful chapel that dates back to the 17th century, as well as a museum that showcases the history of the Catholic faith in America. But you don't have to be Catholic to appreciate its history and beautiful grounds.</p>
