## [Pendry San Diego](/go/pendry-san-diego-hotel/)

*550 J St, San Diego, CA 92101*

### Christmas Delights!

Pendry San Diego, a member of [Preferred Hotels &amp; Resorts](https://preferredhotels.com/), is perfect for those families looking for a cool city kind of Christmas getaway. Located in the heart of the urban and historical Gaslamp Quarter in Downtown San Diego, the Pendry is nestled among high-rise buildings just steps away from city attractions. However, during the holidays, you may not want to venture far from the hotel, because the Pendry pulls out all the stops, including a Neiman Marcus Holiday Dream Suite. When you book this beautifully decorated festive suite, you will be greeted with fresh cookies from Neiman Marcus and have all your shopping and gift wrapping needs met by a shopping concierge.

If your family is like mine, then the first thing you will love is Nason's Beer Hall. It is a family-friendly on-site gastro-pub that pays homage to the Nason &amp; Co. Farmer's Market, which once occupied the space in the 1900s. During the Christmas season, Nason's goes all out with over-the-top decor and Christmas lights. Even sweeter for the kids is the Pancakes and Pajama Brunch with Santa, where kids will receive a complimentary pair of holiday pajamas from P.J. Salvage while they enjoy breakfast and take photos with Santa!

## [Mission Inn Hotel &amp; Spa](/go/mission-inn-hotel-spa-riverside/)

*3649 Mission Inn Avenue, Riverside, CA 92501*

The Mission Inn spares no expense when it comes to holiday decorations. Every corner of the hotel is adorned with holiday finery, including Christmas trees, wreaths, and other seasonal decor, creating a warm and inviting atmosphere.

The Festival of Lights at the [Mission Inn](https://www.missioninn.com/) is a world-renowned holiday event that features millions of twinkling lights, animated displays, and festive decorations throughout the hotel's grounds. Families can stroll through the beautifully illuminated streets and courtyards, creating a magical and immersive holiday experience. There is live music with Grammy award-winning artists, festive treats, photo-opportunities, and much more.

During the holiday season, kids are given the royal treatment with festive Christmas packages that includes an overnight stay complete with a filled holiday stocking, milk, Casey's cupcakes, Christmas stories, gifts, carolers and more!

## [The Ritz-Carlton, Laguna Niguel](/go/ritz-carlton-laguna-niguel-hotel/)

*One Ritz Carlton Dr, Dana Point, CA 92629*

Surf's up for this beachy Orange County Christmas escape at the [Ritz-Carlton Laguna Niguel](https://www.ritzcarlton.com/en/hotels/snarz-the-ritz-carlton-laguna-niguel/overview/). Yes, you can still hit the beach during the holiday season with your family (if you don't mind a little cold), and what better way to kick off this sunny season with a little Surfing Santa competition!

At the end of November, The Ritz-Carlton hosts a surfing and stand-up paddleboarding competition for men, women, and children dressed like Santa or other Christmas characters. It is fun to compete and fun to watch. However, the holiday fun doesn't stop there!

Decorated to the nines, you won't want to miss the tree lighting ceremony, complete with live entertainment and a visit from Santa himself. Throughout the season, you will enjoy festive holiday meals, cocktails, and even Holiday Tea with an ocean view!

Your kids will love cozy holiday story time, gingerbread house, cookie decorating, and gazing upon the Gingerbread Extravaganza in the lobby, which features a giant 24-foot gingerbread scene in the festive lobby! Every year features a different theme, so you will want to keep coming back year after year.

## [The Westgate Hotel San Diego](/go/westgate-hotel-san-diego/)

*1055 2nd Ave, San Diego, CA 92101*

Located on the edge of the Gaslamp Quarter in Downtown San Diego, you and your family will feel like you stepped into the Palace of Versailles. Built in 1970 as a re-creation of the famed palace, the [Westgate Hotel](https://www.westgatehotel.com/) drips in European elegance. During the holidays, the hotel drips Christmas cheer with its festive decor and family fun activities.

Around the holidays, it is all about the indulgences. Enjoy festive and fun themed afternoon teas, like the Westgate Christmas Teas and Teddy Bears Teas. Both feature fun holiday beverages, but the Teddy Bear Teas include special one-on-one time with Santa and a keepsake teddy bear.

In addition to the holiday tea services, there is a fun Breakfast with Santa where the family can enjoy a delicious holiday buffet with some very special company from Mr. and Mrs. Claus. The restaurants are also serving up a delicious Christmas buffet and Christmas dinner.

## [Hotel Del Coronado](/go/hotel-del-coronado-san-diego/)

*** **1500 Orange Ave, Coronado, CA 92118*

The [Hotel del Coronado](https://www.hoteldel.com/), affectionately known as "The Del," is a quintessential Southern California beachfront resort that transforms into a winter wonderland during the Christmas season. Guests can expect a delightful blend of tradition and enchantment as the hotel embraces the holiday spirit.

Speaking of spirit, **[The Del is said to be haunted](/haunted-hotels-san-diego/)**, which adds some excitement throughout the year.

The hotel's lobby and common areas are beautifully adorned with festive decorations, including Christmas trees, wreaths, and twinkling lights, creating a warm and welcoming ambiance.

One of the standout features is the life-sized gingerbread house that graces the lobby, a remarkable sight that's perfect for family photos. Nearby, you'll find the ice-skating rink on the oceanfront Windsor Lawn. Skating by the sea is a unique and magical experience, offering a perfect view of the Pacific Ocean and adding a touch of wintry wonder to this coastal paradise.

The Hotel Del Coronado also offers holiday dining experiences at its restaurants, featuring special menus and seasonal treats. Families can enjoy brunches, dinners, and even a Santa Brunch, where children have the chance to meet Santa and share their Christmas wishes.

For the little ones, Mrs. Claus makes special appearances for storytime sessions, captivating children with heartwarming holiday tales. The hotel frequently hosts beachfront holiday concerts, allowing guests to enjoy live music and caroling while taking in the breathtaking ocean views.

Additional activities for children often include cookie decorating and holiday craft workshops, adding to the festive spirit. The hotel's shops offer a range of holiday gifts and souvenirs, allowing guests to find unique and festive items for their loved ones.

The Hotel del Coronado is known for its annual lighting of the Christmas tree, a special event featuring live entertainment and the illumination of the grand tree, marking the official start of the holiday season. The festivities at The Del provide an ideal setting for families to celebrate the holidays with joy, warmth, and the beauty of the Pacific coast. Please check with the hotel for the most up-to-date information on their Christmas offerings.

## [Westin Carlsbad Resort &amp; Spa](/go/westin-carlsbad-resort-spa/)

*5480 Grand Pacific Dr, Carlsbad, CA 92008*

Set in the quaint beach community of Carlsbad, just north of San Diego, is the lovely [Westin Carlsbad Resort &amp; Spa](https://www.westinnandsuites.com/), where all year long you can enjoy pristine beaches and nearby attractions. They even have a private entrance into LEGOLAND! During the holidays, this laid-back back breezy coastal resort comes alive with Christmas festivities at the Family Friendly Holiday Nights.

For three Thursdays in December, the Westin Carlsbad Resort &amp; Spa holds exciting holiday-themed events for the whole family to enjoy. There are fun rides like the Holly Trolley, train rides Ferris wheel, and even sledding on "real" snow. You and your kids can take fun photos in a giant snow globe and with Santa Claus. There are Christmas crafts, fair games, and even live entertainment. It is a truly magical evening for celebrating this magical season.

## [Terranea Resort](/go/terranea-resort-rancho-palos-verdes/)

*** **1500 Orange Ave, Coronado, CA 92118*

Terranea Resort, situated in the stunning coastal location of Rancho Palos Verdes, California, offers a charming and festive atmosphere during the Christmas season. The resort comes alive with holiday decor, including beautifully adorned Christmas trees, wreaths, and other festive ornaments that create a warm and inviting ambiance. You'll find seasonal decorations throughout the property, from the lobby to the outdoor areas.

One of the highlights is the annual gingerbread village display, featuring intricate and delightful gingerbread creations that showcase the creative talents of the resort's culinary team. This display is often a treat for both adults and children, and it's an excellent spot for holiday photos.

The resort often features outdoor holiday lighting displays, adding to the festive spirit. Enjoy illuminated pathways and courtyards that make evening strolls truly magical. Families visiting the resort can typically find a range of holiday activities for children, such as cookie decorating, holiday craft workshops, and perhaps even a visit from Santa Claus himself.

[Terranea Resort](https://www.terranea.com/) offers special holiday dining experiences, including festive menus at its restaurants. Guests can indulge in holiday-themed dishes and treats while taking in breathtaking ocean views. During the holiday season, Terranea often hosts live entertainment, including carolers and musical performances, creating a joyful and festive atmosphere.
