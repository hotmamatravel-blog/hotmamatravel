### [Angel Stadium of Anaheim](/go/angel-stadium-anaheim/)

* 2000 E Gene Autry Way,&nbsp;Anaheim, CA 92806-6143*

The atmosphere is always fun for the kids and the&nbsp;Angels’ Stadium&nbsp;really caters to families with a family center with games, fireworks on Friday and Saturday nights and even a mini-firework display when our boys hit a home run. Go Angels!

[**See photos, reviews &amp; more**](/go/angel-stadium-anaheim/)

### [Adventure City](/go/adventure-city-anaheim/)

*1238 S Beach Blvd, Anaheim, CA 92804 *

Adventure City in Anaheim is a small amusement park that offers big fun for families. It features rides, a petting zoo, and family dance parties. In my opinion, [Adventure City](https://www.adventurecity.com/) is best suited for younger kids (not teens).

[**See photos, reviews &amp; more**](/go/adventure-city-anaheim/)

### [Anaheim Ducks At The Honda Center](/go/honda-center-anaheim/)

*2695 E Katella Ave, Anaheim, CA 92806*

Looking for high-energy fun in Anaheim that doesn’t involve mouse ears? Head to the **[Honda Center](https://www.hondacenter.com/)**! Whether you’re catching an action-packed Ducks game or rocking out at one of their epic family-friendly events, this spot brings the excitement. It’s loud, it’s lively, and the snacks are *chef’s kiss*. Bonus: the kids get to scream without anyone giving you side-eye. Total win.

[**See photos, reviews &amp; more**](/go/honda-center-anaheim/)

### [Anaheim Convention Center](/go/anaheim-convention-center/)

*800 W. Katella Ave. Anaheim, CA 92802*

If your kids are glued to YouTube like it’s their job, then the **[Anaheim Convention Center](https://www.visitanaheim.org/listing/anaheim-convention-center/9/)** is the place to be. Home to epic events like **VidCon**, this massive venue hosts all kinds of family-friendly conventions. Think cosplay, creators, comics, and pure chaos (in the best way). It’s where screen time meets real life, and your teens might actually smile in public.

### [Camelot Golfland](/go/camelot-golfland-anaheim/)

***&nbsp;**3200 E Carpenter Ave, Anaheim, CA 92806*

Ready for a full-blown family fun overload? **[Camelot Golfland](https://www.golfland.com/anaheim/)** in Anaheim is part mini-golf paradise, part arcade wonderland, and part “please don’t let them see the snack bar again.” With themed courses, laser tag, bumper boats, and enough flashing lights to make your kids lose their minds (in a good way), this place is pure chaotic joy. ### Adventure Lagoon

*3255 E Miraloma Ave, Anaheim, CA 92806*

If your kids are bouncing off the walls, let ’em bounce on water instead at[**Adventure Lagoon**](https://www.theadventurelagoon.com/), Anaheim’s hidden gem of inflatable fun! This giant floating obstacle course sits on a crystal-clear spring-fed lake, perfect for swimming, splashing, and burning off all that energy. It’s like American Ninja Warrior, but for families in swimsuits.

### [Knott's Berry Farm](/go/knotts-berry-farm-general/)

*8039 Beach Boulevard, Buena Park, California 90620*

Knott's Berry Farm is a 57-acre amusement park in Buena Park with a cool history. Dating back to the 1920's as a roadside attraction where Walter Knott sold his famous boysenberry products. He had a love for California history he added a replica  ghost town and the park continued to grow into what it is today.

### [Medieval Times](/go/medieval-times-buena-park/)

*7662 Beach Boulevard, Buena Park, California, 90620*

Medieval Times in Buena Park, is like stepping back into a time when knights were heroes, testing their strength, endurance and coordination in front of a cheering crowd. Families get to dine on a four-course medieval-style meal as they root for their assigned knight in this two-hour tournament as he tests his might in jousting, swordsmanship, falconry, horsemanship and more.

[**See photos, reviews &amp; more**](/go/medieval-times-buena-park/)

### [Discovery Cube &amp; Science Center](/go/discovery-cube-orange-county/)

*2500 N. Main Street, Santa Ana, CA 92705*

**[Discovery Cube Orange County](https://www.discoverycube.org/)** is where learning goes hands-on and full throttle! This interactive science center turns STEM into a full-on adventure—think launching rockets, exploring eco-zones, and shopping in a pretend grocery store that teaches healthy living (sneaky AND smart). It’s all about sparking curiosity in kids through play, with exhibits that cover everything from early learning to environmental stewardship. Science just became the coolest part of your trip.

[**See photos, reviews &amp; more**](/go/discovery-cube-orange-county/)

### [Pretend City Children's Museum](/go/pretend-city-childrens-museum-irvine/)

*29 Hubble, Irvine, CA 92618*

**[Pretend City Children’s Museum](https://www.pretendcity.org/)** is like real life, only way more fun and kid-sized! This interactive mini-city lets little ones step into grown-up roles as chefs, doctors, construction workers, and more while learning what it means to be part of a community. It’s imaginative play meets real-world lessons, and yes, they’ll probably make you “buy” fake groceries with fake money.

[**See photos, reviews &amp; more**](/go/pretend-city-childrens-museum-irvine/)

### Orange County Fair

*88 Fair Drive, Costa Mesa, CA 92626 *

The [OC Fair](https://ocfair.com/) runs during the summer months, starting mid-July and going until mid-August. The fair features exciting exhibits, animals, concerts, food vendors, rides, and more. When the fair is not in town, the OC fairgrounds are used for the OC Event Center, where you can still enjoy fun events, shopping, food, and live music.

**[See photos, reviews &amp; more](https://ocfair.com/)**

### [The Queen Mary](/go/queen-mary-long-beach/)

*Long Beach, CA*

Queen Mary set sail on her maiden voyage in 1936 from Southampton and for the next three years functioned as a luxury cruise liner, hosting celebrity guests and British royalty regularly. Then in 1940, the Queen Mary was painted grey and served her country in WWII as the Grey Ghost before returning to her cruise liner glory again in 1947 to 1966. By 1967, her sailing days were over, and the Queen Mary was moved to Long Beach, California, where she sits as a famous landmark hotel today! Oh, and it's reportedly [haunted](/best-haunted-places-with-kids/), and they have an awesome [Scottish Festival](/the-queen-mary-scots-festival-with-kids/).

*San Juan Capistrano, CA*

Mission San Juan Capistrano was founded in 1776 and is the seventh of the 21 California missions established by the Spanish Catholics. ## Fun places to shop near Anaheim

### [Gardenwalk](/go/gardenwalk-anaheim/)

*Anaheim, CA*

Gardenwalk in Anaheim is an outdoor collection of stores, restaurants and entertainment located just a block outside of Disneyland. This is the closest place to shop near Disneyland with kids and has one of my favorite restaurants - Cheesecake Factory.

[**See photos, reviews &amp; more**](/go/gardenwalk-anaheim/)

### [Brea Mall](/go/brea-mall/)

*1065 Brea Mall, Brea, CA 92821*

Need a break from theme parks but still want A/C and snacks? Brea Mall is your go-to. It’s more than just shopping, there’s a kid-friendly play area, sweet treats at every turn, and plenty of spots for tired parents to caffeinate and regroup. Plus, it’s indoors (hallelujah), which makes it a win on hot or rainy SoCal days. Retail therapy for the whole fam? Yes, please.

**[See photos, reviews &amp; more](/go/brea-mall/)**

### [South Coast Plaza](/go/south-coast-plaza-mall-costa-mesa/)

*Costa Mesa, CA*

South Coast Plaza in Costa Mesa is the largest shopping mall on the west coast and has the highest concentration of designer fashion retailers in the United States. If you like shopping high end fashion in a lux mall, this is the place to spend your afternoon.

[**See photos, reviews &amp; more**](/go/south-coast-plaza-mall-costa-mesa/)

### [Outlets at Orange](/go/outlets-at-orange-mall/)

The Outlets at Orange is an open air outlet shopping mall with over 120 value outlets from designer retailers. There are also a number of restaurants and a 3D IMAX movie theatre. Oh, and they have an excellent seafood restaurant called Market Broiler, which is the main reason we like to go there.

Fashion Island in Newport Beach is a fancy open-air shopping center with a cluster of premier designer retailers, lushly landscaped piazzas, boutique shops and restaurants. You can even see the ocean from certain angles at the mall, which helps remind you that you are in friggin Newport Beach!

*Irvine, CA*

Irvine Spectrum in Irvine is a popular outdoor shopping and entertainment center, complete with a Ferris wheel, fun restaurants, and the Irvine Improv! You will also find a cute old-fashioned carousel that the young kids will enjoy.
