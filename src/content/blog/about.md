Hey, I’m Amanda—aka **Hot Mama**—a wife, mom, writer, and bartender who’s made it her mission to prove that family travel can be exciting, affordable, and *just the right amount of [**weird**](/best-haunted-places-to-visit-for-families/)*. Around here, we don’t do boring.

I specialize in **family-friendly nightlife, haunted adventures**, and unexpected destinations that bring the *wow* without sacrificing the *whoa, that’s affordable!*

Yes, I take kids to [**Vegas**](/family-friendly-vegas/). Yes, we’ve hunted ghosts. Yes, we’ve done spooky festivals, late-night light shows, and hotel pools that close way past bedtime. And guess what? The kids *love* it—and so do the adults.
