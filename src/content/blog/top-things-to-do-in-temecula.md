##
Family-Friendly Temecula Attractions


<figure id="europa-village-kid-friendly-wineries-temecula"><a href="/images/europa-village-kid-friendly-wineries-temecula.jpg"><img src="/images/europa-village-kid-friendly-wineries-temecula.jpg" alt="" /></a></figure>

#### **[Wine Tasting](/go/wine-tasting-temecula-ca/)**

This is Temecula's most popular attraction as it is known as Southern California’s Wine Country. With over 40 wineries and growing, you will definitely want to spend some time exploring and tasting your way through some of these beautiful wineries by booking a stay at a [winery hotel](/best-winery-hotels-temecula-ca/) (link will take you to the full guide to Temecula's winery hotels). For those who only have a day, the best way to see the most without having to worry about driving, is booking a wine tasting tour. For more information, see my post featuring the most [unique winery tours](/best-temecula-wine-tours/) in Temecula.&nbsp;

Bringing the kids? No problema. Check out my list of the[best kid-friendly wineries in Temecula](/kid-friendly-wineries-temecula-ca/).&nbsp; Many of the wineries in Temecula have live music, restaurants and events that are for the whole family.

[See photos, reviews &amp; more](/go/wine-tasting-temecula-ca/)





#### [**Hot Air Balloon** Rides](/go/hot-air-balloon-rides-temecula-ca/)

A popular activity in Temecula is soaring over wine country in a hot air balloon. There are several companies to check out, like Temecula Shared Hot Air Balloon Rides and Sunrise Temecula. The tours usually cost around $200 and take about two to four hours. Think about it, the Temecula sky is sprinkled with hot air balloons and you could be in one! See more details at the link below.

[See photos, reviews &amp; more](/go/hot-air-balloon-rides-temecula-ca/)




<figure id="14-best-breweries-temecula-ca"><a href="/images/14-best-breweries-temecula-ca.jpg"><img src="/images/14-best-breweries-temecula-ca.jpg" alt="The best breweries in Temecula" /></a></figure>

#### **[Temecula Breweries](/go/breweries-temecula-ca/)**

Temecula is not only a wine country, but a burgeoning beer country with a booming brewing industry. There are over 20 craft breweries in Temecula that you can visit and enjoy tasting local brews. Some have fantastic patios and live music, while some have great onsite restaurants. Like the wineries, you can book a brewery tour or just pick one and have some fun. Check out my guide to the very [best breweries in Temecula](/best-breweries-in-temecula-ca/).

[See photos, reviews &amp; more](/go/breweries-temecula-ca/)





#### **[Pechanga Resort &amp; Casino](/go/pechanga-resort-casino-temecula-ca/)**

* **45000 Pechanga Pkwy, Temecula, CA 92592***

Pechanga is a popular entertainment hub in Temecula. It is a one stop shop for live music, dancing, fine dining, shows, gambling, golfing, spa, pools and more. Pechanga has the largest casino floor in California with over 5,000 slot machines, 158 table games, a 30 table poker room and a 700 seat bingo room. In addition to the casino, Pechanga is a resort hotel and RV Resort, so there is a lot to do even if you don’t gamble. It is a great place to grab a meal at one of their amazing restaurants, like Great Oak Steakhouse or 1882 Cantina, before catching a music concert or comedy show.

[See photos, reviews &amp; more](/go/pechanga-resort-casino-temecula-ca/)

#### **[Golfing](/go/golf-courses-temecula-ca/)**

If you love to golf, then you will love Temecula. There are several premiere golf courses, some even designed by Arthur Hill. There are seven golf courses located in Temecula, but over 20 more located within 20 miles. Temecula golf courses take advantage of the beautiful surrounding nature, mountains, creeks and oak trees. Check out our guide for a list of [Temecula golf courses](/best-temecula-golf-courses/).

## Fun places to shop in Temecula


<figure id="old-town-temecula-ca"><a href="/images/old-town-temecula-ca.jpg"><img src="/images/old-town-temecula-ca.jpg" alt="" /></a></figure>

#### **[Old Town Temecula](/go/old-town-temecula-ca/)**

Old Town Temecula has the best of both worlds as it marries the past with the present beautifully. Stroll along 19th century buildings as you pop into boutique shops, browse for one-of-a-kind antiques and dine at some of the best restaurants in town.&nbsp;

Here, you can start or end a day of fantastic wine or beer tasting with a delicious meal and some live music. If you love ghost stories, Old Town Temecula has a few including a haunted bar! Find out more with a[haunted pub tour](/go/temecula-cable-car-wine-tour-temecula-ca/).

[See photos, reviews &amp; more](/go/old-town-temecula-ca/)





#### **[Promenade Mall](/go/promenade-mall-temecula-ca/)**

* 40820 Winchester Rd, Temecula, CA 92591*

Temecula’s mall, this shopping complex opened in 1999 and has continued to thrive as a hub for shopping, dining and entertainment. Over the years, the mall has grown and improved. It now features a giant two-story outdoor plaza.

## Top Temecula Restaurants


<figure id="rosas-cafe-tortilla-factory-temecula-ca"><a href="/images/rosas-cafe-tortilla-factory-temecula-ca.jpg"><img src="/images/rosas-cafe-tortilla-factory-temecula-ca.jpg" alt="" /></a></figure>

#### [Rosa’s Cafe &amp; Tortilla Factory](/go/rosas-cafe-tortilla-factory-temecula-ca/)

* 28134 Jefferson Ave, Temecula, CA 92590*

This Mexican restaurant is a favorite among locals as well as this family. It is kind of like a quick-service establishment&nbsp; where you can dine in or take-out. Inside you order up front and grab your meal when your number is called or you can pick drive-through. However, the inside atmosphere and food quality is nothing short of restaurant quality (for a great value). The atmosphere is bright, colorful and festive while the food is beyond amazing. They make the tortillas fresh and you can watch this process through the open kitchen windows. I recommend the beef tacos, queso, carrot cake and sopas! Definitely check it out, it won’t disappoint.

[See photos, reviews &amp; more](/go/rosas-cafe-tortilla-factory-temecula-ca/)





#### **[Penfolds](/go/penfolds-cafe-temecula-ca/)**

* 28250 Old Town Front St, Temecula, CA 92590*

This casual restaurant is a popular spot for breakfast, but they also have a great lunch menu. If you love omelets, then you will love Penfolds. They have a whole page on their menu of different kinds of omelets.

[See photos, reviews &amp; more](/go/penfolds-cafe-temecula-ca/)




<figure id="filippis-pizza-grotto-temecula-ca"><a href="/images/filippis-pizza-grotto-temecula-ca.jpg"><img src="/images/filippis-pizza-grotto-temecula-ca.jpg" alt="" /></a></figure>

#### [Filippi’s Pizza Grotto](/go/filippis-pizza-grotto-temecula-ca/)

* 27309 Jefferson Ave #110, Temecula, CA 92590*

This is a delicious family-friendly Italian restaurant. The atmosphere is warm, cozy and old-school Italian. The food is yummy and comes in generous portions for a great price. It’s a great spot for a date night or a big celebration. The house-dressing, by the way, is amazing as it mixes house Italian dressing with Bleu Cheese in the perfect proportions!&nbsp;

[See photos, reviews &amp; more](/go/filippis-pizza-grotto-temecula-ca/)





#### **[Winery Restaurants](/go/winery-restaurants-temecula-ca/)**

The wineries offer a lot more than wine tasting. In fact, the wineries are a great place to grab a delicious meal with a beautiful view. Often there is even live music on the weekends too. Many of the wineries in Temecula have beautiful restaurants, but some of the more popular ones to check out are The Vineyard Rose Restaurant at South Coast Winery, Bolero Restaurante, The Restaurant at Ponte Family Estate Winery and Cafe Champagne at Thornton Winery, just to name a few. There are about 20 winery restaurants to visit, so plan an extended trip and try them all!&nbsp;

If you want to explore more about Temecula wineries, see my guide on the [best family-friendly Temecula wineries](/kid-friendly-wineries-temecula-ca/), the [best winery hotels](/best-winery-hotels-temecula-ca/) and the most[unique wine tours in Temecula](/best-temecula-wine-tours/).

[See photos, reviews &amp; more](/go/winery-restaurants-temecula-ca/)




<figure id="pechanga-restaurants-great-oak-steakhouse-temecula-ca"><a href="/images/pechanga-restaurants-great-oak-steakhouse-temecula-ca.jpg"><img src="/images/pechanga-restaurants-great-oak-steakhouse-temecula-ca.jpg" alt="" /></a></figure>

#### **[Pechanga Restaurants](/go/pechanga-resort-casino-temecula-ca/)**

*45000 Pechanga Pkwy, Temecula, CA 92592*

At Pechanga, Temecula’s resort and casino, there are about ten different dining options that are worth going to, even if you are there to gamble. There is something for everyone, including The Great Oak SteakHouse,&nbsp; Umi Sushi &amp; Oysters, 1882 Cantina, Blazing Noodles, Paisano’s, Bamboo and more. There are also a number of bars and even a food court for more options!

[See photos, reviews &amp; more](/go/pechanga-resort-casino-temecula-ca/)





#### **[Old Town Temecula Restaurants](/go/old-town-temecula-ca/)**

* Old Town Front Street, Temecula, Ca 92590*

There are a ton of great dining options in Old Town Temecula, along Front Street! Some of the most notable are [Baily’s Old Town](/go/bailys-restaurant-old-town-temecula-ca/), which is a dining and entertainment venue that has weekend brunch and live music and The Bank, which is a great Mexican restaurant located in a historical bank where you can dine in the old vault! Both restaurants have great patios too! However, that is just two of many. Restaurants like 1909, Small Barn, Goat &amp; Vine, Be Good Restaurant and many many more are super popular and definitely worth popping in for a meal and drink. It is wise to have an empty stomach and go explore.&nbsp;

[See photos, reviews &amp; more](/go/old-town-temecula-ca/)

#### **[Vail Lake RV Resort](/go/vail-lake-rv-resort-temecula-ca/)**

* **38000 Hwy 79 South, Temecula, CA 92592***

At Vail Lake RV Resort you will have non-stop fun outdoors. On-site, there are three swimming pools, Happy Camper Cafe, Bunkhouse Bar, disc golf, mini- golf, horseshoe, pickleball and basketball courts. There is also access to bike trails and hiking trails. Plus there are always seasonal events to look out for, like Winter Wonderland Weekends. #### **[Temecula Creek Inn](/go/temecula-creek-inn-golf-courses-temecula-ca/)**

* **44501 Rainbow Canyon Rd, Temecula, CA 92592***

This hotel offers 127 rooms plus a private three-bedroom home as well as access to three premiere golf courses. For your enjoyment the rooms all have a private balcony or patio, lending to a lovely view. There is an on-site restaurant as well as a swimming pool and hot tub. Additionally, as a guest at Temecula Creek Inn you have access to The Cove, the luxurious pool at Pechanga.

Indulge in a luxury hotel stay at Pechanga Resort where you can book a room or a deluxe suite in the Resort Tower. While at the resort you have access to The Cove pool, several on-site restaurants, The Journey Golf Course, live entertainment, fitness center and of course the casino!
