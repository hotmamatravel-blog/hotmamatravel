<div class="wp-block-stackable-heading stk-block-heading stk-block-heading--v2 stk-block stk-e9bf107" id="full-nyny-hotel-review" data-block-id="e9bf107"><style>.stk-e9bf107 .stk-block-heading__bottom-line{background-color:#ff0000 !important;}</style><h4 class="stk-block-heading__text">Full NYNY Hotel Review</h4><div class="stk-block-heading__bottom-line"></div></div>
<!-- /wp:stackable/heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>I will just say it. We love, love, LOVE staying at the <a href="https://newyorknewyork.mgmresorts.com/en.html" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">New York New York Hotel</a> in Las Vegas with kids. By comparison to other gargantuan hotels and family resorts on the Vegas Strip, the NYNY will feel small, quaint, and manageable. The smaller size is by no means a bad thing, as it allows for a lot of great family activities in Vegas to be packed in efficiently.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Kids will especially appreciate the Big Apple roller coaster and arcade, and families will love hanging out in the new Central Park-themed outdoor/indoor collection of restaurants and activities called <em>The Park</em>, which is part of a new area called <em>The Neighborhood </em>that includes Park MGM Resort and the T-Mobile Arena.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>There are live events and concerts in The Park year-round, and unique restaurants with huge Jenga game and cornhole game sets that the kids can play while you enjoy a drink (or two!) al fresco. And where it lacks in Las Vegas family shows (Mad Apple is 18+), it makes up for it in free live music at the Irish Pub.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>My all time favorite reason to stay at NYNY, is our tradition of having a nightcap pint of Guinness at Nine Fine Irishmen, which is still the finest Irish Pub on the Strip with authentic Irish Music and Dance nightly by Sin e Ri Ra. Best of all, NYNY is one of the most affordable family hotels in Vegas and one of the best values in our experience.<strong>&nbsp;<a href="/go/nyny-las-vegas-expedia/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Learn more at Vegas.com</a></strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:stackable/heading {"uniqueId":"22add38","textTag":"h4","showBottomLine":true,"bottomLineColor":"#f40404"} -->
<div class="wp-block-stackable-heading stk-block-heading stk-block-heading--v2 stk-block stk-22add38" id="what-families-will-love-about-nyny" data-block-id="22add38"><style>.stk-22add38 .stk-block-heading__bottom-line{background-color:#f40404 !important;}</style><h4 class="stk-block-heading__text">What Families will love about NYNY</h4><div class="stk-block-heading__bottom-line"></div></div>
<!-- /wp:stackable/heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Great value.&nbsp;</strong>The NYNY hotel has consistently lower rates than the other kid-friendly Vegas hotels on our list, except for the nearby Luxor hotel, which also has comparable rates. With a few more amenities geared toward children and the fact that NYNY has the Nine Fine Irishmen Pub, this should easily be the go-to budget hotel for families in Las Vegas.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>The best Irish Pub.&nbsp;</strong>Did I mention we love Irish Music and Irish beer!? We are not huge fans of Irish food, but the Colcannon Soup, and the Fish and Chips at Nine Fine Irishmen are so good that it totally changed our minds on that. Seriously, nothing beats a long day in Vegas like spending an hour or two sipping pints of Guinness on the warm patio of Nine Fine Irishmen. Kids are allowed in the restaurant and on the patio until about 9 or 10pm.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Tons of kid-friendly food options!</strong> My kids love all the New York-style street food dining options. From New York Pizza to Crepes, there are so many fun things to try, you'll want to eat all day and kill time before you can eat again.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:stackable/heading {"uniqueId":"a7cd5c6","textTag":"h4","showBottomLine":true,"bottomLineColor":"#fe0404"} -->
<div class="wp-block-stackable-heading stk-block-heading stk-block-heading--v2 stk-block stk-a7cd5c6" id="suites-and-amenities-for-families-at-nyny" data-block-id="a7cd5c6"><style>.stk-a7cd5c6 .stk-block-heading__bottom-line{background-color:#fe0404 !important;}</style><h4 class="stk-block-heading__text">Suites and Amenities for Families at NYNY</h4><div class="stk-block-heading__bottom-line"></div></div>
<!-- /wp:stackable/heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Rooms at New York New York will not be as impressive as at Mandalay or MGM, but they are very nice and well-maintained - and in our experience, very cost-effective. I mean, in any other city in the US, a room this nice will cost several hundred dollars, but on the Vegas Strip, with the intense competition, we can often find rates at NYNY under 50 bucks per night! A family of 4 will find ample room in the 350 sq ft Park Avenue Queen room.&nbsp;<strong><a href="/go/nyny-las-vegas-expedia/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Check rates and availability at Vegas.com</a></strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":5} -->
<h5>Family Suites for Larger Families</h5>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The largest room for families at NYNY is the 800 sq ft Players Suite, which has two queen beds and a max occupancy of 5. If you need a room to fit more than 5 members of your family or group, I would recommend checking out the Marquee Suite at MGM or the Panoramic Two Bedroom Queen Suite at Mandalay Bay.&nbsp;<strong><a href="/go/nyny-las-vegas-expedia/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">See more images and all room types at Vegas.com</a></strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":5} -->
<h5>New York New York&nbsp;Pool</h5>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The Pool at NYNY will get the job done, but it is not one of the top Vegas pools for families. My kids always enjoy it, though. They do have a bar and drink service, and you can rent a cabana for the day. There is also a nice water feature in the middle of the pool that the kids love. However, there is no restaurant to eat poolside, which is kind of a bummer.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:stackable/heading {"uniqueId":"83beb15","textTag":"h4","showBottomLine":true,"bottomLineColor":"#f30404"} -->
<div class="wp-block-stackable-heading stk-block-heading stk-block-heading--v2 stk-block stk-83beb15" id="family-entertainment-at-nyny" data-block-id="83beb15"><style>.stk-83beb15 .stk-block-heading__bottom-line{background-color:#f30404 !important;}</style><h4 class="stk-block-heading__text">Family Entertainment at NYNY</h4><div class="stk-block-heading__bottom-line"></div></div>
<!-- /wp:stackable/heading -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":5} -->
<h5>New York New York Vegas Attractions</h5>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong><a linkid="13357" href="/go/big-apple-coaster-nyny-las-vegas/">The Big Apple Roller Coaster</a></strong>: This roller coaster is pure fun and excitement for the kids, and adults will also enjoy zipping around the New York-style hotel with views of the Vegas Strip. With a $26.00 scream pass, your kids can ride all day as much as their hearts desire. Not bad!</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>For tech-savvy kids and families, NYNY also just introduced a Virtual Reality version of the coaster ride in which you wear VR goggles on the actual roller coaster and get a whole new crazy experience in a virtual world synced to the twists and turns of the actual coaster. I haven't tried it yet, but it sounds futuristic, and I bet lots of people will find it unique and thrilling.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>The Big Apple Arcade</strong>: The arcade at NYNY is on the second floor just off the lobby, and it is HUGE. Kids<a href="/best-arcades-in-las-vegas/" target="_blank" data-type="post" data-id="21049" rel="noreferrer noopener"><strong> love arcades</strong></a>; what else needs to be said?</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Hershey's Chocolate World</strong>: If you have a family member who just LOVES Hershey's chocolate, then this place is a must-see. Chocolate World is a two-story shop that features everything Hershey's, including an interactive retail experience where you can make the unique Hershey treat of your dreams. It is a fun, <a href="/free-family-activities-las-vegas/" target="_blank" data-type="post" data-id="9291" rel="noreferrer noopener"><strong>free Vegas activity</strong></a>, even if you just want to snap some unique pictures with a giant Hershey kiss.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>The Park:</strong>&nbsp;The Park is located in a beautiful tree-filled space between NYNY and Monte Carlo and stretches all the way back to the T-Mobile Arena. There is a 40-foot statue, park benches, and design features reminiscent of Central Park in New York. There are a host of kid-friendly restaurants serving killer food and drink all along the park, so you feel completely transported to the Big Apple.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:stackable/heading {"uniqueId":"266ad24","textTag":"h4","showBottomLine":true,"bottomLineColor":"#f60d0d"} -->
<div class="wp-block-stackable-heading stk-block-heading stk-block-heading--v2 stk-block stk-266ad24" id="family-friendly-restaurants-at-nyny" data-block-id="266ad24"><style>.stk-266ad24 .stk-block-heading__bottom-line{background-color:#f60d0d !important;}</style><h4 class="stk-block-heading__text">Family-Friendly Restaurants at NYNY</h4><div class="stk-block-heading__bottom-line"></div></div>
<!-- /wp:stackable/heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>There are lots of affordable, family-friendly Vegas restaurants at the NYNY Hotel. While I appreciate that Vegas is a place for celebrity chef restaurants, we are not the type of family to eat at these expensive, fancy restaurants. This actually makes the dining options at NYNY perfect for families like ours.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Tom's Urban:&nbsp;</strong>Casual American dining in an eclectic modern space that has huge family-friendly circular booths. Tom's Urban is a mesh of American classics and world-inspired street food. They have creative craft cocktails and craft beers galore. Open for breakfast, lunch, or dinner from 7 am to 2 am daily.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Il Fornaio:&nbsp;</strong>Amazing Italian Cuisine at a family-friendly price point. We eat at Il Fornaio at least once every time we stay at NYNY. Lovely warm bread is served before the meal, and the pizzas and pastas are all fantastic. A great selection of wines too, this place has a nice ambiance located right along the casino floor in the heart of all the action. It is open for breakfast, lunch, and dinner, as well.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>America:&nbsp;</strong>This is a classic Vegas cafe and the perfect place to get eggs, hash browns, and a black cup of Joe in the morning. America has a rustic American road trip vibe to it and is cost-friendly. They have really good Bloody Marys to boot! America is open 24 hours a day, every day, ya'll.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Nine Fine Irishmen: </strong>It is so funny that this place is always crowded with visitors speaking in Irish accents. Like families actually travel from Ireland to Vegas and then plant themselves, of all places, in an Irish Pub. That is such an Irish thing to do (and I love it)! And I can't blame them. Love everything about this place for over a decade. Open until 11 pm Monday through Friday, the live music starts at 9 pm, but I recommend getting there a wee bit early to reserve a spot. Find out more about my <a href="/kid-friendly-live-music-las-vegas/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener"><strong>family-friendly Las Vegas restaurants with live music</strong></a>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Village Street Eateries:</strong>&nbsp;On more than one occasion, we have had a "second" nightcap at the Street eateries near the Times Square Bar in NYNY. What does this mean? Well, the kids are sometimes hungry, bordering on hangry, after spending the evening at the Irish Pub, and it is the promise of a slice of NY-style pizza that keeps them well behaved while we enjoy our stout.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>But seriously, this is a fun place to grab a cheap bite, pizza, hot dog, or NY deli-style sandwich, and a cheap beer, and sit in an area just on the outskirts of the casino floor. The kids feel like they are part of the fun and can experience the crazy live music from a safe distance, going on at the Times Square Bar.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":5} -->
<h5>Restaurants located specifically in "The Park"</h5>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Hello Kitty Cafe:</strong> This small pop-up cafe is located inside The Park near the T-Mobile Arena and is designed with the signature Hello Kitty aesthetic, featuring pink and white decor and Hello Kitty-themed treats and merchandise. The menu includes a variety of sweet treats like macarons, cupcakes, and cookies, as well as coffee, tea, and other beverages.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Customers can also purchase Hello Kitty-themed merchandise like plush toys, keychains, and t-shirts. The cafe is a popular spot for fans of Hello Kitty and is a fun and unique destination for a sweet treat or a quick snack in Las Vegas.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Shake Shack: </strong>This popular fast-food restaurant specializes in burgers, fries, and milkshakes. The menu features classic items like the ShackBurger and Crinkle-Cut Fries, as well as frozen custard and hand-spun milkshakes in a variety of flavors. The restaurant has a casual and laid-back atmosphere, making it a great spot for a quick and satisfying meal.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Beerhaus:&nbsp;</strong>Designed after classic beer halls, Beerhaus has a creative menu that will satisfy craft beer connoisseurs and casual drinkers alike, with a range of sausages and sandwiches to pair with delicious beers in an outdoor patio environment. Best of all, they have a daily happy hour from 2-6 pm with $4 pints and other specials! Count us in.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- /wp:stackable/heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>No, NYNY Hotel does not have the best pool or the best kid-friendly Vegas shows. What NYNY does have is a down-to-earth and fun party-like atmosphere that we have found to be super family-friendly. They have family-friendly (and budget-friendly) restaurants, a roller coaster and arcade, a park inspired by Central Park with unique attractions, and did I mention they have a fun Irish pub with free live music every night! Well, to me it was worth mentioning again.</p>
