<span style="color: #000000;">Overseas travel can seem a bit overwhelming, especially you have never traveled abroad, don’t do travel often or taking the kids for the first time. The more I travel the more I learn ways to make things easier for myself and my family. I get asked questions about overseas travel all the time and I have the answers you are looking for right here to make your next international trip a breeze.</span>
<h2><a href="/10-tips-for-overseas-travel/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><img class="aligncenter wp-image-3194" title="Keeping kids entertained during overseas travel" src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/08/IMG_5357-1024x1024.jpg" alt="Keeping kids entertained during overseas travel" width="640" height="640" /></a>
<span style="color: #000000;"><em><strong>1. Passports:</strong></em> </span></h2>
<span style="color: #000000;">You can’t go abroad without them.</span>
<ul>
 	<li><span style="color: #000000;">Apply for your passport early, even if you have no current plans for travel aboard. You never know when the opportunity to travel will arise and you don’t want to be stalled by unexpected setbacks.</span></li>
</ul>
<span style="color: #000000;">For example, My dad who is 81 years old didn’t realize, until he was trying to get his passport for Scotland, that he has never had the original birth certificate, which delayed the process of getting his passport by a couple weeks.</span>
<ul>
 	<li><span style="color: #000000;">Check passport validity rules for the country you want to enter. some countries require 6 months validity before entry, which is another reason having passports ready to go early is a must.</span></li>
</ul>
<ul>
 	<li><span style="color: #000000;">Get kids their passports while still under 16. Again, even if you don’t plan on traveling internationally, it is best to get it as soon as you can. First, it is cheaper and you’ll just have to pay to renew. Second, unexpected opportunities to travel may pop up and you’ll everything ready. For instance, I made a plan to get my kids passports in January as part of my New Year’s Resolution without any international travel plans. Coincidentally, HotDaddy got asked to work in Scotland in June and thankfully, we were all ready to go!</span></li>
</ul>
<a href="/wp-content/uploads/2015/08/1495386_801090953305017_3598390383881243936_o.jpg" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><img class="aligncenter wp-image-3181" title="International travel tips: Get your kids passports as young as possiple " src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/08/1495386_801090953305017_3598390383881243936_o-1024x1024.jpg" alt="International travel tips: Get your kids passports as young as possiple " width="640" height="640" /></a>
<ul>
 	<li><span style="color: #000000;">Ladies, listen up, because this affects us the most. So what happens if your name has changed and doesn’t match your passport? According to TSA, you’re the<strong> name on your flight ticket MUST match the name on your passport</strong>. So, if you recently got married and changed your name, book your flight with the name that is on your passport!! Travel with a copy of your marriage license just in case.</span></li>
</ul>
<h2><span style="color: #000000;"><em><strong>2. International Flights are Awesome:</strong></em> </span></h2>
<span style="color: #000000;">I love international flights, because there are a lot of perks.</span>
<ul>
 	<li><span style="color: #000000;">You don’t need to be first class to get complimentary meals, snacks, beer and wine.</span></li>
</ul>
<a href="/wp-content/uploads/2015/08/Complimentary-Drinks.png" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><img class="aligncenter wp-image-3186" title="Complimentary Drinks on Transatlantic Flights" src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/08/Complimentary-Drinks.png" alt="Complimentary Drinks on Transatlantic Flights" width="640" height="640" /></a>
<ul>
 	<li><span style="color: #000000;">Kids and parents stay entertained with free games and movies.</span></li>
</ul>
<a href="/10-tips-for-overseas-travel/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><img class="aligncenter wp-image-3187" title="Free games and movies on transatlantic flights" src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/08/IMG_6460-1024x768.jpg" alt="Free games and movies on transatlantic flights" width="640" height="480" /></a>
<ul>
 	<li><span style="color: #000000;">Transatlantic flights are smooth! I have a fear of flying and any turbulence even minor bumps freak me out. So, I was pleasantly surprised when I discovered that transatlantic flights are typically very smooth.</span></li>
</ul>
<ul>
 	<li><span style="color: #000000;">Unless you love nonstop, break up the flight halfway with a decent layover, especially when traveling with kids. We travel to Europe from California and we love breaking up the flight in Newark, NJ. It just makes the flights seem less daunting and gives the kids a chance to run around and burn off energy. See more tips on flying with kids, see our post <strong><a href="/flying-with-kids/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><span style="color: #ff0000;">HERE</span></a></strong>.
</span></li>
</ul>
<a href="/10-tips-for-overseas-travel/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><img class="aligncenter wp-image-3188" title="Layovers are great for burning energy" src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/08/IMG_0242-768x1024.jpg" alt="Layovers are great for burning energy" width="640" height="853" /></a>
<ul>
 	<li><span style="color: #000000;">Checked bags are free!</span></li>
</ul>
<ul>
 	<li><span style="color: #000000;">Prepare for landing card. When you land in an international country you will be asked to fill out a landing card. Have a pen on you and have your travel information ready such as where you will be staying and the contact information.</span></li>
</ul>
<h2><span style="color: #000000;"><em><strong> 3. Avoid carrying on too much:</strong></em></span></h2>
<span style="color: #000000;"> I’m guilty of not following my own advice all the time, especially when traveling with kids. It is a pain in the butt to haul a bunch of stuff with you while trying to navigate through airports and planes, but it is hard to avoid with kids. They need stuff. <strong>A lot of stuff.</strong></span>
<ul>
 	<li><span style="color: #000000;">Try to consolidate as much as you can. If your child is old enough, let them carry their own backpack (although, I still get stuck carrying those too).</span></li>
</ul>
<ul>
 	<li><span style="color: #000000;">Also, try not to bring back too many souvenirs. Grams went a little crazy on our last trip and bought a bunch a fragile stuff she had to carry on and it was ridiculous</span></li>
</ul>
<h2><span style="color: #000000;"><em><strong>4. Credit Cards:</strong></em></span></h2>
<ul>
 	<li><span style="color: #000000;">Get a good travel rewards credit card. This can save you foreign transaction fees and earn you rewards for money you are already spending. Plus it is nice to have some extra backup when abroad.</span></li>
</ul>
<ul>
 	<li><span style="color: #000000;">Although most credit cards are accepted in most places, double check. For example, AMEX is not as widely accepted in the UK. I lucked out in Scotland, because most restaurants did accept my favorite travel rewards card.</span></li>
</ul>
<ul>
 	<li><span style="color: #000000;">Don’t exchange money at home bank or departing airport. Instead, just wait and withdraw cash at the ATM when you land at your desired destination. You will need cash for taxis and tipping.</span></li>
</ul>
<a href="/10-tips-for-overseas-travel/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><img class="aligncenter wp-image-3189" title="Withdraw new currency at ATMs" src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/08/IMG_6473-1024x1024.jpg" alt="Withdraw new currency at ATMs" width="640" height="640" /></a>
<ul>
 	<li><span style="color: #000000;">Get a credit card with NO foreign exchange fee. This will save you money and we like to save money.</span></li>
</ul>
<ul>
 	<li><span style="color: #000000;">Find out if your bank card has partners abroad and use those ATMs when traveling, avoiding more fees. YAY!</span></li>
</ul>

<h2><span style="color: #000000;"><em><strong>5. Don’t Skip Travel Insurance!</strong></em></span></h2>
<ul>
 	<li><span style="color: #000000;">It may seem like an unnecessary expense, but it is minor compared to having an emergency.</span> <span style="color: #000000;">This is perfect example as to way it is important to be safe rather than sorry. Plus the cost isn’t that crazy. We insured our whole family of 6 for $150 with AAA. That was including Grams and Papa and the kids were free with adults.</span></li>
</ul>
<h2><span style="color: #000000;"><em><strong>6. Learn Simple Phrases:</strong></em> </span></h2>
<span style="color: #000000;">Going to some place where they speak a language that is different than your own? Great! Learn some simple phrases before you go.</span>
<ul>
 	<li><span style="color: #000000;">It shows respect for another culture and is usually met with positive reactions from locals.</span></li>
</ul>
<ul>
 	<li><span style="color: #000000;">Learning a little of the language builds confidence as a traveler. You will feel more comfortable in your new surrounding if you don’t feel completely lost in conversation. Even being able to ask for directions or where the restrooms are is a major confidence boost.</span></li>
</ul>

<h2><span style="color: #000000;"><em><strong>7. Important Documents:</strong></em></span></h2>
<ul>
 	<li><span style="color: #000000;">For security purposes always travel with copies of your important documents. I make copies of everything, kids’ birth certificates, marriage license, passports, I.D cards, etc. Anything I might need while out of the country.</span></li>
</ul>
<ul>
 	<li><span style="color: #000000;">Make a copy of your passport for sure! Use the copy while out exploring and leave the original somewhere safe. You don’t want you original documents stolen or lost.</span></li>
</ul>
<ul>
 	<li><span style="color: #000000;">I also print copies of travel confirmations, just in case something happens to my phone and I can’t bring up my email. Also, consider keeping documents safe by wearing a secure <span style="color: #ff0000;"><strong><a style="color: #ff0000;" title="5 Best Women’s Travel Vest for Safer Travel: Reviews and Buyer’s Guide" href="/best-womens-travel-vest/" target="_blank" rel="nofollow noopener noreferrer">travel vest</a> </strong></span>with hidden pockets.</span></li>
</ul>

<h2><span style="color: #000000;"><em><strong> 9. Be Aware of Advisories:</strong></em> </span></h2>
<span style="color: #000000;">Be a smart traveler and know the situation you might be entering by doing your research.</span>
<ul>
 	<li><span style="color: #000000;">Know what shots are required before entering a country.</span></li>
</ul>
<ul>
 	<li><span style="color: #000000;">Find out what Visas are required before entering a country.</span></li>
</ul>
<ul>
 	<li><span style="color: #000000;">Understand what the political situation is in the country you are planning to visit. For example, I have an alert program for my phone and it warned me about Anti-austerity protests in Glasgow and Edinburgh being held on a certain day. I was able to avoid crowds and potential chaos with this alert and planned my itinerary that day to be outside the major cities.</span></li>
</ul>
