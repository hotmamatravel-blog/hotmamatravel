There is no reason that you should let go of this task when it comes to traveling because there is absolutely no need! #### Rest When You Need It

First, you need to make sure that you are [resting when you need it](https://www.solosophie.com/how-to-sleep-well-while-travelling/). Traveling can be exhausting, and if you are not careful then you are going to end up burning yourself out. This is a problem for a multitude of reasons, the first being that it’s going to ruin your travel adventure as you’re going to be too tired to do anything but sleep, the second is that it’s really bad for your health. Your body needs to rest, and that means that you have to give it sufficient time to do so.

You’re not going to miss out on anything because you allowed yourself to sleep through the night, or take an hour nap in the day. If your body is tired, let it rest.

#### Don’t Forget Your Self Care Routine

Just because you are out and about, doesn’t mean that you have to forget [your self care routine](/self-care-when-traveling/). You should be keeping up with this as much as you can to the best of your ability, making sure that you are in the best possible condition. This means things like skin care, taking the time for your mental health, ensuring that you’re getting enough exercise and not only consuming food that is bad for you. All of these things are important, so you should be making time for them.

#### Remember To Try New Things Sometimes

Last but not least, sometimes it’s nice to try new things! If you are struggling with something like chronic pain or stress, then you may be struggling to manage it effectively. It’s okay, it happens to lots of people, but there are so many things that you can try and it’s worth looking into them all until you find the one that works for you. For example, some people swear by [cbd oil](https://www.thecbdistillery.com/), while others swear by certain types of tea. It’s a trial and error situation, but eventually you will come across what you’re looking for! Be patient and be willing to try new things!
