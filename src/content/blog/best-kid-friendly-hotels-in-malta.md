<p class="has-drop-cap">Malta has a fascinating culture and history dating back 7,000 years. Through various transformative chapters in its history, there are now so many amazing sights to see and experiences to dive into that it has become a very popular tourist destination.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>So, if you’re headed to<a href="/malta-with-kids/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener"> Malta with the kids</a>, you’ll be glad to know there are tons of attractions that are unique, exciting, and educational. In the City of Valletta alone, you and your children can check out a variety of museums and historic structures, ride the Malta Fun Trains, or go paddleboarding. As for where you’ll be staying, there are great hotels in the country that are very kid-friendly. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":4} -->
<h4 id="medieval-times"><a href="/go/westin-dragonara-resort-malta/" linkid="16512">The Westin Dragonara Resort</a></h4>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><em> Dragonara Road St. Julian's, STJ 3143, Malta</em></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The Westin Dragonara Resort is quite popular for being a five-star option that is very family-friendly. Kids of all ages will enjoy the variety of activities offered here, with snorkeling, scuba, water skiing, and bicycle rentals readily available.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>While parents can enjoy the casino and explore the boutique shops, there is also a kid’s club that ensures your younger children will have something to occupy their time in a fun and safe environment.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>There are also lots of diving courses available for both kids and adults if you’re feeling a little adventurous. Shallow reefs are available for kids to enjoy with trained guides, while experienced adults can explore more exciting locations and depths like <a href="https://maltadives.com/sites/hmshellespont/en" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">the haunting Hellespont Wreck</a>. It’s been underwater for more than two decades, but the wreck is still largely intact.</p>

<!-- wp:heading {"level":4} -->
<h4 id="medieval-times"><a href="/go/db-seabank-resort-spa-malta/" linkid="16517">Db Seabank Resort and Spa</a></h4>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><em> Marfa Road, Il-Mellieħa MLH 9064, Malta</em></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The big clincher for the db Seabank Resort and Spa is their special offer that one child stays free if you book directly through their website using Book Direct. The hotel manages to take grown-up events and create great kiddie versions that children can enjoy. One major highlight is the evening kids’ disco where they can dance and enjoy some kid-friendly tunes.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Aside from having a kid’s club to hang out in, there is also a playhouse, bowling alley, and a shallow water beach. These are great options for kids that really want to enjoy the sea without parents having to worry about high tides and sudden drops. Finally, there’s also a lagoon pool and a splash pool with a pirate ship that children absolutely love.</p>

<!-- wp:heading {"level":4} -->
<h4 id="medieval-times"><a href="/go/labranda-riviera-hotel-spa-malta/" linkid="16518">LABRANDA Riviera Hotel &amp; Spa</a></h4>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><em> Marfa Bay, Mellieha, Malta</em></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Labranda is a generally kid-friendly hotel with the basic amenities you’d expect. An interesting offer they have is free accommodation for children celebrating their first holy communion and confirmation. They even provide a complimentary cake for groups of ten and above.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Arguably the real highlight for kids, though, is the fact that it is just moments away from <a href="https://random-times.com/2020/09/24/popeye-village-an-abandoned-set-from-the-1980-film-claimed-and-repurposed-as-a-theme-park-by-creative-locals/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">the iconic Popeye Village</a> in Anchor Bay. What started as a film set got abandoned and repurposed into a makeshift town-slash-tourist-haven. Kids will enjoy the old sets and likely have a ton of fun with the different on-site attractions, including puppet shows, rides, seasonal performances, mini-golf, playhouses, and popcorn.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<h4 id="medieval-times"><a href="/go/the-suites-piazza-kirkop-malta/" linkid="16521">The Suites - Piazza Kirkop</a></h4>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><em> The Suites Piazza Kirkop, Misrah San Anard Hal Kirkop, KKP 1051, Malta</em></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The Suites are very welcoming and accessible, with strictly non-smoking premises that are definitely safe for kids. They also offer complimentary cribs if needed.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>That said, the real reason to go to this hotel if you have kids is its proximity to sightseeing hotspots. It is close to some of the <a href="https://www.loveholidays.com/holidays/best-cultural-locations-in-malta/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">best cultural destinations in Malta</a>. There’s something for every child, so you can take them to the National Museum of Archeology, explore the Blue Grotto, check out the Tarxien Temples or even visit the Tomasina Cat Sanctuary.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<h4 id="medieval-times"><a href="/go/corinthia-st-george-malta/" linkid="16523">Corinthia St. George’s Bay</a></h4>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><em> St Georges Bay, St Julians STJ 3301, Malta</em></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>This particular hotel is very popular among traveling families. They have a children’s program with a vast range of activities, an outdoor kid’s pool, a watersports center, a kids’ spa menu, and even a babysitting service for those times you want to go off and check out the nearby bars or restaurants.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Corinthia also has lots of complimentary items with children in mind, like toddler pillows, nightlights, pushchairs, potties, nappy bags, playpens, and socket covers. You can even purchase a stuffed Corinthia bear.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>From here, you can drive over to Mdina and visit <a href="https://www.telegraph.co.uk/travel/lists/game-of-thrones-filming-locations-in-pictures/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">the fortress city used in Game of Thrones</a>. Even if this may be a reference for older kids, even younger ones will be enamored by the ancient and almost magical feel of the place.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The real beauty of Malta is that you can have a completely relaxed vacation while also spending time discovering cool sites with the kids. Due to its immense popularity, it has actually become one of the top 20 holiday destinations in Europe, so you know you’re in for a real treat.</p>
