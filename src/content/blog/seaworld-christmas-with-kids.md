<p class="has-drop-cap">It is Christmas time in San Diego, and what better way to celebrate the holidays by the sea than by hanging out with some of the ocean’s most awesome wildlife friends at SeaWorld? It is a coastal Christmas complete with whales, dolphins, sharks, sea turtles, and Santa, of course. We may not have blizzards here in Southern California, but we sure know how to do the holidays! So if you are looking for a <strong><a href="/christmas-getaways-for-families/" target="_blank" data-type="page" data-id="19806" rel="noreferrer noopener">family holiday adventure</a></strong>, you will love the exciting activities at SeaWorld Christmas Celebration!</p>

<!-- /wp:ugb/heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Pass larger-than-life Christmas trees as you enter a whimsical little village with various cottages, offering tons of holiday fun. The haunting scents of holiday treats waft through the air as the cheerful sounds of live music make you bounce to the beat.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>What a warm pie or comforting mac n’ cheese? Stop by the first cottage, where that looks it was crafted from pie crust.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Maybe a cup of coffee and a fluffy waffle? Cottage number two has what you need.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>In the middle is Santa’s Cottage, where kids line up to see the main man. Funny elves roam around the village, entertaining the kids as they patiently wait to get inside. The cottage is wonderfully decorated as any cozy cabin should be, with a roaring fire, overstuffed chair, milk and cookies displayed on a vintage globe mini bar, as well as fun little trinkets scattered about the room.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:ugb/heading {"showSubtitle":false,"showBottomLine":true,"bottomLineColor":"#ff0000","uniqueClass":"ugb-f329d4a","contentAlign":"center"} -->
<div class="wp-block-ugb-heading ugb-heading ugb-f329d4a ugb-main-block"><style>.ugb-f329d4a .ugb-heading__title{text-align:center}.ugb-f329d4a .ugb-heading__bottom-line{background-color:#ff0000 !important;margin-left:auto !important;margin-right:auto !important}.ugb-f329d4a .ugb-inner-block{text-align:center}</style><div class="ugb-inner-block"><div class="ugb-block-content"><h2 class="ugb-heading__title">Meet Santa's Reindeers</h2><div class="ugb-heading__bottom-line"></div></div></div></div>
<!-- /wp:ugb/heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>One of the sweetest surprises at <a href="https://seaworld.com/san-diego/events/christmas/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">SeaWorld’s Christmas Celebration</a> is getting to meet Santa’s very own reindeer up close. The kids absolutely <em>light up</em> when they spot those fluffy noses, cozy winter coats, and antlers straight out of a storybook. It feels like you’ve stepped right into the North Pole, minus the frostbite. The reindeer handlers share fun facts, the photo ops are adorable, and the whole experience adds that perfect touch of Christmas wonder to your day. Trust me, your little ones will walk away fully convinced they just met Santa’s A-team.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:ugb/heading {"showSubtitle":false,"showBottomLine":true,"bottomLineColor":"#ff0000","uniqueClass":"ugb-098c953","contentAlign":"center"} -->
<div class="wp-block-ugb-heading ugb-heading ugb-098c953 ugb-main-block"><style>.ugb-098c953 .ugb-heading__title{text-align:center}.ugb-098c953 .ugb-heading__bottom-line{background-color:#ff0000 !important;margin-left:auto !important;margin-right:auto !important}.ugb-098c953 .ugb-inner-block{text-align:center}</style><div class="ugb-inner-block"><div class="ugb-block-content"><h2 class="ugb-heading__title">Meet Rudolph!</h2><div class="ugb-heading__bottom-line"></div></div></div></div>
<!-- /wp:ugb/heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Over in the Holly Jolly Marketplace, the star of the season is waiting, Rudolph the Red-Nosed Reindeer himself! The kids practically sprint when they spot that glowing red nose and jolly grin. The meet-and-greet is pure Christmas magic, complete with big hugs, adorable poses, and a photo op that’s basically guaranteed to land on your holiday cards. It’s cheerful, it’s cute, and it’s one of those SeaWorld moments that sticks.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:ugb/heading {"showSubtitle":false,"showBottomLine":true,"bottomLineColor":"#ff0000","uniqueClass":"ugb-2f59e0f","contentAlign":"center"} -->
<div class="wp-block-ugb-heading ugb-heading ugb-2f59e0f ugb-main-block"><style>.ugb-2f59e0f .ugb-heading__title{text-align:center}.ugb-2f59e0f .ugb-heading__bottom-line{background-color:#ff0000 !important;margin-left:auto !important;margin-right:auto !important}.ugb-2f59e0f .ugb-inner-block{text-align:center}</style><div class="ugb-inner-block"><div class="ugb-block-content"><h2 class="ugb-heading__title">Holiday Shows At SeaWorld</h2><div class="ugb-heading__bottom-line"></div></div></div></div>
<!-- /wp:ugb/heading -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>Clyde &amp; Seamore’s Christmas Special</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Clyde &amp; Seamore’s Christmas Special is an absolute riot. SeaWorld’s prank-loving sea lions team up with their trainers (and an adorable otter cameo, of course) for a holiday show packed with laughs. It’s silly, it's interactive, it’s festive, and it’s honestly one of our kids’ all-time favorites. We never miss it, and you shouldn’t either!</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>Toymaker Trouble with Mrs. Claus and the Elves</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Toymaker Trouble is a cute and spirited holiday show where things at the North Pole aren’t going quite as planned. Santa’s elves are hustling to finish the last batch of toys, but there’s a snag. Sparky, the head Toymaker Elf, is running dangerously low on Christmas spirit. Since that magic is basically the secret sauce to Santa’s whole operation, Mrs. Claus steps in with a couple of her top elves to help Sparky rediscover the joy, heart, and sparkle that make Christmas… well, Christmas. It’s sweet, funny, and full of holiday charm.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>Jingle Squad</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Catch the Jingle Squad in Holiday Plaza, where this lively trio turns classic holiday music into a full-on festive party. Their upbeat tunes and cheerful energy make it impossible not to smile, consider it your instant dose of Christmas spirit.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>A Stocking Full of Soul</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Stocking Full of Soul at the Nautilus Amphitheater brings the holidays to life with a soulful, high-energy twist that we absolutely love. The music is fantastic, the performers are electric, and the whole show is packed with surprises, including magical snowfall that makes the crowd go wild. It’s interactive too, so the kids can dance, sing, and jump right into the holiday spirit. This production is a must-see and one of our family’s favorites every year.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>Happy Harbor Holiday</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Happy Harbor Holiday at Mission Bay Theater is all about big friendship vibes and festive fun with our guy, Sir Winston the Walrus. This show is perfect for the littles. It's interactive, super sweet, and totally indoor (aka clutch for those chilly nights). And yes… it <em>usually</em> snows inside. Because why not? Kids absolutely eat this one up.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>Mrs. Claus' Christmas Parade</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>What's a Christmas celebration without a parade? During SeaWorld's Christmas Celebration, you can catch Santa, Mrs. Claus, and all their helpers pump up the holiday spirit as they parade through the park. The music plays, the crowds cheer, and it is just a really good time. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>Donner and Blitzen’s Lazer Light Show</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Indulge in the Christmas spirit as you submerge under a canopy of laser lights set to holiday music. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>30 ft. Tree Lighting</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>At the end of the day, don't miss the interactive tree lighting ceremony. It is a fun little show, complete with some of SeaWorld's favorite friends, music, and yes, snowfall. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>A Christmas Wish Fireworks Show</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>On select nights during the holiday season, through New Year's Eve, SeaWorld lights up the sky with a brilliant fireworks display above the sea. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:ugb/heading {"showSubtitle":false,"showBottomLine":true,"bottomLineColor":"#ff0000","uniqueClass":"ugb-9381f65","contentAlign":"center"} -->
<div class="wp-block-ugb-heading ugb-heading ugb-9381f65 ugb-main-block"><style>.ugb-9381f65 .ugb-heading__title{text-align:center}.ugb-9381f65 .ugb-heading__bottom-line{background-color:#ff0000 !important;margin-left:auto !important;margin-right:auto !important}.ugb-9381f65 .ugb-inner-block{text-align:center}</style><div class="ugb-inner-block"><div class="ugb-block-content"><h2 class="ugb-heading__title">Snowflake Seeker’s: A Christmas Quest!</h2><div class="ugb-heading__bottom-line"></div></div></div></div>
<!-- /wp:ugb/heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Kick off the fun by buying your scavenger hunt map at The Christmas Shoppe, then hit the park and track down all the bright, cheerful snowflakes hiding around SeaWorld. Once you’ve cracked the case and filled your map, swing back by The Christmas Shoppe to score your special prize. It’s cute, it’s festive, and kids get <em>way</em> into it.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:ugb/heading {"showSubtitle":false,"showBottomLine":true,"bottomLineColor":"#ff0000","uniqueClass":"ugb-40bf5cc","contentAlign":"center"} -->
<div class="wp-block-ugb-heading ugb-heading ugb-40bf5cc ugb-main-block"><style>.ugb-40bf5cc .ugb-heading__title{text-align:center}.ugb-40bf5cc .ugb-heading__bottom-line{background-color:#ff0000 !important;margin-left:auto !important;margin-right:auto !important}.ugb-40bf5cc .ugb-inner-block{text-align:center}</style><div class="ugb-inner-block"><div class="ugb-block-content"><h2 class="ugb-heading__title">Holiday Decor &amp; Lights!</h2><div class="ugb-heading__bottom-line"></div></div></div></div>
<!-- /wp:ugb/heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The magic of Christmas takes over SeaWorld during the holidays. Every inch of the park is lit up, especially on Coca-Cola Lane. Families can enjoy gorgeous photo ops and festive fun at the Coca-Cola Market, and of course, no Christmas celebration would be complete without nightly snowfall! </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:ugb/heading {"showSubtitle":false,"showBottomLine":true,"bottomLineColor":"#ff0000","uniqueClass":"ugb-7cf7ceb","contentAlign":"center"} -->
<div class="wp-block-ugb-heading ugb-heading ugb-7cf7ceb ugb-main-block"><style>.ugb-7cf7ceb .ugb-heading__title{text-align:center}.ugb-7cf7ceb .ugb-heading__bottom-line{background-color:#ff0000 !important;margin-left:auto !important;margin-right:auto !important}.ugb-7cf7ceb .ugb-inner-block{text-align:center}</style><div class="ugb-inner-block"><div class="ugb-block-content"><h2 class="ugb-heading__title">SeaWorld Christmas Shops &amp; Merch</h2><div class="ugb-heading__bottom-line"></div></div></div></div>
<!-- /wp:ugb/heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Love a good festive souvenir? You will be overwhelmed by all the darling merch and holiday finds at the Christmas Shoppe and Holly Jolly Marketplace. It is one of my favorite spots to hit up with my mom and sister first thing once we enter the park. The home decor is fabulous, the ornaments are amazing, and the holiday apparel and toys are so much fun. Happy shopping.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:ugb/heading {"showSubtitle":false,"showBottomLine":true,"bottomLineColor":"#ff0000","uniqueClass":"ugb-92e4e86","contentAlign":"center"} -->
<div class="wp-block-ugb-heading ugb-heading ugb-92e4e86 ugb-main-block"><style>.ugb-92e4e86 .ugb-heading__title{text-align:center}.ugb-92e4e86 .ugb-heading__bottom-line{background-color:#ff0000 !important;margin-left:auto !important;margin-right:auto !important}.ugb-92e4e86 .ugb-inner-block{text-align:center}</style><div class="ugb-inner-block"><div class="ugb-block-content"><h2 class="ugb-heading__title">Festive Treats, Eats &amp; Drinks</h2><div class="ugb-heading__bottom-line"></div></div></div></div>
<!-- /wp:ugb/heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>I am a sucker for seasonal treats and drinks, and SeaWorld during the holidays does not disappoint. Throughout the park, you will find a variety of festive craft beers, cocktails, mocktails, dishes, and treats. Don't count calories during your visit! Just enjoy all the yummy gifts of the season. For those 21 and over, stop by the Twinkle Tree Cafe for more holiday-themed cocktails</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:block {"ref":17013} /-->
