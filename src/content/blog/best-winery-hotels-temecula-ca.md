#### [Wilson Creek Winery Manor](/go/wilson-creek-manor-temecula-ca/)

*35250 Loma Ventosa Ln, Temecula, CA 92592*

Family Friendly&nbsp; -&nbsp; Whole House Available&nbsp; -&nbsp; 10 Bedrooms&nbsp; -&nbsp; 10 Bathrooms&nbsp; -&nbsp; Game Room&nbsp; -&nbsp; Outdoor Pool &amp; Hot Tub&nbsp;

Stay in an 11,000 square foot estate mansion at Wilson Creek Manor. Here, you will feel like royalty as you enter the manor through the Grand Salon, complete with a grand fireplace, 24-seated dining room table, two large seating areas, wet bar, chess table and poker table. There are 10 bedrooms and bathrooms, sleeping a total of 24 guests. Nine of the suites have a spa tub. There are five king beds, six queen beds, a double bed and a sleeper sofa. It makes for a great place to stay for a wedding, because the manor has a 1,600 square foot dressing room with beauty stations.&nbsp;

For entertainment, the Wilson Creek Manor has a game room with a pool table, ping pong, shuffleboard, foosball and board games. Outside you can enjoy a sparkling pool, hot tub, outdoor bar, several fire pits, seating areas, chaise lounges, horseshoe pits, gardens and large grassy areas for exploring and playing. For a unique experience, dine alfresco under party lights at the large vineyard table.

#### [Europa Village](/go/eupora-village-winery-temecula-ca/)

* 41150 Via Europa, Temecula, CA, 92591*

Family-Friendly&nbsp; - Hotel&nbsp; -&nbsp; Casitas&nbsp; -&nbsp; Onsite Restaurants&nbsp; -&nbsp; Spa&nbsp; - Shops&nbsp; -&nbsp; Live Music - Complimentary Breakfast&nbsp;&nbsp;

At Europa Village, you have two accommodation options, Bolero Casitas and The Inn. A mini collection of romantic European countries represented in Temecula, Europa Village has three themed wineries, Bolero (Spain), C’est La Vie (France) and Vienza (Italy) on one property. It is such a fun and family-friendly winery to explore that you may want more than just a day to enjoy.&nbsp;

The Bolero Casitas allow you to immerse yourself in the Spanish experience. Complete with king beds, vaulted ceilings and private patio. The Inn is set on the hill, overlooking the village. All king suites, the rooms at the inn have wonderful balconies and terraces overlooking the vineyard, cypress or courtyard. Your stay at The Inn includes complimentary breakfast at Bolero Restaurante.

#### [South Coast Winery](/go/south-coast-winery-temecula-ca/)

* 34843 Rancho California Rd, Temecula, CA 92591*

Family-Friendly&nbsp; -&nbsp; Hotel&nbsp; -&nbsp; Pool&nbsp; -&nbsp; Spa&nbsp; -&nbsp; Fitness Center&nbsp; -&nbsp; Fireplace&nbsp; -&nbsp; Vineyard Views&nbsp; -&nbsp; Private Patios&nbsp; -&nbsp; Onsite Restaurant&nbsp; -&nbsp; Events &amp; Activities

At South Coast Winery, you have the option of staying in a luxury villa or suite.&nbsp; The villas have private patios and hot tubs while the suites have double tubs and balconies. Both the villas and suites have fireplaces and mini fridges.&nbsp; For those staying with family, they do offer a deluxe two queen villa As a guest of South Coast Winery Resort, you have access to the fitness center, pool and hot tub area, onsite restaurant and spa services in addition to the winery.

#### [Inn at Churon Winery](/go/inn-churon-winery-temecula-ca/)

* 33233 Rancho California Rd, Temecula, CA 92591*

Guests 21 +&nbsp; -&nbsp; Fireplaces&nbsp; -&nbsp; Marble Bathtubs&nbsp; -&nbsp; Complimentary Breakfast&nbsp; -&nbsp; Complimentary Wine&nbsp; -&nbsp; Onsite Cafe

When you stay at the Inn at Churon Winery, you will like you have stepped out of Temecula and into France. Enjoy the luxuries of your suite decorated in an elegant French-style with views of the vineyard, fireplaces and oversized marble tubs made for two. Spend your day exploring the winery, tasting award-winning wines, taking in the countryside and enjoying nearby Temecula attractions. The Cafe Churon is open for breakfast and lunch, closing at 4:30 pm just before the winery closes at 5:00 pm, but you are surrounded by many great dining options in Temecula’s wine country for a lovely dinner out.&nbsp;&nbsp;

#### [Ponte Vineyard Inn](/go/ponte-vineyard-inn-temecula-ca/)

*35001 Rancho California Rd, Temecula, CA 92591*

Private Balconies or Patios&nbsp; -&nbsp; Palomar Mountain Views&nbsp; -&nbsp; AAA Four Diamond&nbsp; -&nbsp; Pool&nbsp; -&nbsp; 4 Onsite Restaurants&nbsp; -&nbsp; Spa Treatments&nbsp; -&nbsp; Fitness Center&nbsp; -&nbsp; Gift Shop

This 90 room boutique Inn in Temecula wine country makes for a great stay when visiting Temecula Wine Country. Choose between a king or double queen room or step it up to a deluxe or grand suite, either way you are in for a real treat at Ponte Vineyard Inn. Rooms are walking distance to three wineries and feature mini fridges, granite countertops, bath robes and slippers and lush linens and towels. Additionally, all guest rooms have either a covered private patio or balcony. The balconies have gorgeous views of the Palomar Mountains. Spa treatments to your room can also be arranged, At the resort, you will enjoy a semi-Olympic sized pool and hot tub, a fitness center, gift shop and four onsite restaurants, in addition to the winery.

#### [Domaine Chardonnay Vineyard](/go/domaine-chardonnay-vineyard-temecula-ca/)

* 33810 Madera De Playa Dr, Temecula, CA 92592*

Complimentary Continental Breakfast&nbsp; -&nbsp; Fireplaces&nbsp; -&nbsp; Private Patios&nbsp; -&nbsp; Vineyard Views&nbsp;

Set on a Chardonnay vineyard, Domaine Chardonnay is reminiscent of the Loire Valley in France. The Chapel Suite is designed to look like a French chapel set in a small town. Popular among newlyweds, this room features a king bed, a kitchenette, mini bar, fireplace, sparkling chandeliers, and a private backyard overlooking the vineyards. The Vineyard Suite has the same amenities, except features a California King and is decorated in a more modern style. The Barrel Room is the smaller of the three suites and does not have a fireplace, but does have the king bed, kitchenette, mini bar and private patio. All stays come with a complimentary continental breakfast served to your room.&nbsp;

* 34450 Rancho California Rd, Temecula, CA 92591*

Complimentary Continental Breakfast&nbsp; -&nbsp; Complimentary Bottle of Wine&nbsp; -&nbsp; Onsite Restaurant&nbsp; - Pool&nbsp; -&nbsp; Private Patios

There are two accommodation options at the Carter Estate, Bungalows and Vineyard Estate Suites. The Bungalows sleep two while the Carter Estate Suites can sleep up to four. All rooms come with complimentary continental breakfast for two, king beds,&nbsp; fireplaces, private patios, bath robes and wine refrigerators. Onsite, you can enjoy a dip in the beautiful pool and hot tub and a meal at Vineyard Grill. Wander the gorgeous winery grounds while stopping to experience wonderful wine tastings.
