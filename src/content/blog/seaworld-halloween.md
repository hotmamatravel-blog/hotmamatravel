<span style="color: #000000;">Halloween is great time of year to let loose and let your freak flag fly. I love dressing up, being silly and laughing our fears in the face. Although I do enjoy the creepy side to Halloween, it doesn’t have to be a scary holiday. In fact, there are many ways to celebrate this wonderful holiday in the fall. One way is to visit during the weekends for a SeaWorld Halloween Spooktacular in San Diego.</span>

<a href="/seaworld-halloween/"><img class="aligncenter wp-image-1454 size-large" title="SeaWorld Halloween" src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/10/DSCN7748-1024x683.jpg" alt="SeaWorld Halloween" width="640" height="427" /></a>
<h2><span style="color: #000000;"><em><strong>Keep Cost in Check:</strong></em></span></h2>
<span style="color: #000000;">Go for the annual pass if you're local. It really is the best deal and then you don’t have to pay again to visit for other holiday events held throughout the year. A great way to save is to buy day and get the rest of the year free. This year there was a special promotion going on, so when I went to upgrade my day pass to annual I received the following full year for the same price! Needless to say, I was pretty excited. A great deal is the two-year pass. <strong><a href="http://www.tkqlhce.com/click-8336032-11076152" target="_blank" rel="nofollow noopener noreferrer"><span style="color: #ff0000;">Check Deals</span></a></strong>.
</span>

<span style="color: #000000;">Traveling with kids and you are only able to visit once? Sometimes there are special events when kids are free. For example, this year kids were free in October with the purchase of a specific adult ticket. I made the mistake of buying an adult ticket from another source which didn’t work to get </span>Firecracker <span style="color: #000000;">in free. Oops. So, pay attention to any promotions that might be going on before you plan your trip. <a href="http://www.dpbolvw.net/click-8336032-12811606" target="_blank" rel="nofollow noopener noreferrer"><span style="color: #ff0000;"><strong>Get your Single Day Ticket</strong></span></a>.</span>
<h2><span style="color: #000000;"><em><strong>Drop Your Bags:</strong></em></span></h2>
<span style="color: #000000;">There are plenty of options for nearby accommodations, but I do have my favorites. I love Old Town San Diego, so whenever I plan a trip to SeaWorld I try to combine the visits. I prefer the Hacienda Hotel, because of the AAA discount and its convenient location to historical plaza.</span>

<span style="color: #000000;">If I’m RVing at Mission Bay RV Resort, I usually squeeze in a visit to SeaWorld. It is literally around the corner. During the month of October you can get really reasonable site rates at Mission Bay RV Resort, which makes for a very fun and affordable trip.</span>

<h2><span style="color: #000000;"><em><strong>Get Your Grub On:
</strong></em></span></h2>
<span style="color: #000000;">During the month of October there was a promotion that kids eat free for the dining with Shamu. A pretty pricey, but amazing dining experience, this was a really good deal. Not sure if this will be an annual promotion, but I’ll keeping my eyes peeled.</span>
<h2><span style="color: #000000;"><em><strong>Chug-A-Lug:</strong></em></span></h2>
<span style="color: #000000;">If you buy a beer, keep your glass. Beer refills are offered, saving about $4 a glass. SeaWorld offers a really nice selection of local craft brews too! I.P.A, Wreck Ally, Stone and much more! When you consume beer like we do, that’s a big savings. It is also the same with the souvenir soda cup. When you buy a cup soda refills are a $1. We save all our cups and remember to bring for every visit.</span>

<a href="/seaworld-halloween/" rel="attachment wp-att-4550"><img class="aligncenter size-large wp-image-4550" src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/10/IMG_6052-1024x1024.jpg" alt="SeaWorld Halloween" width="640" height="640" /></a>
<h2><span style="color: #000000;"><em><strong>Entertain Me:</strong></em></span></h2>
<span style="color: #000000;">Follow the music until you arrive at Jumbo Pink Octopus. Once you gaze upon the many colorful characters, kids and parents grooving to the beat you know you have arrived at the Halloween Spooktacular.</span>

<a href="/seaworld-halloween/"><img class="aligncenter wp-image-1456 size-large" title="SeaWorld Halloween" src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/10/DSCN7746-1024x683.jpg" alt="SeaWorld Halloween" width="640" height="427" /></a>

<span style="color: #000000;">Dance. Dance. Dance. As D.J Rock Candy blasts the hottest mixes. Although the kids are encouraged to come out and shake their tail feathers, I busted out a few moves myself.</span>

<span style="color: #000000;">In typical carnival fashion, there are various booths set up were kids can get yummy treats like popcorn or snow cones, paint pumpkins or play carnival style games.</span>

<a href="/wp-content/uploads/2014/10/spooktacular.png"><img class="aligncenter wp-image-1457" title="SeaWorld Halloween" src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/10/spooktacular.png" alt="SeaWorld Halloween" width="640" height="480" /></a><a href="/wp-content/uploads/2014/10/spooktacular2.png"><img class="aligncenter wp-image-1458" title="SeaWorld Halloween" src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/10/spooktacular2.png" alt="SeaWorld Halloween" width="640" height="480" /></a>

<span style="color: #000000;">There are also plenty of photo opportunities with all the crazy Halloween characters roaming around the party.</span>

<a href="/seaworld-halloween/"><img class="aligncenter wp-image-1459" title="SeaWorld Halloween" src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/10/photoop.png" alt="SeaWorld Halloween" width="640" height="480" /></a><a href="/seaworld-halloween/"><img class="aligncenter wp-image-1460" title="SeaWorld Halloween" src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/10/photoop2.png" alt="SeaWorld Halloween" width="640" height="480" /></a>

<span style="color: #000000;">Throughout the park there are various spots to gather more treats. Dress your kids up in costume and take them trick-or-treating during SeaWorld Halloween. I personally passed on this activity. Although I dressed my kids up in Halloween themed wear, full out costumes seemed uncomfortable for a full day at SeaWorld. Plus, the lines for trick-or-treating can get pretty long, which would not work well with my kids’ short attention spans. It is wildly popular though and adds to the holiday thrills for many.</span>

<span style="color: #000000;">The fun doesn’t stop there. Many of the attractions have hints of Halloween scattered throughout. My favorite is the Shark Encounter, which is spooky anyway, but against the haunting glow jack-o-lanterns it’s even better.</span>

<a href="/wp-content/uploads/2014/10/decor2.png"><img class="aligncenter wp-image-1461" title="SeaWorld Halloween" src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/10/decor2.png" alt="SeaWorld Halloween" width="640" height="480" /></a> <a href="/wp-content/uploads/2014/10/decor.png"><img class="aligncenter wp-image-1462" title="SeaWorld Halloween" src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/10/decor.png" alt="SeaWorld Halloween" width="640" height="480" /></a>

<span style="color: #000000;">Be sure to catch the show! Clyde and Seymour’s Spooky Adventure is a silly show, showcasing the talents of two very funny sea lions. Also, the Sesame Street Halloween Show is very musical and entertaining. </span>Cuddle Bear <span style="color: #000000;">danced throughout the whole performance. So grab a drink and your popcorn and enjoy the show.</span>

<a href="/wp-content/uploads/2014/10/shows.png"><img class="aligncenter wp-image-1463" title="SeaWorld Halloween" src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/10/shows.png" alt="SeaWorld Halloween" width="640" height="480" /></a>
<h1><span style="color: #000000;"><em><strong>That’s A Wrap:</strong></em></span></h1>
<span style="color: #000000;">A SeaWorld Halloween is definitely not a scary event, but rather a happy celebration of embracing our childish side, letting loose and having fun! SeaWorld’s Spooktacular starts at the end of September and runs every weekend until the end of October. Make sure you caught the detail about <strong>weekends only</strong>. Unlike Disneyland at Halloween (see post <span style="color: #ff0000;"><strong><a style="color: #ff0000;" href="/halloween-disneyland/">HERE</a></strong></span>), where holiday festivities continue throughout the week, SeaWorld Spooktacular is only on the weekends. I made this mistake when I brought my kids on a Monday and had to make a second trip on the weekend, because I got them so excited about Halloween party. Can't say I hated it. We love seeing all the sea life. Oh well. Live and learn, but now you can learn from my mistake. Happy haunting everyone!</span>
