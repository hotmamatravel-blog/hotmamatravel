### A Family Home

</div>

Back in 1853, the Mendocino Coast saw the arrival of Silas Coombs, a visionary whose actions would shape the course of the area's history. Coombs didn't just build a mill; he laid the foundation for what would become a vibrant logging town, igniting a fire of prosperity that would spread across the Little River region. Amidst his endeavors, he also crafted a family haven right where the heart of Little River Inn now beats.

In those early days, the Coombs family stood as pioneers on the Mendocino Coast, blazing trails for others to follow. The allure of logging and fishing drew intrepid souls to the rugged coast, with dreams of securing their fortunes in the industries and trades that thrived at the time. Their efforts painted the backdrop of a community that was bound by common goals and shared ambitions, etching the Coombs family and their legacy deeply into the tapestry of the region's history.

### Little River Inn Opens

</div>

In 1939, the Little River Inn entered a new chapter in its storied history with the visionary guidance of son-in-law, Ole Hervilla. Hervilla, an immigrant from Norway, saw the potential in the property and its captivating coastal surroundings. Drawing from his background in carpentry and a deep appreciation for hospitality, he embarked on a journey to transform the inn into a destination that would capture the essence of Mendocino's natural beauty and charm.

Under Ole's skilled craftsmanship and determination, the [Little River Inn](https://www.littleriverinn.com/) underwent a significant expansion and renovation, evolving from a modest roadhouse to a charming coastal retreat. The inn's legacy of family ownership continued as Ole's granddaughter, Cally Dym, and her husband, Marc, took the reins, ensuring that Ole's vision and passion were carried forward into the modern era. After 80 years, the Little River Inn still remains in the family.

Ole's legacy remains imprinted upon every corner of the Little River Inn, from the welcoming ambience to the scenic vistas that have drawn generations of travelers. His journey from Norway to the Mendocino Coast has left an indelible mark on the inn's history, transforming it into a cherished haven where families and adventurers alike can experience the enduring magic of the California coastline.

### The Story of the Abalone

</div>

You may notice a lot of mentions of Abalone. Here it the story behind this controversial shellfish. Ole Hervilla, the visionary behind the transformation of the Little River Inn, had a unique connection to the abalone industry in Mendocino. Ole's journey from Norway to the California coast led him to not only shape the inn's legacy but also intersect with the Abalone controversy that emerged in the region.

Abalone was a very popular dish at the Little River Inn, but commercial fishing and harvesting of Abalone became highly regulated due to population reduction. This caused a some big controversy for the time and some trouble for Ole. As a result Abalone is no longer on the menu, but I do wonder what an Abalone Burger must have tasted like.

### Little River Inn Dining Room

</div>

The restaurant at the Little River Inn is a culinary haven that captures the essence of Mendocino's coastal bounty and transforms it into an exquisite dining experience. Overlooking the mesmerizing Pacific Ocean, this dining establishment presents a feast for both the senses and the soul. Led by Executive Chef Marc Dym, the restaurant takes pride in showcasing locally sourced ingredients, allowing guests to savor the flavors of the region's abundant produce and seafood.

The restaurant offers a menu that harmoniously blends classic dishes with contemporary twists, resulting in a culinary journey that's both familiar and exciting. Whether you're indulging in delectable seafood offerings or savoring the creations that pay homage to the local terroir, each bite is a celebration of Mendocino's natural riches. Kiddos will love the selection of options on the kids' menu, but my kids LOVED the cheese plate.

From sunrise to sunset, the restaurant at the Little River Inn offers a culinary journey that mirrors the beauty of the surrounding landscapes, ensuring that every meal becomes a cherished memory etched in the tapestry of your Mendocino getaway.

### Ole's Whale Watch Bar

</div>

If you've followed our blog long, you know I love me a cute bar, especially if I can enjoy it with my kids. Ole's Whale Watch Bar is located just off the Little River Inn dining room and does serve food, so kids are welcomed inside. The name comes form the amazing ocean views, that stretch the length of the bar. There even certain times of the year that you can see whales making their migrations, hence the name of the bar. The nautical-themed decor, complete with maritime artifacts and vintage photos, creates an ambiance that invites you to step into a world of oceanic wonder.

<figure id="Oles-Whale-Watch-Bar-Little-River-Inn-kids"><a href="/images/Oles-Whale-Watch-Bar-Little-River-Inn-kids.jpg"><img src="/images/Oles-Whale-Watch-Bar-Little-River-Inn-kids.jpg" alt="Whale watch bar little river inn" /></a></figure>

The bartenders serve up delicious craft cocktails, including the Chef's Kiss made with Silas Coombs 5-year Rye. You can only get the Silas Coombs Rye at Ole's Whale Watch Bar, and I definitely recommend trying a dram. Also, only found at the bar is the exclusively brewed Saison ale made with candy-capped mushrooms. We tried it, and it was delightful, fruity, and fresh.

<figure id="Oles-Whale-Watch-Bar-Little-River-Inn-Cocktails"><a href="/images/Oles-Whale-Watch-Bar-Little-River-Inn-Cocktails.jpg"><img src="/images/Oles-Whale-Watch-Bar-Little-River-Inn-Cocktails.jpg" alt="Craft beer ole" /></a></figure>

On the bar snacks menu I highly recommend ordering the Cracklins. They are made in-house, seasoned to perfection, and served with hot sauce. Needless to say, we wolfed them down and ordered another round.

### Golf

</div>

The Little River Inn in Mendocino offers more than just breathtaking coastal views and comfortable accommodations; it also features its very own golf course, adding an extra layer of leisure and recreation to your stay. The Little River Inn Golf Course, established in 1957, is a nine-hole course that winds its way through the stunning landscapes of the Mendocino coast.

This family-friendly golf course welcomes golfers of all skill levels, from beginners looking to learn the sport to experienced players seeking a unique coastal challenge. The course's design incorporates the natural beauty of the surroundings, with ocean views, towering trees, and rolling fairways that create a scenic and enjoyable golfing experience.

Whether you're a seasoned golfer or a casual enthusiast, playing a round of golf at the Little River Inn Golf Course offers a chance to immerse yourself in the serene coastal atmosphere while enjoying a game that blends relaxation and recreation. And after a day on the greens, you can return to the inn to relish the comfort and charm that await you, creating a perfect balance between active pursuits and rejuvenating relaxation.
