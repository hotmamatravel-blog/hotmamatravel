<!-- wp:paragraph -->

Piff the Magic Dragon is actually a bit cheeky and requires a minimum age of 13. This comedy/magic show features a grown man in a dragon costume accompanied by his chihuahua, Mr. Piles. His sharp wit and clever comedy create nonstop laughs while the magic tricks will leave you and your teen in wonder!</p>

<!-- wp:heading {"level":4,"anchor":"medieval-times"} -->
<h4 id="medieval-times" class="wp-block-heading"><a linkid="13356" href="/go/real-bodies-ballys-las-vegas/">Real Bodies </a></h4>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><em> 3645 S Las Vegas Blvd, Suite A1-A3, Las Vegas, NV 89109 </em></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"dropCap":true} -->
<p class="has-drop-cap">What teen doesn't love a little shock value? This exhibit, at <a href="https://www.caesars.com/horseshoe-las-vegas" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Horseshoe Las Vegas</a>, is super intriguing, using REAL human specimens to showcase the complexity and, yes, even beauty of the human anatomy. There are 11 different galleries focusing on different aspects of the body. The “Breathe” exhibit gives you a deeper look into the respiratory system, while the “Love” exhibit teaches you the science behind physical attraction. You and your teen will not only find it hard to look away, but you will also gain a greater appreciation for your own being as you learn about the physical, cultural, historical, and emotional context of the human body.&nbsp;&nbsp;<br /></p>

<!-- wp:heading {"level":4,"anchor":"medieval-times"} -->
<h4 id="medieval-times" class="wp-block-heading"><a href="/go/las-vegas-mob-museum/" linkid="13347">Mob Museum</a></h4>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><em> 300 Stewart Ave, Las Vegas, NV 89101 </em></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"dropCap":true} -->
<p class="has-drop-cap">Located in Downtown Las Vegas, the Mob Museum takes you on an interactive journey through the shadows of the mob life. It is a darker side to American history and even Vegas history that I think teens as well as parents will find fascinating. Each floor has different exhibits that take you from the start of the mob to modern day headlines. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>There is even a cool underground floor that takes you back time to the prohibition-era when the mob thrived. The museum also has a crime lab and firearm simulator, so teens can learn enforcement methods for finding and bringing criminals to justice.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Parents, listen up! Now at the Mob Museum there is an underground speakeasy, The Underground. The speakeasy features specialty cocktails, live music and exciting events, like Moonshine Day, Ardbeg Scotch tastings, and more!</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p> <em>600 E Charleston Blvd, Las Vegas, NV 89104 </em></p>

<p class="has-drop-cap">Maybe I am alone in this, but especially as a teenager, the spookier, weirder, creepier something was the more I loved it! I think many teens find something captivating about a haunted museum. Many are familiar with Zak from his television show <a href="https://www.travelchannel.com/shows/ghost-adventures" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">“Ghost Adventures,”</a> which takes viewers on a spine-tingling journey to haunted places around the world. </p>

<p>Now you get the chance to experience all that is spooky right here in Las Vegas! The historic building, which houses the Haunted Museum, was built in 1938 and is also said to be haunted, in addition to the many objects it holds. As you wander through creepy, dark hallways and secret passages, you will find yourself surrounded by one fascinating artifact after another.</p>

<p class="has-drop-cap">Las Vegas is a giant playground for the young and old. Teens especially will find many of the attractions perfect for their thrill-seeking, high-adrenaline needs.  </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":4,"anchor":"medieval-times"} -->
<h4 id="medieval-times" class="wp-block-heading"><a href="/go/rio-voodoo-zipline-las-vegas/" linkid="13355">VooDoo Zipline</a></h4>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><em> 3700 W Flamingo Rd, Las Vegas, NV 89103 </em></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"dropCap":true} -->
<p class="has-drop-cap">I don’t know about you, but my kids LOVE ziplines. The VooDoo Zipline at the <a href="https://www.riolasvegas.com/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Rio</a> is not for the faint of heart. It soars over the city at 500 feet in the air going 33 miles per hour! Yeah, crazy. There are other ziplines in Las Vegas, but the VooDoo is the tallest and fastest in the city and you know teens are about being extreme. There is no age requirement during the day, but you do have to be over 21 after 7:30pm.</p>

<h4 id="medieval-times" class="wp-block-heading"><a href="/go/vegas-indoor-skydiving/" linkid="13354">Vegas Indoor Skydiving</a></h4>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><em> 200 Convention Center Dr, Las Vegas, NV 89109 </em></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"dropCap":true} -->
<p class="has-drop-cap">If your teen loves adrenaline rushes, then they might enjoy Vegas Indoor Skydiving. This is a body flight simulated skydive experience with the safety of a trampoline floor and padded walls. Teens will get to feel what it is like to freefall from an airplane through a vertical wind tunnel. The whole experience takes about an hour, but the rush lasts for hours.  </p>

<h4 id="medieval-times" class="wp-block-heading"><a href="/go/area-15-las-vegas-teens/" linkid="15282">AREA 15</a></h4>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><em> 3215 S Rancho Dr, Las Vegas, NV 89102</em> </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"dropCap":true} -->
<p class="has-drop-cap">This attraction just opened in 2020, and I am so excited about it! This is Meow Wolf’s latest immersive experience, and it is going to be awesome. We <a href="/tips-visiting-santa-fe-with-kids/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener"><strong>first experienced Meow Wolf</strong></a> at their House of Eternal Return in Santa Fe, and I can’t wait to see what they came up with for Area 15 in Las Vegas. This will be a retail and entertainment complex complete with art installations and interactive experiences. It will definitely be out of this world and something you should check out with your teenagers when it opens.</p>

<h4 id="medieval-times" class="wp-block-heading"><a href="/go/big-apple-coaster-nyny-las-vegas/" linkid="13357">The Big Apple Coaster &amp; Arcade</a></h4>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><em> 3790 S Las Vegas Blvd, Las Vegas, NV 89109 </em></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"dropCap":true} -->
<p class="has-drop-cap">This isn’t just any roller Coaster, this roller coaster soars above the Las Vegas Strip, surrounding the <a href="https://newyorknewyork.mgmresorts.com/en.html" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">New York New York Hotel &amp; Casino</a>. As if that isn't intense enough, you can upgrade your experience with virtual reality headsets that make you feel as if you are in a galaxy battle with Aliens. After you ride, your teen might be interested in hanging out at the attached arcade for some more fun and games. Plus, Vegas is home to many <a href="/best-arcades-in-las-vegas/" data-type="post" data-id="21049"><strong>awesome arcades</strong></a> that teens will love.</p>

<!-- wp:heading {"level":4,"anchor":"medieval-times"} -->
<h4 id="medieval-times" class="wp-block-heading">Vegas Pools</h4>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"dropCap":true} -->
<p class="has-drop-cap">During the day, the pool is the place to hang out in Las Vegas, especially during the summer. Who doesn’t love a good pool, right? Well, there are a ton of <a href="/best-family-pools-in-vegas/" data-type="post" data-id="13741" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener"><strong>fantastic family pools in Las Vegas</strong></a>, but I think teens will most enjoy the pools at <a linkid="8713" href="/go/mandalay-las-vegas-expedia/"><strong>Mandalay Bay</strong></a> and <a href="/go/nyny-hotel-las-vegas/" linkid="18434"><strong>New York New York</strong></a>. Teens will love the sandy beach-style pool at the Mandalay Bay, which has a wave pool, a lazy river and a lagoon.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The Mirage pool, which has always been my favorite pool all around, has a beautiful tropical setting complete with lagoons, bridges and cascading waterfalls. Keep in mind that you do have to be a guest of the hotel to use the pools, so you will want to look into this before booking your stay.</p>
