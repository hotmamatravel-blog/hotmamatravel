<div class="wp-block-uagb-advanced-heading uagb-block-42a30e40"><h3 class="uagb-heading-text">Update first</h3></div>
<!-- /wp:uagb/advanced-heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"dropCap":true} -->
<p class="has-drop-cap">Make sure all your gadgets are up to date. This includes your smartphone, tablet, and laptop. Updates often include crucial security patches that safeguard your devices from the latest threats. Cybercriminals are constantly searching for weaknesses in outdated software, which they can exploit to gain unauthorized access to your devices. By keeping your devices updated, you strengthen your first line of defense against these threats. I recommend not waiting until the final moment to perform this task. We've all had experiences where an update went awry, and that's the last thing you'd want to happen just before you set off.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->

<div class="wp-block-uagb-advanced-heading uagb-block-80a02b7c"><h3 class="uagb-heading-text">Secure your connections</h3></div>
<!-- /wp:uagb/advanced-heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"dropCap":true} -->
<p class="has-drop-cap">The Wi-Fi router in your home provides a protective shield, guarding individual devices from unwanted traffic. However, when you're on the go, employing a mobile firewall can`t effectively manage inbound and outbound traffic on your device.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Before you depart, it is advisable to disable auto-connect options and remove network SSIDs from device memory to prevent potential machine-in-the-middle attacks. Additionally, ensure Bluetooth is disabled when not in use to further enhance security. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

Not only this is important, but also choosing the right <a href="https://veepn.com/blog/how-to-set-up-vpn-on-iphone/">iPhone VPN</a> so that it can reliably protect against attacks. Our favorite is VeePN, it has strong encryption, tracking protection, support for all existing platforms, and connects up to 10 devices with one account.</p>

<div class="wp-block-uagb-advanced-heading uagb-block-b1b1340c"><h3 class="uagb-heading-text">Locks &amp; passwords</h3></div>
<!-- /wp:uagb/advanced-heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"dropCap":true} -->
<p class="has-drop-cap">Any security measures to protect your smartphone will be useless if it is notlocked. Enable one or more security measures: password, pattern, or FaceID. This is a basic security measure that is wise to use anywhere and anytime. It is logical that the password and pattern must be sufficiently reliable. Otherwise, your data can be stolen in just a couple of minutes, while you went to the toilet from the cafe.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->

<!-- /wp:paragraph --></div></div>

<div class="wp-block-uagb-advanced-heading uagb-block-4102308a"><h3 class="uagb-heading-text">Enable two-factor authentication</h3></div>
<!-- /wp:uagb/advanced-heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"dropCap":true} -->
<p class="has-drop-cap">Two-factor authentication (2FA) means that you need to go through several stages of authorization to access your accounts. If one of them is compromised, then the second level will contain the attacks and will not allow you to lose data or money. Most often, confirmation by mail, SMS, or call is used as the second stage. The downside is that you will have to spend a little more time logging in, but it's worth it.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->

<div class="wp-block-uagb-advanced-heading uagb-block-6ea480a9"><h3 class="uagb-heading-text">Beware of phishing</h3></div>
<!-- /wp:uagb/advanced-heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"dropCap":true} -->
<p class="has-drop-cap">Stay alert for any unsolicited emails or messages that request your personal details. Fraudsters frequently intensify their phishing attempts during peak travel periods by impersonating airlines or travel agencies in order to deceive you into divulging personal information. Prior to clicking on any links or sharing any information, it is advisable to confirm the sender's authenticity.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->

<div class="wp-block-uagb-advanced-heading uagb-block-6b23a699"><h3 class="uagb-heading-text">Location sharing</h3></div>
<!-- /wp:uagb/advanced-heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"dropCap":true} -->
<p class="has-drop-cap">Sharing photos and locations on social media while on vacation has become a popular practice among travelers. However, it's important to be mindful of potential risks. Cybercriminals can exploit this information to target your hotel rooms, home, or belongings. Always exercise caution when sharing on social media, particularly regarding your whereabouts.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->

<div class="wp-block-uagb-advanced-heading uagb-block-59e5818a"><h3 class="uagb-heading-text">Skimmer devices</h3></div>
<!-- /wp:uagb/advanced-heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"dropCap":true} -->
<p class="has-drop-cap">A skimmer device, employed by criminals, stealthily captures credit card information without any contact. Simply hovering this device over your card for seconds allows them to copy its data for their own gain. Even holding it over your wallet or pocket suffices. Embrace a solution by using an RFID wallet or cardholder to erect a digital barrier that thwarts data theft.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->

<div class="wp-block-uagb-advanced-heading uagb-block-0f21756e"><h3 class="uagb-heading-text">Turn on Find My device</h3></div>
<!-- /wp:uagb/advanced-heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"dropCap":true} -->
<p class="has-drop-cap">For added security, both Android and iOS provide features to track your device. Enable this option beforehand, enabling you to remotely wipe your device or leave a customized message on the screen in case it gets lost.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->

<div class="wp-block-uagb-advanced-heading uagb-block-476ae600"><h3 class="uagb-heading-text">Use a password manager</h3></div>
<!-- /wp:uagb/advanced-heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"dropCap":true} -->
<p class="has-drop-cap">Remember to bring along your trusty password manager and 2FA device. There's nothing more frustrating than going through multiple "I forgot my password" procedures. Speaking of passwords, it's essential to secure all your devices with a unique PIN or password.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<div class="wp-block-uagb-advanced-heading uagb-block-fe747128"><h3 class="uagb-heading-text">Back up your data</h3></div>
<!-- /wp:uagb/advanced-heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"dropCap":true} -->
<p class="has-drop-cap">Even before you travel, ensure the safety of your valuable data by backing it up. You can choose to safeguard your files and photos either on an external hard drive or through the convenience of a cloud service. By doing this, even if your device goes missing or falls into the wrong hands, you can still enjoy uninterrupted access to your critical information. Regular backups also act as a reliable shield from potential hazards.</p>
