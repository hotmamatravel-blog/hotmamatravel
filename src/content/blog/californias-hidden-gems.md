Palm Springs is an excellent option for stargazers, since there so many fun activities to enjoy during the say and starry skies by night. See our <a href="/palm-springs-with-kids/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">guide to visiting Palm Springs with kids</a> as well as the <a href="/best-family-resorts-palm-springs/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">best Palm Springs resorts for families</a> for the ultimate weekend getaway.
