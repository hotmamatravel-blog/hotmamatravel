<p class="has-drop-cap">Every season, we like to take our family on a holiday-inspired trip. One of our favorites is checking into the Christmas Wonderland that is the Apple Farm Inn. Here, we let our holiday spirit be filled with cheer. Sparkling lights drape over every surface, casting a warm glow, jumbo garland adorned with oversized ornaments frames the windows, doors, and staircase, and a fire roars in the lobby as guests are invited to enjoy complimentary cookies and refreshments. It truly is as breathtaking as it sounds, which is why we chose to spend our holiday vacation experiencing an Apple Farm Christmas. Come along, see what we saw!</p>

<p>Apple Farm San Luis Obispo  is not a bargain hotel; it is top-notch all the way, and the rates don’t ever really drop. Rooms always range from about $200 to $300 a night, but the best value is to book a package. Throughout the year, they offer a variety of seasonal packages that include additional activities, goodies, and dining/shopping credits with your room. Our favorite packages during the holidays for an Apple Farm Christmas. However, each year the holiday packages available are different, offering slightly different experiences.</p>

<p>The Apple Farm Restaurant San Luis Obispo is the restaurant at the inn and it is sooo yummy! We dined there both breakfast and dinner, because…why not? The atmosphere is cozy, the food is amazing, the drinks selections are great and guests are provided with dining credit. Oh, and during the holidays there is live Christmas music performed from 6:00 pm to 8:00 pm Friday and Saturday nights.</p>

That's just one of many daily specials going on, so be sure to check dates!</p>

<h3><strong>Welcome Drinks:</strong></h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Check in and get your sip on. Upon check-in, every guest is greeted with a welcome glass of Apple Farm wine. We love the Cabernet, so that’s what we enjoyed right from the start. While we adults decompressed after a long drive, enjoying the warm, cheerful décor and deep red wine, the kids had a blast exploring the lobby while sipping on hot cider and cocoa.</p>

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3><strong>Daily Wine Reception at Apple Farm:</strong></h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>More wine! Every night at the Apple Farm, guests are invited to the free welcome wine reception from 3:30 pm to 5:00 pm. This is a great chance to try a variety of wines from the local wineries. A table of little nibblers is set out to munch on with your wine. This is held in the Garden Room, which is located near the Trellis Court rooms.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>During the holidays there is usually a holiday movie playing throughout the reception to add to the Christmas cheer. There are also special holiday additives throughout the season to be aware of, like Candy Cane day or Mulled Wine Day.&nbsp;</p>
