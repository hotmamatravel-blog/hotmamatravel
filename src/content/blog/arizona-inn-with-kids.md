<span style="color: #000000;">85 years ago an extraordinary woman by the name of Isabella Greenway opened her simply fabulous inn in Tucson, Arizona. Surrounded by the comforts of home, yet equipped with all the luxuries of an extravagant resort, the Arizona Inn remains timeless. During the depression, the inn was a symbol of hope in dark times. Today, it continues stand to strong, rising out of the desert like an oasis, beckoning guests of all types to come, stay and play awhile.
</span>

<span style="color: #000000;">And beckon guests it does.</span>

<span style="color: #000000;">For decades the inn has attracted a number of famous guests, including (time for some name dropping) President F.D.R, Clark Gable (swoon), Gary Cooper, President Kennedy, the Rockefellers, Danny Kaye, John Wayne and Bettie Davis, just to name a few. The list goes on and on. Fancy names for a fancy place, but this is family travel blog and I haven’t forgotten about the kids. Still a family-owned business, Arizona Inn is classic glamour, but very family-friendly. </span>

So, come on and explore what it is like to stay at the <span style="color: #ff0000;"><strong><a style="color: #ff0000;" href="http://www.anrdoezrs.net/links/8336032/type/dlg/https://www.expedia.com/Tucson-Hotels-Arizona-Inn.h170100.Hotel-Information" target="_blank" rel="nofollow noopener noreferrer">Arizona Inn</a> </strong></span>with kids.</span>

<h2><span style="color: #000000;"><em><strong>Drop Your Bags:</strong></em></span></h2>
<span style="color: #000000;">The rooms! You can’t make me leave. I won’t, I just won’t! Immediately upon my arrival I felt like Orphan Annie visiting Daddy Warbucks’ estate for the first time. We squealed, my kids twirled, I sang, “I think I’m going to like it here!” I know. We are dramatic, but we are excitable and after a long day traveling with kids the luxuries of Arizona Inn were much appreciated.</span>

<span style="color: #000000;">There are three types of accommodations offered at Arizona Inn. There are rooms, suites and houses. We stayed in a Deluxe Room, which is the largest of the rooms. It had two queens, a pull-out sofa bed, a sitting area, wet bar and private patio and it was class all the way. Every detail is considered from the elegant furniture, to the romantically lit wet bar, to the pristine bathrooms and even the darling screens on the front and patio doors.</span>

<h3><span style="color: #000000;"><strong>Rates:</strong></span></h3>
<span style="color: #000000;">Like most hotels, rates fluctuate depending on time of year and day of week. Generally, off season (summer in AZ) offers the</span>
<span style="color: #000000;"> lowest rates, which start at $170 Deluxe Room. During peak season rates can start at $230 and above.</span>

<span style="color: #000000;">There are two houses on the property. The Breasted House is the former 1950’s home of Isabella’s daughter and family. This eight room house with a private pool cannot be booked online. So, to find out rates you will need to call the Arizona Inn directly for information.</span>

<span style="color: #000000;">House Six is a two-bedroom, two-bathroom house with front and back yards. This house can be booked online and can run anywhere from $500-$700 a night, depending on the season and time of week.</span>
<h2><span style="color: #000000;"><em><strong>Get You Grub On</strong></em></span></h2>
<span style="color: #000000;">There are several places to dine at Arizona Inn, but the menu remains the same throughout the resort with slight variations depending on time of day.</span>
<h3><span style="color: #000000;"><strong>Main Dining Room:</strong></span></h3>
<span style="color: #000000;"> Open of lunch and dinner, this dining room is both elegant and cozy. The tall cathedral ceilings, table cloths, warm woods, fireplace and romantic lighting come together to create a truly wonderful atmosphere. The Filet Mignon, French Onion Soup, Paella and Scallops were amazing!
