Temecula, California over the years has become quite a popular travel destination with increasingly more and more things to enjoy, such as [wineries](/kid-friendly-wineries-temecula-ca/), [breweries](/best-breweries-in-temecula-ca/)and a huge casino resort. No resort town is complete without a few premiere golf courses, am I right? Temecula actually has seven amazing golf courses that take advantage of the beautiful surrounding nature and give you a fun-filled day outdoors!

#### **[The Legends Golf Club &amp; Course (Temeku Hills Golf)](/go/legends-temeku-hills-golf-course-temecula-ca/)**

***41687 Temeku Dr, Temecula, CA 92591***

Designed by Ted Robinson, this 72 par, 18-hole course is 6,636 yards with a 131 slope on Bermuda grass. Golfers love that the course is challenging and affordable. The course features five lakes, several doglegs, rolling greens and challenging bunkers.

[See photos, reviews &amp; more](/go/legends-temeku-hills-golf-course-temecula-ca/)

#### **[Temecula Creek Inn, Oaks Cours](/go/temecula-creek-inn-golf-courses-temecula-ca/)e**

* **44501 Rainbow Canyon Rd, Temecula, CA 92592***

Also designed by Ted Robinson, this 9-hole course is over 3,436 yards with a par of 36 on Bermuda grass. Oak lined fairways make a very scenic landscape. This is a more traditional course that golfers love.

[See photos, reviews &amp; more](/go/temecula-creek-inn-golf-courses-temecula-ca/)

</div></section>

<section class="wp-block-uagb-section uagb-section__wrap uagb-section__background-undefined uagb-block-e2e26769"><div class="uagb-section__overlay"></div><div class="uagb-section__inner-wrap">
<figure id="temecula-creek-inn-golf-creek-course">[![](https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/temecula-creek-inn-golf-creek-course.jpg)](/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/temecula-creek-inn-golf-creek-course.jpg)</figure>

#### **[Temecula Creek Inn, Creek Course](/go/temecula-creek-inn-golf-courses-temecula-ca/)**

***44501 Rainbow Canyon Rd, Temecula, CA 92592***

This 9-hole, 36 par course is 3,348 yards on Bermuda grass. Designed by Ted Robinson, the Creek Course has an open layout allowing for direct approaches for the best shot at birdies. This course also features creeks, giving you a tranquil environment for a day on the green.

[See photos, reviews &amp; more](/go/temecula-creek-inn-golf-courses-temecula-ca/)

</div></section>

<section class="wp-block-uagb-section uagb-section__wrap uagb-section__background-undefined uagb-block-632cec7c"><div class="uagb-section__overlay"></div><div class="uagb-section__inner-wrap">
<figure id="temecula-creek-inn-golf-stone-house-course">[![](https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/temecula-creek-inn-golf-stone-house-course.jpg)](/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/temecula-creek-inn-golf-stone-house-course.jpg)</figure>

#### **[Temecula Creek Inn, Stonehouse Course](/go/temecula-creek-inn-golf-courses-temecula-ca/)**

* **44501 Rainbow Canyon Rd, Temecula, CA 92592***

The Stonehouse Course is set along Temecula Creek Inn’s historic cottage. This 9-hole, 36 par course is over 3,257 yards and designed by Ted Robinson. This is the most challenging course of the three courses at Temecula Creek Inn with demanding tee shots and dramatic changes in elevation and bends.

[See photos, reviews &amp; more](/go/temecula-creek-inn-golf-courses-temecula-ca/)

</div></section>

<figure id="redhawk-golf-course-temecula-ca">[![](https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/redhawk-golf-course-temecula-ca.jpg)](/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/redhawk-golf-course-temecula-ca.jpg)</figure>

#### **[RedHawk Golf Course](/go/redhawk-golf-course-temecula-ca/)**

* **45100 Redhawk Pkwy, Temecula, CA 92592***

Home to the famous “Island Green,” this 18-hole is&nbsp; over 7,180 yards with a par of 72 and 149 slope on Bermuda grass. Designed by Ron Fream and Mark Lamar, this course features dramatic elevation changes, an island green, amazing views and shot value.

[See photos, reviews &amp; more](/go/redhawk-golf-course-temecula-ca/)

</div></section>

<section class="wp-block-uagb-section uagb-section__wrap uagb-section__background-undefined uagb-block-86040ba7"><div class="uagb-section__overlay"></div><div class="uagb-section__inner-wrap">
<figure id="best-golf-course-temecula-journey-pechanga">[![](https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/best-golf-course-temecula-journey-pechanga.jpg)](/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/best-golf-course-temecula-journey-pechanga.jpg)</figure>

#### **[The Journey at Pechanga](/go/journey-golf-course-pechanga-temecula-ca/)**

* **45000 Pechanga Pkwy, Temecula, CA 92592***

The Journey at Pechanga is the highest rated golf course in the Temecula area. It also happens to be&nbsp; the longest golf course in Temecula at 7,219 yards. This 18-hole, 72 par course was designed by golf legend Arthur Hills. Set below the South Temecula hillside, the course is carved right out of the native landscape, lending to breathtaking views.

[See photos, reviews &amp; more](/go/journey-golf-course-pechanga-temecula-ca/)

</div></section>

#### [**Cross Creek Golf Club &amp; Cours**e](/go/cross-creek-golf-course-temecula-ca/)

* **43860 Glenn Meadows Rd, Temecula, CA 92590***

This pristine 18-hole, 71 par course is over 6,940 yards with a 142 slope on Rye grass. This course was designed by Arthur Hills with a secluded natural environment, close tight cuts on tee boxes and well defined aprons around the greens.

[See photos, reviews &amp; more](/go/cross-creek-golf-course-temecula-ca/)
