If you’re anything like me, you know that [**Las Vegas isn’t just for adults**](/las-vegas-with-kids/). This dazzling city is also a paradise for kids and the young at heart, especially when it comes to the amazing arcades found in some of the [**top family hotels**](/best-family-hotels-in-las-vegas/). As a lifelong Vegas visitor and a mom who’s navigated Sin City with babies, toddlers, kids, and teens, I’m here to share my insider knowledge on the best arcades in Las Vegas. So, let’s dive in and get ready to play!

### [The Big Apple Arcade at New York-New York Hotel &amp; Casino](/go/nyny-hotel-las-vegas/)

3790 S Las Vegas Blvd, Las Vegas, NV 89109
</div>

Imagine the excitement of New York City right in the heart of Las Vegas! The Big Apple Arcade at [New York-New York Hotel &amp; Casino](https://newyorknewyork.mgmresorts.com/en.html) is a must-visit. This vibrant arcade offers a mix of classic games and the latest interactive attractions. Whether you’re a fan of skee-ball, air hockey, or the newest video games, there’s something here for everyone. Plus, the adjoining Big Apple Coaster is a thrilling bonus for adrenaline junkies in the family. My littles love this arcade, and my teens love the coaster, often riding multiple times during a visit. There is usually a discount when you purchase multiple rides, too. Check out my list of [**cool things to do in Vegas with teens**](/things-to-do-in-las-vegas-with-teens/).

### [Fun Dungeon at Excalibur Hotel &amp; Casino](/go/excalibur-hotel-las-vegas/)

3850 S Las Vegas Blvd, Las Vegas, NV 89109
</div>

Step into a world of medieval fun at the Fun Dungeon inside [Excalibur](https://excalibur.mgmresorts.com/en.html). This arcade is packed with over 200 games, including carnival classics, video games, and redemption games. My kids love trying their hand at the claw machines and winning tickets to trade in for awesome prizes. The medieval theme adds a unique twist, making it a magical experience for younger children and a nostalgic trip for teens and parents.

In the Fun Dungeon, you will also find the entrance to the [**Tournament of Kings**](/go/tournament-kings-dinner-show-las-vegas/), one of the best [**family-friendly dinner and show packages**](/las-vegas-dinner-and-show-packages/) on the Strip. See my full list of awesome [**Las Vegas shows for families**](/las-vegas-shows-for-kids/) to up your entertainment game.

### [Midway at Circus Circus Hotel &amp; Casino](/go/circus-circus-show-las-vegas/)

2880 S Las Vegas Blvd, Las Vegas, NV 89109
</div>

If you’re looking for an arcade that doubles as a circus, head straight to the Midway at [Circus Circus](https://www.circuscircus.com/). This expansive arcade features a variety of games that will keep the entire family entertained for hours. What sets Midway apart are the [**free circus acts**](/free-family-activities-las-vegas/) that perform throughout the day. From acrobats to jugglers, there’s always something amazing to watch while you’re there. It’s a perfect blend of gaming and live entertainment that my family absolutely loves.

### [The Adventuredome at Circus Circus Hotel &amp; Casino](/go/circus-circus-show-las-vegas/)

2880 S Las Vegas Blvd, Las Vegas, NV 89109
</div>

Yes, Circus Circus makes the list twice, but for good reason! The Adventuredome, America’s largest indoor theme park, also houses an impressive arcade. In addition to thrilling rides and attractions, the arcade offers an array of games that guarantee fun for the entire family. It’s an all-in-one entertainment destination that’s hard to beat.

### [Arcade at Horseshoe](/go/horseshoe-las-vegas/)

3645 Las Vegas Blvd S, Las Vegas, NV 89109
</div>

Located in the [**heart of the Strip**](/walking-las-vegas-strip/), the arcade at [Horseshoe Las Vegas](https://www.caesars.com/horseshoe-las-vegas) is a hidden gem. This modern arcade offers a wide range of games, from vintage pinball machines to the latest virtual reality experiences. It's a perfect spot for families looking for a mix of nostalgia and cutting-edge fun. The atmosphere is vibrant and welcoming, making it a fantastic place for kids and parents alike to unwind and enjoy some quality gaming time together.

### [Hyper X Arena at Luxor](/go/the-luxor-hotel-las-vegas/)

3900 S Las Vegas Blvd, Las Vegas, NV 89119
</div>

Gamers, this one's for you! The HyperX Arena at [Luxor](https://luxor.mgmresorts.com/en.html) is an eSports paradise, offering an incredible gaming experience for both casual players and serious competitors. This state-of-the-art arena features high-end gaming PCs, consoles, and a massive LED video wall for live events and tournaments. It’s an exciting spot for teens and adults who are passionate about gaming and eSports. A great part is that it is a super [**affordable Vegas activity**](/affordable-family-activities-las-vegas/).

### [The Arcade at the Orleans](/go/orleans-hotel-las-vegas/)

4500 W Tropicana Ave, Las Vegas, NV 89103
</div>

This delightful arcade is located in [The Orleans Hotel &amp; Casino](https://orleans.boydgaming.com/). With a mix of classic and modern games, it’s a fun spot for the whole family. The relaxed atmosphere and variety of games make it a great place to spend an afternoon. Plus, The Orleans offers a range of other family-friendly amenities, including a bowling alley and movie theater, ensuring a full day of entertainment.

### [Sugarloaf Entertainment at South Point](/go/south-point-las-vegas/)

9777 S Las Vegas Blvd, Las Vegas, NV 89183
</div>

Sugarloaf Arcade, located inside the [South Point Hotel](https://southpointcasino.com/), is another fantastic option for families. Although this [**hotel is not directly on the Strip**](/las-vegas-strip-hotel-map/), the arcade is filled with a wide variety of games, from timeless classics to the latest in gaming technology. South Point is known for its family-friendly environment, and the arcade is no exception. It’s a great place to let the kids blow off some steam and enjoy some friendly competition. They also have a fun bowling alley to make it a complete night of family fun.

**[See Photos, Reviews, &amp; More](/go/south-point-las-vegas/)**

### [F1 Arcade at Caesars Palace](/go/caesars-palace-hotel-las-vegas/)

3570 S Las Vegas Blvd, Paradise, NV 89109
</div>

New and thrilling, the F1 Arcade at [Caesar's Palace](https://www.caesars.com/caesars) opens in 2025, bringing the excitement of Formula 1 racing to the heart of Las Vegas. This immersive racing experience features state-of-the-art simulators that let you feel the thrill of the track. It's perfect for kids, teens, and adults who are motorsport enthusiasts. The high-energy environment and realistic simulations make it an unforgettable experience for the whole family.

### [Area 15](/go/area-15-las-vegas-teens/)

3215 S Rancho Dr, Las Vegas, NV 89102
</div>

Area 15, home to [Meow Wolf's](https://meowwolf.com/) Omega Mart as well as many other cool experiences, is not your typical arcade, but it's an immersive entertainment complex that's perfect for families. Omega Mart, a sequel to their popular [**House to Eternal Return**](/tips-visiting-santa-fe-with-kids/), which we visited in Santa Fe, is another win! Area 15 is a futuristic, neon-lit wonderland that offers a range of interactive experiences, shops, and arcade games. My kids can't get enough of the virtual reality attractions and the out-of-this-world experiences. It's a blend of art, technology, and fun that appeals to all ages, making it a must-visit on your Vegas adventure.

### [Velocity Esports at Times Square](/go/times-square-las-vegas/)

6587 S Las Vegas Blvd #171, Las Vegas, NV 89119
</div>

While not inside a hotel, Velocity Esports at [Town Square](https://tslv.com/) is worth the short trip from the Strip. This massive entertainment center is a gamer's paradise, featuring an extensive collection of arcade games, eSports, bowling, and more. The variety here is incredible, catering to all ages and interests. Plus, the dining options are perfect for refueling after a long gaming session. It’s a great spot for a day trip if you’re staying in any nearby hotel.
