Whether you're <a href="/halloween-for-kids-in-las-vegas/" data-type="post" data-id="21227" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener"><strong>visiting Las Vegas during Halloween</strong></a> or your family is just into ghost stories and curious about some eerie history, here’s a look at a few haunted hotels in Las Vegas that might just give you goosebumps.</p>

<div class="wp-block-uagb-advanced-heading uagb-block-42a30e40"><h3 class="uagb-heading-text"><a href="/go/horseshoe-las-vegas/" linkid="21117">Horseshoe </a></h3><p class="uagb-desc-text">3645 Las Vegas Blvd S, Las Vegas, NV 89109</p></div>
<!-- /wp:uagb/advanced-heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"dropCap":true} -->
<p class="has-drop-cap">The recently renamed former Bally's has its share of ghost stories, dating back to when the property was the original MGM Grand. In 1980, a <a href="https://www.8newsnow.com/news/local-news/deadly-mgm-grand-fire-changed-worldwide-safety-standards/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">devastating fire</a> claimed the lives of 85 people, and ever since, guests and staff have reported ghostly encounters. From flickering lights to eerie noises, some say the spirits of those who perished still linger in the hotel.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Visitors who check into the newly refurbished North Tower of <a href="https://www.caesars.com/horseshoe-las-vegas" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Horseshoe Las Vegas</a> often report unsettling experiences, from the heavy smell of smoke to the faint but haunting echoes of screams drifting down the hallway. But the eeriest tale centers around an elderly woman spotted in the casino. She supposedly haunts a cluster of slot machines, calmly playing two at a time while her dress is engulfed in ghostly flames. Her presence adds a spine-chilling twist to this historic hotel, making it one of the most haunting spots in Vegas.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Family-Friendly Fun:</strong> Don’t worry; there’s more than just ghost stories here! Horseshoe Las Vegas is also home to the hilarious, <a href="/las-vegas-shows-for-kids/" data-type="post" data-id="8596" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener"><strong>family-friendly Vegas show</strong></a>, <a linkid="14749" href="/go/potted-potter-vegas-show/"><strong>Potted Potter</strong></a>, which condensed all seven Harry Potter books into 70 minutes of laughter.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Families will also love the quirky and interactive “Twilight Zone Mini Golf,” an indoor, glow-in-the-dark mini-golf course that’s perfect for kids and adults alike. It’s a fun way to explore the mysterious atmosphere without getting too spooked!</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Kids and teens will totally dig the 7,000-square-foot <a href="/best-arcades-in-las-vegas/" data-type="post" data-id="21049" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener"><strong>Las Vegas Arcade</strong></a> at Horseshoe! This neon-lit playground is packed with 90 games for all ages. Whether you’re into classics or the latest hits, this spot has something for everyone.</p>

<div class="wp-block-uagb-advanced-heading uagb-block-80f53cb4"><h3 class="uagb-heading-text"><a href="/go/the-luxor-hotel-las-vegas/" linkid="18437">The Luxor</a></h3><p class="uagb-desc-text">3900 S Las Vegas Blvd, Las Vegas, NV 89119</p></div>
<!-- /wp:uagb/advanced-heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"dropCap":true} -->
<p class="has-drop-cap">The Luxor’s pyramid shape and ancient Egyptian theme set the stage for some <a href="https://www.mercurynews.com/2024/02/06/the-haunted-history-of-this-years-super-bowl-media-hotel/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">spine-tingling stories</a>. Over the years, the hotel has seen its fair share of accidents and unusual occurrences. Guests have reported strange shadows, cold spots, and mysterious voices. Some say the hotel’s architecture, modeled after tombs and pyramids, may be to blame for its paranormal activity.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Unfortunately there have been <a href="https://www.casino.org/blog/deaths-in-luxor/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">several actual deaths</a> at <a href="https://luxor.mgmresorts.com/en.html" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">the Luxor</a>, including multiple workers during the construction of the hotel as well as two guests jumping to their deaths from the interior balconies.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The Luxor's&nbsp;<strong><a linkid="8729" href="/go/titanic-exhibition-las-vegas-bov/">Titanic Artifact Exhibition</a></strong>&nbsp;as even had reports of ghostly happenings, like moving objects and apparitions. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Family-Friendly Fun:</strong> Kids will be in awe of the Luxor’s incredible pyramid structure and the family-friendly attractions it offers, like the interactive <a href="/things-to-do-in-las-vegas-with-teens/" data-type="post" data-id="13348" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener"><strong>exhibits that teens will love</strong></a> at the “Bodies... The Exhibition” and “Titanic: The Artifact Exhibition.” These exhibits are both educational and entertaining, making it a great way for families to spend some time learning together.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Luxor is also home to one of the best <a href="/las-vegas-shows-for-kids/" data-type="post" data-id="8596" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener"><strong>Las Vegas shows for families</strong></a>, <a linkid="8727" href="/go/blue-man-group-las-vegas-bov/"><strong>The Blue Man Group</strong></a> as well as my kids favorite spot game, the HyperX Arena.</p>

<div class="wp-block-uagb-advanced-heading uagb-block-4629e7b7"><h3 class="uagb-heading-text"><a href="/go/flamingo-las-vegas-exp/" linkid="9275">The Flamingo Hotel and Casino</a></h3><p class="uagb-desc-text">3555 Las Vegas Blvd S, Las Vegas, NV 89109</p></div>
<!-- /wp:uagb/advanced-heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"dropCap":true} -->
<p class="has-drop-cap">The Flamingo is one of the oldest and most iconic hotels on the Strip, but it’s also rumored to be haunted by the ghost of notorious mobster Bugsy Siegel. Siegel, who played a big role in <a href="https://www.history.com/this-day-in-history/bugsy-siegel-opens-flamingo-hotel" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">building the Flamingo</a>, met a tragic end in 1947, and some guests and employees claim they’ve seen his ghost roaming the grounds, especially near the memorial garden. There have also been sightings of Bugsy in the Presidential Suite that contain the golden bathroom fixtures from his original room.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Family-Friendly Fun:</strong> While ghost sightings might be spooky, <a href="https://www.caesars.com/flamingo-las-vegas/hotel" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">the Flamingo</a> is also home to a fantastic wildlife habitat, a <a href="/free-family-activities-las-vegas/" data-type="post" data-id="9291" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener"><strong>free Las Vegas attraction</strong></a> that kids will love! The <a linkid="20719" href="/go/wildlife-habitat-flamingo-las-vegas/"><strong>Flamingo Wildlife Habitat</strong></a> features beautiful gardens, waterfalls, and flamingos. It’s a peaceful retreat from the hustle and bustle, perfect for families looking for some downtime.</p>

<div class="wp-block-uagb-advanced-heading uagb-block-d680c366"><h3 class="uagb-heading-text"><a href="/go/westgate-hotel-las-vegas/" linkid="13332">The Westgate Las Vegas Resort &amp; Casino</a></h3><p class="uagb-desc-text">3000 Paradise Rd, Las Vegas, NV 89109</p></div>
<!-- /wp:uagb/advanced-heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"dropCap":true} -->
<p class="has-drop-cap">Once known as the Las Vegas Hilton, the <a href="https://www.westgateresorts.com/hotels/nevada/las-vegas/westgate-las-vegas-resort-casino/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Westgate</a> is steeped in history and rumored to be haunted by the King himself, Elvis Presley! Elvis performed here during his Vegas years, and many believe his spirit never left. From unexplained sightings to unusual occurrences in the hotel’s theater, the ghostly presence of Elvis is said to be alive and well. Here is a fun fact, <a href="https://www.cityam.com/elvis-brother-still-lives-in-the-kings-las-vegas-hotel-we-met-him/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Elvis's brother still lives at the Westgate</a>!</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Family-Friendly Fun:</strong> Families will love the <a href="/las-vegas-family-suites/" data-type="post" data-id="20911" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener"><strong>huge family suites</strong></a>, complete with bunkbeds as well as the hotel’s massive pool, which even has a bar and grill nearby for convenient snacks. For a trip down memory lane, there’s also the “Elvis Presley Exhibition,” where fans can learn more about the legend and see his personal memorabilia.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Westgate is also home to the most beautiful Benihana restaurant I've ever been dined. It is definitely one of our favorite <a href="/best-family-restaurants-in-las-vegas/" data-type="post" data-id="20281" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener"><strong>Vegas family restaurants</strong></a>.</p>

<div class="wp-block-uagb-advanced-heading uagb-block-1e3eedf4"><h3 class="uagb-heading-text"><a href="/go/hotel-apache-las-vegas/" linkid="21509">Hotel Apache</a></h3><p class="uagb-desc-text">128 Fremont St, Las Vegas, NV 89101</p></div>
<!-- /wp:uagb/advanced-heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"dropCap":true} -->
<p class="has-drop-cap">Stepping into <a href="https://www.binions.com/meetings/accommodations/hotel_apache.php" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Hotel Apache</a> is like traveling back in time, with its vintage décor and old-school charm. But this historic gem at Binion’s also comes with some spooky baggage. Built in 1932, the hotel is rumored to be one of the <a href="https://www.ktnv.com/news/haunted-las-vegas-paranormal-activity-at-historic-hotel-apache" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">most haunted spots</a> in Las Vegas. Over the years, guests and staff have reported ghostly apparitions, flickering lights, and strange sounds in the dead of night. Some say spirits from the hotel’s early days never left, adding an eerie vibe to the place.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The most chilling stories revolve around unexplained footsteps in empty hallways and ghostly figures that vanish into thin air. Guests have even reported feeling sudden cold spots, as if someone walked right through them. The spirits here seem to have a soft spot for mischief – moving objects, turning TVs on and off, and even tapping on windows. Staying at Hotel Apache might just give your family a ghost story to share for years!
