<p class="has-drop-cap">Growing up in Southern California, I’ve always been a beach lover, and luckily, my kids inherited the same obsession. We never pass up a chance to be by the ocean. Around here, sandy toes and salty hair are basically part of the family dress code.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>One of our favorite local escapes is <strong><a href="/go/newport-dunes-rv-resort-newport-beach-ca/" linkid="13849">Newport Dunes Waterfront Resort &amp; Marina</a></strong>, tucked along the calm waters of the Back Bay. While Newport Beach attracts visitors from all over the world, this hidden gem gives families a relaxed way to enjoy the coast without the crashing waves or crowded shoreline.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>What makes it extra special is the variety of ways you can stay. Families can glamp in cozy waterfront cottages, park an RV in one of the scenic waterfront sites, or roll in for a beachside camping experience right on the bay. The protected swimming lagoon is perfect for kids, there are wide sandy areas to play, and you’re just minutes from great restaurants, attractions, and everything that makes Newport Beach such a classic Southern California destination.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Whether you're planning a <strong><a href="/best-california-glamping-sites/" target="_blank" data-type="post" data-id="14782" rel="noreferrer noopener">family glamping getaway</a></strong> or you’re an RV traveler cruising through Southern California looking for a waterfront stop, <a href="https://www.newportdunes.com/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Newport Dunes</a> offers the perfect mix of beach relaxation, family fun, and easy coastal adventure.</p>

<h3>Moe B's Munchies Beach Bar &amp; Grill:</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>When you need a break from the beach, head over to Moe B's Munchies, the casual beachfront eatery right inside Newport Dunes. It’s the perfect place to refuel after swimming, kayaking, or a long afternoon building sandcastles.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The menu is full of easy, crowd-pleasing favorites made fresh to order. You’ll find juicy burgers, stacked sandwiches, bacon-wrapped hot dogs, and giant 14-inch pizzas, all made with high-quality ingredients. One thing you definitely shouldn’t skip? The signature seasoned baked fries—they’re technically shareable, but no judgment if you keep them all to yourself.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>For the grown-ups, the beachfront bar serves hand-shaken margaritas, cocktails, and refreshing drinks, perfect for sipping while enjoying the relaxed bayfront setting. Grab a seat in the shaded promenade seating area and soak up the laid-back coastal vibe.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>And if you feel like venturing out, remember that Newport Dunes sits in the heart of <strong><a href="/beaches-near-anaheim/" target="_blank" data-type="post" data-id="5610" rel="noreferrer noopener">Newport Beach</a></strong>, which means fantastic dining is just minutes away. From classic seafood spots to waterfront restaurants, you’ll have plenty of options nearby, including local favorites like <strong>The Crab Cooker</strong> and the beautiful dining spots along <strong><a href="/newport-coast-with-kids/" data-type="post" data-id="863" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Newport Coast</a></strong>.</p>
