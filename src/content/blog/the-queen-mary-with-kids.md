<p class="has-drop-cap">Luxury, legends, and a little spooky… visiting the Queen Mary with kids is anything but ordinary.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Built during the Great Depression, the Queen Mary set sail on her maiden transatlantic voyage in 1936, redefining luxury at sea. This legendary ocean liner hosted Hollywood icons like Clark Gable, welcomed British royalty, and later earned the nickname <em>The Grey Ghost</em> when she was transformed into a top-secret WWII troopship.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After the war, the Queen Mary returned to passenger service for two more decades before <a href="/save-the-queen-mary-in-long-beach-ca/" target="_blank" data-type="post" data-id="15489" rel="noreferrer noopener"><strong>permanently docking in Long Beach, California, in 1967</strong></a>. Today, she’s one of the most unique places in Southern California where families can tour, dine, and even spend the night aboard a historic ship.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>And yes… she also happens to have a <strong><a href="/best-haunted-places-with-kids/" target="_blank" data-type="post" data-id="9154" rel="noreferrer noopener">reputation for being one of the most haunted places in the world</a></strong>, which you know we absolutely love over here at HotMamaTravel.com.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>So if you’re wondering whether <a href="/go/the-queen-mary-ship-hotel/" linkid="22641"><strong>visiting the Queen Mary with kids</strong></a> is worth it (history, spooky stories, and all), you’re in the right place. Let’s explore what families can expect when staying, eating, touring, and making unforgettable memories aboard this iconic ship.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Add in themed events, kid-friendly dining options, and the ship’s famous haunted reputation (perfect for curious tweens and teens), and the Queen Mary becomes a memorable family adventure, not just a hotel stay.</p>

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>Queen Mary Room Options for Families</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The Queen Mary offers a wide range of stateroom options to fit different family sizes and travel styles. Families will love the <strong>Family Suites</strong>, which sleep up to four guests and are available with <strong>one king and one twin bed or two queen beds</strong>, perfect for parents and kids sharing a space comfortably. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Larger groups can upgrade to <strong>Mini Suites</strong> (sleep 4) or <strong>Full Suites</strong> (sleep up to 6), while couples or smaller families can choose from <strong>Deluxe</strong> or <strong>Standard Staterooms</strong> that sleep two. ADA-accessible rooms are also available, and every room offers multiple layout configurations to choose from.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>For the bravest families (or ghost-loving parents), the Queen Mary also offers the legendary <strong>Paranormal Suite, Stateroom B340</strong>, known for one of the highest volumes of recorded paranormal activity on the ship. It's one of our favorite <a href="/haunted-hotels-in-los-angeles/" target="_blank" data-type="post" data-id="16536" rel="noreferrer noopener"><strong>haunted hotels in L.A</strong></a>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Reports dating back to Queen Mary’s final voyage in 1967 include unexplained knocking in the middle of the night, lights turning on by themselves, faucets running without reason, and bathroom doors slamming shut. The ship’s logs are full of these encounters… but are you brave enough to stay?</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

Unless, of course, you are keeping one eye open to spy one of the many ghosts said to haunt this historic floating hotel. Yup, I said ghosts. </p>

<h3>Queen Mary Restaurants for Families</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":4} -->
<h4><strong>Observation Bar</strong></h4>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>This Art Deco lounge is my favorite hangout on the Queen Mary with kids. It is kid-friendly, with great drinks and small bites, fantastic views, and live music. You can’t beat it. It just takes you back to a more glamorous era. Every time I visit, I secretly (okay, not so secretly) wish I would spot one having a cocktail in the observation bar.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":4} -->
<h4>Promenade Café</h4>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Like the other dining options on the ship, the Promenade Café has gorgeous views of the ocean and delicious food. Very family-friendly, the café is open for breakfast, lunch, and dinner.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":4} -->
<h4>Chelsea Chowder House &amp; Bar</h4>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Chelsea's doesn't just have any chowder; they specialize in chowders as well as other delicious seafood options and steak. Surf and turf, baby! This is a fine dining experience for those looking to upgrade their time on The Queen Mary. Nothing sounds better than some warm chowder and a chilled glass of wine when you are on a boat surrounded by the blue ocean.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":4} -->
<h4>Midship Marketplace</h4>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The Midship Marketplace is your go-to stop on the Queen Mary when you need a caffeine fix or a quick bite between exploring the ship. Serving Starbucks coffee, this easy grab-and-go spot offers fresh sandwiches, salads, pastries, and drinks. It is perfect for fueling up without slowing down your day. Whether it’s your morning coffee or a mid-afternoon snack, it’s a convenient option for families on the move.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":4} -->
<h4>Afternoon Tea</h4>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Fashioned after British afternoon tea, it is available on Saturdays from 2:00 pm to 4:00 pm. Afternoon Tea offers British tea service as well as delicious salads, soups, and sandwiches. The Tea Room is elegant and will make you feel a bit like a royal. Kids love the tea experience, while mama can enjoy a glass of wine.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":4} -->
<h4>Royal Champagne Sunday Brunch</h4>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>One of my favorite things to do on a Sunday is brunch, and the Queen Mary has a fantastic one! Held in the Grand Salon, the endless buffet of delicious dishes from all over the world wraps around the entire dance floor. Live music plays and champagne flows. It is truly a Sunday Funday.</p>

<p>Translation: history that feels alive, not dusty.</p>
