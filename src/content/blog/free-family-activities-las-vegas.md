<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 id="medieval-times"><a linkid="16012" href="/go/las-vegas-strip-attractions/">Walk the Strip</a></h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"dropCap":true} -->
<p class="has-drop-cap">There is a lot to see in Vegas, and although it occupies just a single boulevard, it is bigger than it looks. A great way to soak it all in for free is to simply <a href="/walking-las-vegas-strip/" data-type="post" data-id="16286" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener"><strong>walk the strip</strong></a>. We prefer to walk the strip at dusk as the temperature starts to drop and the millions of light bulbs come alive, setting the strip ablaze.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>&nbsp;Yes, the <a href="/las-vegas-strip-hotel-map/" data-type="post" data-id="9253" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener"><strong>Las Vegas Strip</strong></a> is much calmer in the morning, but it is also much less lively (aka boring) in the A.M., and it's HOT. It would just be wise not to stay out on the strip too late after dark if you have little kiddos, but a one-hour evening stroll would do just fine.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 id="medieval-times"><a linkid="16013" href="/free-family-activities-las-vegas/">See Live Mermaids at Silverton’s Aquarium</a></h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><em> 3333 Blue Diamond Rd, Las Vegas, NV 89139 </em></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"dropCap":true} -->
<p class="has-drop-cap">Who doesn’t love mermaids? Take the opportunity to see live mermaids swim, flip, and blow bubble kisses from a jumbo aquarium inside a Las Vegas Casino. <a href="https://silvertoncasino.com/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Silverton</a> Mermaids swim every Thursday through Sunday. Swims start at noon until 8:15 pm, except on Sundays, which start at 10:30 and end at 6:45.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The mermaids are super interactive with the kids, which makes it all the more magical. We all love it so much that visiting the mermaids is the first thing we do when we get to Las Vegas.&nbsp; Also, two times a day, at 1:30 pm and 4:30 pm, visitors can enjoy an interactive feeding show.</p>

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 id="medieval-times"><a linkid="16011" href="/go/fall-atlantis-show-caesars-palace-las-vegas/">Fall of Atlantis Show at Caesar’s Palace</a></h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><em> 3570 S Las Vegas Blvd, Las Vegas, NV 89109</em></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"dropCap":true} -->
<p class="has-drop-cap">Located within the Great Roman Hall at the Forum Shops, is the Fall of Atlantis show. What appears to be a regular fountain adorned with Roman statues, magically transforms every hour into a seven minute epic tale that is worth the stop. The statues come alive as they tell the tale of how Atlantis was destroyed.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>There are fiery pyrotechnics, a light show on the ceiling, and tons of excitement. It is amazing for a free show, considering there are a ton of awesome <a href="/las-vegas-shows-for-kids/" data-type="post" data-id="8596" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener"><strong>family-friendly shows in Las Vegas</strong></a>. The great thing is that it is located in front of <a href="/best-family-restaurants-in-las-vegas/" data-type="post" data-id="20281" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener"><strong>several dining establishments</strong></a>, so you can enjoy your meal and a drink while the kids are being entertained. Also, at the base of the fountain is a huge saltwater aquarium with over 100 different species of ocean life, including sharks!</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><a href="https://www.caesars.com/caesars-palace" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Caesar's Palace</a> is next door to Hard Rock and is in a great location on the strip. It is a great hotel choice for families visiting Vegas as well.</p>

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 id="medieval-times"><a linkid="8733" href="/go/bellagio-las-vegas-expedia/">See an Amazing Water Show at Bellagio</a></h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><em>3600 S Las Vegas Blvd, Las Vegas, NV 89109</em></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"dropCap":true} -->
<p class="has-drop-cap">The Fountains of <a href="https://bellagio.mgmresorts.com/en.html" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Bellagio</a> show is a beautifully choreographed water show set to music and lights. Free to the public and viewable from some <a href="/las-vegas-hotels-balcony-rooms/" data-type="post" data-id="16987" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener"><strong>balcony rooms</strong></a>, the dancing waters span more than 1,000 feet and can shoot up to 460 feet into the air. The Fountain shows are always different, and musical selections range from operatic to classical, from Broadway to current popular tunes.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Monday through Friday, the show starts at 3:00 pm and runs every 30 minutes until 8:00 pm, before switching to 15-minute intervals until midnight. On the weekends and holidays, the shows start earlier, around noon. You can see the spectacular fountain show from many different vantage points along the Vegas strip.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><em>(Hot Tip: a great way to see the show from a whole new aerial perspective is to go up the <a href="/go/eiffel-tower-paris-las-vegas/" linkid="15580">Eiffel tower at Paris</a> and wait on the observation deck until the water show begins across the street! We did this once and it was pretty cool.)</em></p>

<h3 id="medieval-times"><a linkid="16020" href="/go/las-vegas-shopping/">Stroll the Many Indoor Shopping Areas</a></h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"dropCap":true} -->
<p class="has-drop-cap">Shopping may not be free, but it costs nothing to take a wander through the grand Las Vegas malls. These aren’t your typical shopping malls. Oh no, Las Vegas is where all the major designers come to open flagship stores alongside upscale restaurants and world class entertainment that only Vegas can provide. Just along the strip, there are a number of high retailers. For example, Miracle Mile and Fashion Show have 200 boutiques and stores each!</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>In addition, many hotels have luxury shops as well, like The Forum Shops at Caesars Palace (where you can find the world's largest H&amp;M), The Wynn Esplanade, Via Bellagio, Shoppes at Mandalay Place, and The Grand Canal Shops at The Venetian, which looks as though you are strolling along the canals of Venice, gondolas and all.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The great thing is that these shopping malls are outrageously decorated and have plenty to be entertained without buying a thing.</p>

<p><em> 3770 S Las Vegas Blvd, Las Vegas, NV 89109</em></p>

Wander under the 50 foot raised glass ceiling and take in the fragrant smells and the vibrant colors. This is a super relaxing and zen-like way to spend a free hour or two in Vegas. </p>
