No longer just a playground for Hollywood elite, Palm Springs CA is a desert oasis that is quickly becoming one of the most popular family vacation spots in the US.

*Why, you ask?*

First, Palm Springs has this quirky, fun, vibrant and laid-back culture that you really can't find anywhere else. Families also really love that there are tons of unique places to eat, shop, play, and explore.

It also doesn't hurt that Palm Springs has sunny weather pretty much all year-round! Making it a great destination to [spend the day poolside](/best-family-resorts-palm-springs/).

#### First, a little bit of history. For history lovers like me...

Palm Springs is located about a hundred miles east of Los Angeles and 125 miles north of San Diego, making it a historically popular vacation destination for families all across Southern California and beyond.

Palm Springs is well known for its iconic architectural style described as mid-century (1950s/60s era) "desert" modern. In fact, some have argued that Palm Springs was the original inspiration for the suburban style of housing we see today strewn across Los Angeles and Orange Counties.

Think open floor plans, air conditioning, backyard pools and tall windows inviting sunshine into the home. We have the original canvass of Palm Springs to thank for this quintessentially laid-back SoCal style.

Luckily, Palm Springs has stayed pretty true to its roots over the years, and visitors can still experience a modern version of this vintage style. The area around downtown Palm Springs has many original buildings and hotels that date back almost a hundred years, for example Viceroy Palm Springs (est. 1930's, now called Avalon Hotel Palm Springs), and Colonial House (est. 1936, now called Colony Palms Hotel), among many others including La Quinta Hotel (est. 1927, now called La Quinta Resort and Club) in nearby city of La Quinta.

![Desert Air Hotel in Rancho Mirage circa 1950s](https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/Desert-air-hotel-circa-1950s.jpg) Desert Air Hotel circa 1950s

Also nearby in Rancho Mirage (12 miles south of downtown), there are several larger resorts that cater specifically to families on vacation with popular  golf courses, huge pools and water parks, kid-friendly activities and basically everything you need all in one place.

Though not as old as some places in downtown Palm Springs, many of these resorts have their own interesting history that reaches back to the 1950s. For example, Omni Rancho Las Palmas Resort &amp; Spa (Spoiler alert: our pick for the top family hotel in Palm Springs) is on the site of the former [Hollywood celebrity-laden](https://www.desertsun.com/story/news/local/2015/01/03/desert-air-hotel-rancho-mirage-hank-gogerty/21210529/) Desert Air Hotel (above), which allowed visitors to fly their own planes onto its landing strip and park right next to the hotel while advertising activities in 1952 such as swimming, tennis, horse riding, polo, dancing, golfing, chuckwagon breakfasts, and a vibrant bar and cocktail scene.

### Activities

#### Pool:

There are 3 different pool areas on-site including:

- **Splashtopia**, the family-friendly water park and play zone that has various fun water features/sprinklers, sandy beach, lazy river and water slides.

### Location

#### Neighborhood overview:

The Rancho Mirage area is a ritzy, upscale enclave between Palm Desert to the south and Palm Springs/Cathedral City to the north. It is best known for its superlative golf courses and tennis courts as well as a number of fine dining establishments and luxury resorts. With the recent addition of a 1,000 seat outdoor amphitheater to the remodeled community park, there are a host of events occurring throughout the year to add excitement to any travelers itinerary.

#### Transportation:

Omni does not have a shuttle service, but they do work with Yellow Cab of the Desert and Cardiff Limousine for your transportation needs. Honestly though, I suggest just using Uber for getting around the area. If you are driving your own, then getting around Palm Desert is a breeze.

#### Nearby family attractions:

- The **McCallum Theater** is a performing arts center 2 miles away from the resort. The venue consistently ranks in the top 10 worldwide, acquiring praise from both performers and visitors alike, making it a great choice to catch a show while in town.

- **Children’s Discovery Museum** of the Desert is located just a little over 3 miles from Omni Rancho Las Palmas Resort &amp; Spa. As if the kids don’t have enough to do at the resort already, the Children’s Discovery Museum makes for a fun and educational outing with over 50 interactive exhibits to explore.

- The **Living Desert Zoo &amp; Botanical Gardens** is a great opportunity to learn about the Sonoran Desert life. Only 4 miles from the Omni, the Living Desert Zoo is aimed at desert conservation by preserving a portion the Colorado Desert in its natural state while helping populations of endangered desert plants and animals flourish.

- **Agua Caliente Casino** is great for a little mommy daddy fun just 5 miles away. Now, if you didn’t bring a mommy helper with you on the trip, no worries. Agua Caliente is also a resort and means there are things to do besides gamble, making it suitable for families. It is great place to grab a meal or some live entertainment.

- **Big League Dreams Sports Park** is perfect for the sports fan in your life. Located just over 6 miles in nearby Cathedral City, is a chance to feel what is like to be in big leagues. You can pop over to catch a little league game, take an awesome photo shoot with the kids, or dine at the Stadium Club restaurant.

- There are **frequent annual festivals** in the area such as Coachella, Stagecoach Palm Desert Food &amp;Wine and the BNP Paribas Open.

### Activities

#### Pool:

- **Las Brisas Pool **is the largest pool on the property and great for families. Kids will love the 75-foot waterslide and the many poolside activities while parents can enjoy Cabana rentals, two spas and pool attendants.

- **Las Hadas Pool **is the adult only pool featuring a spa and bar.

- **La Paloma Pool** is the smaller and more peaceful pool, great for just taking a soak. Cabanas rentals and bar are available.

#### Kid's activities:

- **Westin Habitats &amp; Resort Animals **are located throughout the resort with signs providing additional information about each animal. The resort encourage photo ops which makes for some fun exploring for the little ones. Starting at the lobby you will find the **Westin Terrarium **which is home to a variety of reptiles and amphibians. Then take a second to take a closer look at the **ponds** around the resort house colorful koi, Red-Eared Slider turtles, and huge catfish. Kids will enjoy catching feeding times daily at 11:00 am. Then head over to **Scooter’s Den **is where you can meet the resort’s two African Spurred Tortoises, Scooter and Shelley with daily feeding at 10:30 am. Next door to Scooter’s Den, meet Billy and Bella **Flemish Giant Rabbits.** The Westin even has a **Bee Apiary **with tours offered October through April. Keep your eye peeled, because all around the resort is a variety of **Resident Birds** such as Mallard ducks, Canadian geese, Hummingbirds, swans named Dolly and Bert and the Night Heron named George.

- **The Oasis Den &amp; Hideaway **is a fun lounge area filled with arcade games and more. This is definitely a place the whole family will love.

- **Twilight Golf** is an experience for the whole family. After 3:00 pm, kids play golf with complimentary Callaway club rental with a paying adult.

- **Family Movie Night** is held in the Oasis Den on Friday &amp; Saturday nights. You find featured movies on events calendar.

- **Westin Family: Kids Club Discovery Room &amp; Kids’ Night Out** is a space dedicated to the kids. Join the Director of Fun daily to play and learn with a variety of activities, movies, music, computers, crafts and more. The Discovery room is open daily from 9:00 am to 4:00 pm with extended evening hours on the weekends. Sessions cost $15 per hour or $125 for a full day including breakfast and lunch.

- **The Fun Zone** is where kids can have fun making arts and crafts like pottery painting, sand art, balloon animals and more.

- **Bicycle Rentals** are offered on the property for an hourly rate. Families can enjoy riding the Nirve Beach Cruisers around the resort or around Rancho Mirage.

#### Adult activities:

- **The Spa at Westin Mission Hills **is where adults can renew and relax while the kids are having fun in the Discovery room. Treats available at the spa include, but are not limited to body wraps, facials manicures and pedicures, massages, therapy baths, yoga and men’s services. Everything you need to feel refreshed.
