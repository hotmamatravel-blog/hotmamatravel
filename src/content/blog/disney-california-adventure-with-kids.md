<span style="color: #000000;">Opening in February of 2001, Disney California Adventure stole the show (a least in my opinion). The theme park recently went through a complete remodel this year, whimsically representing California and highlighting the state’s diversity. Visitors can wander through Hollywood, stroll along the boardwalk or hike through the wilderness before taking a break to sip wine in Napa or listen to the Mariachi Divas over a margarita. It is all here to enjoy. I love the vibe at Disney California Adventure with kids and it is definitely our favorite park to visit. Find out why.</span>

<a href="/disney-california-adventure-with-kids/"><img class="aligncenter wp-image-654 size-large" title="Disney California Adventure with kids" src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/06/DSCN5741-1024x768.jpg" alt="Disney California Adventure with kids" width="640" height="480" /></a>
<h2><span style="color: #000000;"><em><strong>Keep Cost in Check:</strong></em></span></h2>
<span style="color: #000000;">In regards to Disneyland and California Adventure, the best value is the </span><strong>annual passport</strong><span style="color: #000000;">. If you can’t make it to So Cal more than once, leave enough time to take advantage of the hopper tickets. The single day/single park tickets just aren’t worth it.</span>
<h2><span style="color: #000000;"><em><strong>Drop your Bags:</strong></em></span></h2>
<strong>Paradise Pier</strong><span style="color: #000000;">: Embracing California’s beach culture, Paradise Pier is the epitome of seaside resort (minus the sea). In the heart of Anaheim, every day is a beach party at this hotel. We’ve stayed here a few times, because we just love the laid-back hang-ten vibe. The rooms have splashes of beach accents like the beach ball pillows that lay playfully on the bed. The pool is on the rooftop which is kind of fun and unique. Like all great Disney resorts it has an amazing water slide for the kiddos. In the hotel is kids movie area set up like a day at the beach with darling little Adirondack chairs, perfect for their little bodies.</span>

<a href="/wp-content/uploads/2014/06/P1010590.jpg"><img class="aligncenter wp-image-652 size-large" title="Disney California Adventure with kids" src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/06/P1010590-768x1024.jpg" alt="Disney California Adventure with kids" width="640" height="853" /></a>

<span style="color: #000000;">Even when we are not staying at the Paradise Pier we like to visit the </span>Disney PCH Grill<span style="color: #000000;">. It is a really fun character dining experience. I like it more than Goofy’s Kitchen, because of the all music and dancing. It is constant entertainment and a buffet of yummy food which makes it a great value. Breakfast is my favorite time to go the PCH Grill to the day started off right.</span>

<a href="/wp-content/uploads/2014/06/P1010027.jpg"><img class="aligncenter wp-image-649 size-large" title="Disney California Adventure with kids" src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/06/P1010027-768x1024.jpg" alt="Disney California Adventure with kids" width="640" height="853" /></a>

<a href="/wp-content/uploads/2014/06/P1010373.jpg"><img class="aligncenter wp-image-650 size-large" title="Disney California Adventure with kids" src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/06/P1010373-1024x768.jpg" alt="Disney California Adventure with kids" width="640" height="480" /></a>

<strong>Grand Californian:</strong> <span style="color: #000000;">The mountain lodge theme makes this hotel so cozy, despite the grand size. We often escape to the lobby to enjoy a nice cocktail surrounded by the warm woods, dim lighting, comfy conversational seating and piano music. There is story time by the roaring fireplace for the kids (my daughter’s favorite). There are even sweet miniature rockers for the kids to sit and enjoy Disney cartoons while Mama and Daddy sip their wine. When we are ready to head back the park we just go through the entrance conveniently located off the hotel lobby. The Grand Californian is really something special, plus this is home to my nephew’s favorite pool.</span>

<a href="/disney-california-adventure-with-kids/"><img class="aligncenter wp-image-653 size-large" title="Disney California Adventure with kids" src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/06/DSCN4130-1024x768.jpg" alt="Disney California Adventure with kids" width="640" height="480" /></a>
<h2><span style="color: #000000;"><em><strong>Get your Grub On:</strong></em></span></h2>
<span style="color: #000000;">At California Adventure there a ton of </span>dining options <span style="color: #000000;">ranging from the full-service to quick service.</span>
<h3><strong>Carthay Circle Restaurant</strong></h3>
<span style="color: #000000;">This is fine dining 1920’s style. It is beautiful for a romantic dinner for two or a really nice dinner with the family. Like Disneyland, above Carthay Circle is a secret restaurant for members only.</span>
<h3><strong>Pacific Wharf</strong></h3>
<span style="color: #000000;">I like to be outdoors whenever possible which makes me a bit of a patio freak. If the restaurant has a patio then you can bet that I am on it for sure. Luckily for me, California Adventure has plenty of options. I like hanging at the Pacific Wharf not only because it is outdoors, but it is on the water. Plus they have AMAZING Chowder in a sourdough bread bowl. Holla!</span>

<a href="/disney-california-adventure-with-kids/"><img class="aligncenter wp-image-657 size-large" title="Disney California Adventure with kids" src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/06/DSCN4556-1024x682.jpg" alt="Disney California Adventure with kids" width="640" height="426" /></a>
<h3><strong>Ariel’s Grotto</strong></h3>
<span style="color: #000000;">This is a character dining experience overlooking the water and Ferris wheel. What is really cool is the World of Color package where guests get to dine while getting front row seats to this spectacular water show.</span>
<h3><span style="color: #000000;">Chimichangas!</span></h3>
<span style="color: #000000;">Yes, the restaurants are nice, but in all honesty my family’s favorite eat at California Adventure is the chimichangas sold of a cart in the Hollywood section. We never leave the park without one.</span>

<a href="/disney-california-adventure-with-kids/"><img class="wp-image-656 size-large aligncenter" title="Disney California Adventure with kids" src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/06/DSCN9322-1024x768.jpg" alt="Disney California Adventure with kids" width="640" height="480" /></a>

<a href="/disney-california-adventure-with-kids/"><img class="wp-image-692 size-large aligncenter" title="Disney California Adventure with kids" src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/06/DSCN4940-1024x768.jpg" alt="Disney California Adventure with kids" width="640" height="480" /></a>
<h2><span style="color: #000000;"><em><strong>Places to grab a drink</strong></em></span></h2>
<span style="color: #000000;">Okay. My favorite part!!! Drinks at Disney!! Yes, Disney California has alcohol. From craft beer to craft cocktails, Disney can be fun for parents too.</span>
<h3><strong>Carthy Circle Lounge</strong>:</h3>
<span style="color: #000000;">For a real pre-prohibition craft cocktail experience, this is the place. It is my husband’s favorite. The dim lit dark bar with leather seating, marble tables and chandeliers takes you back in time to early 1900’s. What I love about Disney is that I can rolled up with my double wide stroller and still get this HotMama a drink at really classy joint with no judgment. I definitely recommend the Manhattan! Simply superb.</span>

<a href="/disney-california-adventure-with-kids/"><img class="aligncenter wp-image-659 size-large" title="Disney California Adventure with kids" src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/06/IMG_2419-764x1024.jpg" alt="Disney California Adventure with kids" width="640" height="858" /></a>
<h3><span style="color: #000000;">Beer Trucks:</span></h3>
<span style="color: #000000;">There are plenty of beer stops around the park and yes, I have my route mapped out. My favorite place to grab a beer at California Adventure is the Karl Strauss beer truck. Centrally located, this is my first stop once we enter the park.</span>

<a href="/disney-california-adventure-with-kids/"><img class="aligncenter wp-image-661 size-large" title="Disney California Adventure with kids" src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/06/DSCN4929-1024x683.jpg" alt="Disney California Adventure with kids" width="640" height="427" /></a>
<h3><strong>Cove Bar:</strong></h3>
<span style="color: #000000;">Located just upstairs from Ariel’s Grotto, this bar is outside with gorgeous views of the park and delicious cocktails.</span>

<a href="/disney-california-adventure-with-kids/"><img class="aligncenter wp-image-663 size-large" title="Disney California Adventure with kids" src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/06/IMG_0660-1024x764.jpg" alt="Disney California Adventure with kids" width="640" height="478" /></a>
<h3><strong>Mad T Party:</strong></h3>
<span style="color: #000000;">Hands down,<strong> my favorite thing at California Adventure</strong>. I love dancing to the Mad Hatter Band while sipping on my light up beers and cocktails. It is MAD!!</span>

<strong>*Disclaimer: Sadly, Mad T Party is not in operation as of March 2016. I will update as soon as I know the replacement.</strong>
<h2><span style="color: #000000;"><em><strong>Entertain Me:</strong></em></span></h2>
<h3><strong>Attractions &amp; Entertainment</strong>:</h3>
<span style="color: #000000;">Disney California Adventure with kids has a plethora of attractions and shows, everything from the thrilling to the mild. We have been enough times that we are pretty relaxed and don’t waste our time with really long lines. If there is a really popular ride that is a must, make sure to get a fast pass first thing.</span>
<h3><span style="color: #000000;"><strong>Hollywood:</strong></span></h3>
<span style="color: #000000;">The Tower of Terror ride is extremely popular and heart stopping, but my three year old loves it. Think that is normal?</span>

<a href="/disney-california-adventure-with-kids/"><img class="aligncenter wp-image-668" title="Disney California Adventure with kids" src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/06/IMG_1874.jpg" alt="Disney California Adventure with kids" width="640" height="640" /></a>

<span style="color: #000000;">The Monster’s Inc ride which is a favorite of my kids is it located by the Mad T Party, so between sets we take the kids for a ride.</span>

<a href="/disney-california-adventure-with-kids/"><img class="aligncenter wp-image-670 size-large" title="Disney California Adventure with kids" src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/06/DSCN4947-768x1024.jpg" alt="Disney California Adventure with kids" width="640" height="853" /></a>
<h3><span style="color: #000000;"><strong>Mad T Party:
</strong></span></h3>
<span style="color: #000000;">My favorite! The band is fantastic, the music is great, there is beer, cocktails, mocktails, dancing and best of all the kids can party along with you! My kids love dancing the night away at this MAD party. Although it is no longer happening, I have my fingers crossed it will come back.
</span>

<a href="/disney-california-adventure-with-kids/"><img class="aligncenter wp-image-671 size-large" title="Disney California Adventure with kids" src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/06/DSCN3349-1024x684.jpg" alt="Disney California Adventure with kids" width="640" height="428" /></a>

<a href="/disney-california-adventure-with-kids/"><img class="aligncenter wp-image-672 size-large" title="Disney California Adventure with kids" src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/06/DSCN5727-682x1024.jpg" alt="Disney California Adventure with kids" width="640" height="961" /></a>
<h3><span style="color: #000000;"><strong>Disney Junior Show:</strong> </span></h3>
<span style="color: #000000;">Great for the little ones. My kids just love this interactive show, but beware it is floor seating and my foot falls asleep every darn time!</span>

<a href="/disney-california-adventure-with-kids/"><img class="aligncenter wp-image-675 size-large" title="Disney California Adventure with kids" src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/06/DSCN4559-1024x768.jpg" alt="Disney California Adventure with kids" width="640" height="480" /></a>
<h3><span style="color: #000000;"><strong>Aladdin Musical:</strong> </span></h3>
<span style="color: #000000;">Really great show. The characters come out into the audience, holding the attention of my toddlers the entire time.</span>

<a href="/wp-content/uploads/2014/06/P1010176.jpg"><img class="aligncenter wp-image-676 size-large" title="Disney California Adventure with kids" src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/06/P1010176-1024x768.jpg" alt="Disney California Adventure with kids" width="640" height="480" /></a>
<h3><span style="color: #000000;"><strong>Muppet 3D:</strong> </span></h3>
<span style="color: #000000;">Really cute and fun for the kids. It is a great escape from the heat as well.</span>
<h3><span style="color: #000000;"><strong>Bugs Land:</strong> </span></h3>
<span style="color: #000000;">Very kid-friendly for the little ones in your group. There are also plenty of places to get cooled off on those hot day trips.</span>

<a href="/disney-california-adventure-with-kids/"><img class="aligncenter wp-image-678 size-large" title="Disney California Adventure with kids" src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/06/DSCN0515-685x1024.jpg" alt="Disney California Adventure with kids" width="640" height="957" /></a>

<a href="/disney-california-adventure-with-kids/"><img class="aligncenter wp-image-679 size-large" title="Disney California Adventure with kids" src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/06/DSCN5449-768x1024.jpg" alt="Disney California Adventure with kids" width="640" height="853" /></a>

<a href="/disney-california-adventure-with-kids/"><img class="aligncenter wp-image-680 size-large" title="Disney California Adventure with kids" src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/06/DSCN9320-1024x683.jpg" alt="Disney California Adventure with kids" width="640" height="427" /></a>
<h3><span style="color: #000000;"><strong>Cars Land:</strong> </span></h3>
<span style="color: #000000;">The newest land added to the park. Looks just like Radiator Springs. Very fun and very cute, but home the most popular ride: Radiator Springs Racers! Want to ride this get a fast pass early or be willing to wait in an hour long line. Ugh. Avoid the Luigi’s Flying Tires, it barely moves and even my toddlers were bored.</span>

<a href="/disney-california-adventure-with-kids/"><img class="aligncenter wp-image-682 size-large" title="Disney California Adventure with kids" src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/06/DSCN4435-1024x684.jpg" alt="Disney California Adventure with kids" width="640" height="428" /></a>
<h3><span style="color: #000000;"><strong>Paradise Pier:</strong> </span></h3>
<span style="color: #000000;">Another staple in the family, the Ariel ride. We have to ride every trip. My kids just love it. This is a fast loading ride so the line moves really quickly.</span>

<a href="/disney-california-adventure-with-kids/"><img class="aligncenter wp-image-683 size-large" title="Disney California Adventure with kids" src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/06/DSCN2981-1024x768.jpg" alt="Disney California Adventure with kids" width="640" height="480" /></a>
<h3><span style="color: #000000;"><strong>King Triton’s Carousal:</strong> </span></h3>
<span style="color: #000000;">My son could ride this all day if I’d let him. Now that my daughter (3) has moved up to the more daring rides like Tower of Terror, it is a bit slow for her. I love the sea creatures and the whimsical colors.</span>

<a href="/disney-california-adventure-with-kids/"><img class="aligncenter wp-image-684 size-large" title="Disney California Adventure with kids" src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/06/DSCN4453-1024x768.jpg" alt="Disney California Adventure with kids" width="640" height="480" /></a>

<span style="color: #000000;">Phineas and Ferb Rockin’ Dance Party pops up at scheduled times throughout the day in front of Pacific Wharf. It is really fun for the kids to get up and dance with scouts.</span>

<a href="/disney-california-adventure-with-kids/"><img class="aligncenter wp-image-685 size-large" title="Disney California Adventure with kids" src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/06/DSCN8071-683x1024.jpg" alt="Disney California Adventure with kids" width="640" height="960" /></a>
<h3><span style="color: #000000;"><strong>Grizzly Peak:</strong> </span></h3>
<span style="color: #000000;">Home of the Redwood Creek Challenge Trail, perfect for burning up those kiddos’ excess energy. Plus they get to meet the characters of UP.</span>

<a href="/disney-california-adventure-with-kids/"><img class="aligncenter wp-image-686 size-large" title="Disney California Adventure with kids" src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/06/DSCN8943-768x1024.jpg" alt="Disney California Adventure with kids" width="640" height="853" /></a>

<a href="/disney-california-adventure-with-kids/"><img class="aligncenter wp-image-687 size-large" title="Disney California Adventure with kids" src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/06/DSCN4433-683x1024.jpg" alt="Disney California Adventure with kids" width="640" height="960" /></a>
<h3><span style="color: #000000;"><strong>Condor Flats: </strong></span></h3>
<span style="color: #000000;">Where my husband’s favorite ride is located, Soarin’ over California. It really is a full sensory experience.</span>

<span style="color: #000000;">Throughout the park and on Buena Vista Street is parade and impromptu concerts all day. The Pixar Parade is the main parade that travels the entirely of the park featuring all your favorite Pixar characters. Don’t miss the World of Color which is the last show of the evening before closing.</span>
<h2><span style="color: #000000;"><em><strong>That’s a Wrap:</strong></em></span></h2>
<span style="color: #000000;">Tired already? Prepare for meltdown.</span>

<a href="/disney-california-adventure-with-kids/"><img class="aligncenter wp-image-688 size-large" title="Disney California Adventure with kids" src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/06/IMG_2358-1024x764.jpg" alt="Disney California Adventure with kids" width="640" height="478" /></a>

<span style="color: #000000;">Disney California Adventure has literally become our favorite park to visit. It is easy to get around, less crowded than Disneyland and it has real drinks! Everyone is happy. I understand for those who don’t get to visit Disney often seeing the original park is important, but Disney California Adventure with kids is way more fun in my opinion. Grab the kids, grab a cold one and have some fun!</span>
