<!-- wp:paragraph {"dropCap":true} -->
<p class="has-drop-cap">The <a href="https://www.lvmonorail.com/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Las Vegas Monorail</a> is a fantastic option for families, especially if you're staying along the Strip. The monorail is completely electric with zero-emission trains, and runs from the MGM Grand to the SAHARA Las Vegas, with seven stops in between. It's fast, reliable, and offers stunning views of the Strip. Kids will love the smooth ride and the chance to see the city from above.</p>

<p><strong>Tickets:</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>There are a variety of tickets you can purchase to ride the monorail, from a one day single ride pass to a multiple day unlimited ride pass. It really depends on your needs during your time in Las Vegas.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Tickets can be purchased at any of the tram vending machines, located at the entrance of each station or at the monorail customer service offices. Note that<strong> kids under 5 ride free</strong> and you can also purchase <strong>discounted E-tickets</strong> online or via their <a href="https://www.lvmonorail.com/mobile-ticketing/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">mobile app</a>. There are discounts available for group ticketing, Nevada residences, and conventions.</p>

<p class="has-drop-cap">Don’t underestimate the power of walking in Las Vegas! The Strip is about 4.2 miles long, and walking is a great way to soak in the sights, sounds, and lights of the city. To help you get the most out of your walking adventure, I have put together a full <a href="/walking-las-vegas-strip/" data-type="post" data-id="16286" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener"><strong>walking the Strip guide</strong></a> with more details specific to families and their safety, like which time of day and side of the Strip is better for walking with your kids. So go check that post out for all that information.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Remember to wear comfortable shoes, stay hydrated, and take breaks in the many <a href="/free-family-activities-las-vegas/" data-type="post" data-id="9291" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener"><strong>free family-friendly spots</strong></a> along the way. The Strip is loaded with exciting <a href="/best-family-hotels-in-las-vegas/" data-type="post" data-id="8418" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener"><strong>family hotels</strong></a> with <a href="/affordable-family-activities-las-vegas/" data-type="post" data-id="9304" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener"><strong>fun, affordable attractions</strong></a> and <a href="/best-family-restaurants-in-las-vegas/" data-type="post" data-id="20281" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener"><strong>great restaurants</strong></a>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Connected Las Vegas Hotels And Pedestrians Bridges</strong>:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Las Vegas is designed for easy pedestrian movement, with a network of inside tunnels and overhead bridges connecting major hotels and casinos. The outdoor overhead bridges are a safe way to cross the wildly busy streets of the Las Vegas Strip and can be accessed via escalators or elevators. The inside pathways are not only convenient but also air-conditioned, providing a comfortable way to get around, especially during the hot summer months. </p>

<!-- /wp:ugb/heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"dropCap":true} -->
<p class="has-drop-cap">For times when you need to travel further distances or prefer a direct route, taxis and ride-sharing services like Uber and Lyft are readily available. <strong>Taxis</strong> can be easily hailed from hotel taxi stands or flagged down on the street. They’re a quick way to get to your destination without the hassle of parking. You can also call an <strong>Uber or Lyft</strong> by simply using the app to request a ride. These services are often more affordable than taxis and provide the convenience of cashless transactions. Many hotels have designated pick-up and drop-off points for ride-shares.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:ugb/heading {"showSubtitle":false,"showBottomLine":true,"bottomLineColor":"#ff0000","uniqueClass":"ugb-7bd1ea0","contentAlign":"center"} -->
<div class="wp-block-ugb-heading ugb-heading ugb-7bd1ea0 ugb-main-block"><style>.ugb-7bd1ea0 .ugb-heading__title{text-align:center}.ugb-7bd1ea0 .ugb-heading__bottom-line{background-color:#ff0000 !important;margin-left:auto !important;margin-right:auto !important}.ugb-7bd1ea0 .ugb-inner-block{text-align:center}</style><div class="ugb-inner-block"><div class="ugb-block-content"><h2 class="ugb-heading__title">Las Vegas RTC Buses</h2><div class="ugb-heading__bottom-line"></div></div></div></div>
<!-- /wp:ugb/heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The Regional Transportation Commission of Southern Nevada (RTC) operates an extensive bus system that's family-friendly and affordable.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Deuce on the Strip:</strong> This double-decker bus runs 24/7 along the Strip, making frequent stops at major hotels and attractions. It's a fun way to sightsee as you travel.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>SDX (Strip &amp; Downtown Express):</strong> Offers express service between the Strip and Downtown Las Vegas, including stops at the Premium Outlets and Fremont Street Experience.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Fares:</strong> Kids 6 and under ride free. Consider purchasing a day pass for unlimited rides.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->
