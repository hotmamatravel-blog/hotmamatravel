<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>Day 1: Morning Drive from Las Vegas to <a href="/go/oatman-arizona/" linkid="15384">Oatman, AZ </a></h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><em>Distance: 125 miles, or about 2.25 hours</em></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"dropCap":true} -->
<p class="has-drop-cap">Oatman is this cute ghost town off one of the most scenic stretches of road on historic Route 66. There are wild donkeys that literally roam the streets, an old mine to walk through, and quite a few restaurants and gift shops to keep you and your family busy for several hours exploring the city of Oatman.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":20859,"sizeSlug":"full","linkDestination":"media"} -->
<figure id="burros-oatman-arizona"><a href="/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/oatman-arizona.jpg"><img src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/oatman-arizona.jpg" alt="Wild Burros in Oatman Arizona" class="wp-image-20859"/></a><figcaption class="wp-element-caption">Like I said, there are donkeys EVERYWHERE and the kids just loved it!</figcaption></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We visited Oatman on our <a href="/route-66-family-road-trip-itinerary/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener"><strong>Route 66 family road trip</strong></a>, so you can read more details about the history of the town and our experience <a href="/route-66-kingman-classic-stops/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener"><strong>HERE</strong></a>. Needless to say, this is a unique place to visit near Vegas by car that really couldn't be more different from the glitz and glam of Las Vegas.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>You should plan to eat lunch in town and spend a few hours exploring before continuing your adventure down Route 66 to the nearby area of Needles/Topock (that you've probably never heard of...until now!) located on the Colorado River.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3> Day 1: Afternoon Drive from <a href="/go/oatman-arizona/" linkid="15384">Oatman</a> to<a href="/go/topock-arizona/" linkid="15387"> Topock, AZ</a></h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><em>Distance: 30 miles, or about 40 mins</em></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"dropCap":true} -->
<p class="has-drop-cap">In less than an hour you will be at your destination - a wild, pirate-themed resort situated on the Colorado River just north of popular Lake Havasu in an area called Topock. Technically, Pirate Cove Resort is just across the river and on the California side (hence the address is in the city of Needles, but this is still generally considered "Topock".</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":20858,"sizeSlug":"full","linkDestination":"media"} -->
<figure><a href="/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/Topock-arizona.jpg"><img src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/Topock-arizona.jpg" alt="Topock Arizona Road Trips from Las Vegas" class="wp-image-20858"/></a></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We haven't stayed at Pirate Cove Resort ourselves, but we got a strong recommendation to stay here from a family friend with two young children. There is so much to do here that is fun for kids and adults, with everything from water sports activities (you can rent boats and jet skis) to off-roading or just lounging by the river.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Best of all, you can stretch out and rent these cute and luxurious cabins with multiple bedroom and bathrooms, washer/drying, fridge and kitchen, and a beach patio with BBQ and nice views of the river.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3> Day 2: Hang out in <a href="/go/topock-arizona/" linkid="15387">Topock</a>!</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"dropCap":true} -->
<p class="has-drop-cap">It's time to choose some exciting outdoor activities, or you can simply choose to relax. They have a pirate-themed <a href="https://www.piratecoveresort.com/menu/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">restaurant on-site</a> with live music, so be sure to enjoy at least one meal there during your stay! There are more than enough options to have a remarkable day away from Vegas.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3> Day 3: Drive back to Vegas</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><em>Distance: 155 miles, or about 3 hours</em></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"dropCap":true} -->
<p class="has-drop-cap">On your scenic drive back to Las Vegas in time for <a href="/las-vegas-dinner-and-show-packages/" data-type="post" data-id="16861" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener"><strong>dinner and a show</strong></a>, or you can stop by other interesting cities for lunch or to explore for a few hours.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong><a linkid="15388" href="/go/laughlin-arizona/">Laughlin AZ</a></strong> is a fun town on the border of Nevada and California with resorts and casinos somewhat reminiscent of Vegas but with a small-town feel.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong><a linkid="15389" href="/go/kingman-arizona/">Kingman AZ</a></strong> is a classic Route 66 stop with retro diners and a historic downtown district that is worth seeing to remember the good ole days of the Mother Road (i.e., Route 66).</p>

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>Day 1: Morning drive from Vegas to<a href="/go/st-george-utah/" linkid="15390"> St. George, Utah</a></h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><em>Distance: 120 miles, or about 2 hours</em></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"dropCap":true} -->
<p class="has-drop-cap">On the first day of this 3-day road trip from Vegas, you will first drive a couple of hours across the state line of Arizona and then Utah to reach the small town of St George. I recommend grabbing lunch in town, and there are a few nice upscale options like <a href="https://painted-pony.com/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Painted Pony Restaurant</a> and <a href="https://www.cliffsiderestaurant.com/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Cliffside Restaurant</a>, or you can grab a cheap bite at the local Cracker Barrel.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":20862,"sizeSlug":"full","linkDestination":"media"} -->
<figure id="st-george-utah-road-trip"><a href="/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/st-george-arizona.jpg"><img src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/st-george-arizona.jpg" alt="Road Trips from Las Vegas in St. George Utah" class="wp-image-20862"/></a></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Next, you will drive less than 10 minutes to reach the Pioneer Hills Trailhead at the <a href="https://www.blm.gov/programs/national-conservation-lands/utah/red-cliffs-nca" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Red Cliffs National Conservation Area</a> for a short afternoon hike. Enjoy the scenery of this mild hike and take some lovely photos of yourself in the great outdoors with red sand and distinct desert plants and animals. Just remember to bring some bottled water!</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>Day 1: Evening drive from <a href="/go/st-george-utah/" linkid="15390">St. George</a> to <a href="/go/zion-national-park-utah/" linkid="15391">Zion</a></h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><em>Distance: 40 miles, or about 45 mins</em></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"dropCap":true} -->
<p class="has-drop-cap">After your hike at Red Cliffs, you will drive for less than an hour to reach your hotel near Zion National Park. There are several great-looking hotels and lodges near the park entrance, including<a linkid="21989" href="/go/best-western-plus-zion-canyon-inn/"><strong> Best Western Plus Zion Canyon Inn &amp; Suites</strong></a>, which we would recommend since Best Western is our favorite "go-to" <a href="/best-western-family-travel/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">f<strong>amily-friendly hotel chain for road trips</strong></a> (click that link to find out all the reasons why!). It also reminds me of one of our favorites, the <a href="/grand-canyon-with-kids-williams-az/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener"><strong>BW hotel in Williams Arizona</strong></a>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Depending on the time of year, you may also want to glamp and soak up your outdoor adventure in Zion. If you would like to know where you can have an amazing glamping experience, check out my post, <a href="/glamping-zion-national-park/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener"><strong>Mind-Blowing Glamping Sites in Zion National Park</strong></a>.</p>

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>Day 2: Explore <a href="/go/zion-national-park-utah/" linkid="15391">Zion National Park</a></h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"dropCap":true} -->
<p class="has-drop-cap">Today you will spend the whole day at Zion, which is a national treasure and one of the most beautiful places you will ever see. You might want to start at <a href="https://www.nps.gov/zion/index.htm" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Zion Canyon Visitor Center</a> (just a mile or so from the BW or other nearby hotels) to get the lay of the land. And be sure to wear comfortable hiking shoes and bring plenty of snacks and water!</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>You won't want to miss seeing the Narrows, which is a popular spot for instagram-envy photos, and don't skip on the Canyon overlook trail, which will lead you to a place with an epic view of Zion Canyon. It is only a 1 mile hike round trip, so most people should be able to reach this point with no problem (which also means it could be busy with tourists depending on time of year).</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><em>Distance: 160 miles, or about 2.75 hours</em></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->

It is a small town with a few casinos and an amazing Golf Course called <a href="https://golfwolfcreek.com/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Wolf Creek</a> (if that is your sort of thing) that my husband has played several times over the years with his family.</p>

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>Day 1: Morning drive along Historic Route 66 to<a href="/go/williams-arizona/" linkid="15392"> Williams, AZ</a></h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><em>Distance: 220 miles, or about 3.5 hours</em></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>So Williams, AZ is called "the gateway to the Grand Canyon," and it is the place you want to be to explore the <a href="/grand-canyon-with-kids-williams-az/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener"><strong>southern rim of the Grand Canyon</strong></a>. The drive from Vegas to Williams is quite long if you choose to do it all in one shot, but it is beautiful for significant stretches of roadway. There are two ways you can go, depending on your patience level:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list {"ordered":true} -->
<ol><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>You can take the faster, smoother route on Highway 40 </li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>You can actually take an old section of Route 66 through Hackberry, Peach Springs, and Seligman</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ol>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>When you arrive in the high desert where Williams lives, you will be greeted by a beautiful Ponderosa Pine forest and fresh air. We have stayed in Williams on two occasions, the first to do the <a href="/polar-express-in-arizona/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener"><strong>Polar Express Train Ride</strong></a>, in which we stayed at the Grand Canyon Railway Hotel, and the second on our Route 66 road trip, in which we stayed at the <a href="/go/best-western-plus-inn-williams/" linkid="10827"><strong>Best Western Plus Inn of Williams</strong></a>. We can highly recommend both hotels (as you can see in the video below)!</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>If you would like to stay in the Grand Canyon, I have put together some awesome glamping sites you may want to check out: <a href="/grand-canyon-glamping/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener"><strong>Grand Canyon Glamping Sites</strong></a>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>Day 2: <a href="/go/williams-arizona/" linkid="15392">Williams</a> to <a href="/go/grand-canyon-south-rim-arizona/" linkid="15393">Grand Canyon South Rim</a></h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><em>Distance 60 miles, about 1 hour (if you choose to drive it)</em></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Ok, today is an exciting day of sightseeing! Plan to wake up bright and early to make the train ride from Williams Station/Grand Canyon Depot into the Grand Canyon. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>As an alternative, you can drive there, but why would you want to do that when you can take a historic train ride? This is a great family-friendly ride that involves live music entertainment and a mock robbery by friendly cowboys on the way back to Williams</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The views of the South Rim are just spectacular. I think this photo speaks for itself, which is one we took on our latest trip there.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":20872,"sizeSlug":"full","linkDestination":"media"} -->
<figure id="grand-canyon-arizona"><a href="/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/grand-canyon-arizona.jpg"><img src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/grand-canyon-arizona.jpg" alt="Grand Canyon Southern Rim Arizona" class="wp-image-20872"/></a></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>There are a few places to grab food at the South Rim before you set out on a hike or other adventure. We enjoyed views of the Canyon while eating a full service meal at the Arizona Steakhouse in the <a href="https://www.grandcanyonlodges.com/dine/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Bright Angel Lodge</a>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Be sure to leave a few minutes at the end of your visit (before the train picks up) to tour the historic and classy El Tovar Hotel.</p>

<h3>Day 3: Drive back to Vegas</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><em>Distance: 220 miles, or about 3.5 hours</em></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>If you chose not to take the scenic Route 66 on the way from Vegas to Williams, now is your chance to check it out on the way back. For example, in Peach Springs, you can check out the Grand Canyon Caverns, which are the largest dry caverns in the U.S. In Hackberry, you can bathe in Route 66 nostalgia at the Hackberry General Store. It's a bit rough and windy, but it is a very pretty and pleasant drive if you are not in a hurry.</p>
