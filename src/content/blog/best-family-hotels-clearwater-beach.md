### Here's why you will love it

<div class="uagb-separator-wrap">
What families love most about the Hilton Clearwater Beach is the proximity to the pier. You will not need a car, because if the resort doesn’t have what you need, you can easily walk to Pier 60 for more activities and restaurants. However, there is plenty to do at the Hilton Clearwater Beach Resort itself.
</div>

The resort has a two Gulf-front pools, one is an infinity pool and the other is a zero-entry walk- in pool. The resort is beach front, so you have all the excitement of a beach day with hotel beach amenities available. There are a few kid’s activities like sports, games and crafts to take to take home. Finally, there are several on-site restaurants like the Spotted Donkey Cantina, Tommy’s Tiki and Carambola. There is also a fun 360 degree pool bar to stay "refreshed" while the kids splash around!
