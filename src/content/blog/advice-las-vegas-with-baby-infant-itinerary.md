<div class="wp-block-uagb-advanced-heading uagb-block-7249f94e"><h4 class="uagb-heading-text"><strong>Lunch at Cracker Barrel</strong></h4></div>
<!-- /wp:uagb/advanced-heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Right off Dean Martin Dr, <a href="https://www.crackerbarrel.com/Locations/States/nv/n-las-vegas/742/" data-type="link" data-id="https://www.crackerbarrel.com/Locations/States/nv/las-vegas/741/?utm_source=google&amp;utm_medium=maps&amp;utm_campaign=crackerbarrelurl" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Cracker Barrel</a> is conveniently located right as you enter Las Vegas (from the south), making for the perfect spot to stop for a yummy lunch. The casual country dining room is perfect for families. Plus, not only is the food hearty comfort food, but the portions are huge, and the prices are low. Eating in Vegas can be pretty pricey most places, so this is a nice way to ease into the trip (and your wallet)!</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":10301,"align":"center"} -->
<figure id="silverton-mermaids-kids-activities-las-vegas"><img src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/Silverton-Mermaids-las-vegas-with-baby-kids.gif" alt="Kids watching Las Vegas Mermaids at Silverton" class="wp-image-10301" title="Mermaids at Silverton Las Vegas for kids"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":4} -->
<h4><strong>See the Mermaids at Silverton</strong></h4>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After lunch you can walk or drive across the parking lot to the <a href="https://silvertoncasino.com/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Silverton Casino Hotel</a>. There you will find the giant 117,000 gallon reef aquarium, where your babies and kids will LOVE watching the live mermaids swim. The mermaids are so sweet and really do great job interacting with kids, keeping them fully entranced. Mermaid swims are every 15 minutes, Thursday through Sunday starting at noon.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Thursday:</strong> 12:00 – 1:15pm 2:00pm – 3:15pm 5:00pm – 8:15pm </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Friday:</strong> 12:00 – 1:15pm 2:00pm – 3:15pm 5:00pm – 8:15pm </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Saturday:</strong> 12:00 – 1:15pm 2:00pm – 3:15pm 5:00pm – 8:15pm </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Sunday:</strong> 10:30am – 1:10pm 2:00pm – 3:15 p.m. 5:00 p.m. – 6:45pm</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Hot Tip:</strong> While you are there, take a stroll through the calming ambiance of Bass Pro Shop's Outdoor World. The entrance is adjacent to the aquarium.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":4} -->
<h4><strong>Check into hotel</strong></h4>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After seeing the mermaids, it is a good time to check into your hotel. You can get your stuff up to the room, freshen up and let the baby relax a little bit. If baby is crawling or walking, this is good opportunity to let them get their wiggles out.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":10302,"align":"center"} -->
<figure id="family-friendly-luxor-hotel-las-vegas"><img src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/Luxor-baby-friendly-vegas-hotel.gif" alt="Baby-friendly Vegas Hotel Luxor" class="wp-image-10302" title="Baby-friendly Vegas Hotel Luxor"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We recently stayed at the <a linkid="18437" href="/go/the-luxor-hotel-las-vegas/"><strong>Luxor</strong></a>, and it was a great choice for our family since it was a freezing winter and we could get to multiple hotels (<strong><a linkid="18436" href="/go/mandalay-bay-hotel-las-vegas/">Mandalay</a>, <a linkid="18440" href="/go/excalibur-hotel-las-vegas/">Excalibur</a></strong>) easily through indoor connecting bridges.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Hot Tip:</strong> If you want help picking the right hotel for your family, check out our <em>very</em> detailed guide on the best family hotels in Las Vegas <a href="/best-family-hotels-in-las-vegas/"><strong>HERE</strong></a>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Walk around explore hotel/have drink</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Once you feel refreshed, take your crew down to the casino floor and explore your home for the weekend. This is a fun way to see all the exciting things your hotel of choice has to offer. You may even find a entertaining place to pop into for a quick snack or drink before dinner. We like popping into places like, <a href="https://newyorknewyork.mgmresorts.com/en/restaurants/beerhaus.html" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Beerhaus</a>, since they have a great happy hour and lots of games for the little ones. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":4} -->
<h4><strong>Dinner at Casa Di Amore</strong></h4>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Once you book your hotel, be sure to make reservations at <a href="https://casadiamore.com/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Casa Di Amore</a> and arrange for them to <em>pick you up</em>. The pickup shuttle is a complementary service and they will also return you to your hotel after dinner. Our driver (Steve) was very nice and fun to chat with. Just be sure to tip your wonderful driver for their service.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":10303,"align":"center"} -->
<figure id="las-vegas-family-restaurant-casa-di-amore"><img src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/Casa-di-Amore-family-restaurant-Las-Vegas.gif" alt="Las Vegas family restaurant Casa di Amore" class="wp-image-10303" title="Las Vegas family restaurant Casa di Amore"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Casa Di Amore is a happening <a href="/best-family-restaurants-in-las-vegas/" data-type="post" data-id="20281" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener"><strong>family-friendly Italian restaurant in Las Vegas</strong></a>. This local favorite has live music, a dark, cozy atmosphere oozing with old Las Vegas charm, and delicious cuisine. The best part is that it is family-friendly, so you can roll up with your babies and be welcomed with open arms, wine, and pasta!</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":4} -->
<h4><strong>Switch off gambling or go back to room</strong></h4>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Once you are back to your hotel, the night is up to you. You may be tired from traveling the first night, so you may just want to call it a night. If you are up to gambling, here are some options.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Travel with other adults like grandparents that help you switch off with childcare for a couple hours. That is what we typically do. We do a variety of switching throughout the trip.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Utilize local babysitting services. Some hotels have in-house services or will help you arrange with an outside local company. <a href="https://nahusa.com/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Nannies &amp; Housekeepers USA</a> is a trusted company by many of the major hotels. They are licensed and insured as well as certified in CPR and first-aid. You can also contact <a href="https://www.care.com/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Care.com</a> for local Las Vegas childcare.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:stackable/heading {"uniqueId":"5db53b0","textTag":"h3"} -->
<div class="wp-block-stackable-heading stk-block-heading stk-block-heading--v2 stk-block stk-5db53b0" id="day-2" data-block-id="5db53b0"><h3 class="stk-block-heading__text">Day 2</h3></div>
<!-- /wp:stackable/heading -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":10304,"align":"center"} -->
<figure id="las-vegas-with-a-baby-breakfast"><img src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/Breakfast-with-kids-Las-Vegas-baby.gif" alt="Breakfast in Las Vegas with a baby" class="wp-image-10304" title="breakfast with baby in las vegas"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":4} -->
<h4><strong>Long relaxing Breakfast at Café or Buffet</strong></h4>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Ease into the morning with long relaxing breakfast at either the hotel’s café or buffet. It just depends on your style and your appetite. My only recommendation is to take your time and relish your first morning in Vegas.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":4} -->
<h4><strong>After Breakfast/Brunch</strong></h4>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Cold Weather:</strong> If you're visiting during the winter, stay inside as much as possible, because baby, it is COLD outside. This is a great time of day to explore the many exciting shopping areas within hotels or check out an exhibit. We stayed at the <a linkid="18437" href="/go/the-luxor-hotel-las-vegas/"><strong>Luxor</strong></a>, and it is interconnected with <a linkid="18436" href="/go/mandalay-bay-hotel-las-vegas/"><strong>Mandalay Bay</strong></a> as well as <a linkid="18440" href="/go/excalibur-hotel-las-vegas/"><strong>Excalibur</strong></a>, so there was a lot to do and see without setting foot outside. This is also a nice feature of staying at a <a href="/best-family-hotels-in-las-vegas/"><strong>family-friendly resort</strong></a> on the Vegas Strip.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Warm weather:</strong> If you are visiting during the summer or spring, enjoy a pool day. Luxor has one of the largest pools in Las Vegas, but Excalibur has a waterslide and waterfalls. MGM and Mandalay also have <a href="/best-family-pools-in-vegas/" target="_blank" data-type="post" data-id="13741" rel="noreferrer noopener"><strong>good family pools</strong></a>, which you can read more about in our ultimate <a href="/las-vegas-with-kids/"><strong>guide to Vegas with kids</strong></a>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":4} -->
<h4><strong>See a pre-dinner show</strong></h4>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>An awesome evening activity to do with babies in Vegas is to see a pre-dinner show. There are also a ton of family-friendly shows that have <a href="/las-vegas-dinner-and-show-packages/" data-type="post" data-id="16861" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener"><strong>dinner and show packages</strong></a>. Yes, there are shows in Las Vegas that allow babies. We took ours to the <a href="/go/tournament-kings-las-vegas-bov/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener"><strong>Tournament of Kings</strong></a> at Excalibur. There is so much action going on at all times that a loud baby will not be a bother.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":10305,"align":"center"} -->
<figure id="tournament-of-kings-kid-friendly-show-las-vegas"><img src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/Tournament-of-Kings-Vegas-Show-with-baby.gif" alt="Vegas show for kids at Excalibur" class="wp-image-10305" title="Tournament of the Kings family show in Las Vegas"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Hot Tip:</strong> We have carefully researched a number of shows in Las Vegas (21 to be exact!) that allow kids, and we wrote a detailed article to help you pick the right show for your family <strong><a href="/las-vegas-shows-for-kids/">HERE</a></strong>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":4} -->
<h4><strong>Go to Dinner with live music</strong></h4>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After the show, it is time to hit up one of the many electrifying Las Vegas dining options. We don’t like the fun to stop, so we prefer something with live music. I recommend an Irish pub, like <a href="https://newyorknewyork.mgmresorts.com/en/restaurants/nine-fine-irishmen.html" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Nine Fine Irishmen</a> at <a linkid="18434" href="/go/nyny-hotel-las-vegas/"><strong>NYNY</strong></a> or Ri Ra at <a href="https://mandalaybay.mgmresorts.com/en.html" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Mandalay Bay</a>. They are cozy, family-friendly, and the music is lively. We have a <a href="/kid-friendly-live-music-las-vegas/" data-type="post" data-id="13071" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener"><strong>full list of cool Las Vegas restaurants</strong></a> that are kid-friendly and that have live music for more options. Vegas is also filled with a ton of <strong><a href="/celebrity-chef-restaurants-las-vegas/" data-type="post" data-id="16836" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">celebrity and celebrity chef restaurants</a>, which </strong>are fun for the family to experience too!</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":10306,"align":"center"} -->
<figure id="kid-friendly-las-vegas-ri-ra-irish-pub"><img src="https://hotmamatravel.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/Irish-Pub-Live-Music-with-baby-Las-Vegas.gif" alt="Ri Ra Irish Pub Las Vegas Live Music" class="wp-image-10306" title="Kid-friendly Ri Ra Irish Pub with live music in Las Vegas"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":4} -->
<h4><strong>After Dinner</strong></h4>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>If your baby is all good and you still have the energy to keep going here are some options for after dinner.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Cold weather:</strong> The shops and many of the indoor activities will still be open, so feel free to wander around with your stroller until you stumble upon something that interest you. Some hotels even have free indoor entertainment like the <a href="/free-family-activities-las-vegas/" data-type="post" data-id="9291" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener"><strong>Fall of Atlantis</strong></a> show in the Forum Shops.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Warm weather:</strong> When the nights are warm, that is a great time to walk the strip, because city sparkles with lights and is loaded with outdoor entertainment. The main strip is very touristy and loaded with families. If you are worried about safety, I would just suggest to not go out too late. This is a good opportunity to catch some free classic outdoor shows like the Fountains at <a href="/go/bellagio-las-vegas-expedia/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener"><strong>Bellagio</strong></a>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After two full days for activities, you will be exhausted. Time to sink into those comfy hotel beds and dream about all the fun you had in Las Vegas with baby.</p>
