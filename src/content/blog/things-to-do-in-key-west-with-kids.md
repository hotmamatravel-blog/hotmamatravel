<!-- wp:paragraph {"dropCap":true} -->
<p class="has-drop-cap">The Ernest Hemingway House and Museum is a must-visit destination for a Key West family vacation! Not only is it a fascinating historical site, but it's also home to dozens of six-toed cats, which are sure to delight animal-loving kids. Your kids will love exploring the beautiful grounds of the Hemingway House and learning about the life and works of one of America's greatest writers. They can see the desk where Hemingway wrote some of his most famous works and learn about his adventurous life through exhibits and guided tours. And of course, they can't miss out on meeting the friendly cats, who are descendants of Hemingway's beloved pet cats. The Hemingway House and Museum is a unique and educational experience that the whole family will love!</p>

<h4 id="medieval-times"><a href="/go/dry-tortugas-national-park-key-west-fl/" linkid="17708">Dry Tortugas National Park</a></h4>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"dropCap":true} -->
<p class="has-drop-cap">If you're looking for a unique and adventurous family day trip from Key West, then a visit to Dry Tortugas National Park is a must! This beautiful island park is only accessible by boat or seaplane and offers a once-in-a-lifetime experience for families. Your kids will love exploring the historic Fort Jefferson, which is the centerpiece of the park and was once used as a military prison during the Civil War. They can also swim, snorkel, and sunbathe on the park's pristine beaches, where they can see an abundance of marine life such as tropical fish, sea turtles, and even shipwrecks. The crystal-clear waters are perfect for snorkeling, and the park offers rentals and tours for those who want to explore the underwater world. With its stunning scenery, fascinating history, and unique recreational opportunities, Dry Tortugas National Park is an unforgettable destination for families.</p>

<h4 id="medieval-times"><a href="/go/fort-zachary-taylor-historic-state-park-key-west-fl/" linkid="17716">Fort Zachary Taylor Historic State Park</a></h4>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p> &nbsp;<em>601 Howard England Way, Key West, FL 33040</em></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"dropCap":true} -->
<p class="has-drop-cap">Fort Zachary Taylor State Historic Park is a popular tourist attraction located in Key West, Florida. It is a beautiful and historic park that offers a wide range of activities and experiences that make it an excellent destination for families.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The park is home to the historic Fort Zachary Taylor, a Civil War-era fortress that played a significant role in the Union's blockade of Confederate ports. Visitors can take a guided tour of the fort and learn about its history and significance in the Civil War.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>In addition to the fort, the park is also known for its stunning beach, which is one of the best in Key West. Families can enjoy Key West kids activities, like swimming, snorkeling, and sunbathing on the beach. The water is crystal clear, and there are plenty of opportunities to see a variety of marine life, including tropical fish and sea turtles.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The park also has several hiking trails that wind through the natural vegetation and offer beautiful views of the ocean. Families can take a leisurely stroll or a more challenging hike, depending on their preferences.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>For families interested in outdoor activities, Fort Zachary Taylor State Historic Park has several picnic areas and BBQ grills where visitors can enjoy a family picnic. There is also a playground for younger children.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The park hosts several events throughout the year, including historical reenactments, live music, and cultural festivals. These events offer a fun and educational experience for families, and there is always something new and exciting to discover.</p>

<p> &nbsp;<em>Southard St, Key West, FL 33041</em></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"dropCap":true} -->
<p class="has-drop-cap">The US Coast Guard Cutter Ingham Maritime Museum is a historical museum located in Key West, Florida. The museum is housed inside the USCGC Ingham, a decommissioned US Coast Guard Cutter that served for over 50 years in the Atlantic and Pacific Oceans. The museum provides an exciting and interactive experience for visitors of all ages, making it a great activity to do with kids.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>One of the reasons why the museum is a great activity for families is because it offers an opportunity to learn about the history of the US Coast Guard and its role in protecting the country's coastal waters. The museum showcases a variety of exhibits and displays, including artifacts, photographs, and interactive displays that provide insight into the daily life of Coast Guard members.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Kids can explore the various parts of the ship, including the bridge, engine room, and living quarters, which gives them a glimpse into what it was like to live and work on a Coast Guard Cutter. There are also several hands-on activities and interactive exhibits that allow children to experience what it's like to be a Coast Guard member, such as trying on life jackets and participating in a mock search and rescue operation.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The museum also has knowledgeable and friendly staff who are eager to answer questions and share their knowledge with visitors, making the experience even more engaging and educational.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Finally, the USCGC Ingham is a unique and impressive vessel that is rich in history and provides an excellent backdrop for photos and selfies with the family. The ship has a rich and storied history, having served in World War II, the Vietnam War, and other conflicts, making it a fascinating piece of American history that families can explore together.</p>

<!-- wp:heading {"level":4,"UAGDay":[]} -->
<h4 id="medieval-times"><a href="/go/oversease-highway-key-west-fl/" linkid="17718">The Overseas Highway</a></h4>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"dropCap":true} -->
<p class="has-drop-cap">The Overseas Highway is a scenic route that connects the Florida Keys to the mainland, stretching 113 miles from Key Largo to Key West. It is an iconic and breathtaking drive that offers stunning views of the ocean and the unique landscapes of the Florida Keys.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Families will love the Overseas Highway because of the many exciting activities and attractions along the route. The highway passes through several quaint towns and villages, each with its own charm and character, and there are plenty of places to stop and explore.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>One of the highlights of the highway is the Seven Mile Bridge, a famous landmark that offers spectacular views of the ocean and surrounding islands. Families can stop and take photos, or even walk or bike across the bridge for a truly memorable experience.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The highway also passes through several state and national parks, including John Pennekamp Coral Reef State Park and Bahia Honda State Park. These parks offer opportunities for snorkeling, swimming, kayaking, and other outdoor activities, as well as opportunities to see a variety of wildlife and marine life.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Families can also visit several attractions along the highway, such as the Florida Keys Aquarium Encounters, the Dolphin Research Center, and the Turtle Hospital. These attractions offer educational and interactive experiences for children and adults alike.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The Overseas Highway is also known for its delicious seafood restaurants, many of which offer stunning views of the ocean. Families can enjoy fresh seafood and other local specialties while taking in the beauty of the Florida Keys.</p>
