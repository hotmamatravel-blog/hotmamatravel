### [Fairmont Waterfront Vancouver](/go/farmont-waterfront-vancouver-british-columbia/)

</div>

4-Star Hotel  -  Family Suites  -  Connecting Rooms  -  Pool  -  Kids Activities  -  Onsite Restaurant  - Complimentary Bike Rentals for All Ages  -  No Fridge  -  No Complimentary Breakfast

One of the best hotels for kids in Vancouver is the Fairmont Waterfront. They really cater to families, taking the extra step to add details that make a stay special for kids. They have child-size bathrobes, child toiletries, a stepping stool, potty seats, and more. They offer complimentary pool toys as well as complimentary bike rentals, including child-size bikes and child trailers. At the onsite restaurant, ARC, kids 5 and under eat free while kids 6-11 can enjoy an entrée of the adult menu at half price. ARC uses fresh and local ingredients in their dishes and cocktails, including fresh honey from their rooftop bee apiary and herbs from their own herb garden.

The [Fairmont Waterfront](https://www.fairmont.com/en/hotels/vancouver/fairmont-waterfront.html) is located at the cruise terminal and even works with Disney Cruise Line, handling the transfer of luggage. In addition, this hotel’s waterfront location yields some pretty awesome views. The hotel takes advantage of this waterfront splendor by providing ceiling-to-floor windows in the guestrooms and an amazing heated rooftop pool! It is this attention to detail and awesome location that make Fairmont Waterfront our choice as the top kid-friendly hotel in Vancouver.

### [Residence Inn by Marriott Vancouver Downtown](/go/residence-inn-marriott-vancouver-downtown-vancouver-british-columbia/)

</div>

3-Star Hotel  -  Fridge in Rooms  -  Complimentary Breakfast  -  Connecting Rooms  -  Pool  -  No Family Suites  -  No Onsite Restaurant

The Residence Inn Vancouver Downtown is one of the best hotels in Vancouver for families and a great choice if you have a busy itinerary and want a convenient location for a budget-friendly price. Start the morning with a delicious and complimentary breakfast buffet, before exploring the exciting city of Vancouver. There really is no need for an onsite restaurant, because the Residence Inn Vancouver Downtown is walking distance from a plethora of awesome Vancouver dining options.

Although there are suites, the best room for families is the Two-Queen Suite. It is not huge, but will sleep a family of four easily. The [Residence Inn](https://www.marriott.com/en-us/hotels/yvrdr-residence-inn-vancouver-downtown/overview/) does not permit rollaways, but they do offer cribs for those traveling with babies. At the end of the day, come back to the hotel for a nice swim or relax on the outdoor patio around the fire pit. A very nice hotel with good rates is a winner in my book.

### [Century Plaza Hotel &amp; Spa](/go/century-plaza-hotel-spa-vancouver/)

</div>

3-Star Hotel   Family Suites  -  Connecting Rooms  -  Onsite Restaurants  -  Pool  -  Tour Bus Pick Up Location  -  No Airport Shuttle  -  No Complimentary Breakfast

We love Century Plaza Hotel and Spa, and we actually stayed here on our most recent trip to Vancouver. Read our [**Family Visitor's Guide to Vancouver**](/vancouver-with-kids/). Your family will love the spacious rooms, complete with two beds, a pullout sofa bed, and a kitchenette. The hotel has a fantastic location right in the mix of downtown action, offering easy, walkable access to many restaurants and attractions. It is a great option if you are looking for a family-friendly hotel in Vancouver.

If you want to dine onsite, they do have a fabulous restaurant. C Prime was so good that we ate there twice during our visit! They have amazing craft cocktails, a beautiful atmosphere, and homemade pasta. However, if you feel the need to venture out, just walk outside the hotel because [Century Plaza](http://www.century-plaza.com/) is the pickup location for many sightseeing tours. The Capilano Suspension Bridge Trolley, the [**Westcoast Sightseeing bus tour**](/go/westcoast-sightseeing-vancouver-bc/), and the TransLink city bus all have stops right in front of the hotel, which we found SUPER convenient. We definitely recommend visiting [**Capilano Suspension Bridge Park**](/visiting-capilano-suspension-bridge-park/).

Delta Hotels By Marriott Vancouver Downtown Suites shines as the perfect haven for families exploring the lively city.
