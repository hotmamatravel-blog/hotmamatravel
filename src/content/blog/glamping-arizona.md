#### [Grand Canyon Under Canvas](/go/under-canvas-grand-canyon-glamping/)

*979 Airpark Ln, Grand Canyon Junction, AZ 86046*

Grand Canyon Under Canvas is a premier glamping destination situated near the iconic Grand Canyon in Arizona. It offers a unique and unforgettable experience for families seeking to immerse themselves in the beauty of nature while enjoying the comforts of modern amenities.

One of the key reasons why Grand Canyon Under Canvas is ideal for families is its spacious and comfortable accommodations. They provide a range of luxurious tents, equipped with comfortable beds, cozy linens, and private bathrooms. These well-appointed tents offer a rustic yet stylish atmosphere, allowing families to relax and unwind in a serene environment.

The location itself is another highlight for families. Being in close proximity to the Grand Canyon, it allows easy access to explore one of the world's most awe-inspiring natural wonders. Families can engage in a variety of activities such as hiking, guided tours, and stargazing, creating cherished memories together.

Grand Canyon Under Canvas offers family-friendly amenities and services to cater to the needs of all age groups. From on-site dining options serving delicious meals to communal areas for socializing and bonding, the resort ensures a comfortable and enjoyable experience for every member of the family.

#### [Village Camp Flagstaff](/go/village-camp-flagstaff-az/)

*5560 Forest Service 171 Rd., Bellemont, AZ 86015*

Village Camp Flagstaff is ideal for family glamping with two styles of accommodation options, like spacious Adventure Cabins or RV sites These accommodations are designed with comfort in mind, featuring comfortable beds, private bathrooms, and charming rustic décor.

The campsite itself is nestled amidst the scenic beauty of Flagstaff, surrounded by tall pines and breathtaking mountain views. Families can indulge in a wide range of outdoor activities, including hiking, fishing, mountain biking, and even zip-lining. The campsite also offers organized activities and entertainment for children, such as arts and crafts, campfire storytelling, and nature exploration programs, ensuring that every member of the family has an enjoyable and engaging experience.

Village Camp Flagstaff also provides family-friendly amenities and facilities, like a pool with slides and splash pads. There are communal gathering areas where families can socialize and bond, as well as barbecue and picnic areas for shared meals. Additionally, the campsite offers convenience with on-site dining options and stores for any necessary supplies.

Moreover, the location of Village Camp Flagstaff is advantageous for families, as it is situated near popular attractions such as the Grand Canyon, Sedona, and the Walnut Canyon National Monument. This allows families to easily explore and discover the natural wonders and cultural treasures of the region.

#### [The Shash Dine' Eco-Retreat](/go/shash-dine-eco-retreat-glamping-az/)

*HWY 89, Navajo Route 6211 On the Navajo Nation 12 Miles South of Page, Page, AZ 86040*

Shash Dine' Eco-Retreat is a unique and eco-friendly glamping destination located in Page, Arizona. It offers a one-of-a-kind experience for families who want to immerse themselves in the serene beauty of the Navajo Nation while enjoying sustainable accommodations and cultural enrichment.

One of the reasons families will love Shash Dine' Eco-Retreat is its emphasis on sustainable and eco-friendly practices. The retreat features traditional Navajo-style earth dwellings known as hogans, which are made with natural and locally sourced materials. These cozy accommodations provide a comfortable and authentic experience while minimizing the environmental impact.

Families staying at Shash Dine' Eco-Retreat can participate in a range of educational and cultural activities. The retreat offers guided tours and workshops led by Navajo hosts, where families can learn about Navajo traditions, storytelling, arts and crafts, and even try their hand at traditional cooking. These activities provide an immersive experience, fostering a deeper understanding and appreciation of the Navajo culture for both children and adults.

The location of the eco-retreat is another highlight. It is nestled amidst the stunning landscape of the Navajo Nation, surrounded by red rock formations and breathtaking views. Families can explore nearby attractions such as Antelope Canyon and Lake Powell, go hiking, stargazing, or simply enjoy the tranquility of the natural surroundings.

Shash Dine' Eco-Retreat offers a peaceful and secluded atmosphere, providing families with a chance to disconnect from the hustle and bustle of everyday life and reconnect with nature and each other. The retreat encourages unplugged experiences, allowing families to bond and create lasting memories without the distractions of technology.

#### [Under Canvas Zion](/go/under-canvas-glamping-zion/)

*3955 Kolob Terrace Rd, Virgin, UT 84779*

Under Canvas Zion is a captivating glamping retreat located near the stunning Zion National Park in Utah. It offers families a remarkable combination of outdoor adventure, luxurious accommodations, and a deep connection with nature.

One of the reasons families will love Under Canvas Zion is its prime location. Situated just minutes away from the entrance of Zion National Park, families have easy access to the park's majestic red rock canyons, scenic hiking trails, and breathtaking vistas. This proximity allows families to explore and immerse themselves in the natural wonders of the park, creating unforgettable memories together.

The accommodations at Under Canvas Zion are designed to provide both comfort and a sense of adventure. Families can choose from a variety of spacious and beautifully furnished safari-style tents, complete with plush bedding, cozy linens, and private en-suite bathrooms. These tents offer a unique blend of rustic charm and modern amenities, ensuring a comfortable stay for everyone.

Under Canvas Zion also offers family-friendly amenities and activities. The retreat provides a communal gathering area where families can relax, socialize, and enjoy meals together. Additionally, there are organized activities available for children, such as stargazing, campfire storytelling, and guided nature walks. These experiences allow families to bond, learn, and appreciate the wonders of nature in a safe and engaging environment.

Under Canvas Zion understands the needs of families and provides convenience and comfort throughout the stay. The retreat offers on-site dining options, including delicious campfire-cooked meals, as well as convenient access to nearby shops and restaurants for any additional necessities.

#### [Tanque Verde Ranch](/go/tanque-verde-ranch-glamping-az/)

*14301 E Speedway Blvd, Tucson, AZ 85748*

Tanque Verde Ranch is a highly acclaimed destination located in Tucson, Arizona, known for its exceptional family-friendly atmosphere and wide array of activities tailored for all ages. It offers a unique blend of adventure, relaxation, and Western hospitality, making it an ideal choice for families.

One of the key reasons why Tanque Verde Ranch is perfect for families is its extensive range of activities and amenities. The ranch provides numerous opportunities for horseback riding, allowing families to explore the beautiful desert landscapes together. There are also guided hikes, mountain biking trails, tennis courts, a fitness center, and a fishing pond, ensuring that families have plenty of options to stay active and entertained.

The supervised kids' program at Tanque Verde Ranch is another highlight. The ranch offers engaging and age-appropriate activities for children, such as horseback riding lessons, nature walks, arts and crafts, and even cowboy cookouts. This program allows children to have fun, make new friends, and learn about the unique Western heritage of the ranch.

Accommodations at Tanque Verde Ranch are spacious and comfortable, providing families with a cozy retreat after a day of adventure. The ranch offers a variety of lodging options, including Southwest-style casitas and larger haciendas, allowing families of different sizes to find the perfect accommodation to suit their needs.

Dining at Tanque Verde Ranch is a delightful experience for the whole family. The ranch serves delicious and hearty meals, including Southwestern cuisine, with family-style dining options available. Special dining events such as cowboy cookouts and outdoor barbecues add a touch of excitement and create memorable dining experiences for families.

The natural beauty and peaceful setting of Tanque Verde Ranch also contribute to its family appeal. Surrounded by the Sonoran Desert and stunning mountain vistas, the ranch provides a serene and picturesque backdrop for family adventures and relaxation.
