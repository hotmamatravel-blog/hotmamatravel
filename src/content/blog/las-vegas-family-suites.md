Las Vegas may be famed for its vibrant nightlife and glitzy casinos, but it's also an exciting destination for families. With a [**plethora of attractions**](/las-vegas-with-kids/) that cater to all ages, from [**world-class entertainment**](/las-vegas-shows-for-kids/), [**sparkling pools**](/best-family-pools-in-vegas/), to large Las Vegas family suites, it's no wonder many families are choosing Sin City for their vacations.

The first step to a successful family trip to Vegas is finding the perfect accommodation for your family. Luckily Las Vegas is loaded with amazing [**family-friendly hotels**](/best-family-hotels-in-las-vegas/). If you traveling with a family of 5 or more, a standard two-queen room may be too tight.

I will say that you can create family suites by reserving two connecting rooms, which is what we do as a family of eight. You also always stay in a [**vacation rental or villa**](/las-vegas-accommodations-large-family-group/) too. However, you may want even more space and comfort and therefore require a more luxurious family suite inside one of Las Vegas many iconic[**hotels along the strip**](/las-vegas-strip-hotel-map/). I've got you! Here’s a guide to some of the best family suites in Las Vegas that promise comfort, convenience, and plenty of fun for everyone.

### [Westgate Las Vegas Resort](/go/westgate-hotel-las-vegas/)

3000 Paradise Rd, Las Vegas, NV 89109
</div>

Westgate Las Vegas, a family-friendly oasis in the heart of the Strip! With spacious suites, top-notch amenities, and endless entertainment options, [Westgate](https://www.westgateresorts.com/hotels/nevada/las-vegas/westgate-las-vegas-resort-casino/) is the perfect choice for families looking to make unforgettable memories in Vegas. From the dazzling pool area to [**kid-friendly dining**](/best-family-restaurants-in-las-vegas/) and activities, there's something for everyone to enjoy.

**Family Suite** is probably the best room I have seen for families. This 700 sq. ft room sleeps up to eight guests with one king bed and three double beds. The double beds are super cute, bunkbed style with exciting bedding for the kids. Plus the room as everything a family would need, like sofas, arcade games, smart TVs, two bathrooms, and lots of colorful artwork.

**Four Bedroom Presidential Suite** is perfect for a multi-family trip. This room is 2,500 sq. ft and can sleep up to 12 guests. There are four king beds and 2 queen sofas, so for those traveling with small children, you can definitely squeeze in more bodies.

**[See Photos, Reviews, &amp; More](/go/westgate-hotel-las-vegas/)**

### [Mandalay Bay](/go/mandalay-bay-hotel-las-vegas/)

3950 S Las Vegas Blvd, Las Vegas, NV 89119
</div>

Families will experience the best of Las Vegas in a luxurious and fun-filled setting at [Mandalay Bay](https://mandalaybay.mgmresorts.com/en.html). With its spacious suites, [**world-class pool complex**](/best-family-pools-in-vegas/), and a variety of [**family-friendly attractions**](/affordable-family-activities-las-vegas/), Mandalay Bay offers an unforgettable escape. Kids will love the [**Shark Reef Aquarium**](/go/shark-reef-mandalay-bay-las-vegas/), while everyone will love the [**restaurants with live music**](/kid-friendly-live-music-las-vegas/)!

**The Panoramic Two Bedroom Suite** is 1,450sq. ft and features a two queen beds and one king bed, sleeping up to six people. If you have little ones, you might be able to squeeze in a couple more. The room also offers city views, two bathrooms and two televisions.

**Orchid Suite** is 3,825 sq. ft and sleeps up to six people with three queen beds. Bigger than most people's houses, this room also features views of [**The Strip**](/walking-las-vegas-strip/) as well as five bathrooms and televisions.

**Three Bedroom Presidential **is 5,610 sq. ft offers city views and sleeps up to six guests. The rooms offers three king beds, five bathrooms, and 10 televisions!

**Four-Bedroom Presidential Suite** is the ultimate in luxury and the biggest suite offered by Mandalay, sleeping up to eight guests. This 5,610 sq. ft room features four bedrooms, four king bed, five bathrooms, 10 televisions. Of course, it also has amazing city views of the iconic Las Vegas Strip too.

**[See Photos, Reviews, &amp; More](/go/mandalay-bay-hotel-las-vegas/)**

### [Excalibur](/go/excalibur-hotel-las-vegas/)

3850 S Las Vegas Blvd, Las Vegas, NV 89109
</div>

Step into the world of [Excalibur](https://excalibur.mgmresorts.com/en.html), where the magic of medieval times meets modern-day fun for families. With spacious family suites, a fun-filled arcade, and the captivating [**Tournament of Kings dinner show**](/go/tournament-kings-dinner-show-las-vegas/), Excalibur is the perfect blend of entertainment and comfort. Kids will love the castle-themed decor and the exciting pool area, while parents can unwind and enjoy the vibrant atmosphere.

**Resort Luxury 2 Bedroom** is 1,034 sq. ft and offers one king bed, plus two queens or another one king. They say it sleeps four, but if you are traveling with children, you can definitely fit more. The room also offers city views, two televisions and bathrooms.

**Royal Two Bedroom Luxury Suite** is also 1,034 sq. ft, but can sleep up seven with an additional pullout bed. It also features more luxury amenities such an extra bathroom and television.

**[See Photos, Reviews, &amp; More](/go/excalibur-hotel-las-vegas/)**

### [SKYLOFTS at MGM Grand](/go/mgm-grand-hotel-las-vegas/)

MGM Grand Las Vegas, 3799 S Las Vegas Blvd, Las Vegas, NV
</div>

Experience unparalleled luxury at [SKYLOFTS at MGM Grand](https://mgmgrand.mgmresorts.com/en/hotel/skylofts-mgm-grand.html), ideal for families seeking an extraordinary stay in Las Vegas. These spacious lofts offer breathtaking views, personalized service, and top-tier amenities. Families will appreciate the stylish design, private butler service, and access to [MGM Grand’s](https://mgmgrand.mgmresorts.com/en.html) extensive entertainment options, including shows, dining, and the incredible pool complex.

**SKYLOFTS Two Bedroom** **Loft** sleeps seven guests with one king and two queen beds. This 3,000 sq. ft room also offers two bedrooms, two and a half bathrooms, loft, living space, and media room.

**SKYLOFTS Three Bedroom Loft** sleeps nine with two king beds and 2 queen beds. You of course can fit more if you have small children. This room is 6,000 sq. ft with three and a half baths, media room, living space, and butler's pantry.

**[See Photos, Reviews, &amp; More](/go/mgm-grand-hotel-las-vegas/)**

### [The Cosmopolitan](/go/cosmopolitan-hotel-las-vegas/)

3708 Las Vegas Blvd S, Las Vegas, NV 89109
</div>

Discover the chic and vibrant ambiance of [The Cosmopolitan](https://www.cosmopolitanlasvegas.com/), perfect for families looking for a unique Las Vegas experience. With its spacious suites, stunning views, and a variety of kid-friendly activities, this hotel offers something for everyone. Enjoy the bustling pool district, delectable dining options, and the convenient location on the Strip, making it easy to explore all that Vegas has to offer.

**Two Bedroom City Suite** is a luxurious 1,615 sq. ft suite that features one bedroom with a king bed and another bedroom with two queen beds. There are two master bathrooms as well as a powder room. During your stay, you can enjoy floor to ceiling windows with breathing views of the Strip from your bedrooms or spacious living spaces.

If you don't need a big room, I suggest checking the terrace rooms at Cosmopolitan, they are one of the few [**Las Vegas hotels with balconies**](/las-vegas-hotels-balcony-rooms/).

### [Caesar's Palace](/go/caesars-palace-hotel-las-vegas/)

3570 S Las Vegas Blvd, Paradise, NV 89109
</div>

Indulge in the grandeur of [Caesars Palace](https://www.caesars.com/caesars-palace), where families can enjoy luxurious accommodations and endless entertainment. The spacious suites provide a comfortable retreat, while the hotel’s iconic Colosseum hosts[**family-friendly shows**](/free-family-activities-las-vegas/). With a variety of dining options, a sprawling pool area, and the exciting Forum Shops, Caesars Palace offers an unforgettable experience for the whole family.

**Caesar's Palace Octavius Petite Suite** is a luxurious experience for families looking to spread out. Not only does this room have a dining room, wet bar, living space, and whirl pool, you also have the option of connecting an additional two queen room the room. The room on it's own features just one king, so families will likely need to expand.

3555 Las Vegas Blvd S, Las Vegas, NV 89109
</div>

Experience the vibrant and lively atmosphere of the[Flamingo](https://www.caesars.com/flamingo-las-vegas), a classic Las Vegas destination perfect for families. The hotel’s spacious suites and tropical pool area provide a relaxing escape, while the** [Wildlife Habitat](/go/wildlife-habitat-flamingo-las-vegas/)** offers fun for kids of all ages. **Flamingo Bunk Bed Suite** is great for families and features two queen beds, each with an overhead bunk bed. This room is 1,478 sq. ft and offers two bedrooms as well as a common area. You do have to make an inquiry for this room

### [The Linq](/go/linq-hotel-las-vegas/)

3535 Las Vegas Blvd S, Las Vegas, NV 89109
</div>

The LINQ is a modern and energetic hotel that families will love. Featuring spacious suites and the High Roller, the world’s tallest observation wheel, [The LINQ](https://www.caesars.com/linq) offers excitement and comfort. [**Kids and teens**](/things-to-do-in-las-vegas-with-teens/) will love the VR experiences and arcade, while parents can explore the diverse dining options and the vibrant atmosphere of the LINQ Promenade.

**Bunk Bed Deluxe Room** is perfect for families with younger kids. The 350 sq. ft suite features two queen beds and a twin bunk. You can also enjoy a 47-inch LED flat-screen TV, USB charging stations, and a chic design with light, modern furnishings and vibrant pops of color.
