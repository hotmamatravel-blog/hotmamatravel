Whether you’re visiting a city or recently moved there, knowing how to get around is vital, especially if you’re [getting around without a car](/getting-around-las-vegas-without-a-car/). Many cities are not all that walkable and might not be easy to drive through, making public transport your best option for getting from A to B. When arriving in such a city, you should ensure you’re ready to make the best use of the transport system.

## **Get a Metro or Bus Pass**

<div class="ugb-heading__bottom-line">

One of the first things to do when you arrive in a new city is to look into getting a metro or bus pass. Most cities offer day passes, multi-day passes, or even weekly options for public transport. These passes often provide unlimited rides for a set period and are much more convenient and cost-effective than purchasing individual tickets for every journey. Not only will this save you money, but it will also save you time spent trying to figure out fares or wait in line at ticket machines whenever you need to hop on a bus or train. Getting a pass or card can make your experience smoother and more enjoyable, allowing you to focus on your travels rather than logistics.

## **Check if They Have a Public Transport App**

<div class="ugb-heading__bottom-line">

To make your commute even easier, check whether the city has a dedicated public transport app. These apps typically provide real-time updates on transport schedules, routes, and possible delays, helping you plan your trips more efficiently. Some apps also allow you to purchase and store tickets directly on your phone, eliminating the need for paper tickets. By using a public transport app, you can also access offline maps, which can be invaluable if you’re traveling in areas with limited internet access. There are also more general [public transport apps](https://citymapper.com/) that aim to map the transport systems of cities across the globe.

## **Research Your Routes Ahead of Time**

<div class="ugb-heading__bottom-line">

Before setting out on your adventure, it’s a good idea to research your routes ahead of time. Understanding the layout of the public transport system can help you avoid confusion and unnecessary delays. You can find maps of most cities’ transport networks online, either on official transport websites or through travel guides. Familiarize yourself with the major stations, stops, and key routes that you'll likely use during your stay. Knowing which lines or bus numbers connect you to your desired destinations can save you from spending too much time figuring out your route on the go. Make note of transfer points where you may need to switch between modes of transport, such as from a bus to a train or from one subway line to another.

## **Don’t Lug Your Bags Everywhere with You**

<div class="ugb-heading__bottom-line">

Traveling with heavy luggage can be a major inconvenience, especially if you’re using public transport. Navigating crowded buses, trains, and stations while hauling bags can be physically exhausting and can slow you down. To avoid this, take advantage of [luggage storage](https://radicalstorage.com/luggage-storage/barcelona/sants-station) services that are available in most cities. These services are often found at train stations, airports, or even near major tourist areas, allowing you to store your bags securely while you explore the city unencumbered.

## **Always Keep Your Ticket Stubs and Receipts**

<div class="ugb-heading__bottom-line">

It’s essential to always keep your ticket stubs and receipts, especially in cities where ticket inspections are common. In many places, inspectors may board buses, trams, or trains to check for valid tickets. If you cannot produce your ticket when asked, you could be fined on the spot, even if you originally paid for your ride. To avoid any unnecessary trouble, keep your tickets or electronic receipts handy until your journey is over.

## **Set Reminders**

<div class="ugb-heading__bottom-line">

When traveling, it’s easy to lose track of time, especially when you’re immersed in exploring a new city. However, it’s important to set reminders to [give yourself enough time](https://www.crobsabroad.com/blog/2014/02/14/never-miss-a-train-again) to get back to your accommodation or catch your transport, especially if you have a flight, train, or bus to catch. Setting alarms or reminders on your phone can help ensure you don’t miss crucial departures. Public transport systems can sometimes experience delays or service disruptions, so it’s wise to give yourself extra time.

## **Find Staff Who Can Provide Information**

<div class="ugb-heading__bottom-line">

When in doubt, don’t hesitate to approach public transport staff for help. Most transport stations have information desks, and staff members are usually willing to assist with directions, ticketing questions, or any concerns you might have. Even if there’s a language barrier, transport staff are typically well-versed in assisting tourists and can often communicate basic information in English or through visual aids like maps.

It’s easy to get lost in any modern public transport system, so be sure to take the time to do the research that you need. It can help you see a lot more and enjoy your city break without the frustrations and stress of getting lost.
