### [Zak Bagans' The Haunted Museum](/go/zak-bagans-haunted-museum/)

600 E Charleston Blvd, Las Vegas, NV 89104
</div>

***Ages 14 and up**

For the brave souls in your family, [Zak Bagans' The Haunted Museum](https://thehauntedmuseum.com/) is a must-visit. Held in the historic Wengert mansion, this creepy treasure trove features haunted artifacts, spooky exhibits, and chilling stories that will give you goosebumps. Although this journey has narrow passages and twists and turns, the museum is wheelchair accessible, ensuring all guests can view the haunted collection. This attraction is not meant for the littlest ones; however, older kids and teens will love the eerie atmosphere. You must be 14 and over to visit the Haunted Museum. See more cool things to do with your [**teen in Las Vegas**](/things-to-do-in-las-vegas-with-teens/).

### [Bodies The Exhibition](/go/bodies-exhibition-las-vegas-bov/)

3900 S Las Vegas Blvd, Las Vegas, NV 89119
</div>

Kids will be absolutely fascinated by The Bodies Exhibition at [Luxor](https://luxor.mgmresorts.com/en/entertainment/bodies-the-exhibition.html) in Las Vegas! This incredible exhibit offers a unique and educational adventure, showcasing real human bodies in a way that’s both awe-inspiring and enlightening. Imagine peering inside the human body and discovering how everything works, it’s like a science class come to life!

This makes for a perfectly spooky Halloween activity, adding a touch of the macabre to your Vegas vacation. It’s not just about scares; it’s about exploring the amazing intricacies of the human form, which is both thrilling and educational. Plus, the eerie ambiance of the exhibit aligns perfectly with the Halloween spirit, offering a fascinating blend of education and excitement that kids will talk about long after the visit.

### [Mob Museum](/go/las-vegas-mob-museum/)

300 Stewart Ave, Las Vegas, NV 89101
</div>

Not only does The Mob Museum showcase artifacts from some of America’s most notorious and violent events, like gruesome tools used for torture, murder, and spreading terror, but it is  a real haunted location in Las Vegas! The building itself used to be the old Vegas courthouse, where countless mobsters, murderers, and criminals faced justice, often receiving life sentences or death penalties. Many believe that the spirits of these condemned souls linger in the halls, making their presence known as shadowy figures.

The Mob Museum makes for an eerie and educational attraction for families all year long, but is a particularly spooky thing do during the Halloween season. If you don't have kids with you, they also have a cool speakeasy that hosts events throughout the year.
