This beautiful Gulf Coast city has everything you need for an unforgettable family vacation, from pristine beaches to exciting outdoor adventures and world-class attractions. </p>

<h4 id="medieval-times"><a href="/go/tradewinds-st-pete-expedia/" linkid="8747">TradeWinds Island Grand Resort</a></h4>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p> <em>5500 Gulf Blvd, St Pete Beach, FL 33706</em></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"fontSize":"small"} -->
<p class="has-small-font-size">Beachfront - 5 Pools  - Waterslide  -  Kids' Club  -  Live Entertainment  -  Watersports  -  Onsite Restaurants  -  Tiki Bar  -  Firepits  -  Pirates &amp; Princess Parties  -  Paddleboats  -  Airport Shuttle</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"dropCap":true} -->
<p class="has-drop-cap">TradeWinds is one of my favorite St. Pete Beach resorts for families, because of all the kid-friendly amenities and activities that are available. This expansive beachfront resort offers an impressive array of family-friendly amenities and activities that cater to guests of all ages. The resort boasts 5 heated swimming pools, including a toddler wadding pool, a 3-story water slide, a 20,000 square foot beachfront play area, and multiple hot tubs for guests to enjoy.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>But that's not all. The TradeWinds Island Grand also has a variety of supervised kids' programs, including a pirate-themed live show and crafts with Red Beard the Pirate, and a supervised KONK Club for kids aged 4-11. For the adults, the resort has a full-service spa, a fitness center, and a range of water sports such as parasailing, jet skiing, and paddleboarding. There is also a lagoon surrounding the resort, that you can take a leisurely paddleboat ride through. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>When it comes to dining, the TradeWinds Island Grand has numerous options to suit every taste and budget. Families can enjoy casual poolside dining, seafood and steak at a formal restaurant, or grab a quick bite at one of the resort's cafes or snack bars. For more in-depth information see our full review of <a href="/tradewinds-island-grand/">TradeWinds St. Pete Beach</a>. <h4 id="medieval-times"><a href="/go/rumfish-beach-resort-tradewinds-st-pete-beach/" linkid="17408">RumFish Beach Resort -   By TradeWinds</a></h4>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><em>6000 Gulf Blvd, St Pete Beach, FL 33706</em></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"fontSize":"small"} -->
<p class="has-small-font-size">Beachfront  -  2 Pools  -  Tanked Aquarium  -  Surf Simulator  -  Zipline  -  Fire pits  -  Smores    -  Onsite Restaurant  -  Touch Tanks  -  Live Entertainment  -  Watersports  -  Aquarium Snorkeling  -  Airport Shuttle </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"dropCap":true} -->
<p class="has-drop-cap">RumFish Beach Resort is another fantastic family-friendly resort in St. Pete Beach. This beachfront property boasts an impressive 11,000-gallon aquarium that serves as a stunning backdrop to the on-site restaurant, RumFish Grill. This unique feature makes dining at the resort an unforgettable experience for families. You can even snorkel in the aquarium, no joke! For ages 8 and up, you can actually go snorkeling in the aquarium! Before dinner, head to the Outfitter Shop for the cool Touch Tank Experience and get up-close and personal with fun little sea creatures. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>But that's not all the resort has to offer. RumFish Beach Resort features two heated swimming pools, a hot tub, and direct access to the beach, making it easy for families to enjoy the sun and water. The resort also offers a range of water sports rentals, including kayaks, paddleboards, and snorkeling gear. There is even a zipline and surf simulator that my kids loved!</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>At night, families can enjoy live entertainment, fire pits and complimentary s'mores on the beach. It is a wonderful way to enjoy the breathtaking St. Pete Beach sunsets. Also at night, the beachfront pool has color-changing lights, so it makes it really fun to do some night swimming. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The resort's accommodations are spacious and comfortable, with many rooms featuring stunning ocean views. Families can choose from a variety of room types, including suites with full kitchens and multiple bedrooms, which are perfect for larger families. To see videos of our family's experience, watch our <a href="https://youtube.com/playlist?list=PLBukqMU4IGfv860ekuHVvlXqwv1msgQ90" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">St. Pete Vlogs</a>. </p>

<p> <em>3400 Gulf Blvd, St Pete Beach, FL 33706</em></p>

<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"dropCap":true} -->
<p class="has-drop-cap">The Don CeSar hotel is an iconic and historic beachfront property that is a fantastic choice for families visiting St. Pete Beach. The hotel's pink facade and grand architecture make it an instantly recognizable landmark on the beach, dating to the 1920's.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The Don CeSar has a variety of family-friendly amenities and activities, including two heated swimming pools, a kids' club, and direct access to the beach. Families can also rent beach umbrellas, chairs, and cabanas for a day of fun in the sun.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The hotel's accommodations are spacious and luxurious, with many rooms featuring stunning ocean views. Families can choose from a variety of room types, including suites with multiple bedrooms and full kitchens, which are perfect for larger families.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The Don CeSar also offers a range of dining options, from casual poolside dining to upscale seafood and steak restaurants. Families can also enjoy a classic American diner experience at the hotel's on-site restaurant, Uncle Andy's Ice Cream Parlor.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The hotel's location also makes it convenient for families to <a href="/st-pete-beach-with-kids/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">explore St. Pete Beach </a>and the surrounding area. The Don CeSar is within walking distance of many restaurants, shops, and attractions, and the hotel also offers a shuttle service to downtown St. Petersburg.</p>
